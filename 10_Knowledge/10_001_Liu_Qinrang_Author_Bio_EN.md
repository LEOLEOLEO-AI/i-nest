# Liu Qinrang — Author Biography (EN)

**教授 / 博士生导师 — Professor / Ph.D. Supervisor**

Qinrang Liu received the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in communications engineering and communications and information systems from Nanjing Institute of Communications Engineering and PLA Information Engineering University, China, in 1997, 2000, and 2004, respectively. He is currently a Professor and Ph.D. supervisor with the Big Data Research Institute, Fudan University, Shanghai, China. He is a core proponent of software-defined interconnection (SDI) and software-defined system-on-wafer (SDSoW). His research interests include software-defined system-on-wafer (SDSoW), topological computing, physical intelligence, computing architectures, and chip-scale interconnection networks. He has published more than 100 papers and applied for 165 invention patents.
