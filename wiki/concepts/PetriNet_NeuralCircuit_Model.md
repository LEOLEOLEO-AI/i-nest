# PetriNet_NeuralCircuit_Model

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

## Definition
A formal mathematical model using Petri nets to represent biological neural circuits as directed graphs of places and transitions, capturing spiking dynamics and synaptic connectivity. Enables fast hardware prototyping by providing a structured, executable specification that can be directly mapped to neuromorphic hardware architectures.
