**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method that directly optimizes spike timing patterns through evolutionary search, treating the temporal dynamics of SNNs as the objective without requiring differentiable approximations. Preserves the temporal coding advantages of SNNs while enabling training in environments where gradient signals are unavailable, a key requirement for in-network neuromorphic processing.
