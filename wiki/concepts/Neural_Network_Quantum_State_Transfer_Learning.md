# Neural_Network_Quantum_State_Transfer_Learning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The practice of initializing a neural-network quantum state with weights from a pre-trained model on related Hamiltonians, then adapting it to a target disordered system. Reduces computational cost for simulating large disordered systems, enabling faster exploration of topology-dependent quantum behaviors in iNEST architectures.
