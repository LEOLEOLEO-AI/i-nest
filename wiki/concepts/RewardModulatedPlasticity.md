# RewardModulatedPlasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning rule where synaptic weights are updated based on the correlation between neural activity and a global reward signal, enabling online RL without backpropagation. Allows on-chip learning in neuromorphic hardware, reducing the need for external training and enabling continuous adaptation in the field.
