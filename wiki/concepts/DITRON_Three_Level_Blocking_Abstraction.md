# DITRON_Three_Level_Blocking_Abstraction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-06

## Definition
A compiler abstraction in DITRON that decomposes tensor computations into three hierarchical tiling/blocking levels to control data placement, parallelism, and reuse across distributed execution resources. It is crucial for mapping neural tensor dataflow onto TCC/iNEST topologies where locality and communication structure determine achievable performance.
