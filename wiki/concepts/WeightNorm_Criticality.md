## Definition
A mechanism where the interaction between weight normalization and weight decay induces sudden loss spikes due to critical sensitivity in the normalized weight norm. This is crucial for TCC/iNEST systems where distributed training over interconnected topologies may amplify such instabilities, affecting convergence reliability.
