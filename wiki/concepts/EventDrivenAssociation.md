# EventDrivenAssociation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mechanism in spike-based trackers where object-to-track matching is triggered by incoming spikes, enabling asynchronous and low-latency data association. Reduces computational overhead and latency in multi-object tracking, making it suitable for real-time, distributed TCC/iNEST systems.
