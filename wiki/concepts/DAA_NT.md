## Definition
A neuromorphic neuron transistor that uses asymmetric electrodes and IGZO thin film to emulate biological neurons' dynamic adaptive activation threshold. It provides a hardware implementation of dynamic sparse activation, overcoming static-threshold limitations and reducing redundant computation in iNEST systems.
