# Information_Sufficiency

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The minimal amount and type of state or payoff information required for Q-learning agents to sustain cooperative behavior in a topology-constrained system. Determining information sufficiency is critical for designing efficient TCC/iNEST interconnects that support emergent cooperation without global knowledge.
