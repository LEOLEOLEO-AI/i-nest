# RelocationOverheadModel

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A cost model that quantifies the energy and latency penalties of migrating computation (state transfer, cache invalidation, re-synchronization) versus the communication savings achieved. Critical for making profitable relocation decisions in TCC, ensuring that dynamic compute movement does not introduce more overhead than it saves.
