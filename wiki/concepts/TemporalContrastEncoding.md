# TemporalContrastEncoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Encoding motion information as time-stamped contrast events rather than full frames, preserving only dynamic scene changes. Reduces data redundancy and enables sparse computation, directly benefiting both event-driven sensors and SNN inference.
