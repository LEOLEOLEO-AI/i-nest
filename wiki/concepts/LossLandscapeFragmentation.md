## Definition
The property of the optimization landscape in spiking neural networks where local minima are separated by high-error barriers, making gradient descent prone to getting stuck. Understanding and reducing fragmentation is critical for reliable training of deep spiking networks on neuromorphic platforms, as it directly affects convergence and final accuracy.
