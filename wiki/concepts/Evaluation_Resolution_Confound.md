# Evaluation_Resolution_Confound

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where the spatial or temporal resolution at which model-brain representational similarity analysis (RSA) is evaluated systematically biases comparisons between different learning rules, such as SDI and STDP. This is critical for TCC/iNEST because it reveals that apparent superiority of one learning rule over another in neuromorphic or topology-centric models may be an artifact of evaluation granularity, not intrinsic algorithmic merit.
