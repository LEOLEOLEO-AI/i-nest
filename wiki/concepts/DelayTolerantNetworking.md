**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A networking approach that handles long delays and frequent disconnections by storing and forwarding data when links become available. Provides the foundational communication model for UAV-assisted and edge networks in TCC/iNEST, where connectivity is sporadic.
