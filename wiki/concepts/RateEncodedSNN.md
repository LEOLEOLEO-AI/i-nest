# RateEncodedSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A spiking neural network that encodes visual information as spike firing rates across populations of neurons, rather than precise spike timing. Enables robust visual place recognition on neuromorphic hardware, aligning with iNEST's goal of in-network, energy-efficient perception.
