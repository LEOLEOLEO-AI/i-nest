# TopologyAwareStateMigration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The technique of transferring computational state (registers, memory, neural weights) between chiplets or neuromorphic cores along paths that minimize communication disruption and preserve data locality. Bridges TCC and iNEST by enabling seamless compute relocation in both conventional and neuromorphic chiplet systems, essential for adaptive and reconfigurable architectures.
