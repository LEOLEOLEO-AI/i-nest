**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method for converting spiking neural outputs into graded muscle activation signals using rate-coded or temporal-coded spike patterns. Bridges spike-based computation and physical actuation, a key enabler for deploying iNEST controllers on robotic platforms.
