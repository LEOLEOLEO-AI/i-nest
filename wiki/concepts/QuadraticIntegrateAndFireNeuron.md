## Definition
A spiking neuron model where the membrane potential dynamics follow a quadratic function, enabling more complex temporal computations than the standard leaky integrate-and-fire model. Demonstrates that neuron model choice significantly impacts gradient-based learning in spiking networks, potentially improving training efficiency and performance in neuromorphic hardware.
