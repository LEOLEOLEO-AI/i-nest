# Reconfigurable_Spiking_Architecture

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An SNN hardware design that allows dynamic reconfiguration of network topology, neuron parameters, and synaptic weights to adapt to different tasks or input distributions. Enables flexible deployment of diverse neuromorphic workloads within the same hardware, crucial for adapting to changing network conditions in TCC environments.
