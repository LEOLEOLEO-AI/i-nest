# SNN_Spike_Timing_Dependent_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
A learning rule in Spiking Neural Networks (SNN) where synaptic weights are adjusted based on the precise timing difference between pre- and post-synaptic spikes, often implemented via STDP. This provides a biologically plausible, local learning mechanism that can be mapped onto in-network neuromorphic hardware, enabling online adaptation without centralized control.
