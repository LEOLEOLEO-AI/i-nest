## Definition
Distinct phases of neural activity—ranging from asynchronous irregular to fully synchronized oscillations—that emerge from network parameters and connectivity. Controlling synchronization regimes is critical for balancing stability and plasticity in neuromorphic systems, affecting energy efficiency and task performance.
