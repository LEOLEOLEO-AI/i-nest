## Definition
A dimension reduction technique that projects higher-order network dynamics onto lower-dimensional manifolds while maintaining the essential topological invariants (e.g., Betti numbers, connectivity) of the original network. Critical for TCC because it allows compact representations of network topologies without losing the structural features that determine computational behavior.
