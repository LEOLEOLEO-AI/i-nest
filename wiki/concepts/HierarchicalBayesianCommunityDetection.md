## Definition
A Bayesian inference framework that models community structure in functional brain networks at multiple hierarchical levels, jointly estimating connectivity and community assignments. Provides a principled probabilistic approach for discovering modular topology in networked systems, which can inform the design of hierarchical neuromorphic architectures in iNEST.
