# ReverseReplay

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Reactivation of a stored experience in reversed temporal order, classically implicated in credit assignment and value propagation back toward the start of a trajectory. Reverse replay is the key substrate for backward credit assignment in spiking iNEST systems, and this work indicates it can emerge from delay structure instead of dedicated reverse-plasticity circuitry.
