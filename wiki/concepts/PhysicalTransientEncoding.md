# PhysicalTransientEncoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The use of a physical system's natural transient response (e.g., RC delays, memristive dynamics) to encode temporal information into spatial reservoir states. Leverages inherent physical dynamics in TCC interconnects and iNEST devices, eliminating the need for explicit memory elements or clocked delays.
