## Definition
The dynamical regime separating ordered (vanishing signal propagation) from chaotic (exploding sensitivity) activity in recurrent neural networks, marked by a critical branching-like gain. Defines the operating point at which in-network neuromorphic substrates maximize dynamic range, memory, and information transmission while remaining stable under perturbations.
