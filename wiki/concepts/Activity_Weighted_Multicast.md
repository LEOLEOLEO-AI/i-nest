# Activity_Weighted_Multicast

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A traffic-aware partitioning objective that weights multicast hyperedges by the measured firing activity of the source neuron, so that high-firing neurons dominate the placement optimization. It shifts mapping cost from static structural connectivity to dynamic spiking workload, which is essential for realistic energy and latency budgeting in neuromorphic NoCs.
