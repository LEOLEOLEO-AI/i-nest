# Conductive_Filament_Switching

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
The memristor switching mechanism in which resistance states are changed by the formation or dissolution of nanoscale conductive filaments. Filamentary dynamics define the reliability and switching behavior of memristive elements used in TCC and iNEST neuromorphic fabrics.
