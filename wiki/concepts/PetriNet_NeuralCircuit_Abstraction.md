# PetriNet_NeuralCircuit_Abstraction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A formal modeling framework that represents biological neural circuits as Petri nets, where places denote neuronal states and transitions denote spike propagation events. Enables fast hardware prototyping by providing a discrete-event abstraction that can be directly mapped to digital logic for neuromorphic implementations.
