# Soft_NoC

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
A network-on-chip instantiated from general-purpose FPGA lookup-table and routing logic rather than dedicated silicon, whose topology and router parameters are configurable at synthesis time. Soft NoCs let TCC researchers experiment with arbitrary topologies (mesh, torus, tree, custom) and directly contrast their emergent behavior against hardened NoC baselines.
