# EntropyBoundaryMaintenance

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The concept that living or cognitive systems must continuously work against the second law of thermodynamics by maintaining low-entropy internal states, which is formalized as a constraint in free energy minimization. Guides the design of resilient network topologies that preserve functional order under noisy or adversarial conditions, a key requirement for TCC architectures.
