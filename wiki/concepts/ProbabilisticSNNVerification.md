**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A formal verification approach that checks probabilistic properties (e.g., spike probability bounds) of spiking neural networks against specifications, rather than just deterministic behavior. This addresses the inherent stochasticity of neuromorphic hardware, providing guarantees needed for safety-critical iNEST applications like real-time control or edge inference.
