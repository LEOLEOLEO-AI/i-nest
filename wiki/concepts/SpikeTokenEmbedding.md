**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method for converting continuous token embeddings into spike trains, preserving information through temporal coding schemes such as rate or latency coding. Enables direct interfacing of text-based models with spiking neural networks, a critical step for deploying language models on neuromorphic hardware.
