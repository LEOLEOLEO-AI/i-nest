# VectorQuantizedSpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method that maps spike trains to discrete vector quantized codes, reducing the memory footprint of spike-based neural network activations on FPGA. Enables memory-efficient deployment of SNNs on resource-constrained neuromorphic hardware, critical for scaling iNEST systems.
