# UniversalScaling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

## Definition
The property that certain physical quantities (like meson masses) depend only on the critical exponents and the scaling dimension of the perturbing operator, not on microscopic details. Enables transfer of insights between different physical realizations, which is crucial for building robust topological or neuromorphic hardware that exploits criticality.
