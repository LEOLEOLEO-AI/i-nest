**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The representation of local learning rules (e.g., Hebbian or meta-plasticity) as evolvable parameters within the network topology itself. Allows neuromorphic hardware to implement adaptive learning without external controllers, reducing latency and energy overhead.
