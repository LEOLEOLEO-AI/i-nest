# EventDrivenFunctionalConnectivity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The dynamic, activity-dependent wiring between ensembles that is strengthened or weakened based on the timing of rare spike events, rather than static synaptic weights. This adaptive connectivity enables self-organizing computation in TCC/iNEST systems, allowing network infrastructure to reconfigure itself in response to data flow patterns without external control.
