## Definition
A memristor whose resistance state is directly set and modulated by optical stimuli rather than electrical signals, enabling light-driven synaptic plasticity. Supports low-power, high-bandwidth photonic neuromorphic processing essential for iNEST's in-network and edge intelligence.
