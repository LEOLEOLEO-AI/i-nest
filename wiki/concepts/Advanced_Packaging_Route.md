# Advanced_Packaging_Route

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The overall manufacturing and integration strategy (e.g., TSMC CoPoS vs Intel EMIB) that determines trade-offs among density, cost, yield, and thermal performance for multi-chiplet systems. Determines the feasible interconnect bandwidth and latency budgets, which are foundational constraints for TCC's in-network compute and iNEST's synaptic communication.
