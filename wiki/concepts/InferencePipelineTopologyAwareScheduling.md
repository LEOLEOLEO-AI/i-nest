# InferencePipelineTopologyAwareScheduling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
The method of scheduling transformer inference stages (e.g., attention, feed-forward) onto network nodes such that communication hops align with the logical dataflow dependencies. This reduces communication overhead and improves end-to-end inference latency, a key optimization target in TCC for real-time AI services.
