# SubstrateTopologyInduction

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method where the physical topology of a tiny substrate (e.g., defects, grain boundaries, or quantum dots) is directly used to induce computational states, rather than programming logic onto it. Enables TCC to leverage inherent material properties for computation, reducing overhead and enabling ultra-low-power, high-density processing.
