# ScalableNeuromorphicSystem

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An architecture that allows the number of neurons and synapses to be increased without proportional degradation in performance or energy efficiency. Critical for deploying neuromorphic computing in large-scale network infrastructures, where both TCC and iNEST require modular and expandable designs.
