# RareEventSignalCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A coding scheme where meaningful information is transmitted through infrequent, precisely-timed spikes rather than through high firing rates, treating rare events as the primary carriers of signal. This enables ultra-low-power neuromorphic hardware by minimizing spiking activity, directly aligning with iNEST's goal of embedding computation in network switches with strict energy budgets.
