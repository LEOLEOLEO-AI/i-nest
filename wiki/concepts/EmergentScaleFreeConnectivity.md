# EmergentScaleFreeConnectivity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The spontaneous formation of scale-free connectivity patterns in evolving networks, where a few nodes accumulate high connectivity while most remain sparse. This mimics biological neural networks' efficiency and robustness, offering a design principle for neuromorphic systems that balance resource use and fault tolerance.
