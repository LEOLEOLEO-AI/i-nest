# Wafer_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-11

## Definition
Wafer interconnect refers to the high-density intra-wafer and inter-wafer communication links that bind dies or wafers into a single logical compute fabric. It is critical for TCC because the topology and bandwidth of these links determine achievable parallelism and scalability for iNEST workloads.
