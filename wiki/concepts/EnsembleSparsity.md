# EnsembleSparsity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The property of a spiking network where only a small subset of neurons within an ensemble are active at any given time, creating a sparse but reliable population code. Sparsity at the ensemble level reduces communication overhead and synaptic traffic in in-network architectures, making it feasible to implement deep spiking networks on resource-constrained network devices.
