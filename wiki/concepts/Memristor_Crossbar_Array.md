## Definition
A two-dimensional grid of memristor devices at each intersection, used to perform vector-matrix multiplications in a single step via Ohm's law and Kirchhoff's current law. This is the primary hardware structure for efficient neural network acceleration, directly supporting TCC's topology-centric dataflow and iNEST's in-network computation.
