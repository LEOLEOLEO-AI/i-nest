# CrossLayerBiasPropagation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where bias introduced in early layers of a spiking neural network (due to data or hardware) propagates and amplifies through subsequent layers, affecting final output fairness. Understanding this propagation is essential for designing mitigation strategies at both the network and hardware levels, bridging TCC's circuit-level concerns with iNEST's algorithmic fairness.
