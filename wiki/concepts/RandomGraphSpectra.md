The study of eigenvalue distributions of random graphs, particularly how randomness affects spectral properties and the emergence of universal patterns. Provides statistical baselines for understanding the impact of randomness on network dynamics, crucial for designing robust TCC topologies and neuromorphic systems that tolerate noise and variability.
