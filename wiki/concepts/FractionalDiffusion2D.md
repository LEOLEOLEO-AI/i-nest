# FractionalDiffusion2D

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A transport phenomenon in two-dimensional systems where particle displacement follows a fractional (non-Gaussian) diffusion law without requiring quenched disorder or random impurities. Provides a theoretical basis for designing deterministic, low-disorder interconnect topologies that still exhibit anomalous transport, relevant for efficient signal spreading in TCC networks.
