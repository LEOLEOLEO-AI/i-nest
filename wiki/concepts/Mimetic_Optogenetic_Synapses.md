## Definition
Artificial synaptic devices that mimic biological synaptic plasticity using light-based stimulation to modulate conductance in a large-scale array. Enables scalable, energy-efficient physical implementations of neural networks with high connectivity and tunable dynamics for neuromorphic computing.
