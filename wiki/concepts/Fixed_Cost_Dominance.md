# Fixed_Cost_Dominance

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A regime in computing systems where the energy or latency contribution of non-data-dependent operations (e.g., clocking, memory refresh, control logic) exceeds that of data-dependent operations, making further data sparsity ineffective. Applies to both TCC (topology-driven data movement) and iNEST (spike-driven computation), as it determines when architectural changes (e.g., reducing static power) are more impactful than algorithmic sparsity.
