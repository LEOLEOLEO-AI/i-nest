## Definition
An imec optical I/O architecture that integrates silicon photonics with 2.5D packaging to provide high-density, low-power die-to-die optical interconnects. It supplies a packaging-level building block for topology-centric systems that require dense, energy-efficient optical links between compute chiplets.
