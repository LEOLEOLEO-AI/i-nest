# ThresholdBasedSpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method for encoding status updates as binary spike events triggered when a monitored value crosses a predefined threshold, rather than continuous sampling. Enables event-driven communication in neuromorphic systems, reducing data transmission and energy consumption compared to periodic updates.
