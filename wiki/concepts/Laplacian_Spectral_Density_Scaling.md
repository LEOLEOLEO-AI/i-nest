# Laplacian_Spectral_Density_Scaling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The scaling form of the eigenvalue density of the graph Laplacian near zero, ρ(λ) ~ λ^{d_s/2 − 1}, whose exponent directly encodes the effective spectral dimension of a network or inhomogeneous medium. It gives a practical, spectrally computable diagnostic for identifying the effective dimensionality of candidate TCC/iNEST interconnect topologies without running long random-walk simulations.
