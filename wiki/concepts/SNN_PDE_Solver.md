## Definition
A spiking neural network framework that maps partial differential operators, such as diffusion, Poisson, and advection, onto spike-based neuron dynamics to solve PDEs iteratively. This enables low-power, event-driven PDE computation on neuromorphic network hardware, which is central to iNEST's in-network processing vision.
