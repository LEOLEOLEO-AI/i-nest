# RateCodingPrecisionTradeoff

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The limitation of rate coding that averages spike counts over many timesteps, sacrificing temporal precision and adding latency. It motivates novel neuron designs such as MSF for real-time and energy-efficient neuromorphic computing.
