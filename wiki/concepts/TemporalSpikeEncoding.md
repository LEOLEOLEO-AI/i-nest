# TemporalSpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method that encodes continuous token embeddings into temporal spike trains, preserving information through precise spike timing rather than rate coding. Allows high-fidelity representation of dense embeddings in spiking networks, critical for maintaining model accuracy in neuromorphic inference.
