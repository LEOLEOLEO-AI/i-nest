# PreFusionEdgePruning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Pre-fusion edge pruning removes redundant or low-utility edges from Bayesian network structures before they are merged. It is critical for keeping fused inference tractable under the resource, latency, and bandwidth constraints of TCC and iNEST topologies.
