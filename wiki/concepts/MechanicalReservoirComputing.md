**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A computational approach where the robot's physical body dynamics act as a reservoir, transforming proprioceptive inputs into high-dimensional states for classification. Bridges TCC and iNEST by showing that mechanical compliance can serve as a computing substrate, reducing the need for explicit neural network layers.
