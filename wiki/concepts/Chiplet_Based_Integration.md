# Chiplet_Based_Integration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A design and manufacturing paradigm where functions are partitioned into small dies (chiplets) that are integrated at the package level via advanced packaging. Chiplets are the modular building blocks for TCC/iNEST systems, allowing specialized computation and communication primitives to be mixed within a single package.
