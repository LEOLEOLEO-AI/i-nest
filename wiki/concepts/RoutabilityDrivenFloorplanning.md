## Definition
A floorplanning method that optimizes the physical layout of package components (dies, interposers, etc.) with explicit consideration of routing congestion and wirelength constraints. Directly addresses the interconnect bottleneck in advanced packages, enabling higher-density and higher-performance TCC systems.
