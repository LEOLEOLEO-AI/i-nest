# Network_on_Chip_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
The structural arrangement of routers, links, and processing nodes within a chip that defines how data flows across the network-on-chip. It directly determines latency, bandwidth, energy efficiency, and scalability, making it a core design concern for topology-aware computing.
