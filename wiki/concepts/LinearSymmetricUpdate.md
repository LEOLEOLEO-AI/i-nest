# LinearSymmetricUpdate

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A synaptic weight update characteristic where potentiation and depression nonlinearity factors (αₚ, α_d) approach equal and near-ideal values, enabling precise analog learning. Linear symmetric update is critical for accurate inference and training in neuromorphic systems, directly impacting learning capability in iNEST architectures.
