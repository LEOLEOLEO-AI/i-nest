# TopologyAsLogic

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The theoretical stance that logical operations and computational semantics can be directly encoded in the connectivity patterns of a system, rather than in explicit instruction sets or state transitions. This reframes network design as a form of logic synthesis, allowing TCC architectures to implement computations via topology rewiring and iNEST substrates to exploit structural plasticity for learning.
