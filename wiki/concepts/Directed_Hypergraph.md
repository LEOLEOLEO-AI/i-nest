# Directed_Hypergraph

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A higher-order network in which hyperedges have an orientation, encoding asymmetric many-body interactions among three or more nodes. Directed hypergraphs extend topology-centric computing beyond pairwise graphs, enabling richer asymmetric coupling structures for in-network computation.
