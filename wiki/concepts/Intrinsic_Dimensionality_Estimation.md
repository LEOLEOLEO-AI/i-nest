## Definition
A method to quantify the effective number of degrees of freedom in a dataset, often used to predict when modular architectures will outperform monolithic ones in continual learning. Provides a measurable criterion for dynamically deciding whether to allocate new modules or reuse existing ones in a neuromorphic topology, improving resource efficiency.
