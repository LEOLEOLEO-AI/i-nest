# ArtificialProprioception

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
A sensing paradigm where an autonomous robot infers ground conditions from internal mechanical states (e.g., joint angles, forces) rather than external visual or tactile sensors. Enables robust terrain classification in unstructured environments without reliance on vision, aligning with neuromorphic principles of embodied perception.
