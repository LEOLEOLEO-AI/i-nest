# ExactlyFlatBand

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A band with zero dispersion across the entire Brillouin zone, resulting in a macroscopic degeneracy of states that are perfectly localized in real space. Exactly flat bands offer a platform for storing and processing information with zero group velocity, enabling ultra-stable memory elements and energy-efficient computation in TCC/iNEST systems.
