# EventDriven_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A communication mechanism where data transfer is triggered by neural spike events rather than clock cycles, reducing idle bandwidth and power consumption. Critical for iNEST to achieve energy-efficient, low-latency communication across many cores, and for TCC to design topology-aware routing that exploits event sparsity.
