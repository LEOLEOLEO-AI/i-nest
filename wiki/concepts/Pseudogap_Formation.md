# Pseudogap_Formation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A phenomenon in correlated electron systems where a partial gap in the electronic density of states develops at the Fermi level without full superconductivity or insulating order. Provides a theoretical framework for understanding emergent electronic phases that could be mapped to computational state transitions in neuromorphic or topology-based architectures.
