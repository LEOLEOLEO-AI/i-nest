# Resilience_ByTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design approach where network survivability is achieved through redundant, reconfigurable topological structures rather than solely through protocol-level retransmission. Enables TCC architectures to maintain mission-critical flows even when individual links or nodes are destroyed or jammed.
