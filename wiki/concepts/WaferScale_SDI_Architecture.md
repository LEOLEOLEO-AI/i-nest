**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A software-defined interconnect architecture deployed on a wafer-level substrate using a base Mesh as the unified physical interconnect fabric. It enables large-model training and inference systems to physically reconfigure communication paths at wafer scale, addressing latency bottlenecks in distributed neural network workloads.
