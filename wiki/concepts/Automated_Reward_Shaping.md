**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The process of algorithmically generating or adjusting reward signals for RL agents, here driven by LLM outputs, to guide policy learning toward desired network performance metrics. This reduces human effort in designing effective RL rewards, which is critical for scaling iNEST-style adaptive systems to complex, real-world network conditions.
