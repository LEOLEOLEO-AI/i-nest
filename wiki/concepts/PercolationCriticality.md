## Definition
The critical behavior near the percolation threshold of connected rigid or soft regions during the amorphous-amorphous transition, characterized by diverging correlation length and power-law scaling. Offers a theoretical framework for understanding how local structural changes propagate globally, relevant for designing scalable, fault-tolerant in-network computing substrates.
