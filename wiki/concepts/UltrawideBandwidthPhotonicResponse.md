# UltrawideBandwidthPhotonicResponse

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The capability of a photonic memristor to respond to light wavelengths spanning 375 to 1064 nm, covering ultraviolet, visible, and near-infrared ranges. Expands the optical operating window for neuromorphic devices, enabling flexible and wavelength-multiplexed in-network computation.
