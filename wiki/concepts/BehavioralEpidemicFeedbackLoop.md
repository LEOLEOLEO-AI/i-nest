# BehavioralEpidemicFeedbackLoop

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A closed-loop mechanism where epidemic prevalence alters public opinion, which in turn changes vaccination uptake, creating nonlinear dynamics that can lead to oscillations or multiple equilibria. Highlights the need for neuromorphic controllers that can anticipate and stabilize such feedback loops in distributed networks, rather than relying on static policies.
