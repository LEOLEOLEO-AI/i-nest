# Orthogonal_Minimality

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A design principle stating that no primitive may be equivalently replaced by the remaining primitives in O(1) or O(log N) steps, otherwise it is downgraded to an SDK library function. It prevents redundancy in the hardware primitive set, ensuring each primitive contributes uniquely to computational efficiency and resource minimization.
