# EventDrivenMotionDeblurring

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A hybrid approach combining traditional RGB frames with event camera data to reconstruct sharp images from motion-blurred inputs by leveraging high-temporal-resolution brightness change events. Enables efficient motion deblurring in low-light or fast-motion scenarios, which is critical for neuromorphic vision systems operating in real-world dynamic environments.
