# StochasticPathDiversity

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The deliberate use of probabilistic forwarding decisions to create multiple, non-deterministic paths between source and destination. Enables topology-agnostic resilience in iNEST systems where fixed routing tables are impractical due to extreme topological dynamism.
