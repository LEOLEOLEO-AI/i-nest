# LowRankPerturbation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A parameter update strategy where only a small number of directions (low-rank subspace) are perturbed during evolution, reducing search dimensionality. Improves sample efficiency and scalability of evolutionary training for high-dimensional SNN models, relevant for both TCC and iNEST.
