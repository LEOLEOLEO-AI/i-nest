## Definition
A set of low-level computational primitives defined directly on the routing fabric, such as path-based reduction, topology-aware broadcast, or switch-level logic, that serve as the building blocks for higher-level algorithms. This provides the instruction-set-like abstraction for TCC, enabling programmers to express algorithms that leverage the interconnect as the primary compute engine rather than treating it as passive wiring.
