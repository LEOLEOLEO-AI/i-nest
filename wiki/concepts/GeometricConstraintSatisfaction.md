## Definition
A class of problems where constraints are defined by geometric containment relationships (e.g., points inside an ellipsoid) rather than discrete logical clauses, extending SAT frameworks to continuous domains. Offers a bridge between continuous geometry and discrete satisfiability, relevant for TCC's topology-aware placement and embedding of computational tasks in physical network geometries.
