# Dynamic_Topological_Bonding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A structural plasticity mechanism inspired by chemical bond diversity, in which software-defined memristor arrays reconfigure their connection patterns to strengthen or weaken synaptic links. It enables 'use it or lose it' adaptive rewiring at microsecond speeds, far beyond the static adjustments possible in GPU-based simulations.
