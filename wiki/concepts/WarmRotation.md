## Definition
A technique for smoothly transitioning model parameters between domain configurations using rotational interpolation, preserving prior knowledge while adapting to new domains. Reduces catastrophic forgetting and computational overhead during domain shifts, key for resource-constrained neuromorphic and topological computing.
