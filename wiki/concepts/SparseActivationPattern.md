**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The specific set of neurons or channels that produce non-zero outputs for a given input, forming a data-dependent sparse code. Sparse activations can be exploited for event-driven, low-power neuromorphic processing and efficient interconnect traffic in TCC topologies.
