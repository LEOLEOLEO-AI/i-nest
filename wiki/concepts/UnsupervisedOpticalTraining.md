# UnsupervisedOpticalTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training paradigm for photonic networks that uses local, Hebbian-style rules (e.g., spike-timing-dependent plasticity) without external labels or backpropagation, relying solely on optical signal correlations. Removes the need for electronic supervision, reducing latency and power overhead, and aligns with in-network learning for real-time data streams.
