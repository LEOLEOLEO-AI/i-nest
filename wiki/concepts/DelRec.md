## Definition
A learning method for recurrent spiking neural networks that treats synaptic delays as trainable parameters alongside weights to capture temporal dependencies. It enables topology-aware temporal learning in neuromorphic systems by jointly optimizing recurrent connectivity and delay-based spike routing.
