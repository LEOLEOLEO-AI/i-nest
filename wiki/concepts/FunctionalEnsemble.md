# FunctionalEnsemble

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A group of spiking neurons that collectively encode a computational primitive or signal, activated only during rare, meaningful events rather than continuous activity. Shifts computation from dense spike trains to sparse, event-driven ensembles, enabling energy-efficient and robust processing in neuromorphic hardware.
