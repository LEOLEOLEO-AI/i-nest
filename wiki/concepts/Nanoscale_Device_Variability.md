# Nanoscale_Device_Variability

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The device-to-device and cycle-to-cycle variations in electrical characteristics (e.g., resistance states) of nanoscale memristive devices, arising from fabrication and operational stochasticity. Variability challenges the deterministic mapping of topology and weights in TCC and iNEST, requiring topology-aware compensation or stochastic computing paradigms.
