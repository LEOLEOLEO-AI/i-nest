**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A class of quantum circuits whose two-qubit gates remain unitary when the roles of space and time are exchanged, leading to exactly solvable dynamics. Provides an analytically controllable substrate for quantum reservoir computing in cross TCC/iNEST architectures.
