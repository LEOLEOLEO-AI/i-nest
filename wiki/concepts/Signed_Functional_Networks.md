# Signed_Functional_Networks

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Functional connectivity graphs with both positive and negative edge weights that encode excitatory/inhibitory or correlated/anticorrelated interactions. Such signed networks provide a richer substrate for neuromorphic in-network processing than unsigned graphs.
