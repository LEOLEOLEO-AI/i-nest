# Energy_Based_Constraint

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A regularization mechanism using a contrastive energy function to assign low energy to plausible world states and high energy to impossible ones. It provides a principled objective for constraining representations and dynamics in TCC/iNEST systems, improving stability of in-network learning.
