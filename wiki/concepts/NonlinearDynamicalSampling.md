# NonlinearDynamicalSampling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A sampling paradigm where the acquisition process is governed by a nonlinear dynamical system (the reservoir), rather than linear periodic sampling, to encode temporal information more efficiently. This enables novel sampling strategies that exploit system dynamics, relevant for both TCC's physical-layer signal acquisition and iNEST's event-driven processing.
