## Definition
The deliberate introduction of heterogeneous dynamical responses across reservoir nodes (e.g., varying time constants, noise profiles, or nonlinearities) to improve the richness of the feature space. Enhances the separability and generalization of neuromorphic reservoirs, critical for in-network processing where node heterogeneity is naturally present.
