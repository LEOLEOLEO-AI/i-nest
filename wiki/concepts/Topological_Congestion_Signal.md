# Topological_Congestion_Signal

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A routing signal derived from local topological properties, especially negative or positive curvature, that indicates where congestion is likely to form or propagate. It gives TCC and iNEST systems a topology-native congestion indicator that can be computed locally without global traffic-state knowledge.
