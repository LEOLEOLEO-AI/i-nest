# SpikingTopologyEvolution

**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The evolutionary optimization of the structural connectivity (synaptic wiring and neuron placement) of spiking neural networks, including both feedforward and recurrent pathways. This bridges TCC and iNEST by treating network topology as a first-class design variable, enabling hardware-aware SNN architectures that map efficiently onto neuromorphic chips.
