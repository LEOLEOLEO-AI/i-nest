## Definition
A framework for studying quantum systems under continuous measurement, where the interplay of unitary evolution and projective measurements generates nontrivial topological and symmetry properties. Provides a theoretical basis for understanding how measurement-induced phenomena can be harnessed in topological quantum computing and neuromorphic architectures.
