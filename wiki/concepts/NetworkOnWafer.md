# NetworkOnWafer

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A NoC-style communication fabric scaled to span an entire wafer, routing packets among many dies through on-wafer links and switches instead of a conventional intra-chip interconnect. It bridges TCC topology abstractions with iNEST in-network computing by allowing computation to be embedded at wafer-scale routing nodes.
