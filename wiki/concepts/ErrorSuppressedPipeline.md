## Definition
A multi-stage quantum optimization workflow integrating error mitigation techniques at each step to suppress noise accumulation in gate-model hardware. Enables reliable execution of nontrivial binary optimization problems on noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices, which is critical for validating quantum advantage in TCC-relevant combinatorial tasks.
