# TopologyInducedCriticality

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The idea that the underlying network topology of interconnected agents determines whether and how macro-criticality emerges, independent of individual agent dynamics. Central to TCC, as it implies that designing network structures can control emergent critical phenomena, enabling predictable and robust system behavior.
