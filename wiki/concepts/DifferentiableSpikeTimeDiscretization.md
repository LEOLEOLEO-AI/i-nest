# DifferentiableSpikeTimeDiscretization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method that converts continuous-time spike events into discrete time bins using a differentiable approximation, enabling gradient-based training of spiking neural networks. This bridges the gap between biologically realistic continuous-time SNNs and standard backpropagation, making scalable training feasible for neuromorphic hardware.
