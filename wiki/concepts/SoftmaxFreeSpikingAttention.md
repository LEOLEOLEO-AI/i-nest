# SoftmaxFreeSpikingAttention

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An attention mechanism in spiking neural networks that replaces the softmax operation with a spiking-based, event-driven computation to reduce computational cost and enable energy-efficient processing. This is critical for iNEST as it enables scalable, low-power attention mechanisms suitable for in-network neuromorphic hardware, avoiding the high overhead of traditional softmax in spiking language models.
