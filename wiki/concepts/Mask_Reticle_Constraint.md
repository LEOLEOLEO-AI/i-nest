# Mask_Reticle_Constraint

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The physical limit on monolithic die area, capped at approximately 858 mm² (26×33 mm) by photolithography mask reticle size. This constraint forces TCC research to move toward chiplet-based, interconnect-centric multi-die systems to continue scaling AI, HPC, and ADAS performance.
