# Hydrogen_Bond_Driven_Synapse

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
An artificial synapse where PVA and CsPbI₃ form O─H···I⁻ hydrogen bonds to stabilize ion migration and improve synaptic characteristics. This enables more reliable and linear synaptic behavior in perovskite-based neuromorphic devices, addressing a key materials-level challenge for in-network neuromorphic computing.
