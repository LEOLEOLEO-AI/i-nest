# Sleep_Stage_Classification

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The automated categorization of sleep into stages (e.g., deep, REM, light) based on EEG signal features, here using criticality metrics rather than traditional spectral or spindle-based features. Demonstrates that a single, computationally lightweight criticality feature can replace multi-channel polysomnography, reducing sensing and processing overhead for edge neuromorphic devices.
