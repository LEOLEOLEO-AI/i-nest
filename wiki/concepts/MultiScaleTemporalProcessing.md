## Definition
A computational paradigm where a neuron or network simultaneously processes inputs at multiple time constants, allowing detection of both fast transients and slow trends. Addresses a core challenge in neuromorphic computing: representing and integrating diverse temporal dynamics in spiking networks for robust sensory processing.
