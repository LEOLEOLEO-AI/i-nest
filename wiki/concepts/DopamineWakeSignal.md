## Definition
Dopaminergic neurons that signal wakefulness and suppress sleep, acting as a neuromodulatory input that biases the sleep-wake switch. Demonstrates how a single neuromodulator can control global state transitions, informing design of sparse, high-impact control signals in neuromorphic networks.
