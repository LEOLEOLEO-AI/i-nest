# Neuromorphic_Maximum_Likelihood_Decoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The use of neuromorphic processors (e.g., spiking or Ising machines) to perform maximum-likelihood decoding by evolving the system toward the ground state corresponding to the most probable transmitted codeword. It provides a potential low-latency, energy-efficient alternative to traditional digital decoders for error correction in communication systems.
