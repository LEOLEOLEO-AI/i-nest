# AdaptiveEntanglementManagement

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A runtime mechanism that dynamically allocates and reconfigures entangled qubit pairs across quantum cores based on current workload and network conditions. Enables efficient resource utilization in quantum multi-core systems where entanglement is a scarce, perishable interconnect resource.
