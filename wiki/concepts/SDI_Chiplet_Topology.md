**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A wafer-scale chiplet interconnect topology based on SDI that arranges dies to match the data-flow patterns of a target computation. This topology-centric chiplet architecture is a core enabler for scaling TCC systems with energy-efficient, high-bandwidth communication.
