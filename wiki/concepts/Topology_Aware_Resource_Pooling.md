# Topology_Aware_Resource_Pooling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
A resource management strategy that dynamically allocates compute, memory, and network resources based on the physical topology of the supernode to minimize communication distance. This is critical for scaling to 1024 cards, as naive flat topologies become inefficient; TCC must exploit locality to sustain performance and energy efficiency.
