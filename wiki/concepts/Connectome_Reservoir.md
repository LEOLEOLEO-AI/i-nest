## Definition
A reservoir computing substrate whose recurrent network topology is derived from biological connectome data, such as the whale brain's neural wiring. Provides a biologically grounded, high-dimensional dynamical system for neuromorphic computing, potentially outperforming random or engineered reservoirs.
