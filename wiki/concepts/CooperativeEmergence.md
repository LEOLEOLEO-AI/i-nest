**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process by which cooperative behavior spontaneously arises among self-interested agents through iterative interactions and reputation-based feedback. Understanding this is key for designing self-organizing network topologies and in-network neuromorphic systems that can achieve global goals without centralized control.
