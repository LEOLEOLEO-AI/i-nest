## Definition
The concept that the hierarchy of energy barriers in a landscape is determined by the topological structure (e.g., graph connectivity and cycles) rather than by the specific energy values assigned to vertices. Shifts focus from energy parameters to graph topology, enabling design of computing architectures where barrier structure is engineered via network connectivity, crucial for TCC's topology-driven computation.
