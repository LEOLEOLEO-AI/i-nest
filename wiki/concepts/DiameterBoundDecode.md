## Definition
The interconnect property constraining Decode's high-frequency micro-reduction traffic, where network diameter (hop count) dominates latency for thin-vector GEMV operations. For latency-sensitive Decode, TCC must minimize diameter to support many small, frequent messages, contrasting with Prefill's bandwidth-centric needs.
