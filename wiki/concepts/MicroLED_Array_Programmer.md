# MicroLED_Array_Programmer

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A compact array of micro-scale light-emitting diodes used as a spatial light modulator to deliver patterned optical signals directly to a photonic device array for parallel programming. Offers a scalable, low-power, and high-density optical interface for programming large-scale neuromorphic arrays, overcoming bottlenecks of serial electrical addressing.
