## Definition
A population of spiking neural networks that are evolved simultaneously, with each network's topology and parameters optimized in the context of the others to form a synergistic ensemble. This concept is central to scaling neuromorphic computing beyond single-network limitations, allowing distributed spike-based processing across multiple interacting SNN modules.
