# Interconnect_Energy_Bottleneck

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The fundamental cost of physically shuttling data among sensors, buses, memories, and processors, accounting for about 90% of energy consumption in traditional systems. This bottleneck motivates both TCC and iNEST to pursue convergence architectures that minimize data movement and integrate processing directly into the network.
