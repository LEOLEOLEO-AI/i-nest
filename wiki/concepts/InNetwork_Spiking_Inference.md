**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
Execution of spiking neural inference directly within network fabric elements, co-locating computation with data movement to eliminate memory-wall overhead. Forms the foundational principle of iNEST, allowing NSLLM token processing to occur where spikes travel rather than in centralized accelerators.
