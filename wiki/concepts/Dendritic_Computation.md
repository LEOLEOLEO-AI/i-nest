**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The paradigm where dendrites perform nonlinear integration and local processing of synaptic inputs, rather than merely passing signals passively to the soma. This motivates designing neuromorphic network nodes that execute complex local computations, enabling richer in-network processing for iNEST architectures.
