## Definition
A method that reconstructs network couplings from dynamics that are universal across different node types, assuming the observed behavior follows a known class of oscillator or excitable models. Provides a model-agnostic approach to extract topology from neuromorphic or interconnect systems without per-node calibration, key for heterogeneous iNEST hardware.
