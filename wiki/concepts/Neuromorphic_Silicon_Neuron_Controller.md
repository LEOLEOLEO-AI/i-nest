## Definition
A hardware-based controller using silicon neurons to emulate biological neural dynamics for real-time adaptive control of deep brain stimulation. Enables closed-loop, energy-efficient neuromodulation that can adapt to neural state changes, a key step toward implantable intelligent neurotherapeutics.
