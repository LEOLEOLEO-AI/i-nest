# Distributed_Behavior_Control

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A control architecture in which behavioral decisions and actions are coordinated across multiple local circuits and long-range pathways rather than dictated by a central brain command center. This challenges centralized neuromorphic designs and motivates in-network architectures where processing is distributed along communication pathways, matching biological resilience and adaptability.
