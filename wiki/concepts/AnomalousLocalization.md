# AnomalousLocalization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A phenomenon in magnetically doped 2D topological insulators where electron localization deviates from standard Anderson localization due to the interplay of topological edge states and magnetic impurities. This concept is critical for understanding how magnetic doping alters the robustness of topological protection, which directly impacts the design of fault-tolerant topological interconnects in TCC architectures.
