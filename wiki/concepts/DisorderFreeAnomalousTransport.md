## Definition
The mechanism by which anomalous (sub- or super-diffusive) transport emerges purely from the geometric/topological structure of a 2D lattice, not from random disorder. Enables topology engineers to engineer predictable, reproducible diffusion-like behavior in network fabrics without relying on stochastic elements, improving reliability in TCC architectures.
