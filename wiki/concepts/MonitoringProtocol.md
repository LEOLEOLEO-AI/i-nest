## Definition
A specific scheme for performing measurements on the quantum reservoir, including the timing, basis, and feedback strategy, which determines the computational properties of the reservoir. The choice of monitoring protocol directly impacts the information processing capacity and stability of quantum reservoirs, making it a key design parameter for TCC/iNEST integration.
