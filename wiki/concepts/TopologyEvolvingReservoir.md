## Definition
A reservoir computing architecture where the network topology itself is subject to evolutionary mutation and selection, rather than being fixed or randomly generated. Bridges evolutionary computation with topology-centric design, enabling adaptive network structures that can be mapped onto reconfigurable or in-network hardware.
