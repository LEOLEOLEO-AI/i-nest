# OptomechanicalNonlinearity

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The intrinsic nonlinear coupling between optical and mechanical modes in a cavity, which can be enhanced by two-photon driving to produce strong effective nonlinearities. Provides a building block for topological photonic circuits where nonlinearity enables non-reciprocal or reconfigurable topological behavior.
