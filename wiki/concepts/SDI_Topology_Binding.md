# SDI_Topology_Binding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A software-defined interconnect construct that binds logical connectome graph edges to physical or virtual TCC links, enabling reconfigurable routing of neuromorphic traffic. It is the mechanism by which connectome-derived topologies are instantiated and dynamically rewired on TCC substrates without changing application-level neural semantics.
