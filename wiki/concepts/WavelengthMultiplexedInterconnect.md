# WavelengthMultiplexedInterconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
An interconnect architecture that uses multiple distinct optical wavelengths to carry independent data channels simultaneously over shared waveguides. Enhances aggregate bandwidth and parallelism in TCC/iNEST systems, supporting high-throughput data movement and parallel neuromorphic operations without increasing physical link count.
