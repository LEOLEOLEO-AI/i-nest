# EndToEndTopologicalLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A training paradigm where the entire pipeline from raw topological input to final prediction is optimized jointly, including any lifting or representation learning steps, via differentiable operations. Facilitates the deployment of neuromorphic and in-network systems that require adaptive, online learning of topological features without separate preprocessing stages.
