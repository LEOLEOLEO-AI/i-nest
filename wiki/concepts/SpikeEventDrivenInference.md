# SpikeEventDrivenInference

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A computational paradigm where inference is triggered and executed only upon the arrival of spike events, enabling asynchronous, data-driven processing rather than fixed clock cycles. Highlights the need for topology-aware interconnect and scheduling in TCC systems to handle sparse, event-driven spike traffic efficiently across distributed neuromorphic cores.
