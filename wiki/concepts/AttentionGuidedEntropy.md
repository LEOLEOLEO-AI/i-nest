# AttentionGuidedEntropy

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mechanism in spiking transformers that uses attention maps to guide surrogate gradient adaptation by modulating entropy-based loss terms during training. Enables more stable and efficient training of spiking transformers, which is critical for deploying neuromorphic vision models on energy-constrained edge hardware.
