# WaferLevelChipletInterconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
An integration method that connects multiple chiplets through wafer-level interconnects instead of relying on a monolithic defect-free wafer. This allows wafer-scale computing systems to be assembled from smaller known-good chiplets, improving manufacturability and enabling topology-aware communication.
