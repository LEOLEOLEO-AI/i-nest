# Ising_Formulation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mathematical mapping of a decoding problem onto an Ising spin glass model, where the objective is to find the ground state corresponding to the maximum-likelihood codeword. Enables neuromorphic hardware to solve channel decoding by exploiting physical annealing dynamics instead of conventional digital algorithms.
