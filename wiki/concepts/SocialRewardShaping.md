# SocialRewardShaping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of modifying the reward function in reinforcement learning to include social or reputation-based terms, encouraging actions that benefit the collective. This enables the integration of social dynamics into learning algorithms, which is crucial for optimizing collective behavior in interconnected and neuromorphic computing environments.
