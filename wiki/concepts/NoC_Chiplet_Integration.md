## Definition
The use of Network-on-Chip (NoC) principles to connect chiplets within a heterogeneous integrated system, forming a topology-centric communication backbone. Enables TCC to treat chiplet-to-chiplet links as network edges, enabling dynamic routing and in-network processing across the entire system.
