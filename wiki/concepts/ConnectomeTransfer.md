## Definition
The process of transplanting a neural network topology from one organism to another, preserving synaptic connectivity patterns while adapting to different sensory-motor interfaces. This matters for TCC as it demonstrates how topology-centric computing can abstract neural wiring from biological specifics, enabling reusable network architectures across heterogeneous hardware.
