**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A quantum circuit design where the qubit connectivity and gate placement are derived from an input graph (here, the protein interaction graph), rather than a fixed hardware topology. Highlights the principle of graph-driven circuit compilation, which is central to TCC's approach of mapping application graphs onto compute topologies.
