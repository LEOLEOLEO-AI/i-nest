**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The implementation of neuromorphic reinforcement learning algorithms on conventional von Neumann computing hardware rather than specialized neuromorphic chips. It allows iNEST and TCC researchers to prototype and validate spiking control methods without requiring dedicated neuromorphic hardware.
