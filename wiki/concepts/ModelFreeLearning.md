# ModelFreeLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning approach that does not rely on an explicit internal model of the environment or task, instead using direct interaction or heuristics to adjust processing. This reduces dependency on extensive training data and model accuracy, making neuromorphic systems more deployable in unpredictable in-network scenarios.
