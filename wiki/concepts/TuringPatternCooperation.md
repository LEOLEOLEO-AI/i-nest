## Definition
Spatial patterns (e.g., stripes, spots) formed via reaction-diffusion processes that create localized regions of high cooperation, sustained by differential diffusion rates. Enables design of spatial heterogeneity in neuromorphic or topological networks to maintain stable cooperative states without global coordination.
