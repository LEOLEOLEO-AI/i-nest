**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A hyperedge representing a single spike event that must be delivered to multiple destination neurons in a spiking neural network. It captures the inherent one-to-many communication pattern of SNNs, which is essential for multicast-aware NoC mapping and routing.
