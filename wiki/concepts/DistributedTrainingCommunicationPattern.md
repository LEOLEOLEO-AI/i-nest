# DistributedTrainingCommunicationPattern

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The recurring data exchange patterns (e.g., all-reduce, all-to-all, parameter server) used during synchronous or asynchronous training of large language models across multiple nodes or chiplets. Shapes the design of interconnect topologies and routing protocols, as optimizing these patterns reduces training time and energy overhead in TCC and iNEST systems.
