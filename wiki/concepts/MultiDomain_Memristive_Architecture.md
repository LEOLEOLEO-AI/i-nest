# MultiDomain_Memristive_Architecture

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A fusion architecture combining volatile vanadium-oxide devices for frequency generation and non-volatile tantalum-oxide devices for in-memory computing to realize Fourier transforms at the physical layer. Leverages complementary memristor behaviors to boost compute speed to 500 billion ops/sec while cutting power consumption by an order of magnitude.
