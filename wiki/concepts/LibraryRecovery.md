# LibraryRecovery

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of retrieving or reconstructing a set of pre-trained solution patterns (a library) to initialize new tasks without full retraining. Critical for neuromorphic systems that must reuse learned topologies across tasks, reducing energy and latency in edge deployments.
