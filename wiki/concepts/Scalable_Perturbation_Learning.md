# Scalable_Perturbation_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An online self-supervised learning method that uses controlled perturbations to update network weights without backpropagation. Supports on-chip learning in neuromorphic systems where gradient-based training is impractical due to hardware constraints.
