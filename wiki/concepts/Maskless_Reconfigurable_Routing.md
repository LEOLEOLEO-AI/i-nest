# Maskless_Reconfigurable_Routing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A rewiring method that reconfigures wafer-level interconnects without photomasks, allowing post-bonding customization of signal paths across the integrated wafer. This is critical for TCC because it enables adaptive topology reconfiguration and bypass of defective dies in large-scale systems.
