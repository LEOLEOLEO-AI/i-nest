# FiniteSizeCrossover

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A transition in avalanche statistics from scale-free (power-law) behavior to runaway (system-spanning) events that occurs at a critical system size, driven by thermal activation in amorphous solids. This crossover defines a fundamental size limit for predictable, scale-free dynamics in disordered materials, which is critical for designing neuromorphic or topological computing substrates where avalanche statistics control information propagation.
