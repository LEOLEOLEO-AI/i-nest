# LearnableGradientSurrogate

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A surrogate gradient method where the shape or parameters of the gradient approximation are learned during training rather than fixed heuristically. Improves direct training stability and accuracy of SNNs, essential for deploying learning algorithms on resource-constrained in-network neuromorphic hardware.
