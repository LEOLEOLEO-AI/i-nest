# RandomGeometricGraph

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A graph where nodes are placed randomly in a metric space and edges exist if the Euclidean distance between nodes is below a threshold. Models spatially embedded networks (e.g., physical topologies in TCC and neural tissue in iNEST) where locality and distance constraints shape connectivity and percolation.
