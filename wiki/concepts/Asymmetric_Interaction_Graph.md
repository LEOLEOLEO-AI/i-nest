# Asymmetric_Interaction_Graph

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A network topology where the influence between two nodes is not symmetric, meaning the weight of edge A→B differs from B→A, breaking detailed balance. Enables modeling of directed information flow in neuromorphic fabrics, which is critical for realistic cortical dynamics and non-reciprocal communication in interconnect topologies.
