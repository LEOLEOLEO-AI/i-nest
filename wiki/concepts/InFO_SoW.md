# InFO_SoW

**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
Integrated Fan-Out System-on-Wafer, a packaging technology where chiplets or dies are embedded in and interconnected through a fan-out wafer-level redistribution layer. It provides a high-density, low-latency interconnect substrate for massive parallel computing arrays, exemplified by Tesla Dojo for topology-centric AI workloads.
