**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The phenomenon where probability distribution tails shift from exponential (subcritical) to Gaussian (supercritical) as a system crosses a phase transition point. Identifies critical operating regimes in computing networks, guiding parameter selection for stable vs. chaotic computation in TCC and iNEST systems.
