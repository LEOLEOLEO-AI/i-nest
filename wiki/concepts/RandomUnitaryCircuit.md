# RandomUnitaryCircuit

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A discrete-time quantum circuit model in which random two-qubit unitary gates are applied on a fixed lattice geometry, used to capture generic, non-integrable many-body dynamics without fine-tuned parameters. It supplies a minimal, analytically tractable substrate for studying how information propagates across a network topology, directly informing TCC models of topology-centric information flow and iNEST models of stochastic in-network signal evolution.
