## Definition
A discrete graph curvature measure derived from Forman's analogue of Ricci curvature, assigning a scalar value to edges or nodes based on local combinatorial topology. It provides TCC with a rigorous topological metric for identifying bottlenecks, hubs, and congestion-prone regions in complex network interconnects.
