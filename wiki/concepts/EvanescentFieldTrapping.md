## Definition
The use of the decaying electromagnetic field near a waveguide surface to exert optical forces on dielectric particles, enabling non-contact manipulation without free-space optics. Provides a mechanism for integrating bioparticle manipulation with photonic data paths, potentially enabling hybrid biological-photonic computing nodes in iNEST architectures.
