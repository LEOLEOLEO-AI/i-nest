# OnlinePlasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability of a neural network to modify its synaptic weights or parameters in real-time during inference, without requiring offline retraining. Critical for in-network neuromorphic processors that must adapt to streaming data and changing environments without external supervision.
