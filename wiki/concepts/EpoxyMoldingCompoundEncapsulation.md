# EpoxyMoldingCompoundEncapsulation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A process using epoxy molding compound to encapsulate IC packages at wafer and component levels, providing mechanical protection and environmental isolation. Critical for TCC because it affects the physical integrity and thermal-mechanical reliability of 3D-IC and TSV-based topologies, where stacked dies require robust encapsulation to maintain interconnect integrity.
