# Event_Driven_Spike_Processing

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A computation paradigm where neurons operate and communicate only when their membrane potential crosses a threshold, generating discrete spike events. This mechanism creates inherent sparsity and energy efficiency, but requires specialized hardware to manage irregular event streams, a key focus of iNEST designs.
