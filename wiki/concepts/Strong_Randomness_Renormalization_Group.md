# Strong_Randomness_Renormalization_Group

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A renormalization group approach that systematically integrates out high-energy degrees of freedom in systems with strong spatial disorder, leading to an infinite-randomness fixed point. Enables accurate prediction of critical exponents and scaling behaviors in disordered quantum systems, crucial for engineering robust in-network neuromorphic hardware that tolerates material imperfections.
