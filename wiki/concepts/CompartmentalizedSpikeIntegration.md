# CompartmentalizedSpikeIntegration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method where synaptic inputs are integrated separately in distinct compartments before being combined to generate an output spike, allowing independent temporal filtering per compartment. This provides a biologically inspired mechanism to increase the computational capacity of a single neuron, reducing the need for large networks in temporal tasks.
