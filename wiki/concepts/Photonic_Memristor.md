# Photonic_Memristor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A memristor device whose resistance state is optically programmed and read, enabling direct photonic modulation of synaptic weights. It bridges optical input with non-volatile memory, enabling high-speed low-power in-memory computing essential for neuromorphic vision and sensing.
