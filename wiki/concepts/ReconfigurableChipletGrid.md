# ReconfigurableChipletGrid

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A wafer-scale computing architecture composed of chiplets arranged in a reconfigurable grid, where interconnections can be reprogrammed to create custom topologies for specific workloads. It provides the physical substrate for topology-centric computing, enabling scalable and flexible dataflow at wafer scale.
