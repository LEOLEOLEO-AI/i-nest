# ResourceAwareCompilation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A compilation strategy that optimizes quantum circuits by considering the availability, cost, and timing of physical resources such as magic states, qubits, and gates, rather than only logical correctness. This approach is essential for bridging the gap between abstract quantum algorithms and physical hardware constraints, a core challenge in TCC's topology-centric computing paradigm where resource distribution and routing are key.
