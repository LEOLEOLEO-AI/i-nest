**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A wafer-scale integration approach that uses memristors as reconfigurable or synaptic interconnects between processing elements. It supports dense, non-volatile connectivity directly on the wafer, which is critical for large-scale neuromorphic and topology-centric architectures.
