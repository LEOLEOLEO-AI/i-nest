## Definition
A parameter region in the phase diagram where topologically nontrivial and trivial insulating states exist simultaneously, separated by a mobility gap or boundary. Understanding coexistence regimes is essential for designing TCC devices that require precise control over topological edge channels, as it defines operational windows where both states may interfere.
