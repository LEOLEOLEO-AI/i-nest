# Small_World_Fabric

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A communication interconnect topology engineered to exhibit high local clustering combined with short average path lengths between any two cores, mimicking the small-world property of biological neural networks. It provides a topology-centric design lever for balancing wiring cost against spike-delivery latency in large neuromorphic multicore interconnects.
