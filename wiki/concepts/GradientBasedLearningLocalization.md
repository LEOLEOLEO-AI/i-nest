# GradientBasedLearningLocalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The principle of confining gradient computation and weight updates to local neighborhoods of the network, avoiding global error propagation. Aligns with the distributed, event-driven nature of neuromorphic hardware, enabling scalable learning in large-scale in-network systems without centralized control.
