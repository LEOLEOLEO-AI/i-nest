**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A dynamic token pruning mechanism in vision transformers that selects tokens based on foveated visual attention, mimicking human peripheral vision to prioritize high-salience regions. This reduces computational load in vision transformers, which is critical for energy-efficient neuromorphic and topology-centric processing where dynamic sparsity aligns with event-driven paradigms.
