# FPGA_Triple_Energy_Tax

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The compounded energy overhead of FPGA implementations over ASICs, arising from LUT-based logic (10-14x), programmable interconnect capacitance (5-8x), and an always-on blanket clock tree. Quantifying this triple penalty motivates topology-centric ASIC or chiplet approaches for energy-efficient computing on FPGAs.
