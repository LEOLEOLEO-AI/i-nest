# Arithmetic_Tuning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of controlling the critical exponents of a localization transition by varying the number-theoretic properties (e.g., Diophantine approximation quality) of the quasiperiodic modulation frequency rather than its amplitude. Provides a knob for numerically tuning criticality in topological lattices, which could inform the design of TCC interconnect geometries and iNEST reservoir dynamics that exploit critical scaling.
