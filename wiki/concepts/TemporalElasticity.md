# TemporalElasticity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The property of a spiking model to adapt its temporal receptive field or spike integration window dynamically based on input dynamics, rather than using a fixed time constant. Improves robustness to varying gesture speeds and durations, bridging TCC's dynamic network reconfiguration with iNEST's temporal spike processing.
