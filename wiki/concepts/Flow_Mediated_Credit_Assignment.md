# Flow_Mediated_Credit_Assignment

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A credit-assignment scheme in which the direction and magnitude of correlation flow, rather than explicit error backpropagation, determine which synapses receive credit for downstream activity. It offers a biologically plausible, locality-preserving alternative to global error signals, which is essential for scalable learning in both TCC fabrics and iNEST neuromorphic cores.
