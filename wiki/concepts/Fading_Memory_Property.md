# Fading_Memory_Property

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The characteristic of a reservoir where its state depends more strongly on recent inputs than on distant past inputs, ensuring stability and boundedness of the dynamics. This property is essential for real-time, in-network computation where data streams are continuous and memory must be finite and manageable.
