# Chiplet_Based_Architecture

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design methodology where a system is decomposed into smaller, reusable dies (chiplets) that are integrated via advanced packaging rather than fabricated as a single monolithic chip. Supports modular, reconfigurable TCC nodes and enables mixing of specialized compute, memory, and I/O chiplets to match topology-specific requirements.
