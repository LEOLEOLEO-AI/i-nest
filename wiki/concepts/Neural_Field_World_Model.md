## Definition
A world model that represents the environment's state and dynamics as continuous neural fields (e.g., coordinate-based networks) rather than discrete grids or latent vectors. Enables continuous, topology-aware spatial representations that can be mapped onto network topologies for in-network inference and adaptive routing.
