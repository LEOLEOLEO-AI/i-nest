## Definition
A mathematical problem asking which cohomology classes can be realized as fundamental classes of manifolds, connecting algebraic topology to the classification of topological phases. Resolving this problem helps determine the physical realizability of certain topological phases, guiding the search for implementable TCC/iNEST hardware based on exotic quantum states.
