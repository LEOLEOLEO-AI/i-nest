# Memristive_Wafer_Neuromorphic_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A wafer-scale hardware scheme where memristive elements form the synaptic interconnections between neuromorphic cores, embedding the network topology directly in physical interconnect. It enables the dense, brain-like synaptic connectivity required for TCC architectures and in-network neuromorphic processing to scale toward biological parameters.
