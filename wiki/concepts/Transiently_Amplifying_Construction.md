**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A gradient-free method (DAGGER) for building networks that exhibit transient amplification of input signals, a key dynamical feature for short-term memory and signal processing in spiking systems. Provides a practical construction algorithm for iNEST circuits that require controlled transient responses without backpropagation, crucial for real-time neuromorphic processing.
