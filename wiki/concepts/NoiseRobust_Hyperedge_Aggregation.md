# NoiseRobust_Hyperedge_Aggregation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

## Definition
An aggregation mechanism in hypergraph neural networks that down-weights or reweights contributions from hyperedges suspected of containing noisy labels, using attention or variance-based scoring. Directly improves the robustness of neuromorphic in-network processing where noisy synaptic or spike-based labels can degrade learning dynamics.
