## Definition
The use of surrogate gradient methods to train the spiking Siamese network for tracking, enabling backpropagation through non-differentiable spike activations. Makes it feasible to train complex SNN architectures for high-level vision tasks, bridging the gap between neuromorphic computing and practical applications.
