# SparseTemporalEncoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An encoding strategy that represents data using few, precisely-timed spikes, exploiting temporal sparsity to minimize energy and bandwidth in spike transmission. Directly reduces communication overhead in interconnect fabrics and network-on-chip designs, making it a key enabler for TCC's topology-centric dataflow and iNEST's low-power spike routing.
