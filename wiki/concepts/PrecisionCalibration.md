# PrecisionCalibration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Techniques to correct non-idealities (e.g., device variability, conductance drift, parasitic resistances) in analog memory devices to achieve high numerical accuracy in computations. Precision calibration is critical for TCC because topology-centric algorithms often require reliable numerical outputs, and without it, analog computing would be too error-prone for practical use.
