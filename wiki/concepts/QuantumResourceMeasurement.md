# QuantumResourceMeasurement

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The quantification and characterization of non-classical resources (e.g., entanglement, magic states) in a quantum system via measurement protocols, often in the context of deep thermalization. Enables the evaluation of quantum advantage in distributed computing and neuromorphic networks, guiding the design of resource-efficient TCC/iNEST hardware.
