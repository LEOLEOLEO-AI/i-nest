# SeparableRotationDissipation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mathematical decomposition of the reservoir update rule into two independent operators: one for unitary rotation and one for non-unitary dissipation. Facilitates modular implementation in hardware, where rotation and dissipation can be realized by different physical components, improving design flexibility in iNEST.
