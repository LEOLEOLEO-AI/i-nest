# SuperdiffusiveConnectivityPattern

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A network connectivity pattern derived from superdiffusive axonal growth, where long-range connections are more frequent than in diffusive (Brownian) growth, leading to a heavy-tailed degree distribution. This pattern informs the design of efficient, small-world-like topologies in TCC, enabling low-latency communication and fault tolerance in distributed computing fabrics.
