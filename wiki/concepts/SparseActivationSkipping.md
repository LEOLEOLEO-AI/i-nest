# SparseActivationSkipping

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A technique that detects zero or inactive activations in the input stream and skips corresponding compute operations to save energy. Exploits the inherent sparsity of spiking signals to reduce dynamic power, a key enabler for energy-efficient neuromorphic inference.
