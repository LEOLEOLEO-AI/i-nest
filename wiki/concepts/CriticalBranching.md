## Definition
A dynamical regime in recurrent neural networks where the network operates at the edge of stability, characterized by a branching ratio of approximately 1, enabling maximal information processing and sensitivity to input. This mechanism is crucial for iNEST as it provides a theoretical foundation for designing neuromorphic hardware that balances stability and computational capacity, mimicking biological neural criticality.
