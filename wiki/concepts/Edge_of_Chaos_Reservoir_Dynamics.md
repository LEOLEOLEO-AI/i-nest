# Edge_of_Chaos_Reservoir_Dynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The operating regime of a reservoir computer where its dynamics are neither too ordered nor too chaotic, maximizing memory and nonlinear computational capacity for transient tsunami signals. This regime is essential for capturing the sharp, non-stationary features of tsunami waves, enabling accurate prediction without large training datasets.
