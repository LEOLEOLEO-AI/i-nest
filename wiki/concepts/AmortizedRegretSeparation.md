**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A theoretical framework distinguishing between regret incurred during initial learning (amortized) versus regret accumulated over time, showing that warm-starting can reduce long-term regret. Provides formal guarantees for warm-start strategies in TCC/iNEST, justifying their use in resource-constrained, continuous-learning environments.
