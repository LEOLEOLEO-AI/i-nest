# MemristorDevice

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A two-terminal resistive switching device whose conductance can be programmed to represent synaptic weights, enabling non-volatile analog storage and computation. Memristors are key building blocks for TCC crossbars, offering high density and analog programmability essential for neuromorphic and in-memory computing.
