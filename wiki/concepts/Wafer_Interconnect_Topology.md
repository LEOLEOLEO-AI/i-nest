# Wafer_Interconnect_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The physical and logical graph of die-to-die and intra-wafer links that determines hop count, bandwidth asymmetry, and fault domains in a wafer-scale compute substrate. It is the central object of TCC research, since mapping workloads onto this graph dictates achievable communication efficiency and resilience.
