# SpatialPhotonicProgramming

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A method of programming optoelectronic RRAM devices by projecting patterned light from a compact micro-LED array onto the device array, enabling parallel, spatially resolved weight updates. Enables high-throughput, energy-efficient in-memory computing by replacing serial electrical programming with parallel optical writes, critical for scalable neuromorphic hardware.
