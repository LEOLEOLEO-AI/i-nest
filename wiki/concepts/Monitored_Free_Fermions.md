# Monitored_Free_Fermions

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Free (non-interacting) fermionic lattice systems whose unitary evolution is interspersed with local measurement or particle-loss events, retaining Gaussian structure so that the full measurement-conditioned many-body state can be tracked via covariance matrices. Provides a tractable, exactly simulable testbed for how frequent local read-out reshapes correlations, which is directly relevant to TCC's interest in measurement-driven topology and to iNEST's modeling of measurement-coupled in-network dynamics.
