# Magnetotransport_Evolution

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The systematic field- and temperature-dependent evolution of longitudinal and transverse magnetoresistances that reveals magnetic ordering, scattering mechanisms, and topological transport regimes. It supplies a characterization and control knob for reconfigurable TCC interconnects and iNEST synaptic elements whose state depends on magnetic domain and spin-transport conditions.
