# DimensionalityReductionPrinciple

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A theoretical framework showing that the intrinsic computational manifold of a neural network is much lower-dimensional than the ambient neuron count, enabling compression of the state space without loss of task-relevant dynamics. For TCC, this justifies designing topology-centric architectures that route information through low-dimensional bottlenecks, reducing interconnect overhead and enabling more efficient in-network processing.
