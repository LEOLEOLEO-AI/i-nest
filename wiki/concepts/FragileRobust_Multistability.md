## Definition
A classification distinguishing multiple-equilibria phases that survive perturbations (robust) from those that collapse under small noise or parameter drift (fragile). The fragile-versus-robust boundary determines whether stored attractor states in TCC/iNEST networks remain reliable under noise and fabrication variability.
