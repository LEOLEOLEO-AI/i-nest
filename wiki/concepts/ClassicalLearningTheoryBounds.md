## Definition
The application of capacity and generalization tools such as VC-dimension, Rademacher complexity, and stability analysis from classical learning theory to predict the behavior of linear transformer architectures. These bounds give TCC/iNEST designers formal guarantees about when topology-embedded learning rules will generalize rather than overfit their local connectivity.
