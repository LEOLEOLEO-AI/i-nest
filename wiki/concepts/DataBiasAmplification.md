# DataBiasAmplification

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where biased training data is disproportionately amplified in neural representations due to iterative learning dynamics, especially in deep or recurrent architectures. Critical for iNEST systems where learning occurs in-network, as bias amplification can propagate through topology and degrade neuromorphic decision-making.
