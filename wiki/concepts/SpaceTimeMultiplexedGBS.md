# SpaceTimeMultiplexedGBS

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A photonic quantum sampling architecture that combines spatial and temporal mode multiplexing to scale Gaussian boson sampling to beyond 10,000 photons on a chip. This enables large-scale quantum advantage demonstrations that can inform TCC topology-aware resource mapping and iNEST in-network photonic computing.
