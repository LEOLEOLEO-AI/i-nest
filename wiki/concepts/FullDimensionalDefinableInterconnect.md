**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The capability to redefine all aspects of the network-on-chip (NoC) interconnect—including routing, bandwidth, and logical links—across the wafer in a software-controlled manner. Provides the flexibility needed to reshape dataflow paths for diverse AI/HPC workloads, a core enabler for TCC's topology-driven performance gains.
