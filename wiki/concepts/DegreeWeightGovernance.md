# DegreeWeightGovernance

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The principle that the gross response of a network is primarily determined by node degree and edge weight distributions, rather than fine wiring details. Provides a design heuristic for TCC/iNEST systems where approximate connectivity statistics can be used to predict overall behavior, simplifying fabrication.
