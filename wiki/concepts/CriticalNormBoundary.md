# CriticalNormBoundary

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A threshold value of the weight norm (e.g., L2 norm) that separates stable training regimes from regions where loss spikes are probabilistically triggered. Enables predictive monitoring and proactive reconfiguration of network routing or computation placement to keep weights away from this boundary in distributed TCC environments.
