# LocalInteractionDrivenReconfiguration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mechanism where circuit elements adjust their connections and behavior solely based on information from immediate neighbors, rather than global state. This is key for scalable and resilient in-network processing, allowing neuromorphic and topological systems to self-heal and optimize without global synchronization overhead.
