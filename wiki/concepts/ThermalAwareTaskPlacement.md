# ThermalAwareTaskPlacement

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A scheduling method that maps computational tasks onto specific cores based on their thermal profiles and the chip's thermal distribution, aiming to avoid localized hotspots and balance heat dissipation across the die. In TCC, where communication topology and data movement are critical, thermal-aware placement ensures that high-activity nodes are distributed to prevent thermal throttling, preserving the integrity of the network-on-chip and overall system throughput.
