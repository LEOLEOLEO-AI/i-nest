# ChipletOpticalIO

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Optical input/output interfaces integrated at the chiplet edge for high-speed, low-power data exchange with other chiplets or external memory. Critical for both TCC and iNEST to achieve high bandwidth density and energy efficiency in heterogeneous, tightly coupled systems.
