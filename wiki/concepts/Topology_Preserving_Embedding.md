# Topology_Preserving_Embedding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A placement strategy that maps graph connectivity into a geometric or immersive space while minimizing distortion of adjacency, distance, and neighborhood relations. Preserving topological relationships during embedding is essential for TCC to maintain correct interconnect semantics and for iNEST to keep communication locality meaningful in physical or virtual layouts.
