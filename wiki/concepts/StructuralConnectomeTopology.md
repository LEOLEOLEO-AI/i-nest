## Definition
The graph-theoretic and topological representation of neural connectivity patterns, including higher-order features like cycles and cavities, derived from diffusion MRI or similar imaging. Serves as a biological blueprint for designing neuromorphic or TCC interconnects that mimic efficient, resilient, and controllable network structures.
