# SoftwareDefinedSystemOnWafer

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-05

## Definition
A paradigm where the interconnect topology of thousands of chiplets on a wafer is dynamically reconfigurable via software, rather than being fixed at manufacturing time. Enables runtime adaptation of on-wafer communication patterns, which is foundational for topology-centric computing to optimize collective operations like NCCL.
