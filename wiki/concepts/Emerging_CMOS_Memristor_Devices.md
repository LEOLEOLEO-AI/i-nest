**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
Non-volatile resistive switching devices fabricated using CMOS-compatible processes, offering analog state storage and in-memory computing capabilities for neuromorphic circuits. These devices provide the physical substrate for in-network neuromorphic processing, enabling compact and low-power synaptic emulation within TCC's interconnect fabric.
