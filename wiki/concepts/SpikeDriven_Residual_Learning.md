# SpikeDriven_Residual_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A residual learning mechanism specifically adapted for spiking neurons, where skip connections carry analog-like information to mitigate information loss during spike-based forward propagation. Enables deeper and more accurate SNNs by addressing the vanishing gradient and information bottleneck issues inherent in spike-based computation, crucial for scaling neuromorphic models.
