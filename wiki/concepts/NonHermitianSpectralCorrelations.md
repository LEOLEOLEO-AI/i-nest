**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The study of eigenvalue statistics in non-Hermitian random matrices where off-diagonal elements exhibit long-range spatial correlations, deviating from standard independent-entry ensembles. Provides theoretical foundations for understanding signal propagation and stability in neuromorphic networks with non-reciprocal (asymmetric) synaptic connections and long-range wiring.
