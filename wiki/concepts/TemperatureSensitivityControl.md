## Definition
A method to modulate the reservoir's computational properties by precisely controlling the operating temperature, thereby tuning the superparamagnetic switching rates and nonlinear dynamics. Provides a programmable knob for adapting reservoir behavior to different computational tasks without altering hardware, critical for reconfigurable TCC architectures.
