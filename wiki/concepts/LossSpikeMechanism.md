## Definition
The specific dynamical pathway through which normalization layers amplify weight-decay-induced perturbations into sudden, large increases in training loss. Provides a causal model for intermittent training failures that can propagate through network topologies, affecting fault tolerance and recovery protocols in TCC systems.
