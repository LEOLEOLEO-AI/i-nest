# Mesoscale_Superlinear_Computing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
The principle that mesoscale networks with roughly 10^2 to 10^4 nodes can achieve superlinear computing-power gains by optimizing non-linear topological interactions rather than only scaling node counts. It motivates topology reconfiguration and structural-hole repair as first-class levers for improving computation in large-scale software-defined wafer systems.
