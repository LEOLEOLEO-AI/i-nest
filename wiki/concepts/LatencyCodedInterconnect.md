# LatencyCodedInterconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A communication scheme in network-on-chip or interconnect fabrics where data is represented by the timing of signal edges or pulses rather than by multi-bit parallel buses. Enables low-latency, low-power data transfer between neuromorphic cores, aligning with topology-centric computing's focus on efficient data movement.
