# PostHocExplanationAvoidance

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The strategy of designing models that do not require separate explanation modules, as their internal representations are directly interpretable. This reduces computational overhead in resource-constrained in-network neuromorphic hardware, where additional explanation layers would be impractical.
