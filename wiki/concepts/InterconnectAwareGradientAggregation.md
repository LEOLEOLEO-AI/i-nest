# InterconnectAwareGradientAggregation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method for aggregating gradients in distributed training that exploits the physical topology (e.g., hierarchical rings or trees) to reduce communication volume and latency. Reduces the communication-to-computation ratio, which is a key bottleneck in scaling LLM training across chiplets and neuromorphic cores, benefiting both TCC and iNEST.
