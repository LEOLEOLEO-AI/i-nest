# SpikeDrivenAttention

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
An attention mechanism in which query, key, and value computations are performed using spike trains and spike-based operations rather than dense floating-point matrix multiplications. Reduces computational overhead and power consumption in attention layers, critical for scaling transformers on neuromorphic platforms.
