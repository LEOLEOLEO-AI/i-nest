## Definition
A methodological framework for contrasting the efficacy of different synaptic update mechanisms (e.g., spike-timing-dependent plasticity vs. supervised descent inference) in producing brain-like representations. In TCC/iNEST, fair comparison of learning rules is essential for selecting biologically plausible algorithms that can be implemented in topology-centric or in-network neuromorphic hardware.
