## Definition
A method that uses a physical reservoir's transient dynamics to directly acquire and encode analog signals at sub-Nyquist rates, bypassing conventional high-speed ADCs. Enables ultra-low-power, high-bandwidth signal capture in TCC interconnects and neuromorphic front-ends without digital sampling bottlenecks.
