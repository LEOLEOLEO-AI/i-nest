## Definition
A method that uses a physical reservoir (e.g., a nonlinear dynamical system) to directly acquire and transform analog signals, enabling sub-Nyquist sampling and waveform reconstruction without traditional digital preprocessing. This bridges physical-layer signal processing with neuromorphic computing, enabling low-power, high-bandwidth acquisition in TCC interconnects and iNEST architectures.
