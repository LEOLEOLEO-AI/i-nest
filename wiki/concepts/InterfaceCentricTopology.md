# InterfaceCentricTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A network topology design approach where the connectivity and properties of junction nodes are optimized before considering the computational blocks they connect. Enables topology-level optimization of communication patterns, directly impacting latency and bandwidth in distributed neuromorphic systems.
