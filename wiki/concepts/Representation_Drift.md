# Representation_Drift

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The progressive divergence of neural network feature representations from their initial or biological reference states during training, often due to non-biological gradient signals. Understanding representation drift is essential for maintaining stable and interpretable neural codes in iNEST systems, especially when integrating supervised fine-tuning with unsupervised plasticity.
