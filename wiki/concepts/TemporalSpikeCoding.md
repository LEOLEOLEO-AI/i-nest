# TemporalSpikeCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A coding scheme where information is represented by the precise timing or rate of spikes across recurrent connections, enabling temporal feature extraction. Leverages the inherent temporal dynamics of spiking neurons to process time-varying network data efficiently, a core requirement for iNEST's real-time in-network tasks.
