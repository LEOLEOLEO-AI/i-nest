## Definition
An interconnect architecture that mimics cortical-column connectivity, combining dense local module connections with sparse long-range projections across heterogeneous compute, storage, and communication units. This brain-inspired connectivity reduces information loss across functional modules and supports emergent neural-network-like behaviour on wafer-scale neuromorphic substrates.
