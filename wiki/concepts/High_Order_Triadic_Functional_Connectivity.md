## Definition
A higher-order connectivity measure that captures functional interactions among triplets of neural or network nodes, extending beyond pairwise edge-based connectivity. It provides TCC and iNEST with a concrete multi-node topological primitive for representing and computing non-pairwise dependencies in brain-inspired networks.
