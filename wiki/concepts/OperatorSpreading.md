# OperatorSpreading

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The Heisenberg-picture growth of the spatial support of a initially local operator as it is conjugated by successive circuit layers, producing a linearly expanding light cone of non-commutativity. Operator spreading formalizes how a locally injected perturbation becomes distributed throughout a network, giving TCC a quantitative handle on fault/state delocalization and iNEST a model for how spikes propagate beyond their originating node.
