## Definition
A stochastic process where transition probabilities between states are randomly assigned, modeling the behavior of complex networks under uncertainty. Serves as a mathematical foundation for analyzing the robustness and stability of random or reconfigurable interconnection networks in TCC and neuromorphic substrates.
