**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A paradigm shift stating that AI system scalability is limited primarily by data movement and interconnection bandwidth rather than by raw compute throughput. It reorients system architecture research from maximizing single-chip FLOPs to optimizing cluster-level communication for large-model training and inference.
