## Definition
The phenomenon where supervised training of biologically plausible neural networks rapidly reduces the alignment between artificial and biological early visual cortex representations. Highlights a critical limitation in current neuromorphic training approaches, suggesting that biologically plausible learning rules may not preserve brain-like representations under task-driven optimization.
