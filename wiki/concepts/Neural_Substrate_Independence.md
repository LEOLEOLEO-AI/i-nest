# Neural_Substrate_Independence

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The hypothesis that the computational function of a neural circuit is preserved across different physical substrates (biological neurons, silicon neurons, or FPGA-based spiking networks). This is a foundational assumption for TCC and iNEST, as it justifies porting biological connectomes to in-network or neuromorphic hardware without losing functional fidelity.
