# GroupCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A population coding scheme where information is represented by the collective spiking activity of a neuron group, rather than individual spikes. Group coding increases fault tolerance and information capacity, making it suitable for noisy, resource-constrained in-network neuromorphic processors.
