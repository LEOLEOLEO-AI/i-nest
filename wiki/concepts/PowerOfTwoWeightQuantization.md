# PowerOfTwoWeightQuantization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A weight representation scheme where synaptic strengths are restricted to powers of two, allowing multiplication to be replaced by simple bit-shifts in STDP updates. Drastically reduces hardware complexity and energy consumption for on-chip SNN training, making it feasible for resource-constrained iNEST edge devices.
