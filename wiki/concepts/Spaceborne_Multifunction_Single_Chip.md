# Spaceborne_Multifunction_Single_Chip

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A single-chip solution that integrates inference, training, signal processing, and video processing for spaceborne computing platforms. It enables 'one satellite multiple capabilities' without multiple discrete chips, which is a central TCC goal for reducing cost, weight, and power in satellite systems.
