# MembranePotentialAlignment

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A test-time adaptation technique for spiking neural networks that aligns the membrane potential distributions of neurons between source and target domains to improve decoding performance on non-stationary neural signals. Enables robust intracortical neural decoding without retraining, critical for real-time brain-machine interfaces where neural recordings drift over time.
