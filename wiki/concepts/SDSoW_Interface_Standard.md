## Definition
A formal review standard for interfaces between chiplets in a System-on-Wafer (SDSoW) architecture, covering interconnect, packaging, and wafer-level integration. Defines the critical interface specifications that enable scalable, high-bandwidth, and low-latency communication across chiplets on a wafer, a foundational requirement for TCC systems.
