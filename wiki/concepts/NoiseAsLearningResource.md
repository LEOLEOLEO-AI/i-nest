# NoiseAsLearningResource

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The paradigm of repurposing device-level noise (e.g., thermal or shot noise) from being an error source to being an active component of the learning dynamics, guided by stochastic differential equations. Bridges TCC's focus on physical substrate constraints with iNEST's neuromorphic learning, offering a new design principle for robust, self-adapting computational fabrics.
