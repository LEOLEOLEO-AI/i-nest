## Definition
A measure of quantum information content in a subsystem, used to probe critical phases in quasiperiodic systems by tracking how entanglement or information scales across partitions. Provides a novel observable for identifying phase transitions in quasiperiodic lattices, which could inform the design of neuromorphic substrates with tunable criticality.
