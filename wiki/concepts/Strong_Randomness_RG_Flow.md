## Definition
A renormalization group approach that treats disorder as a relevant perturbation, using infinite-randomness fixed points where the flow is governed by rare regions and activated dynamical scaling. Enables prediction of critical exponents in disordered topological systems, guiding the design of iNEST networks that must operate reliably under noisy, inhomogeneous physical substrates.
