## Definition
The study of how external perturbations or inputs drive a network to and maintain it at a critical state, including the dynamics of phase transitions and self-organized criticality. Understanding these dynamics is essential for both TCC and iNEST to design systems that can self-tune to criticality, enabling adaptive computation and representation in dynamic environments.
