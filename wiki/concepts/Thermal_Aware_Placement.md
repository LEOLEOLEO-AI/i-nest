# Thermal_Aware_Placement

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Design methodology that arranges chiplets and power devices based on heat generation and dissipation profiles to avoid hotspots and maintain performance. Critical for maintaining reliability and performance in dense TCC architectures where high-power SiC/GaN devices and compute chiplets coexist.
