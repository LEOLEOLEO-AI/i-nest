**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A training method that reconstructs network parameters directly from target outputs, bypassing iterative gradient-based optimization for spiking neural networks. Enables globally optimal SNN training without backpropagation, critical for efficient on-chip learning in neuromorphic hardware.
