# FrozenModelDirectMapping

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method where a fully trained (frozen) neural network's weights and connections are physically mapped onto a chip's hardware fabric without further training or adaptation. Enables deterministic, low-latency inference by making the model's topology the physical circuit, critical for real-time edge and in-network processing.
