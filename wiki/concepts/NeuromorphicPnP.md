## Definition
A Perspective-n-Point (PnP) algorithm implemented on a neuromorphic processor using spiking neural networks to estimate object pose from event-based camera data. Enables energy-efficient, low-latency pose estimation directly on neuromorphic hardware, critical for real-time robotic perception in edge iNEST deployments.
