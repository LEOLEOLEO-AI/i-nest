**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A theoretical framework where neural population activity is embedded into low-dimensional geometric manifolds that are 'crystallized' into stable, discrete structures through learning, analogous to phase transitions in physical systems. Provides a mathematical basis for designing neuromorphic architectures that can stably encode high-dimensional information in low-dimensional spiking dynamics, crucial for efficient in-network computation.
