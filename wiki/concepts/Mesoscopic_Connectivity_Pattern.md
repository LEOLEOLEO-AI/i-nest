## Definition
The brain-inspired network motif of locally dense short-range connections combined with sparse long-range connections, as seen in 86 billion neurons linked by 150 trillion synapses. This pattern guides the design of physical interconnection topologies that reproduce nanosecond-level neural signaling efficiency in TCC/iNEST systems.
