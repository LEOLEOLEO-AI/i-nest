## Definition
A class of stochastic interface growth models where the height field evolves via a Brownian-like random walk in time, producing non-Gaussian, heavy-tailed distributions. Provides a mathematical framework for understanding noise-driven dynamics in network topologies, relevant to modeling signal propagation in neuromorphic systems.
