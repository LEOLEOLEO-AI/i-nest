**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A wafer fabrication technique that uses high-level metal wires crossing scribe-line boundaries to interconnect neighboring dies on the wafer. It is the key physical enabler for creating a seamless, uniform on-wafer network across distinct reticle fields.
