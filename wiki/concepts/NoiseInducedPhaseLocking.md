# NoiseInducedPhaseLocking

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism where stochastic fluctuations, rather than deterministic forces, drive oscillators into phase-locked states, enabling synchronization in noisy environments. Critical for reliable spike-timing-dependent plasticity and temporal coding in neuromorphic hardware, where intrinsic noise is unavoidable and can be exploited for synchronization.
