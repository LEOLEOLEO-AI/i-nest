## Definition
The study of how collective behavior in a network arises from pairwise or higher-order interactions among nodes, often modeled via coupled differential equations or stochastic processes. Forms the mathematical backbone for predicting emergent computation and robustness in TCC architectures and iNEST spiking networks.
