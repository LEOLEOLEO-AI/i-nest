# ScaledTorusTrainingLimit

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The observation that 3D Torus scales to 9600 chips for training but becomes suboptimal for inference due to increased network diameter at that scale. This quantifies the practical ceiling of torus topologies and motivates TCC's exploration of alternative interconnect geometries for inference-dominant workloads.
