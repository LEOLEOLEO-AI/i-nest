**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A software-defined interconnect layer that exposes reconfigurable topological routes as a programmable abstraction over underlying best-effort hardware links. It lets TCC experiments retarget digital-evolution and iNEST workloads across different wafer topologies without rewriting the compute kernels.
