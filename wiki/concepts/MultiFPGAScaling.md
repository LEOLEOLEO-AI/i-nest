# MultiFPGAScaling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The methodology of partitioning a large SNN across multiple FPGA devices, using the ring topology to interconnect them, and balancing spike traffic to avoid bottlenecks. Overcomes single-chip resource limits, enabling larger and more complex spiking networks that are essential for real-world neuromorphic applications.
