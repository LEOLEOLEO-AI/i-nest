# SpikeTokenization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of converting continuous multimodal input data (e.g., images, text embeddings) into discrete spike trains or spike-based tokens suitable for SNN-transformer hybrid processing. Critical for aligning heterogeneous data modalities with spiking neuron dynamics, enabling seamless integration of SNN front-ends with transformer back-ends in TCC/iNEST pipelines.
