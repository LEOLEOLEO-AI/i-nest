## Definition
A method for analyzing network behavior at critical phase transitions by examining the tail behavior of wavefront arrival times, distinguishing between exponential and Gaussian regimes. This enables identification of critical operating points in interconnect topologies where performance can degrade abruptly, informing design choices for resilience and stability in TCC systems.
