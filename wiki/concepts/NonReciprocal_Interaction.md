# NonReciprocal_Interaction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A coupling regime in which the influence of node A on node B differs from the influence of B on A, breaking detailed balance and driving the system into non-equilibrium steady states. Non-reciprocal couplings mirror directed, asymmetric signaling in in-network neuromorphic hardware, enabling richer dynamical repertoires than symmetric synaptic weights.
