# HigherOrderNetworkDynamics

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
A framework where dynamics (synchronization, diffusion, percolation) are governed not by pairwise node interactions but by multi-body relational structures and their topological shapes. Shifts TCC from node-centric to connection-centric models, enabling representation of emergent behaviors in neural and climate systems that pairwise networks miss.
