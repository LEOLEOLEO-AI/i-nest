**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A network topology that continuously rewires itself based on the functional relevance of its connections, rather than static or purely traffic-driven rewiring. Offers a new design principle for in-network computing where topology evolves to optimize for task-specific survival, improving resilience and performance in dynamic environments.
