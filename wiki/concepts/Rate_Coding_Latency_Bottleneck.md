# Rate_Coding_Latency_Bottleneck

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The limitation of rate coding where information is encoded by average firing rates over many time steps, causing high latency and loss of temporal resolution. Identifying this bottleneck motivates novel spike-based encodings that are essential for energy-efficient, low-latency iNEST systems.
