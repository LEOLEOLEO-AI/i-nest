# MaleCNS_Connectome

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
MaleCNS v1.0 is a full adult male Drosophila central nervous system connectome dataset that provides the complete neuron-level wiring graph reconstructed from electron microscopy, including cell types and synaptic partners. It supplies the definitive biological ground-truth topology that iNEST architectures can be benchmarked against and physically mapped onto when validating that network structure, not just weights, carries computation.
