# Feedforward_SNN_Memory_Limitation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The property of feedforward spiking neural networks where membrane potentials rapidly decay, causing historical information to be forgotten and preventing retention of context beyond a short window. This limitation motivates the need for dual-memory or recurrent pathways in iNEST algorithms for sparse asynchronous temporal data.
