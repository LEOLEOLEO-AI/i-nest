# Microsecond_Topology_Switching

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The capability of a chip to reconfigure its internal topology within microseconds to dynamically switch between different computational modes. This enables a single chip to support both training and inference on edge devices, allowing local adaptation to corner cases without cloud dependence.
