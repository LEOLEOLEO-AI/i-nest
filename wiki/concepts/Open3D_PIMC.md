## Definition
An open-source framework for 3D compute chips that combines processing-in-memory and chiplet-based 3D stacking to enable domestic AI hardware development. It provides a topology-centric, open design pathway for 3D-stacked compute-memory architectures, reducing dependence on proprietary AI chip ecosystems.
