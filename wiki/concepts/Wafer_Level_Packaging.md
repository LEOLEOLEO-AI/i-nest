# Wafer_Level_Packaging

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A packaging process where integration and interconnection are performed at the full wafer scale before dicing, enabling edge-to-edge chiplet fusion. Scales up the number of inter-chiplet links and reduces packaging overhead, allowing larger and more complex topology-oriented compute fabrics.
