## Definition
A neural mechanism that couples arm swing and leg stepping via shared spiking circuits, enabling synchronized upper-lower limb coordination during locomotion. Demonstrates how iNEST architectures can achieve multi-limb coordination without centralized controllers, critical for embodied neuromorphic agents.
