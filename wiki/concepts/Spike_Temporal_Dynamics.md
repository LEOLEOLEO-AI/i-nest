## Definition
The time-varying behavior of spike events and membrane potentials in SNNs, including refractory periods, synaptic delays, and temporal integration, which must be preserved during FPGA mapping. Critical for maintaining functional fidelity when porting SNNs to hardware, as temporal precision directly impacts inference accuracy in neuromorphic systems.
