# DifferentiablePlacement

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A technique that formulates placement/floorplanning as a differentiable optimization problem, allowing gradient-based learning of component positions and pin locations. Enables end-to-end co-optimization of placement and routing, a key requirement for scalable TCC design automation.
