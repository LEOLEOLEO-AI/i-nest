# NoiseInjectionLayer

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A network component that adds controlled stochastic noise to neuron activations during forward passes to enable gradient estimation. Acts as a local learning signal generator, eliminating the need for global gradient synchronization in distributed TCC/iNEST architectures.
