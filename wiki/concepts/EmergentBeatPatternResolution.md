## Definition
The process of identifying and disentangling periodic or quasi-periodic oscillatory patterns that arise from interactions of multiple stochastic delay components. Critical for interpreting and controlling rhythmic activity in neuromorphic circuits, where beat-like patterns can encode information or indicate instability.
