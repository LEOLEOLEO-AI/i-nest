**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A femtosecond laser-based method for slicing 4H-SiC wafers with self-focusing control to achieve precise depth control, enabling thin, high-quality substrate layers. Enables fabrication of ultra-thin, uniform SiC layers critical for advanced interconnects and substrate integration in TCC architectures.
