## Definition
A reservoir computing system that uses thermally driven superparamagnetic nanomagnets as nonlinear dynamical nodes, where temperature fluctuations govern the reservoir's state evolution. Enables energy-efficient, passive physical reservoirs for TCC by leveraging intrinsic thermal noise rather than active external driving, reducing power overhead in in-network neuromorphic substrates.
