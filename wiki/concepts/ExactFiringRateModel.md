# ExactFiringRateModel

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spiking neuron model where the firing rate is computed exactly from the membrane potential dynamics, enabling precise analytical treatment of network activity. Provides a mathematically tractable framework for studying chaos and symmetry in sparse random networks, which is essential for designing reliable neuromorphic circuits.
