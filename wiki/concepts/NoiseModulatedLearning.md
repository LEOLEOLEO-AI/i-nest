# NoiseModulatedLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A training paradigm where controlled noise is injected into neural network activations, and the resulting statistical fluctuations are used to derive learning signals. Leverages inherent stochasticity of neuromorphic devices as a computational resource rather than a nuisance.
