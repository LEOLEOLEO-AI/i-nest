# Arithmetic_Intensity_Mismatch

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The fundamental mismatch between SNNs' low arithmetic intensity and memory-dominated access patterns versus the dense matrix-multiply strengths of GPU/TPU accelerators. Motivates specialized architectures such as FeNN-DMA and in-network neuromorphic processing to address SNN-specific computational inefficiencies.
