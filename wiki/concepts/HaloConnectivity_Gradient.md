## Definition
A graded connectivity pattern where synaptic or routing resources decrease monotonically from a core region to a peripheral halo, following a complexity-maximizing distribution. Guides the physical layout of in-network neuromorphic cores and their interconnects to achieve efficient resource allocation and high information integration.
