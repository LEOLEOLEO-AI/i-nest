# Switchless_Computing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A paradigm where centralized high-radix switching ASICs are eliminated, and switching functionality is decomposed and embedded into each compute node's internal NoC router as a natural extension. Represents a fundamental architectural shift for TCC, enabling wafer-scale integration by removing costly, power-hungry external switches and reducing end-to-end latency.
