## Definition
A learning principle where the brain or network dynamically modifies the ease of access to computational resources or pathways based on task demands, rather than altering the resources themselves. Provides a theoretical framework for adaptive routing and resource allocation in TCC, enabling efficient reconfiguration of in-network computation without full rewiring.
