# Topology_Dependent_Synchronization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The dependence of collective phase-locking and order-parameter behavior on the graph structure (degree distribution, clustering, spectral gap) of the coupling network rather than on node-level dynamics alone. It is the core TCC claim that topology, not just component behavior, governs computation and criticality in coupled systems.
