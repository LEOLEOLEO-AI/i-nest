# MonitoredQuantumCircuit

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A quantum circuit where projective measurements are interspersed with unitary evolution, leading to measurement-induced phase transitions in entanglement structure. These circuits model the dynamics of measurement-driven information processing, which is fundamental to in-network neuromorphic computing where measurement and feedback are core operations.
