# InSituLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability of a photonic network to adapt its weights or states during operation, directly in hardware, without offline training. Eliminates the need for separate training phases, making the network truly autonomous and energy-efficient for real-time neuromorphic tasks.
