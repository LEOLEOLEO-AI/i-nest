# Direct_Threshold_Sensing

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A sensing mechanism where a biological voltage exceeding V_SET triggers an irreversible memristor state transition, producing a binary alarm output. Eliminates the need for ADC/MCU computation, enabling ultra-low-power and low-latency sensing for in-network neuromorphic edge devices.
