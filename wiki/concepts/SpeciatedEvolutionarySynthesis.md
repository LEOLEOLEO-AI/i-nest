# SpeciatedEvolutionarySynthesis

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A genetic-algorithm-based method that groups candidate circuit topologies into species by similarity to preserve diversity and explore multiple topological niches simultaneously. Enables scalable discovery of non-intuitive interconnect topologies that outperform hand-designed ones in TCC architectures.
