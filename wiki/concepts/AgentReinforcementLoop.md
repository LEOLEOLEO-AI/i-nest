**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A feedback mechanism where individual agents' corrupt actions increase the likelihood of similar actions in connected agents, creating a self-reinforcing cycle. Models positive feedback in distributed systems, relevant to iNEST for understanding how local interactions can lead to global computational states.
