**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The process by which task-driven supervised optimization, even with biologically plausible rules, erodes the emergent unsupervised structure of early sensory representations. Informs TCC/iNEST system designers about trade-offs between task performance and biological fidelity, guiding hybrid training or regularization strategies.
