# Eigenvalue_Degeneracy

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The occurrence of two or more eigenvalues of a matrix being exactly equal, often leading to exceptional points in non-Hermitian systems. Degeneracies in non-Hermitian matrices are critical for understanding phase transitions and sensitivity amplification in neuromorphic and topological computing.
