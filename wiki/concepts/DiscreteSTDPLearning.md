# DiscreteSTDPLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A variant of spike-timing-dependent plasticity where synaptic weight updates are computed in discrete steps rather than continuously, simplifying hardware implementation. Facilitates on-chip learning in neuromorphic hardware, reducing computational overhead for in-network adaptation.
