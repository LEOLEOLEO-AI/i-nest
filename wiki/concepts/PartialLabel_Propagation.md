**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A mechanism that propagates label information through the Hopfield network's connectivity graph to infer labels for unconnected or weakly connected nodes. Enables scalable semi-supervised learning in distributed TCC/iNEST architectures, reducing the need for global label synchronization.
