# MarginalContributionFitness

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A fitness assignment method for evolving spiking neural network ensembles where each network's contribution is evaluated by its marginal impact on the ensemble's collective performance, rather than its standalone accuracy. This enables cooperative co-evolution of SNN ensembles, directly addressing the challenge of credit assignment in neuromorphic systems where individual spiking neurons contribute non-linearly to population-level behavior.
