## Definition
Quantitative and qualitative measures used to assess the presence and severity of bias in model outputs, including group fairness metrics and counterfactual testing. Essential for validating whether in-network neuromorphic implementations preserve or reduce bias compared to conventional architectures, supporting trustworthy deployment.
