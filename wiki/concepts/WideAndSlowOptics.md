# WideAndSlowOptics

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-24

## Definition
A silicon-photonic link design strategy that uses many parallel low-speed optical lanes instead of few high-speed lanes to reduce power consumption by about 40%. It offers a power-efficient signaling paradigm for TCC interconnect fabrics where parallelism and topology can dominate over per-lane speed.
