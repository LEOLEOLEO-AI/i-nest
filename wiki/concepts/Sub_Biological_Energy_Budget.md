**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A design target where per-spike energy consumption is reduced to less than one-thousandth of the human brain's per-neuron energy expenditure. Sets an extreme low-power benchmark for neuromorphic systems, motivating novel device-circuit co-design in both TCC interconnect and iNEST processing layers.
