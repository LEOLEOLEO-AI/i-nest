# AdiabaticMultimodeBend

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A waveguide bend designed with smooth curvature profiles to maintain adiabatic mode evolution, minimizing inter-mode crosstalk and loss in multimode photonic circuits. Enables compact, low-loss routing of multiple optical modes in TCC interconnects, critical for high-bandwidth on-chip communication.
