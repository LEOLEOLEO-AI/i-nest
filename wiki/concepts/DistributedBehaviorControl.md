# DistributedBehaviorControl

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A behavioral control architecture in which sensory-motor processing is distributed across local circuits in the ventral nerve cord instead of being centralized in the brain. Motivates iNEST designs to replace central controllers with in-network, distributed computation at the edges of the system.
