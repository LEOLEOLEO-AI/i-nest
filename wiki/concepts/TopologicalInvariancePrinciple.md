# TopologicalInvariancePrinciple

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The principle that certain computational or logical properties remain invariant under continuous deformations of the underlying network topology, allowing abstraction of function from specific wiring details. Enables TCC systems to maintain robust functionality despite dynamic reconfiguration or faults, a key requirement for scalable and resilient in-network computing.
