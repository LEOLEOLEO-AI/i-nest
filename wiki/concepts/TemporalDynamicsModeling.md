**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The inclusion of time-dependent neural processes such as Hodgkin-Huxley spiking, STDP plasticity, and time-series outputs in neuromorphic simulations. Temporal dynamics are fundamental to iNEST's neuromorphic realism; their absence invalidates any claims about learning or adaptation in network topologies.
