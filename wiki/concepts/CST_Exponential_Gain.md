# CST_Exponential_Gain

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
A conditional theorem stating that under the axioms of multiplicative gains for independent subsystems and additive spatiotemporal coordinates, the only continuous solution for combined system gain is an exponential function of the product of spatial and temporal coordination factors. This provides a mathematical foundation for designing nonlinear gain mechanisms in neuromorphic systems where spatiotemporal synergy is essential for computational efficiency.
