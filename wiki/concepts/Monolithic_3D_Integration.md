# Monolithic_3D_Integration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A fabrication scheme that sequentially builds and directly interconnects device tiers within a single silicon flow using fine-pitch monolithic inter-tier vias, rather than through-silicon vias or microbump bonding. It makes vertical interconnect a first-class design resource, letting TCC map computation graphs onto dense 3D tiers and letting iNEST place neuromorphic elements in layers directly above their memory.
