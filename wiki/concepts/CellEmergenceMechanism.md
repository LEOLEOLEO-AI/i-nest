# CellEmergenceMechanism

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A process in continuous cellular automata where new cells spontaneously arise from continuous state fields, rather than being predefined on a fixed grid. Enables dynamic resource allocation and reconfigurable topology in neuromorphic hardware, supporting lifelong learning and plasticity.
