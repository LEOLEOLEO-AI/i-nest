# Link_Placement_Budget_Constraint

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A formal constraint specifying the maximum number (or wiring cost) of express links that may be added to a base topology. It frames topology optimization as a constrained resource-allocation problem, which is crucial for realistic TCC designs where wiring, power, and area are strictly limited.
