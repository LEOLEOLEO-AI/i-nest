## Definition
A phase where disorder-induced localization coexists with topological edge states, forming a topological Anderson insulator with multifractal wavefunctions. Reveals how disorder can enhance topological protection, offering new pathways for robust signal routing and fault-tolerant interconnect in TCC architectures.
