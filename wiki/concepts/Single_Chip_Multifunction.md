## Definition
A single chip architecture that unifies inference, training, signal processing, and video processing capabilities for multi-role satellite or edge platforms. This concept supports 'one-star-multi-function' spaceborne computing where one TCC/iNEST device must dynamically switch across heterogeneous tasks without additional dedicated chips.
