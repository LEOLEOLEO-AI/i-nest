## Definition
A framework for achieving consistent reservoir computing outputs across runs by actively controlling stochastic thermal effects in superparamagnetic systems. Addresses a key barrier to practical adoption of physical reservoirs in TCC/iNEST, where deterministic behavior is required for integration with deterministic network topologies.
