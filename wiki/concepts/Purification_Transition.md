# Purification_Transition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A trajectory-resolved transition in which a mixed initial state under monitoring either retains a finite entropy density (mixed phase) or is driven exponentially fast into a pure state (pure phase) as measurement rate crosses a critical value. Frames memory retention versus erasure in monitored networks, giving TCC/iNEST a quantitative criterion for when a network can sustain a persistent internal state versus when measurement noise forces rapid reset.
