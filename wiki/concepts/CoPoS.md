# CoPoS

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Chip-on-Package-on-Substrate, a TSMC advanced chiplet integration method where chiplets are stacked on a package that is then mounted on a substrate, enabling high-density 3D interconnects. Enables high-bandwidth, low-latency interconnects between chiplets, critical for TCC's topology-centric data movement and in-network processing.
