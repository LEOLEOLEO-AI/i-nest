# SpikeProbabilityEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A coding scheme where information is encoded in the probability of spike occurrence over a time window, rather than precise spike timing. Facilitates stochastic computing integration and provides robustness to timing jitter, suitable for noisy network environments.
