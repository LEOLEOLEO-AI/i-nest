# SynapticWeightPlasticity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability of a transistor to continuously adjust its conductance (weight) in response to spike-timing or rate-based stimuli, mimicking biological synapse plasticity. Enables on-device learning and adaptation in neuromorphic circuits, reducing the need for external memory and supporting real-time reconfiguration in TCC architectures.
