## Definition
The degree to which the precise, non-random connectivity patterns of a neural connectome, as opposed to statistical network properties, determine the dynamics of the system. This concept is critical for TCC/iNEST because it distinguishes whether neuromorphic hardware should replicate exact wiring or can rely on statistical topology for functional equivalence.
