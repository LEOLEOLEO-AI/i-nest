# Topology_Derived_Computation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
The theoretical stance that the recurrent motif structure of a connectome — hubs, feedback loops, and sparse long-range pathways — itself encodes the circuit's function independently of fine-tuned synaptic weights. It is the core hypothesis TCC must test, and MaleCNS-style connectomes provide the large-scale, causal perturbation-ready data needed to demonstrate topology-driven function in iNEST implementations.
