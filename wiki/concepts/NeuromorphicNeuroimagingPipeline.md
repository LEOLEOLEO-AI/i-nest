**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
An end-to-end processing chain that converts raw fMRI BOLD signals into spike trains suitable for SNN inference, including preprocessing, feature extraction, and spike encoding. Provides a reusable framework for integrating non-spiking neuroimaging data with spiking networks, a critical step for real-world iNEST applications.
