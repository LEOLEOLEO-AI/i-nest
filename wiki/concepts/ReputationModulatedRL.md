## Definition
A reinforcement learning framework where an agent's action selection is modulated by a reputation score derived from the observed behavior of other agents in a network. This concept is relevant to TCC/iNEST because it provides a mechanism for distributed agents in a topology to adaptively cooperate, potentially improving network-level efficiency and robustness.
