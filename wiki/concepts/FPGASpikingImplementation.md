The mapping of spiking recurrent cell computations onto FPGA fabric, utilizing parallel digital logic to emulate neuron and synapse dynamics in real-time. Provides a flexible, reconfigurable platform for prototyping and deploying iNEST architectures without custom ASIC fabrication, accelerating neuromorphic system development.
