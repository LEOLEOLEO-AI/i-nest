# Wafer_Scale_Topology_Control

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The management of interconnect topology across an entire wafer, treating the whole wafer as a reconfigurable network fabric rather than a fixed chip layout. This is critical for TCC because wafer-scale integration offers massive parallelism, and dynamic topology control unlocks its full potential for diverse, changing workloads.
