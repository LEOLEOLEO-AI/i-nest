## Definition
A computational paradigm that represents information as high-dimensional random vectors (hypervectors) and uses algebraic operations like binding and bundling for cognitive tasks. Enables robust, fault-tolerant, and energy-efficient pattern recognition and memory operations, aligning with both TCC's in-network processing and iNEST's neuromorphic principles.
