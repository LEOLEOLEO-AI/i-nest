# Defect_to_Advantage

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A design methodology that treats device defects such as random switching and crosstalk as computational resources rather than failures. This reframes hardware non-idealities as useful features for neuromorphic and in-memory computing.
