# Function_Space_Criticality

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A formulation of criticality defined over the space of input-output functions a network can realize rather than over its parameters, treating the network as a distribution over functionals. Reframes criticality as a property of the computable function manifold, which is the natural language for topology-centric computing where the object of interest is the mapping structure, not the weights.
