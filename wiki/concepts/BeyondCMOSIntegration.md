**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process of incorporating emerging memristive devices into existing CMOS fabrication flows to enable hybrid circuits for advanced computing. Essential for translating self-rectifying memristor research into practical TCC/iNEST systems, ensuring compatibility with VLSI design and manufacturing constraints.
