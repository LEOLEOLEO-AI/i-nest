# WaferScaleTopologyCentricComputing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-05

## Definition
A computing paradigm where the physical and logical topology of a wafer-scale chiplet array is the primary design and optimization target, rather than fixed processor cores. Shifts the focus from compute-centric to topology-centric design, allowing collective communication patterns to be mapped directly onto reconfigurable hardware.
