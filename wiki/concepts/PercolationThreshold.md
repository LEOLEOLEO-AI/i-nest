## Definition
The critical occupation or bond probability at which a graph transitions from fragmented clusters to a globally connected spanning component. Directly governs fault tolerance and connectivity guarantees in NoC/SDI interconnect topologies, defining the fraction of surviving links or routers below which topology-centric computation collapses.
