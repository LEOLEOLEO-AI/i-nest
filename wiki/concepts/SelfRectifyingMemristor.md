# SelfRectifyingMemristor

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A memristor device that exhibits inherent nonlinearity in its current-voltage characteristics, suppressing sneak-path currents in crossbar arrays without requiring a separate selector element. Enables dense, passive crossbar arrays for beyond-CMOS computing by eliminating the need for additional transistor or selector devices, reducing area and power overhead in TCC architectures.
