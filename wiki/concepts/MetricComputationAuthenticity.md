## Definition
The principle that all reported metrics (e.g., SDI, STDP effects) must be computed from actual simulation dynamics rather than replaced by random number generation. Authentic metric computation is essential for comparing iNEST topologies against TCC benchmarks and deriving meaningful performance insights.
