# UV_Intensity_Modulated_HRS

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
The modulation of four distinct high-resistance states in the memristor by applying different intensities of 254 nm ultraviolet light. Allows optical signals to directly program synaptic states, supporting sensor-fused in-network neuromorphic computing.
