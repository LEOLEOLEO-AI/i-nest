# CombinedImportanceScoring

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A pruning criterion that integrates multiple importance metrics (e.g., gradient-based, activation-based, and topology-based) into a single score to decide which spiking neural network connections to remove. Enables more accurate identification of critical synapses in SNNs, reducing energy consumption without sacrificing accuracy, which is essential for resource-constrained neuromorphic hardware.
