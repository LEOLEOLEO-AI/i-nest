## Definition
A digital circuit that autonomously reconfigures its topology and logic functions in response to environmental stimuli or internal state changes without external control. Enables adaptive hardware that can self-heal, optimize, or evolve in-situ, reducing reliance on static pre-designed topologies in TCC systems.
