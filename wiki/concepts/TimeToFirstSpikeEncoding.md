# TimeToFirstSpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A neural coding scheme where information is encoded in the timing of the first spike emitted by a neuron, rather than in spike rate or precise spike patterns. This encoding is highly energy-efficient and biologically plausible, making it a key candidate for low-power neuromorphic hardware in iNEST systems.
