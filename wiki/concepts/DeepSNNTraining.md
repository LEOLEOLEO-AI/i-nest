# DeepSNNTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process of training spiking neural networks with many layers using surrogate gradient methods to overcome the non-differentiability of spike events. Enables the construction of deeper neuromorphic models that can perform complex pattern recognition directly inside network switches or routers.
