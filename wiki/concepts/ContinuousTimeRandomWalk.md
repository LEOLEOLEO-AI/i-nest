## Definition
A stochastic framework where particles (or spins) undergo random jumps with waiting times drawn from heavy-tailed distributions, used to model anomalous diffusion in disordered networks. Provides a mathematical bridge between microscopic spin dynamics and macroscopic transport anomalies, enabling predictive models for signal propagation in irregular network topologies.
