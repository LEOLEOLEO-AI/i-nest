# ApproximateMacroscopicDynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method to derive coarse-grained, population-level equations that approximate the average behavior of spiking neural networks without simulating every spike. Enables scalable analysis and design of large neuromorphic systems by predicting network-level dynamics, critical for in-network processing where computational resources are limited.
