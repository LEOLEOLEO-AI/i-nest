# Mesosynchronous_Clocking

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-24

## Definition
A clocking scheme that maintains a common frequency across a wafer-scale chip while allowing bounded phase differences between regions. It enables reliable synchronous operation over the large physical topologies targeted by TCC without requiring a single global clock tree.
