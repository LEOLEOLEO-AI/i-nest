# SymmetricQuantumCircuit

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A quantum circuit whose gate operations and qubit interactions are arranged in a symmetric pattern, often exploiting reflection or rotational symmetries to simplify routing. Enables predictable and balanced routing paths, reducing the need for dynamic reconfiguration and improving scalability in large-scale quantum networks.
