# SupernonlinearGain

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A design objective in wafer-scale computing where adding compute nodes yields returns greater than linear due to diminished communication overhead and increased network-mediated synergy. This distinguishes TCC from conventional chiplet systems, where expansion is bounded by physical interconnect bottlenecks and the 'short-board effect'.
