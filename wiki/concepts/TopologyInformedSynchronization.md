# TopologyInformedSynchronization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A synchronization protocol that aligns simulation time steps across compute nodes based on the physical network's propagation delays and topology, rather than using global barriers. This reduces idle time and communication overhead in TCC, enabling more efficient and realistic brain-scale spiking simulations.
