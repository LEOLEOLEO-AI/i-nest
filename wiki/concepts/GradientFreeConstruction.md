# GradientFreeConstruction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method for building network topologies using combinatorial or heuristic search rather than backpropagation or other gradient-based optimization. Critical for hardware-constrained neuromorphic systems where differentiable models are unavailable or computationally prohibitive.
