**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The design and fabrication of large-scale crossbar arrays using VLSI techniques, integrating analog memory devices with CMOS control circuitry for scalable in-memory computing. This integration is a key enabler for building practical TCC hardware, bridging device-level analog computing with system-level network-on-chip architectures.
