## Definition
The study of how linear recurrent neural networks evolve their hidden state over time under simplified linear activation, focusing on representational structure and stability. Provides a tractable mathematical framework to analyze temporal dynamics in neuromorphic circuits, guiding the design of stable recurrent topologies for in-network computation.
