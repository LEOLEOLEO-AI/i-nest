**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A pruning strategy that removes connections or nodes based on their contribution to the overall topological complexity rather than just weight magnitude. Enables systematic simplification of network graphs in TCC, preserving critical pathways while eliminating redundant loops or high-order interactions.
