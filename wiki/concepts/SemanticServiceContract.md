## Definition
A machine-readable, ontology-based specification of service capabilities, constraints, and quality-of-service guarantees that agents use for automated discovery and binding. Provides the formal grounding needed for in-network neuromorphic systems to interpret and route service requests based on semantic similarity, not just syntactic headers.
