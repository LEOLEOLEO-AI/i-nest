# FluidIntelligence

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A theoretical framework where artificial general intelligence arises from continuous, fluid-like reconfiguration of computational resources, rather than from fixed neural or logic architectures. This motivates research into dynamically reconfigurable hardware–network co-design, aligning with in-network neuromorphic principles.
