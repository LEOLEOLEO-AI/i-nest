# GradientFreeSNNTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Training spiking neural networks without backpropagation or surrogate gradients, using evolutionary or other black-box optimization techniques. Circumvents the non-differentiability of spike events and reduces memory/compute overhead, making on-chip learning more feasible.
