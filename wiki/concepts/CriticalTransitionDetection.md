# CriticalTransitionDetection

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A methodology for identifying the approach of a tipping point in dynamical systems on networks by monitoring changes in spatial variance and correlation before the transition occurs. Allows neuromorphic computing substrates to preemptively reallocate resources or alter synaptic weights to avoid catastrophic state shifts.
