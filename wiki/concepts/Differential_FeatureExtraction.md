# Differential_FeatureExtraction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
A feature extraction paradigm where two parallel streams (positive and negative) are combined via subtraction to isolate invariant features from common-mode interference. Relevant for TCC as it can be mapped to differential signaling in network-on-chip designs, improving robustness against crosstalk and noise.
