# LongRangeCorrelatedEntries

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Random matrix entries whose statistical dependencies extend across distant positions, unlike standard independent-entry ensembles. Enables modeling of spatially extended or recurrent network topologies where distant nodes influence each other, relevant to both TCC and iNEST architectures.
