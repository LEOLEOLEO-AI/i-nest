# Wafer_Scale_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
An architectural approach that keeps an entire silicon wafer intact as a single computing substrate rather than dicing it into individual chips, enabling massive die-to-die bandwidth and low-latency on-wafer communication. It provides the physical substrate whose interconnect topology directly determines the scalability, yield, and communication cost models studied in TCC research.
