# WaferScaleIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-22

## Definition
Wafer-scale integration is the fabrication and assembly of compute, memory, and interconnect resources across an entire semiconductor wafer to form a single large-scale system. It enables the dense, low-latency topological interconnects required for scalable TCC and iNEST hardware implementations.
