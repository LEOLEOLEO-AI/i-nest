# Rank_Order_N_of_M_Codes

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A sparse distributed memory coding scheme where information is encoded by the rank order of N active neurons among M total, rather than by precise spike timing. Offers a biologically plausible and energy-efficient alternative to temporal coding, applicable to both neuromorphic computing and topological data representation.
