## Definition
A recurrent spiking neural network architecture augmented with Von Economo neurons to simulate social skill acquisition through spike-timing-dependent plasticity and reward-modulated learning. Demonstrates how recurrent spiking dynamics can support complex social behaviors, offering a blueprint for in-network neuromorphic processors that handle social interaction tasks in real time.
