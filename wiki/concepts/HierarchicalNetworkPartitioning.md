# HierarchicalNetworkPartitioning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of dividing the brain-scale spiking network into hierarchical clusters that map onto the physical network's multi-level topology, reducing cross-cluster traffic. This reduces communication bottlenecks in TCC systems, allowing simulations to scale to brain-scale sizes without overwhelming interconnect bandwidth.
