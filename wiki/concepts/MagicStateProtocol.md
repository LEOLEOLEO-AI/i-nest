# MagicStateProtocol

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A protocol for producing and consuming non-Clifford quantum states (magic states) that are essential for fault-tolerant quantum computation, often involving distillation and resource management. Magic state protocols are a critical resource bottleneck in quantum computing, and their optimization directly impacts the feasibility and efficiency of large-scale quantum algorithms, which is relevant to TCC's focus on resource-aware compilation.
