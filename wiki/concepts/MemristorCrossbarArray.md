## Definition
A dense 2D array of memristive devices (e.g., ferroelectric or resistive switching elements) that performs analog matrix-vector multiplication in the physical domain, enabling in-memory computing. Provides the foundational hardware for near-sensor and in-sensor computing, allowing efficient neural network execution directly on flexible substrates.
