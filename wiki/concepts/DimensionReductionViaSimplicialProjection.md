# DimensionReductionViaSimplicialProjection

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that reduces the state-space dimension of higher-order dynamical networks by projecting dynamics onto lower-dimensional simplicial complexes while preserving key topological features. Critical for scalable simulation and analysis of large-scale TCC/iNEST systems where full higher-order state spaces are computationally intractable.
