## Definition
The degree of accuracy with which synaptic weights and neural activations are represented, often traded off against hardware efficiency in neuromorphic designs. Balancing precision with resource constraints is crucial for deploying neuromorphic processors in bandwidth-limited and power-constrained network environments.
