# EntanglementEntropyScaling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The asymptotic growth rate of the von Neumann entanglement entropy of a subsystem with its size, distinguishing area-law (constant) from volume-law (linear) behavior in the steady state. Serves as the key diagnostic for detecting phase transitions and characterizing quantum correlations in monitored systems, essential for validating TCC's entanglement-based computation.
