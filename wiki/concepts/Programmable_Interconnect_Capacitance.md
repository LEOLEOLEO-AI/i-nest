# Programmable_Interconnect_Capacitance

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The additional 5-8x capacitance introduced by FPGA programmable switch matrices that route signals through configurable interconnect, increasing dynamic energy per data movement. It highlights that data movement, not computation, dominates energy in reconfigurable fabrics, reinforcing TCC's focus on minimizing hop distance and wire length in topology design.
