**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The phenomenon where adversarial examples crafted for artificial neural networks (ANNs) transfer to spiking neural networks (SNNs), and vice versa, due to shared decision boundaries or gradient similarities. This challenges the assumed robustness of SNNs and necessitates security-aware design in neuromorphic systems for iNEST deployments.
