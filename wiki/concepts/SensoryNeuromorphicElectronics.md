**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Electronic systems that mimic biological sensory pathways by integrating sensors with neuromorphic circuits to perform perception and cognition in hardware. This bridges the gap between physical sensing and neural computation, a critical requirement for TCC/iNEST to enable autonomous, real-time, and energy-efficient sensory processing at the edge.
