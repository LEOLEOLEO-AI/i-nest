## Definition
A modular interconnect architecture where chiplets communicate via standardized high-speed links (e.g., UCIe) arranged in a network-on-package, enabling flexible topologies. Allows TCC to implement reconfigurable, graph-like communication patterns directly in hardware, essential for mapping neural or topological algorithms onto physical substrates.
