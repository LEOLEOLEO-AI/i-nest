# ReadoutLayer_Training

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

## Definition
The linear output stage of a reservoir computer, trained by regularized regression (typically ridge regression) on collected reservoir states while all internal reservoir connections remain fixed and untrained. It defines the low-cost learning interface that makes physically implemented reservoirs viable in TCC/iNEST systems, confining adaptation to a simple readout rather than requiring backpropagation through the network topology.
