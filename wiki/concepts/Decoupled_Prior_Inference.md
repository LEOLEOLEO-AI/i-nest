# Decoupled_Prior_Inference

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of inferring hidden states by temporarily separating prior constraints from sensory evidence, allowing for alternative belief updates. This enables neuromorphic networks to explore novel computational paths without being locked into prior weight distributions, enhancing adaptability in dynamic topologies.
