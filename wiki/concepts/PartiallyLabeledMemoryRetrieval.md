# PartiallyLabeledMemoryRetrieval

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The capability of a Hopfield network to retrieve complete stored patterns from partially labeled or corrupted inputs, leveraging learned label associations. Enables fault-tolerant and energy-efficient inference in neuromorphic edge devices where full labels are unavailable or costly to obtain.
