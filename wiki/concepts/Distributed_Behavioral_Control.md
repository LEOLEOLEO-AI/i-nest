# Distributed_Behavioral_Control

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A control framework where behavior emerges from interactions among local sensorimotor loops and long-range coordinating pathways, rather than from a top-down command chain. Challenges centralized processing assumptions in TCC/iNEST and motivates embedding decision-making across network nodes instead of at a central controller.
