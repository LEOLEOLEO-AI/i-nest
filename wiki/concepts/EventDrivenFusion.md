# EventDrivenFusion

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A fusion paradigm where data from multiple sources is processed only when spike events occur, rather than on a fixed clock cycle, reducing redundant computation. Minimizes power consumption and latency in neuromorphic and network-on-chip architectures, aligning with TCC's goal of efficient distributed computing.
