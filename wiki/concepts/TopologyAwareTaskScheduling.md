# TopologyAwareTaskScheduling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A scheduling strategy that assigns model partitions or data shards to compute units based on the underlying interconnect topology to minimize communication hops and congestion. Enables efficient utilization of wafer-scale interconnects, directly improving throughput and reducing communication stalls during LLM training.
