# TopologicalObservers

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A framework where observers are defined not by their internal state but by their position and relationships within a topological structure of the network. This reframes observation as a structural property rather than a computational one, enabling topology-aware sensing in distributed systems.
