# ErdosRenyiGraphEnergyLandscape

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The energy landscape defined on the vertices of a random Erdős–Rényi graph, where edges represent allowed transitions and vertex energies are assigned randomly, creating a complex rugged landscape. Serves as a minimal stochastic model to study how graph topology shapes energy barriers, directly applicable to understanding dynamics in random neural networks and in-network computing substrates.
