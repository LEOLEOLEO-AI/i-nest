# Reservoir_Closure_Property

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The property of a reservoir's internal dynamics being closed under its own evolution, ensuring that all computational states remain within the reservoir's state space without leakage or external correction. Ensures stability and reliability in neuromorphic reservoirs, critical for long-term autonomous operation in iNEST deployments.
