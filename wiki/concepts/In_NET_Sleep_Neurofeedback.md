# In_NET_Sleep_Neurofeedback

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The integration of sleep-stage classification and feedback generation directly within network infrastructure or edge neuromorphic chips, avoiding cloud round-trips. Reduces latency and energy consumption, making continuous overnight neurofeedback feasible for portable sleep-aid devices, a key iNEST application target.
