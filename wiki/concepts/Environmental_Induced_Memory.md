# Environmental_Induced_Memory

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Memory effects in a quantum reservoir that arise not from internal dynamics but from the specific way the reservoir is coupled to its external environment. This challenges conventional reservoir design by showing that environmental organization can be a resource for computation, not just noise.
