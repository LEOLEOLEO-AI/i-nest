# SynapticDelayModulatedPhaseResponse

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A framework describing how the timing of synaptic delays in excitatory-inhibitory networks shifts the phase and amplitude of population-level oscillations. Enables design of neuromorphic circuits where controlled delays tune oscillatory dynamics for temporal computation and inter-chip synchronization.
