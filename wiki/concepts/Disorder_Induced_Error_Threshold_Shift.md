**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The modification of the critical error rate in a topological quantum code due to spatial or temporal disorder in the underlying physical system. Understanding this shift is crucial for predicting real-world performance of topological quantum memories, where fabrication imperfections and environmental noise introduce disorder.
