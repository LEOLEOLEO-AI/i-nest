# Bipartite_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An interconnect structure where nodes are partitioned into two disjoint sets, with edges only connecting nodes across sets, enabling specialized communication patterns. This topology is relevant for both TCC and iNEST, as it can reduce routing complexity and improve scalability in neuromorphic and topology-centric computing fabrics.
