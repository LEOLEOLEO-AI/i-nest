# SNN_Topological_Routing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A routing method for spiking neural networks where spike propagation paths are determined by geometric topology rather than conventional logical addressing. This unifies SNN and TCC concepts by making the physical network itself perform the neural computation, a core premise of iNEST.
