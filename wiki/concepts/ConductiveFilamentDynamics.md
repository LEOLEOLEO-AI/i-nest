# ConductiveFilamentDynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The stochastic formation, growth, and rupture of nanoscale conductive filaments inside a resistive switching oxide that governs analog conductance modulation in filamentary ReRAM devices. Filament dynamics determine the synaptic weight update linearity and variability that in-network neuromorphic hardware relies on for reliable analog plasticity.
