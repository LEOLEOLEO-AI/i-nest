# OptimalGeneralisationNearInterpolation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where, in extensive-width shallow networks, the best generalization performance is achieved precisely at or near the interpolation threshold, contrary to classical bias-variance tradeoff predictions. This insight guides TCC/iNEST architecture design by suggesting that network capacity should be tuned to the interpolation point, which can be mapped to interconnect bandwidth and node compute limits in distributed neuromorphic systems.
