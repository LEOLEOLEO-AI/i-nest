# Flexible_Reliability

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A reliability mechanism that adaptively tunes error-correction and retransmission policies based on the criticality and latency sensitivity of the communication task rather than applying a uniform fixed protocol. This balances fault tolerance against latency overhead, enabling topology-centric interconnects to meet both the stringent timing demands of LLM inference and the throughput needs of training.
