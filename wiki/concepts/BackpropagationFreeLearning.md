# BackpropagationFreeLearning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training paradigm that avoids the global error propagation of backpropagation, instead relying on local or approximated gradients to update network parameters. Critical for implementing learning in physical TCC/iNEST substrates where global feedback paths are impractical or impossible.
