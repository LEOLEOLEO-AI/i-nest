## Definition
A recursive metric contraction framework that iteratively applies Urysohn-like distance transformations to create a hierarchical ladder of metric spaces for continual learning. Provides a novel topological mechanism for compressing and reusing learned representations, directly addressing catastrophic forgetting in neuromorphic and topology-centric continual learning systems.
