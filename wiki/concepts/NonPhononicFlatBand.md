# NonPhononicFlatBand

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A band of vibrational modes in amorphous solids that is flat (dispersionless) and not describable by standard phonon theory, arising from localized resonant coupling between structural defects. Provides a fundamental physical mechanism for energy localization and transport anomalies in disordered media, which could inform the design of topological or neuromorphic substrates with engineered flat bands for signal processing.
