## Definition
An imec 3D-integrated optical I/O architecture that stacks photonic and electronic layers to enable wafer-level or die-level optical interconnects with extreme bandwidth density. It enables 3D topology-centric interconnect fabrics that can scale to thousands of GPUs or accelerators while reducing copper interconnect bottlenecks.
