# SpikingTransformer

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A transformer architecture adapted to use spiking neural network (SNN) neurons, where information is encoded as discrete spike events rather than continuous values. Bridges the gap between attention-based models and neuromorphic hardware, enabling energy-efficient, event-driven computation in TCC/iNEST systems.
