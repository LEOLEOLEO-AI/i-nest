# SimulationalScalingAnalysis

**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A numerical method using finite-size scaling of transfer-matrix or exact diagonalization data to extract critical exponents and localization lengths at the mobility edge. Provides quantitative predictions for the critical disorder strength in topological network models, guiding the design of reliable TCC hardware.
