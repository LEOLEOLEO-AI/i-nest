# INT4PrecisionQuantization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The use of 4-bit integer arithmetic for both synaptic weights and activation/error signals during SNN training, drastically reducing memory footprint and computational energy compared to higher-precision formats. Critical for achieving practical energy efficiency in TCC/iNEST hardware, as it minimizes data movement and multiplier complexity while maintaining acceptable accuracy for online learning.
