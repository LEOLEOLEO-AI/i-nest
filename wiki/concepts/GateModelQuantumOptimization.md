## Definition
The use of universal gate-based quantum circuits to encode and solve binary combinatorial optimization problems, as opposed to analog or annealing-based approaches. Provides a flexible, programmable framework for mapping TCC network-on-chip topology optimization problems onto quantum hardware, allowing for scalable and reconfigurable problem encoding.
