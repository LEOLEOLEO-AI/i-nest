**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method to initialize or recover a neural network's weights without using gradient-based optimization, leveraging prior library solutions as starting points. Enables fast adaptation in neuromorphic hardware where gradient computation is costly or unavailable, supporting in-network learning.
