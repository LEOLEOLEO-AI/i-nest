## Definition
A crossbar-oriented neural microarchitecture that uses memristor devices as non-volatile artificial synapses co-located with in-network neuromorphic computation. It enables high-density, energy-efficient synaptic storage and in-place compute, which is essential for deploying brain-like intelligence inside network substrates.
