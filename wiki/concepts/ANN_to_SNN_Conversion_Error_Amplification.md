## Definition
The phenomenon where small approximation errors introduced during the conversion of an Artificial Neural Network (ANN) to a Spiking Neural Network (SNN) are amplified through recurrent feedback loops in continuous control tasks, leading to significant performance degradation. This concept is critical for iNEST because it identifies a fundamental limitation in using converted SNNs for real-time neuromorphic control, guiding the development of error-resilient conversion methods or direct SNN training approaches.
