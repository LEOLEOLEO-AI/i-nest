**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A normalization technique that uses subtraction instead of division to re-scale attention weights, preserving relative ordering while enhancing contrast. Offers a simpler, more hardware-friendly normalization for spike-based or analog neuromorphic circuits, avoiding costly division operations.
