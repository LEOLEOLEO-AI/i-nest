# DeepReinforcementLearningFeatureExtraction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The use of a fixed, non-trained feature extractor to transform raw observations into a compact state representation for reinforcement learning agents. Provides a scalable, hardware-friendly approach for in-network decision-making in iNEST, where training is impractical and latency is critical.
