# EventDriven_Asynchronous_Operation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A design principle where the chip operates without a global clock and activates only in response to input stimuli, reducing standby power. This is essential for ultra-low-power operation in iNEST edge devices, where energy is scarce and activity is sporadic.
