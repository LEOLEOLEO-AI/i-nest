# ChiralTopologicalMatterEncoding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of encoding information in the chiral edge states of topological matter, where data flows unidirectionally along protected boundaries, immune to backscattering and defects. Enables topologically protected data paths in TCC interconnects, reducing error rates and energy dissipation in large-scale neural networks.
