**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A non-volatile electronic component whose polarization state can be switched by an electric field, used as a programmable weight in crossbar arrays for analog computing. Offers high endurance, low power, and compatibility with flexible substrates, making it a key enabler for TCC-based edge AI in wearable and implantable systems.
