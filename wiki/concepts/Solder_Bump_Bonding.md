# Solder_Bump_Bonding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A wafer-scale integration technique that uses solder bumps to bond multiple bare dies onto a single wafer, replacing traditional package-level interconnects. This enables high-density, high-bandwidth chip-to-chip connections essential for tightly coupled AI and HPC logic architectures.
