# SDI_NoC_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The architectural approach of integrating SDI (Serial Data Interconnect) directly into the NPU's Network-on-Chip (NoC) fabric, transitioning from an external add-on module to an on-die fused component. This fusion reduces off-chip latency and power overhead, enabling tighter coupling between memory and compute in topology-centric systems.
