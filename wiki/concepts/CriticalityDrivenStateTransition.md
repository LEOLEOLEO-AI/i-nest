## Definition
A mechanism where the internal state of a neuromorphic network undergoes phase transitions between ordered and chaotic regimes, triggered by input statistics or external control signals. This enables adaptive reconfiguration of neuromorphic circuits in response to task demands, a crucial capability for in-network learning and real-time inference.
