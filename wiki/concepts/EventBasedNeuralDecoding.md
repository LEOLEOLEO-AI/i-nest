# EventBasedNeuralDecoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A decoding paradigm that processes neural signals as asynchronous spike events rather than continuous time-series samples, enabling real-time motor control for neuroprosthetics. This is central to iNEST because it aligns with spiking neural network (SNN) processing, reducing latency and power consumption in neuromorphic prosthetic systems.
