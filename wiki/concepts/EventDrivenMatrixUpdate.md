**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A hybrid method where only active spikes trigger sparse updates within a dense matrix framework, combining the efficiency of event-driven simulation with the regularity of matrix-based computation. This concept is key for TCC and iNEST as it reduces redundant computation in large-scale SNNs, enabling real-time performance on network-centric hardware.
