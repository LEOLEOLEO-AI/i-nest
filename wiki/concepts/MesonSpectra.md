## Definition
The discrete set of energy levels of bound states (mesons) in a quantum field theory, which in this context are derived from the universal scaling behavior near the (1+1)-dimensional Ising critical point. Provides a universal, model-independent characterization of confined excitations, which can inform the design of topological or neuromorphic systems that emulate quantum critical phenomena.
