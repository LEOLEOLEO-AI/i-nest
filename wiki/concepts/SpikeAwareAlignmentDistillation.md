## Definition
A knowledge distillation technique that aligns the spike timing and firing patterns of a student spiking model with a teacher model, preserving temporal information during training. This matters for iNEST because it improves the accuracy and convergence of spiking models on neuromorphic substrates, where precise spike timing is a key computational resource.
