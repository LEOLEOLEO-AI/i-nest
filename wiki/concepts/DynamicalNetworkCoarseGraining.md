# DynamicalNetworkCoarseGraining

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A coarse-graining framework that aggregates nodes or hyperedges in a higher-order dynamical network to form a reduced-order model that approximates the original system's trajectory. Facilitates efficient simulation and control of large-scale interconnected systems in TCC, where full-order models are computationally prohibitive.
