# ActivityEnergyTradeoff

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The relationship between the number of spikes (activity) and the energy consumption in spiking networks, where lower activity generally reduces energy but may degrade task performance. Central to optimizing neuromorphic systems for low-power edge applications, as it quantifies the cost-benefit of sparse coding.
