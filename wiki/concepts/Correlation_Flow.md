# Correlation_Flow

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The directed, edge-resolved transport of pairwise correlation signals through a network, whose divergence and convergence determine where and how strongly synaptic updates are applied. It reframes learning signals as topologically routed correlation currents, giving TCC a concrete quantity to route and iNEST a physical observable to compute in-network.
