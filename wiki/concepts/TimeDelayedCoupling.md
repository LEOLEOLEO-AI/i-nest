# TimeDelayedCoupling

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Interactions between binary stochastic units that incorporate explicit time delays, where the state of one unit affects another only after a finite propagation time. Captures realistic synaptic and axonal delays in neuromorphic systems, which can induce oscillatory or transient dynamics critical for temporal information processing.
