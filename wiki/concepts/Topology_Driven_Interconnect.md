# Topology_Driven_Interconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An interconnect design where the physical arrangement of chiplets and links is optimized for specific communication patterns, rather than treating all nodes as uniformly connected. This is a core TCC principle, as it enables hardware-level support for collective operations, reducing latency and energy compared to generic mesh or ring topologies.
