**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
An architectural principle where the inference process is determined by the topological structure of the network (e.g., connectivity, cycles, and modularity) rather than by explicit algorithmic steps. This concept guides the design of TCC systems where the network topology itself encodes the inference logic, enabling scalable and robust computation in distributed neuromorphic substrates.
