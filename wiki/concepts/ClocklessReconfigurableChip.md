## Definition
A neuromorphic hardware architecture that operates without a global clock signal, using asynchronous spiking dynamics for autonomous computation and reconfigurable routing. Eliminates clock synchronization overhead, enabling energy-efficient, scalable spiking networks that mimic biological timing and support real-time plasticity.
