# ImplicitDepth_Divergence

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The phenomenon whereby the effective number of iterations needed to reach the DEQ fixed point diverges as the model approaches criticality, replacing discrete layer depth with a continuous convergence horizon. Reframes depth as a dynamical resource rather than a fixed layer count, which is essential when mapping equilibrium solvers onto physically recurrent TCC interconnects and iNEST synaptic arrays.
