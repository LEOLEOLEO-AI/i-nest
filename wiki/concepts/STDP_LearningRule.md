# STDP_Learning_Rule

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
Spike-timing-dependent plasticity, a biological learning rule implemented in the artificial synapse, where synaptic weight changes depend on the relative timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes. STDP enables unsupervised and local learning, making the synapse suitable for neuromorphic computing and potential integration into in-network processing units.
