## Definition
The phenomenon where critical exponents and scaling behavior at the boundaries of a system differ from the bulk, often leading to surface-driven phase transitions. Critical for designing edge-of-network computations and understanding how information flows and transforms at the interfaces of topological or neuromorphic substrates.
