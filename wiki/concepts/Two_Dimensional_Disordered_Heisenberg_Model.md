## Definition
A spin-1/2 Heisenberg Hamiltonian on a 2D lattice with random exchange couplings, used to model spin glass phases and magnetic frustration. Serves as a canonical testbed for understanding how disorder and topology interact in low-dimensional magnetic networks, relevant to designing robust topological interconnects.
