## Definition
A framework that reinterprets safety-critical system thresholds as neuron spiking thresholds in spiking neural networks (SNNs), where crossing a threshold triggers a spike event analogous to an alarm or safety response. This bridges safety engineering with neuromorphic computing, enabling SNN-based systems to natively encode and process safety constraints in real-time, which is crucial for iNEST applications requiring low-latency, energy-efficient safety monitoring.
