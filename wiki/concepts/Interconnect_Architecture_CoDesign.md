## Definition
The joint design of chiplet packaging interconnect (e.g., CoPoS/EMIB) with on-chip network protocols to support in-network neuromorphic and topology-aware operations. Enables seamless integration of iNEST's neuromorphic processing with TCC's dataflow, reducing data movement overhead across package boundaries.
