**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A retrieval method that queries and ranks information based on graph connectivity and node/edge features rather than flat text similarity. Provides a mechanism for TCC to retrieve contextually relevant subgraphs, and for iNEST to activate sparse neural pathways based on topological relevance.
