## Definition
A class of hardware designs that map spiking neural network topologies onto FPGA fabrics, enabling reconfigurable and scalable neuromorphic computation. Bridges the gap between flexible network-level computing (TCC) and brain-inspired processing (iNEST), allowing dynamic topology reconfiguration for adaptive in-network intelligence.
