# Topological_Synchronization_Landscape

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A conceptual map of all possible synchronization states of a network, organized by the topology of connections and the free energy barriers between them, where stable states correspond to local minima in this landscape. This provides a design tool for TCC to engineer network topologies that have desired synchronization basins, enabling predictable and robust collective computation in distributed systems.
