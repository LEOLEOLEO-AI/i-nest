# InNetwork_Neuromorphic_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The implementation of synaptic plasticity mechanisms (e.g., STDP) directly within network switches or routers, rather than at end nodes. Moves learning into the interconnect itself, enabling ultra-low-latency adaptation and reducing data movement in iNEST systems.
