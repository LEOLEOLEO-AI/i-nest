# MemristiveSynapticFatigue

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The physical phenomenon in memristor devices where repeated switching events cause gradual resistance drift or reduced switching range, analogous to biological synaptic fatigue. Provides a hardware-level mechanism for implementing fatigue-based learning rules, which can enhance temporal feature extraction and reduce overfitting in spiking networks.
