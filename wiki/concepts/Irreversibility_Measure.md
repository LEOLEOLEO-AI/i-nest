## Definition
A quantitative metric derived from the asymmetry of transition probabilities in Markov chains, capturing the degree of time-reversal symmetry breaking in the system's dynamics. Enables TCC systems to detect and exploit directional information flow in interconnects, potentially improving routing and fault-detection in dynamic topologies.
