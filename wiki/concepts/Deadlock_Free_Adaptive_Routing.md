# Deadlock_Free_Adaptive_Routing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An adaptive routing scheme that dynamically chooses among multiple paths while provably avoiding cyclic channel dependencies and routing deadlock. Adaptive routing is essential for exploiting irregular express links in TCC fabrics and for sustaining the non-uniform, bursty traffic patterns typical of iNEST neuromorphic workloads.
