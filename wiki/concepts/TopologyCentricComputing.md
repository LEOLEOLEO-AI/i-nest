# Topology_Centric_Computing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A paradigm that treats interconnect topology as the primary design concern, making all architectural layers subservient to efficient data movement through reconfigurable topologies. It reframes interconnect from passive infrastructure to an active computational substrate, directly addressing the data-movement bottleneck in large-scale AI systems.
