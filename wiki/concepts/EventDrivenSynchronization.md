# EventDrivenSynchronization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A synchronization scheme where FPGA nodes exchange spikes asynchronously, using event timestamps or local clocks to maintain global temporal consistency without global clock signals. Preserves the asynchronous, event-based nature of SNNs while ensuring correct temporal alignment across distributed hardware, a key challenge in iNEST systems.
