**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuromorphic computing paradigm where an optical nonlinear dynamical system (the reservoir) maps inputs to high-dimensional states for temporal pattern recognition, without training the reservoir itself. Enables low-latency, energy-efficient neuromorphic processing directly in the optical domain, aligning with iNEST's goal of in-network intelligence.
