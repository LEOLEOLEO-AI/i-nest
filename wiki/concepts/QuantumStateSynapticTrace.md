# QuantumStateSynapticTrace

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A trace of quantum state evolution maintained at each synapse, used to compute eligibility traces for Hebbian updates in quantum-probability-driven learning. Allows temporal credit assignment in spiking neuromorphic systems without classical memory, supporting online learning in TCC data planes.
