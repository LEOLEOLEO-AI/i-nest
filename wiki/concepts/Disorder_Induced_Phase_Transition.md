# Disorder_Induced_Phase_Transition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A phase transition that occurs not purely from temperature or field changes, but from the introduction of quenched disorder, shifting the system's critical behavior. Relevant for neuromorphic systems where inherent device variability or noise can be harnessed as a computational resource rather than a limitation.
