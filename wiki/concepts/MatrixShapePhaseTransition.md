# MatrixShapePhaseTransition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-05

## Definition
The shift in interconnect requirements driven by the phase change of matrix shapes during inference, from thick GEMM matrices in Prefill to thin GEMV matrices in Decode. This phase transition dictates that a single interconnect metric is insufficient; TCC must support multiple orthogonal graph-theoretic demands simultaneously.
