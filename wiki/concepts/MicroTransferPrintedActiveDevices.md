# MicroTransferPrintedActiveDevices

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
Active photonic components (e.g., lasers, modulators) that are micro-transfer-printed onto a passive waveguide platform, enabling localized gain and signal processing within a neuromorphic photonic circuit. Allows dense integration of nonlinear and active elements essential for in-network neuromorphic computation, such as optical spiking neurons or synaptic weights, on a scalable photonic substrate.
