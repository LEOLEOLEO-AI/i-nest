# FerroelectricMemoryCell

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A memory cell that uses ferroelectric polarization states to store analog or multi-level data, offering high endurance and low power for in-memory computing. Ferroelectric cells provide an alternative to memristors with better linearity and endurance, which is valuable for both TCC precision requirements and iNEST synaptic plasticity.
