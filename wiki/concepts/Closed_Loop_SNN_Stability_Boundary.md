# Closed_Loop_SNN_Stability_Boundary

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The threshold of accumulated spiking conversion error beyond which a closed-loop SNN controller becomes unstable, even if the open-loop ANN performs well. This provides a quantitative design constraint for TCC/iNEST architectures, enabling prediction of failure points and guiding error-budget allocation in neuromorphic control stacks.
