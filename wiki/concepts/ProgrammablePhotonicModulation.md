# ProgrammablePhotonicModulation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
The capability to use light as an external programming stimulus to modulate the resistance state of a memristor across a broad spectral range. It enables reconfigurable synaptic weights in neuromorphic hardware and could allow direct optical control of in-memory computing arrays.
