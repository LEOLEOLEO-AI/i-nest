# 1M1T1R_SynapticNeuron

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

## Definition
A neuromorphic circuit topology integrating one memristor (1M), one transistor (1T), and one resistor (1R) to emulate a spiking neuron with synaptic weight storage. This compact analog neuron design could drastically reduce energy consumption per spike, potentially approaching biological brain efficiency, which is critical for scalable in-network neuromorphic computing.
