# SupervisedHardwareRepair

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A methodology for repairing hardware faults in AI accelerators that uses labeled data and error signals to guide the reconfiguration of synaptic weights, rather than relying on redundancy or retraining from scratch. Offers a low-latency, in-situ repair strategy that is crucial for maintaining operational reliability in long-lived TCC/iNEST systems deployed in edge or embedded environments.
