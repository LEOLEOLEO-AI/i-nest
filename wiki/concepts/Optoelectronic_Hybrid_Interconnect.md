# Optoelectronic_Hybrid_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A wafer-scale integration scheme combining an optical interconnect layer for long-range high-bandwidth communication and electrical interconnects for short-range links. It delivers low-latency mixed-distance communication essential for emulating the brain's local-dense and long-range-sparse connectivity in hardware.
