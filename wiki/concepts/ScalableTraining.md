# ScalableTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The ability to train large spiking neural networks efficiently, often through gradient-based methods that avoid the limitations of surrogate gradients or local learning rules. Scalability is critical for deploying neuromorphic models in real-world TCC/iNEST applications where network size and training time are bottlenecks.
