# Differential_Encoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-22

## Definition
A coding scheme where information is represented by the difference between two signals rather than absolute values, enhancing robustness to common-mode noise. Directly applicable to differential signaling in TCC interconnects and neuromorphic circuits, improving fault tolerance and reducing interference.
