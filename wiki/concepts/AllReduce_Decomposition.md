# AllReduce_Decomposition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The restructuring of an AllReduce collective into two distinct phases: a ReduceScatter step (recursive vector halving and distance doubling) followed by an AllGather step (recursive distance halving and vector doubling). This decomposition enables balanced, topology-optimized data movement, directly influencing collective communication performance in TCC and in-network computing systems.
