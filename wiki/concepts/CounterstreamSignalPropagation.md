## Definition
A mechanism where feedforward and feedback signals travel in opposite directions through the same synaptic pathways, enabling bidirectional information flow. Enables simultaneous forward inference and backward error correction in a single network topology, critical for real-time adaptive processing in TCC/iNEST systems.
