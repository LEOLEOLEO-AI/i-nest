# Hebbian_Embedding_Dynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning rule that updates synaptic weights based on the geometric distance between neural manifold coordinates, rather than simple spike timing, effectively embedding temporal correlations into spatial topological features. This enables neuromorphic hardware to directly implement manifold-based learning, reducing the gap between theoretical neural geometry and practical in-network synaptic plasticity.
