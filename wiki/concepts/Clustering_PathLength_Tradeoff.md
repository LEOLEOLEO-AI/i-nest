## Definition
The topological design tension in which raising local clustering (to favor short, cheap local spike routes) tends to lengthen global paths, while shortening global paths increases long-range link cost. Quantifying this tradeoff lets TCC researchers select fabric topologies that optimize neuromorphic communication under area and energy budgets.
