# SneakPathCurrentSuppression

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The mechanism by which self-rectifying memristors limit unintended current flow through unselected cells in a crossbar array, preserving read/write accuracy. Critical for scaling crossbar-based in-memory computing and neuromorphic systems, as it directly impacts array size, energy efficiency, and signal integrity in iNEST implementations.
