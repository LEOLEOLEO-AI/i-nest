**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method for backpropagating error signals through spiking or analog neural network topologies using gradient-based rules adapted to non-differentiable neuron dynamics. Facilitates learning and adaptation in in-network neuromorphic systems, enabling real-time synaptic weight adjustments for cognitive tasks.
