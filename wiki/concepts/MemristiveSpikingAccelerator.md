## Definition
A neuromorphic hardware architecture that uses memristive devices to implement spiking neural network (SNN) computations for energy-efficient, compact acceleration. Enables in-network neuromorphic processing with low power and area footprint, critical for deploying SNNs in resource-constrained edge and network devices.
