# MetastableStateTransitions

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process by which a system moves between quasi-stable configurations due to internal or external disturbances, without fully collapsing to a global attractor. Enables design of neuromorphic or topological computing substrates that exploit transient dynamics for computation, rather than relying on fixed point attractors.
