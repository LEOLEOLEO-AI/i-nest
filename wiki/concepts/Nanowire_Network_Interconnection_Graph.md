**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A graph representation extracted from photomicrographs of nanowire networks, where nodes and edges correspond to physical nanowire junctions and segments. Provides a direct structural mapping from physical nanowire morphology to a computable graph, enabling topology-aware analysis for neuromorphic and interconnect applications.
