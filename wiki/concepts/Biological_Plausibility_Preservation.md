# Biological_Plausibility_Preservation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

## Definition
The principle that the Petri net model retains key biological features such as synaptic delays, refractory periods, and probabilistic firing, despite being abstracted for hardware. Ensures that hardware prototypes remain faithful to biological neural function, which is critical for neuroscience research and brain-inspired computing applications.
