## Definition
The propagation of activity fronts through the network as traveling waves, whose speed and shape are determined by local coupling strengths and graph heterogeneity. Kinematic waves provide a mechanism for temporal coordination and information transfer across distributed neuromorphic hardware, enabling efficient spike-based communication.
