**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
Encoding each reasoning step as a fixed-length vector or spike pattern that can be propagated through neural layers. Facilitates direct implementation of chain-of-thought in neuromorphic hardware where step transitions map to synaptic delays or temporal coding.
