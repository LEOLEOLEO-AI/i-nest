# ForwardReplay

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Reactivation of a stored experience in its original temporal order, typically associated with prospective evaluation and planning of future trajectories. In-network neuromorphic substrates need a hardware-plausible route to forward-sequence recall for prediction and planning, and delay-driven forward replay offers one that requires no symmetric weight updates.
