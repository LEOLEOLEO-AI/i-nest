# Sloppiness

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A property of neural models where only a few parameter combinations (stiff directions) significantly affect behavior, while most are loosely constrained (sloppy directions). Enables robust and hardware-tolerant implementations in neuromorphic circuits, where precise parameter control is difficult.
