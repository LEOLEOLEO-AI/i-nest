## Definition
The study of how local or global perturbations propagate through a simplicial oscillator network and alter its synchrony, revealing which topological features are dynamically resilient or sensitive. Understanding perturbation sensitivity is essential for assessing fault tolerance and robustness of topologically structured computing fabrics in TCC and neuromorphic in-network systems in iNEST.
