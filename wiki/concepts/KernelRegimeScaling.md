## Definition
The theoretical regime in which the network's behavior is governed by a fixed kernel (e.g., random feature or NTK) due to the extensive width scaling, enabling analytical characterization of learning curves. This regime allows TCC/iNEST researchers to predict and optimize system-level performance using kernel methods, which are computationally lightweight and suitable for in-network inference on constrained hardware.
