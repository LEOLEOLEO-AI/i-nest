# TemporalDataBias

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Bias arising from the temporal distribution of spike events in training data, where certain time windows or spike patterns are overrepresented, leading to skewed learning. SNNs process temporal information natively, so this bias type is unique to spiking systems and directly impacts the reliability of iNEST models in time-sensitive tasks.
