## Definition
The complete neural wiring diagram of the nematode C. elegans, comprising 302 neurons and their synaptic connections, used as a biological reference for network architecture. Serves as a minimal, fully mapped biological neural network that can inspire or validate neuromorphic computing architectures and in-network processing topologies.
