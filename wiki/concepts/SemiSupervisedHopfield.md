## Definition
A Hopfield network variant that integrates labeled and unlabeled data during training, using the network's energy landscape to propagate labels to unlabeled patterns. Enables learning from sparse labeled data in distributed memory systems, reducing the need for extensive supervision in TCC/iNEST deployments.
