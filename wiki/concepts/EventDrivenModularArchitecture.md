# EventDrivenModularArchitecture

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A neuromorphic hardware design where computation is triggered by incoming spikes and organized into modular, reconfigurable processing blocks rather than fixed sequential pipelines. Enables scalable and flexible SNN inference by allowing dynamic reconfiguration of network topology, which is essential for adapting to diverse spiking workloads in iNEST systems.
