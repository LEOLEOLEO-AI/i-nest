# Sensory_Computing_Integration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
An on-chip architecture that merges sensing and neural computing units in a single neuromorphic processor, avoiding separate sensor-processor interfaces. This reduces data movement latency and energy overhead, critical for real-time in-network neuromorphic sensing and inference.
