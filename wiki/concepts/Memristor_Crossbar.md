## Definition
A dense array of memristive devices at each intersection of crossbar wires, used to perform analog vector-matrix multiplication and store synaptic weights in situ. It provides a scalable, energy-efficient substrate for implementing SNN weights and has direct relevance to topology-centric dataflow in in-memory computing.
