# NetworkFragility

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The susceptibility of a network to degradation or failure under targeted attacks or random perturbations, often linked to its topological structure. Understanding fragility helps TCC architects design robust topologies that maintain computational integrity even when links or nodes fail, critical for reliable in-network processing.
