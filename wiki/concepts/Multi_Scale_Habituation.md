# Multi_Scale_Habituation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The observation that habituation occurs at multiple organizational levels (e.g., synaptic, neuronal, network) and that these levels interact to produce overall adaptive behavior. Guides the hierarchical design of neuromorphic systems where local and global habituation mechanisms can be coordinated for efficient in-network signal processing and memory.
