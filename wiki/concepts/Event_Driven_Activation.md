# Event_Driven_Activation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism where the neuromorphic trigger's output spike gates the power or computation of subsequent processing stages, keeping them idle until a relevant audio event is detected. This drastically reduces average power consumption in continuous monitoring scenarios, a key advantage of iNEST over conventional always-on DSP.
