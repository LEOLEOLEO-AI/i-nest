# HigherOrderIsingMachine

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An Ising machine that incorporates higher-order interactions (beyond pairwise) among spins to represent and solve complex combinatorial optimization problems more directly. Enables scalable neuromorphic solvers for high-dimensional optimization tasks that are intractable with standard pairwise Ising models, expanding the applicability of in-network neuromorphic computing.
