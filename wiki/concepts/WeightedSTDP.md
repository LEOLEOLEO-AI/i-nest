# WeightedSTDP

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spike-timing-dependent plasticity rule where synaptic weight updates are modulated by additional factors such as distance, topology, or activity strength, beyond simple timing differences. Weighted STDP allows topology-dependent learning, crucial for adapting network connectivity in physical neuromorphic substrates with spatial constraints.
