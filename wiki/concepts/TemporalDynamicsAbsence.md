# TemporalDynamicsAbsence

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The complete lack of time-dependent neural dynamics (e.g., Hodgkin-Huxley models, STDP, or time-series outputs) in simulation experiments, reducing them to static or non-physiological approximations. Without temporal dynamics, iNEST simulations cannot capture learning, plasticity, or real-time information processing, which are fundamental to neuromorphic computing and brain-inspired network behavior.
