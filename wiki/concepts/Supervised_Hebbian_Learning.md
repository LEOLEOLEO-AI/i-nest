# Supervised_Hebbian_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training method for spiking neural networks that combines Hebbian plasticity rules with error-driven supervision signals in a counterstream architecture. Provides biologically plausible, energy-efficient learning for neuromorphic systems, reducing reliance on backpropagation and enabling on-chip adaptation.
