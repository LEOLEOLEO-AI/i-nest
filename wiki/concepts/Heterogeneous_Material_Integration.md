**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The co-integration of material systems such as silicon CMOS, compound semiconductors, and photonic devices within a single packaged microelectronic subsystem. This is essential for iNEST because optical and compound-semiconductor devices can be tightly coupled with neuromorphic silicon to enable network-native computation.
