## Definition
A topology-centric interconnect scheme (SDI) that organizes network links based on spatial or semantic distance, enabling efficient routing for search and discovery workloads. Directly impacts query latency and bandwidth utilization in TCC systems, especially for rare-regime discovery where targeted traversal of specific topology regions is needed.
