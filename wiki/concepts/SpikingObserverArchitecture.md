# SpikingObserverArchitecture

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An architecture where spiking neurons implement the observer dynamics, encoding error signals as spike trains and using synaptic plasticity for adaptation. Enables direct deployment on neuromorphic chips, reducing latency and power consumption compared to conventional digital observers.
