## Definition
A reservoir computing framework that uses parameterized quantum gates on NISQ hardware to map multivariate time series into a high-dimensional feature space without training the reservoir itself. Enables low-overhead temporal feature extraction on quantum hardware, relevant for TCC's in-network processing of time-series data streams.
