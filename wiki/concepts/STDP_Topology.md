# STDP_Topology

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A spike-timing-dependent plasticity mechanism applied to network topology, where synaptic strengths and structural connections are reorganized based on the relative timing of spikes. It is the core local learning rule for in-network neuromorphic systems, allowing self-organization and adaptive rewiring from streaming data.
