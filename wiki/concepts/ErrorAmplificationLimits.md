# ErrorAmplificationLimits

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where small conversion errors between ANN activations and SNN spike rates are amplified through recurrent or deep layers, leading to catastrophic divergence in continuous control tasks. Identifies a fundamental bottleneck in ANN-to-SNN conversion for real-time control, guiding the design of error-resilient spiking architectures in iNEST.
