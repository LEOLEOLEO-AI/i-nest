# Random_Unitary_Circuits

**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A minimal model of quantum many-body dynamics in which local two-qubit gates drawn from a random ensemble are applied in a fixed spacetime circuit geometry. Provides a substrate-agnostic, analytically tractable testbed for how information is routed and mixed across a network's local connectivity, which directly informs topology-centric design of both TCC fabrics and iNEST recurrent architectures.
