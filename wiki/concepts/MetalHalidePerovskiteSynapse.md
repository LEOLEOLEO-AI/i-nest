# MetalHalidePerovskiteSynapse

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A memristive device using metal halide perovskites as the switching medium to emulate biological synaptic plasticity for neuromorphic computing. Provides a novel material platform for implementing energy-efficient, analog synaptic devices in in-network neuromorphic hardware.
