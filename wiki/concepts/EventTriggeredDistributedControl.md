# EventTriggeredDistributedControl

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A control strategy where agents in a multi-agent system update their control actions only when predefined event conditions are triggered, rather than continuously or periodically. This reduces communication and computation overhead in distributed networks, which is critical for scalable and energy-efficient TCC/iNEST architectures.
