## Definition
A multistage interconnection topology derived from FFT butterfly diagrams, where recursive divide-and-conquer communication patterns connect N nodes through logN stages of paired swaps. It provides the mathematical and structural foundation for low-diameter, high-bisection-width communication topologies essential to Topology-Centric Computing.
