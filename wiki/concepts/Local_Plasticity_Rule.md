# Local_Plasticity_Rule

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A synaptic weight update mechanism that relies only on information available at the individual synapse (e.g., pre- and post-synaptic spike timing) without global error signals, enabling online learning in spiking networks. Critical for distributed neuromorphic hardware where global backpropagation is impractical, allowing adaptive learning directly within network nodes or edge devices.
