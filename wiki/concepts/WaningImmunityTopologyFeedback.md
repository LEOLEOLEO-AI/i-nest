## Definition
The process where temporary immunity (waning over time) causes periodic re-susceptibility, leading to oscillatory topological reorganization and recurrent epidemic waves. Models time-varying reliability of links in in-network neuromorphic systems, where synaptic weights or connections decay and must be periodically refreshed.
