# Altermagnetic_MnTe

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
MnTe in its altermagnetic phase is a collinear magnet with alternating spin sublattices related by rotation, producing momentum-dependent spin splitting without a net magnetization. It offers a stray-field-free magnetic material platform for TCC/iNEST devices where spin-polarized transport and topology can be electrically reconfigured.
