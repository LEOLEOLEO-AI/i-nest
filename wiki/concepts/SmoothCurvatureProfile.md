# SmoothCurvatureProfile

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A continuous, non-abrupt curvature function along a bend path, often with zero derivative at endpoints, to avoid scattering and mode conversion. Critical for adiabatic operation in compact photonic interconnects, allowing tighter bends without performance degradation in TCC networks.
