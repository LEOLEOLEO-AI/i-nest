## Definition
A framework for performing belief propagation on state variables of nonlinear dynamical systems, where the system's evolution is modeled by coupled differential equations. Extends neuromorphic inference beyond static graphical models to temporal and physical systems, critical for robotics and control in TCC/iNEST.
