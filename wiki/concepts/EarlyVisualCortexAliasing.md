# EarlyVisualCortexAliasing

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A phenomenon where supervised training of spiking neural networks rapidly degrades the neural representations in early visual cortex layers, causing aliasing-like distortions. This highlights a critical limitation in current SNN training approaches, informing the design of biologically plausible learning rules for neuromorphic vision systems.
