**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A communication mechanism where spike events are routed through a reconfigurable network on-chip based on their timing and destination, without a global clock or centralized scheduler. Enables decentralized, low-overhead spike delivery in large neuromorphic fabrics, directly aligning with TCC's focus on topology-centric, communication-bound computing.
