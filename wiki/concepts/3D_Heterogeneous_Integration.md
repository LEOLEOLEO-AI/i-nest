## Definition
A packaging paradigm that vertically stacks disparate chiplets with different functions and fabrication nodes into a single 3D structure to overcome monolithic scaling limits. Enables TCC to compose specialized compute and memory chiplets with dense vertical interconnects, supporting topology-aware dataflow beyond 2D planar constraints.
