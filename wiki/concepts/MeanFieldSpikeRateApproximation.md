**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An approximation that replaces detailed spike timing with a smooth rate variable derived from the transport equation solution, reducing computational complexity. Facilitates real-time or near-real-time simulation of neuromorphic networks, supporting in-network processing where low latency is critical.
