**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The practice of computing or approximating the spatiotemporal complexity (CST) metric directly within the network or near-network infrastructure rather than on centralized hosts. This reduces full-data backhaul costs and enables scalable, real-time complexity validation under TCC's interconnect constraints.
