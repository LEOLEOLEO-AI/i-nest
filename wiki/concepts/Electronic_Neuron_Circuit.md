## Definition
A hardware implementation of a neuron model using electronic components (e.g., transistors, capacitors) to reproduce the mathematical dynamics of biological bursting neurons. Provides a physical substrate for scalable neuromorphic computing, allowing real-time, low-power emulation of neural dynamics in iNEST architectures.
