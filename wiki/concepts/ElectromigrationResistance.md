# ElectromigrationResistance

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The capacity of an interconnect metal to withstand atomic migration under high current density, preventing void formation and open circuits. Critical for long-term reliability of dense 3D interconnects where current densities are higher than in conventional 2D layouts.
