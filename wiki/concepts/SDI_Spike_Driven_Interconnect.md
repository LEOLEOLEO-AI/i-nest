# SDI_Spike_Driven_Interconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A topology-centric interconnect scheme that transports only sparse spike events between neuromorphic nodes, replacing synchronous bus or NoC packet traffic with event-triggered routing. SDI is the core TCC abstraction for reasoning about how spike traffic topology, fan-out, and link energy determine the feasibility of persistent low-power monitoring workloads.
