# Operator_Spreading

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The growth of the spatial support of a initially local operator as it is conjugated by successive circuit layers, typically described by a front that broadens ballistically with diffusive edges. Characterizes how a perturbation injected at one node propagates through a network's topology, giving TCC/iNEST a quantitative handle on locality, fan-out, and information containment in interconnect graphs.
