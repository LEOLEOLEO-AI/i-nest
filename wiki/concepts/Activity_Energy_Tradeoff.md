# Activity_Energy_Tradeoff

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The relationship between the number of spikes (or neural activations) and the total energy consumption, where reducing activity lowers dynamic energy but eventually hits a floor set by static/overhead energy. Central to designing energy-proportional spiking accelerators in iNEST, as it dictates the optimal operating point for given workloads and network topologies.
