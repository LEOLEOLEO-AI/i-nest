# GoalNormalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A neural mechanism in Drosophila FC2 where multiple goal representations are normalized rather than competitively selected to guide behavior. Provides a biologically grounded alternative to winner-take-all selection for neuromorphic goal-directed systems, enabling robust multi-goal integration.
