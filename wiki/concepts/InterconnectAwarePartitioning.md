# InterconnectAwarePartitioning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The strategy of mapping model layers or tensors to specific compute nodes based on the underlying network topology to reduce cross-node traffic. Critical for TCC because it enables efficient use of wafer-scale or chiplet interconnects by aligning data flow with physical routing paths.
