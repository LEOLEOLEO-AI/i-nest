**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The process of aligning the characteristic timescales and nonlinear responses of a reservoir with the underlying dynamics of the input signal to maximize computational fidelity. This is critical for TCC because it ensures that physical computing elements can faithfully represent and process complex temporal patterns in network traffic or other signals.
