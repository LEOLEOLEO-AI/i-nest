# PolarityMultiSpikeMapping

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A conversion technique that maps quantized neural network activations to multiple spikes of both positive and negative polarity within a single timestep, enabling near-lossless ANN-to-SNN conversion. This enables high-accuracy SNN inference at the first timestep, drastically reducing latency and energy costs in neuromorphic hardware.
