# MultiProtocolSwitchChip

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A switching chip integrating protocol controllers for RapidIO, PCIe, Fibre Channel, 204B/204C, and TSN, supporting both single- and multi-protocol exchange. It provides the physical substrate and protocol bridging needed to build heterogeneous topology-centric computing fabrics.
