# StructuralConstraintAwareLearning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning paradigm where the training algorithm explicitly incorporates the physical or logical topology constraints (e.g., link bandwidth, node degree) into the optimization objective. Enables design of in-network computing systems where learning must adapt to pre-existing interconnect fabrics rather than assuming unconstrained connectivity.
