# Memristive_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The use of memristors as the physical medium for interconnects in the iNEST architecture, combining memory and resistance switching to support both data transfer and synaptic weight storage. Memristive interconnects bridge TCC's topology-centric routing with iNEST's neuromorphic plasticity, enabling in-network computation and non-volatile state retention critical for energy-efficient brain-like processing.
