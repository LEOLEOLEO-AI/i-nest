# Compute_Centric_NoC

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A network-on-chip architecture where compute nodes (SMs) also act as communication endpoints, forcing collective operations to compete with computation for bandwidth and scheduling slots on the same physical plane. Understanding this baseline is essential for TCC researchers to identify bottlenecks that topology innovations must overcome to achieve scalable, low-latency inter-chiplet communication.
