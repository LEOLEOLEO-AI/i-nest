# StochasticBinaryDynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A computational framework where each neuron updates its binary state probabilistically based on the weighted sum of delayed inputs from other neurons. Provides a probabilistic computing substrate that maps naturally to spiking neuromorphic circuits and supports energy-efficient stochastic inference.
