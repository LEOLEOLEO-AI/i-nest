## Definition
The expansion of the parameter space over which a system exhibits critical-like behavior, often achieved through coupling or heterogeneity, as opposed to a single critical point. Enables topology-centric computing architectures to maintain adaptive, high-information processing across varying network conditions, improving fault tolerance and dynamic reconfiguration.
