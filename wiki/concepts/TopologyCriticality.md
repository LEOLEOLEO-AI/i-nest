## Definition
The property of a network topology where small changes in connectivity or parameters lead to dramatic shifts in global signal propagation characteristics, akin to phase transitions in physical systems. Understanding topology criticality helps in designing interconnect architectures that avoid fragile operating regimes, ensuring predictable and scalable performance in large-scale TCC deployments.
