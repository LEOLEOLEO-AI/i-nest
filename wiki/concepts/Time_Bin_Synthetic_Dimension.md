# Time_Bin_Synthetic_Dimension

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
An encoding in which photonic modes are represented by discrete arrival-time slots along a delay loop, so that temporal position acts as an extra synthetic lattice dimension orthogonal to the physical waveguide layout. It extends topology-centric design into synthetic dimensions, allowing effective graph connectivity to be programmed by timing rather than by physically laying out more links.
