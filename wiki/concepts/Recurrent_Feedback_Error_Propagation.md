# Recurrent_Feedback_Error_Propagation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The mechanism in recurrent spiking networks where conversion errors are not isolated but propagate and accumulate through feedback connections, exacerbating output deviations in closed-loop control scenarios. This concept is key for TCC because it underscores the importance of designing topology-aware conversion or training strategies that account for recurrent dynamics, rather than treating layers independently.
