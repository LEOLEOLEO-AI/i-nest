## Definition
A computational paradigm where a fixed, randomly connected photonic network (the reservoir) maps input signals into high-dimensional nonlinear states, which are then read out by a simple linear layer. Enables energy-efficient, high-speed analog computation in optical hardware, directly relevant to TCC's topology-driven processing and iNEST's neuromorphic photonic architectures.
