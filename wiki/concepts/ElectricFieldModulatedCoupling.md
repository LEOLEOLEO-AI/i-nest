# ElectricFieldModulatedCoupling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where an external or internally generated electric field dynamically alters the effective coupling conductance between neurons in a network. Provides a mechanism for reconfigurable synaptic weights in topological computing architectures without physical rewiring.
