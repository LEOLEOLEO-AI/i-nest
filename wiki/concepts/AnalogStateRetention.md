# AnalogStateRetention

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability of an organic transistor to maintain its programmed conductance state over time without significant drift, crucial for stable synaptic weight storage. Ensures reliable long-term operation of neuromorphic circuits, which is critical for persistent in-network learning and memory in TCC/iNEST systems.
