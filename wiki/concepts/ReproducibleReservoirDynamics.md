## Definition
The property that a thermally driven superparamagnet reservoir yields consistent, repeatable state trajectories under identical temperature and input conditions, despite stochastic thermal fluctuations. Ensures deterministic computation and reliable inference in TCC systems, which is essential for reproducibility in neuromorphic co-processors and in-network decision making.
