# PostHocInterpretabilityContrast

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The distinction between interpretability that is achieved after model training through external methods (post-hoc) versus interpretability that exists inherently during the model's operation. This contrast informs TCC/iNEST system design choices, as inherent interpretability eliminates the computational overhead of post-hoc explanation modules in resource-constrained network nodes.
