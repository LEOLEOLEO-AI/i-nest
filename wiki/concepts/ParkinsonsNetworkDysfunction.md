## Definition
The characteristic disruption of basal ganglia-thalamocortical circuits and dopaminergic signaling that underlies motor and cognitive deficits in Parkinson's disease. Serves as a model for pathological network states, informing the design of fault-tolerant or self-repairing in-network computational architectures.
