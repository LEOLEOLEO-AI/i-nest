# MatureProcessSystemIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
A design strategy that uses mature semiconductor processes together with advanced packaging and chiplet interconnects to build systems that do not rely entirely on leading-edge process nodes. It provides a viable independent technical route for advanced computing systems when advanced processes are restricted, making it central to TCC's mission of topology-centric system design.
