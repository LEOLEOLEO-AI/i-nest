# Hardware_Determinism_Contract

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
The architectural guarantee that the hardware provides deterministic execution semantics as a first-class contract to software, rather than relying on runtime scheduling to enforce ordering. This contract simplifies verification and synchronization for TCC/iNEST systems that require strict timing and ordering guarantees.
