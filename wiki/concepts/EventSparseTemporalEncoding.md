**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method that converts raw event camera spikes into compact, temporally structured representations that capture action-relevant motion dynamics while discarding redundant events. Reduces computational and memory overhead on neuromorphic chips, enabling efficient continual learning from high-bandwidth event streams in real-time.
