# OpticalNetworkOnChip

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A network-on-chip design that employs photonic waveguides and optical switches to route data packets between processing elements, replacing traditional electronic routers. This matters for TCC as it reduces communication latency and power consumption, which are key constraints in densely packed chiplet architectures.
