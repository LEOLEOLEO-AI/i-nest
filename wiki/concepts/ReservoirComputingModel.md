# ReservoirComputingModel

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-12

## Definition
A computational framework where a fixed, randomly connected recurrent network (the reservoir) maps inputs to high-dimensional states, and only readout weights are trained. Provides a biologically plausible and energy-efficient paradigm for neuromorphic computing, directly applicable to in-network processing and learning.
