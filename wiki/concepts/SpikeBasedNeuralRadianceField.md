## Definition
A neural radiance field (NeRF) architecture implemented using spiking neural networks (SNNs) where neuron activations are binary spikes over time, encoding scene geometry and appearance through temporal spike patterns. Bridges neuromorphic computing with 3D vision, offering energy-efficient, event-driven rendering that aligns with iNEST's goal of in-network, low-power visual processing.
