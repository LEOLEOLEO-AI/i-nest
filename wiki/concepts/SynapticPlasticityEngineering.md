**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A methodology for precisely tuning the weight-update rules of memristive synapses to achieve desired neural precision and temporal learning characteristics in spiking neural networks. Enables the design of neuromorphic hardware with controllable learning dynamics, critical for building reliable and efficient in-network neural processing systems.
