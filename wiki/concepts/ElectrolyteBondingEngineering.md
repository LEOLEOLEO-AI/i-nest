# ElectrolyteBondingEngineering

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A fabrication technique that modifies the bonding structure of the electrolyte layer in GeTe-based conductive-bridge RAM (CBRAM) to improve device uniformity and switching reliability. Enables highly uniform analog switching in CBRAM devices, which is critical for reliable synaptic weight updates in neuromorphic computing systems.
