# Liquid_Topology_Computing

**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A computing paradigm where the interconnect topology is dynamically reconfigurable rather than fixed, enabling adaptive network structures that respond to workload demands. It directly challenges the traditional fixed-topology assumption in AI compute systems, offering a path to overcome the data-movement wall by reshaping communication paths at runtime.
