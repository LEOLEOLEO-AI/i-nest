# RunawayAvalanche

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An avalanche event that spans the entire system, exhibiting a characteristic size that grows with system size, in contrast to scale-free avalanches that have no characteristic size. Runaway avalanches represent catastrophic, non-local events that can disrupt computation in in-network or topological architectures, making their onset a key design constraint for fault-tolerant systems.
