# OnChipOpticalTweezers

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A photonic device that uses integrated optical waveguides to trap and manipulate bioparticles on a chip via gradient forces from evanescent fields. Enables high-throughput, reconfigurable manipulation of particles directly within photonic circuits, potentially serving as a novel actuation mechanism for TCC-based lab-on-chip systems.
