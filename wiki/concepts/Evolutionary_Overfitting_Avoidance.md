# Evolutionary_Overfitting_Avoidance

**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A principle in swarm-based topology search that prevents the optimized network from being overly specialized to a single training signal, preserving generalization across tasks. Ensures that optimized connectome reservoirs remain robust and reusable in dynamic in-network environments, not just on benchmark data.
