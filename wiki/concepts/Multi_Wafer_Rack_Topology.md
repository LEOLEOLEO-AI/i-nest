# Multi_Wafer_Rack_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-11

## Definition
A rack-level organization in which two or more wafer-scale engines are coupled into one logical AI computer, requiring a hierarchical topology that spans intra-wafer mesh links and inter-wafer links. CS-4's three-wafer rack pushes TCC beyond single-wafer assumptions, making cross-wafer topology modeling, partition placement, and inter-wafer traffic minimization central open problems.
