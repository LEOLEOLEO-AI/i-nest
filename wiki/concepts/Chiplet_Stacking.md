## Definition
Chiplet stacking is a 3DHI method where multiple functional dies are vertically integrated, enabling modular design and heterogeneous composition of compute, memory, and I/O. For TCC, chiplet stacking provides the physical substrate for constructing reconfigurable, topology-centric compute fabrics with diverse resource types.
