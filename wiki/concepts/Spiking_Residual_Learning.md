**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A training framework that adapts residual connections to the discrete, event-driven nature of spiking neurons, allowing gradients to flow through binary spike activations. It bridges the gap between deep learning architectures and neuromorphic hardware, making it feasible to deploy high-capacity models on in-network spiking processors.
