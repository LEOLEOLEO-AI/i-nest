## Definition
The framework in which a system poised at a critical point is treated as a computational substrate, exploiting its long-range correlations, avalanche dynamics, and sensitivity for tasks such as reservoir computing. Justifies designing iNEST hardware to self-organize toward criticality rather than away from it, linking thermodynamic descriptors to computational performance.
