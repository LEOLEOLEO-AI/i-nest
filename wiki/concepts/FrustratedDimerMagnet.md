## Definition
A magnetic system composed of coupled spin dimers with competing interactions that prevent simultaneous satisfaction of all bonds, leading to geometric frustration and quantum fluctuations. Serves as a natural substrate for studying topological and quantum effects, offering insights into how frustration can be harnessed for robust, energy-efficient neuromorphic computing in interconnect topologies.
