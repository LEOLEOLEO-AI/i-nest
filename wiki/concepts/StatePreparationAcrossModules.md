**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process of initializing a desired quantum state (e.g., a logical qubit or entangled resource) distributed across multiple modules, using noisy inter-module operations. Efficient state preparation is critical for TCC because it determines the overhead and latency of establishing the initial topology-aware resource states needed for computation.
