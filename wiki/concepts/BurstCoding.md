# BurstCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A neural coding scheme where information is represented by the timing and density of rapid spike bursts rather than individual spikes. Enables higher temporal resolution and energy-efficient communication in neuromorphic hardware, critical for in-network spike processing.
