# Interconnect_Scaling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The trend of shrinking wire dimensions and increasing density of on-chip and off-chip interconnects, including optical and RF alternatives. Determines the latency and energy cost of each hop in a TCC network, and influences whether in-network neuromorphic processing (iNEST) can be embedded at the link level.
