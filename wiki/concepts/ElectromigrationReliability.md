# ElectromigrationReliability

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The failure mechanism where high current density causes metal atoms to migrate, leading to voids and open circuits in interconnect lines. Reliability of interconnects is paramount for TCC's long-term operation in large-scale neuromorphic or topology-centric systems, where current densities are high and failures are costly.
