# SpikeMulticastRouting

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
NoC routing in which a single spike packet is replicated and delivered to multiple destination cores, matching the fan-out structure of axonal spike distribution in neuromorphic hardware. Multicast is the dominant communication primitive in in-network neuromorphic systems, making its support central to iNEST interconnect and SDI design.
