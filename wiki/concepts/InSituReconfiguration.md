# InSituReconfiguration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The ability to modify the physical parameters of a wave-guiding network during operation, without halting or external recalibration, to adapt to changing computational tasks. Essential for runtime-reconfigurable TCC fabrics, enabling dynamic topology changes for task-specific dataflow without costly off-chip reprogramming.
