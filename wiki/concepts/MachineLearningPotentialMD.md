# MachineLearningPotentialMD

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A molecular dynamics simulation approach that uses a machine-learned interatomic potential to model atomic interactions with near-DFT accuracy at reduced computational cost. Enables efficient and accurate study of fracture and mechanical properties in novel materials like θ-TaN, which are relevant for robust interconnect and device design in TCC/iNEST architectures.
