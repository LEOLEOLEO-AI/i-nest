# Mott_Device_Array

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-15

## Definition
An array of niobium-oxide Mott memristive devices whose insulator-metal transition enables nonlinear, history-dependent conductance states for unsupervised learning. Serves as a physical substrate for implementing synaptic plasticity and manifold learning directly in hardware, bypassing von Neumann bottlenecks.
