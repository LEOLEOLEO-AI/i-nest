# SpikeDrivenDataAssociation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A data association mechanism that uses spike-based similarity metrics (e.g., coincidence of spike trains) to match detections to tracks, replacing Hungarian or IoU-based matching. Aligns with topology-centric computing by exploiting the temporal and spatial topology of spike events, reducing computational overhead in distributed tracking systems.
