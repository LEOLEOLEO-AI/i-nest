**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A variant of equilibrium propagation that applies a finite-amplitude output nudge to compute local weight updates in stochastic networks. It enables gradient-based learning in thermal neuromorphic substrates where infinitesimal nudges are impractical due to noise.
