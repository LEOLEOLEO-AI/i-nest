## Definition
The sequential propagation of feature detection signals through the layers of a deep linear network, where each layer's output is influenced by the regularizer-induced state of the previous layer, creating a cascading effect. This concept is critical for mapping multi-layer neural computations onto hierarchical network topologies in TCC, enabling efficient in-network processing of complex features.
