# Event_Driven_Spike_Inference

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
An inference paradigm where computation is triggered only by incoming spike events rather than by dense, clock-driven CNN operations. Event-driven inference enables iNEST systems to achieve low latency and low power by processing sparse neural activity only when spikes occur.
