# Gamma_st_SignAdjustedNMI

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
A revised spatiotemporal coupling metric Γst defined as the sign-adjusted Normalized Mutual Information between spatial and temporal state matrices, where the sign is derived from the Mantel test between distance matrices of activity and functional connectivity. This addresses statistical and differentiability flaws in prior Γst formulations, enabling more reliable spatiotemporal coupling quantification in TCC/iNEST architectures.
