**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A NoC routing policy that prioritizes and schedules SDI video stream packets based on their temporal and spatial dataflow requirements, ensuring deterministic delivery to NPU cores. This ensures quality-of-service for video streams in TCC systems, preventing data loss or jitter during neural inference on continuous media.
