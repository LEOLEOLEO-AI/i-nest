# Deterministic_Execution

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-13

## Definition
Deterministic execution means every instruction has a compile-time-known latency and completion cycle, so hardware never arbitrates or reorders at runtime. Determinism is the key property enabling reproducible, timing-predictable pipelines that both TCC scheduling models and iNEST neuromorphic timing studies require.
