# ModelToChipCompilation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of directly compiling a trained AI model's computational graph into a hardware chip architecture, eliminating the traditional software-to-hardware abstraction layers. This is a core TCC paradigm shift where the model itself becomes the topology, enabling extreme efficiency gains by removing instruction fetch/decode overhead.
