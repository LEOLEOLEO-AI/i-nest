# Spinal_CPG_Coordinated_Control

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A spiking neural architecture that models central pattern generators (CPGs) in the spinal cord to coordinate rhythmic arm and locomotor movements. This provides a biologically plausible, event-driven framework for multi-limb coordination in neuromorphic robots, enabling low-latency and energy-efficient locomotion control.
