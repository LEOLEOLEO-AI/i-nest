**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A coarse-graining methodology that iteratively maps device-level nonlinearities, such as memristor resistance changes, to neuron-, network-, and system-level dynamics. It enables derivation of macroscopic system equations without simulating every microscopic detail, essential for scalable TCC/iNEST design.
