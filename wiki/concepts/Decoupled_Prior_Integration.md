# Decoupled_Prior_Integration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of separating prior knowledge from real-time evidence streams within a free-energy minimization framework, allowing independent update rates for priors and sensory inputs. This enables TCC topologies to maintain stable network priors while rapidly adapting to traffic changes, and in iNEST, it supports hierarchical neuromorphic memory without catastrophic forgetting.
