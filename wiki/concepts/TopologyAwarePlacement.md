# TopologyAwarePlacement

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A floorplanning strategy that explicitly optimizes block positions based on the target interconnect topology, rather than treating wires as uniform. Aligns physical layout with logical network structure, reducing latency and energy in TCC systems where interconnect topology dominates performance.
