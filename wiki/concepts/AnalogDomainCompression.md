# AnalogDomainCompression

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of compressing signal information in the analog domain before digitization, using the reservoir's state as a compressed representation of the input. Reduces ADC bandwidth and power requirements in TCC/iNEST systems, enabling scalable, energy-efficient signal acquisition and processing.
