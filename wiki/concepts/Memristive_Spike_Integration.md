# Memristive_Spike_Integration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-16

## Definition
The use of a memristor's analog state to accumulate and integrate incoming spike signals, mimicking the temporal integration of biological neurons. Enables non-volatile, energy-efficient temporal processing in neuromorphic hardware, essential for real-time spike-based learning in iNEST architectures.
