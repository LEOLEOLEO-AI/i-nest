# DeeplySupervisedSpiking

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A network architecture where auxiliary supervision signals are injected at multiple hidden layers of a spiking neural network, addressing the vanishing gradient problem in temporal credit assignment. This enables training of deeper SNNs, which is critical for complex tasks like traffic sign recognition, and aligns with iNEST's goal of deploying sophisticated models in resource-constrained environments.
