**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A learning principle where adaptation is driven by minimizing the discrepancy between predicted and actual sensory or task outcomes, treating surprise as the primary teaching signal. It reframes neuromorphic learning objectives around predictive error, making iNEST architectures more biologically aligned and capable of continual adaptation.
