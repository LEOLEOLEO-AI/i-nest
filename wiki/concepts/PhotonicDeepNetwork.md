## Definition
A multi-layer neural network architecture implemented using photonic components (e.g., Mach-Zehnder interferometers, phase shifters) where both forward propagation and learning signals remain in the optical domain. Demonstrates the feasibility of scaling photonic neuromorphic systems beyond single-layer reservoirs, addressing depth and expressivity for complex tasks.
