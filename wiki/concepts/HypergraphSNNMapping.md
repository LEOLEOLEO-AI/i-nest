# HypergraphSNNMapping

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Representing spiking neural network communication as hyperedges (multi-terminal nets) and partitioning them onto mesh NoC tiles, instead of the conventional graph-based pairwise edge-cut formulation. Hypergraph partitioning better captures one-to-many spike broadcasts, giving TCC researchers a more faithful abstraction of SNN traffic for topology-centric placement and routing.
