# ShortTermMemory

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The temporary retention of input-induced state changes in the reservoir, allowing past inputs to influence current outputs over a finite time window. Critical for temporal signal processing tasks in reservoir computing, enabling tasks like speech recognition or time-series prediction without external memory.
