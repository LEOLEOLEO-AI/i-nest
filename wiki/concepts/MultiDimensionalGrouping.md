# MultiDimensionalGrouping

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method that groups spiking transformer computations across multiple dimensions (e.g., spatial, temporal, and channel) to reduce redundant processing and enhance energy efficiency. Enables ultra-high energy efficiency in spiking transformers, which is critical for deploying neuromorphic models on resource-constrained in-network hardware.
