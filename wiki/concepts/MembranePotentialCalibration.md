# MembranePotentialCalibration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-12

## Definition
A technique that adjusts quantization scales based on the distribution of membrane potentials observed during inference, rather than only weight/activation ranges. Improves robustness of quantized SNNs to input variability, crucial for in-network processing of streaming data.
