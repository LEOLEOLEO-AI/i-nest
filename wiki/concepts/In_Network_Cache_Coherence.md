# In_Network_Cache_Coherence

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-25

## Definition
A protocol or mechanism where network switches or smart NICs actively participate in maintaining consistency and freshness of KV cache entries across multiple GPU nodes. Reduces the latency and overhead of cache synchronization, making distributed LLM inference more scalable and efficient in TCC architectures.
