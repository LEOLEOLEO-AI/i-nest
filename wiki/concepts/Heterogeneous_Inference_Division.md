**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A computing architecture strategy where different memory technologies (e.g., SRAM and HBM) are assigned distinct roles in the inference pipeline rather than one replacing the other. This concept is central to TCC/iNEST as it guides the design of memory hierarchies and dataflow in topology-centric and in-network neuromorphic systems, optimizing for latency and energy efficiency.
