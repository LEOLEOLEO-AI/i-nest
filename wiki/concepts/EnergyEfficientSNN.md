**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A design principle for spiking networks that minimizes energy consumption by exploiting event-driven computation and sparse spike activity, often quantified by synaptic operations per inference. Energy efficiency is the core value proposition of iNEST, making this concept essential for justifying neuromorphic hardware in edge and network-embedded systems.
