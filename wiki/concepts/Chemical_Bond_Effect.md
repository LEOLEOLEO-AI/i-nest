## Definition
A connectivity mechanism in SDI that forms stable yet reconfigurable links between chiplets, analogized to chemical bonds for constructing network topologies. It enables scalable and selective chiplet-to-chiplet interactions essential for building adaptive plastic networks on wafer-scale systems.
