# SynapticProcessingElement

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A hardware unit dedicated to performing the synaptic weight multiplication and accumulation for a subset of synapses, enabling fine-grained parallel computation in SNN accelerators. Forms the fundamental building block for exploiting synapse-level parallelism, which is a novel approach for high-density neuromorphic computing in iNEST.
