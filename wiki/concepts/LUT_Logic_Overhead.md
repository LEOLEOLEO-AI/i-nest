# LUT_Logic_Overhead

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The 10-14x energy penalty of implementing combinational logic with look-up tables in FPGAs compared to ASIC standard cells, from Kuon & Rose FPGA 2007. This overhead drives TCC research toward dedicated, topology-specific hard logic blocks rather than generic programmable fabrics.
