## Definition
The on-chip interconnect and arbitration mechanism that transports spike events between time-multiplexed neuron units and the pipelined readout stages. Determines the achievable spike throughput and communication bottleneck, directly impacting the efficiency of time-multiplexed SNN accelerators in network-centric computing.
