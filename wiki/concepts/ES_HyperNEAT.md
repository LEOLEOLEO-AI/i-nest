## Definition
Evolvable-Substrate HyperNEAT is a neuroevolution method that evolves neural network topologies by using a CPPN to query spatial coordinates and adaptively subdividing the substrate to place neurons and connections. It provides a topology-generating mechanism for TCC/iNEST to discover scalable network architectures through indirect encoding.
