# In_Package_Optical_Interconnect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Use of optical links within a chiplet package to replace electrical traces for high-speed, low-latency data transfer between compute and memory dies. Reduces communication bottlenecks and energy overhead in iNEST and TCC systems, enabling scalable neuromorphic and graph-centric workloads.
