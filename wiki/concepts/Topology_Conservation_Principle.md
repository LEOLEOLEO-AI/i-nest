# Topology_Conservation_Principle

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The principle that certain topological features of neural circuits (e.g., local recurrent connectivity, feedforward-feedback loops) are preserved across species, while global hierarchical rankings may vary. Directs TCC design to embed conserved local motifs as fixed structural primitives, while allowing higher-level network reconfiguration to accommodate divergent functional demands.
