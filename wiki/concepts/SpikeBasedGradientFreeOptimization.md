# SpikeBasedGradientFreeOptimization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An optimization framework that treats spike timing and firing rates as black-box functions, using evolutionary strategies to search for optimal synaptic weights without relying on gradient information. Provides a biologically plausible and hardware-friendly alternative to backpropagation for neuromorphic systems, crucial for on-chip learning in iNEST architectures.
