# Electrothermal_Phase_Change_Mechanism

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A prior mechanistic approach for emulating chaotic-edge behavior in electronic devices, based on thermally induced phase transitions. Identified as a key limitation due to high power consumption and poor similarity to biological ion dynamics, motivating diffusion-based alternatives.
