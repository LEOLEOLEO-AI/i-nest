# Mesoscopic_Spectral_Dimension

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A scale-dependent spectral dimension extracted over intermediate length/time windows that can differ markedly from the asymptotic value in inhomogeneous or hierarchically structured networks. For iNEST and TCC systems operating at finite graph sizes and finite time horizons, the mesoscopic regime — not the asymptotic one — governs observable wave spread, memory capacity and computation, so device-level design must be matched to this scale-dependent exponent.
