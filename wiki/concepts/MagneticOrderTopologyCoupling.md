## Definition
The coupling between antiferromagnetic spin ordering and the topological band structure, where the magnetic order breaks time-reversal symmetry and renormalizes the Chern number or Z2 invariant. Understanding this coupling enables control of topological states via magnetic fields or spin textures, potentially enabling reconfigurable topological devices for in-network processing.
