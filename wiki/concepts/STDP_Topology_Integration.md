# STDP_Topology_Integration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The combined use of Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP) learning rules with network topology design, where synaptic weight updates depend on the temporal order of spikes and the structural arrangement of neurons. This integration is essential for TCC/iNEST as it enables self-organizing, topology-driven neural networks that can adapt their connectivity and computation in response to spatiotemporal input patterns.
