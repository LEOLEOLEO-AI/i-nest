# Reticle_Limit

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The photomask field-size bound (approximately 858 mm² for a 26×33 mm reticle) that caps the maximum area of a monolithic die. Motivates multi-die/chiplet designs and makes on-package interconnect topology a critical scaling factor for AI, HPC, and ADAS systems.
