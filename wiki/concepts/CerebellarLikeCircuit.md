# CerebellarLikeCircuit

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
A neural circuit motif in electric fish that compares predicted sensory input with actual electrosensory signals and uses plastic synapses to suppress predictable stimuli. Provides a biologically validated blueprint for in-network neuromorphic filters that cancel expected signals and enhance novelty detection.
