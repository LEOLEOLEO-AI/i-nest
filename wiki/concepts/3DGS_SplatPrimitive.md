## Definition
A 3D Gaussian splat is a primitive representation of a scene element defined by a position, covariance matrix, opacity, and color, used as the atomic unit in 3D Gaussian Splatting rendering. Provides a compact, differentiable scene representation that could be mapped to network-embedded state nodes for topology-aware spatial computing.
