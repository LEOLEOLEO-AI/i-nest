**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The nonlinear response of optical materials or devices (e.g., saturable absorbers, Kerr media) that enables the reservoir to perform nonlinear transformations on input signals. Provides the essential nonlinear activation function in photonic reservoirs, mimicking neural dynamics for in-network neuromorphic computing.
