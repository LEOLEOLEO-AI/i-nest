## Definition
The concept that the specific connectivity pattern (e.g., small-world, scale-free, or modular) of a computing network intrinsically determines its ability to withstand node or link failures. Guides the design of topology-centric computing substrates where robustness is a direct outcome of graph structure rather than added redundancy.
