# NeuromorphicHardwareTrojan

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A malicious modification inserted into neuromorphic hardware or its configuration (e.g., synaptic weights, thresholds) that triggers misbehavior under specific input patterns. Matters for TCC/iNEST because hardware-level trojans can subvert both topology and spike routing, undermining trust in the physical substrate of in-network computing.
