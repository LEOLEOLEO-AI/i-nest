# Memristive_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
Memristive plasticity is the history-dependent, non-volatile conductance change of a memristive junction that mimics synaptic weight update under the influence of local voltage or current stimuli. It is the elementary in-network mechanism by which iNEST architectures realize on-device synaptic weight storage and local Hebbian-like adaptation without external memory access.
