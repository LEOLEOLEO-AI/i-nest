## Definition
The intermediate level of brain activity between single neurons and whole-brain networks, characterized by local field potentials and cortical columns, modeled as coherent mesh regions. Bridges the gap between microscopic spiking models and macroscopic brain states, informing the design of neuromorphic hardware that operates at biologically relevant intermediate scales.
