# CollapsibleDepth

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A property of certain learning architectures where the effective depth can be mathematically reduced to a shallower equivalent, revealing that the original depth is not computationally necessary. Enables neuromorphic hardware to implement complex tasks with fewer physical layers, reducing latency and power consumption in in-network processing.
