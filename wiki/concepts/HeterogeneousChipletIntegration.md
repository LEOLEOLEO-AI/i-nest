## Definition
A packaging approach that combines multiple specialized chiplets (e.g., compute, memory, I/O) on a single wafer-scale substrate, each optimized for different functions. Allows flexible composition of diverse compute resources, critical for mapping arbitrary topologies in TCC architectures.
