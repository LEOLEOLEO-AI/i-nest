# Topological_Insulator_Coexistence

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The simultaneous presence of topologically protected conducting surface states and trivial insulating bulk states in a single material system. Enables design of devices where edge-state conduction can be selectively switched or modulated, offering new mechanisms for in-network signal routing and computation.
