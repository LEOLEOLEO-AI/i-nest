## Definition
A network topology metric that quantifies the degree to which causal interactions among nodes are integrated (non-decomposable) versus segregated, derived from integrated information theory. Guides the design of interconnection topologies that maximize global computational coherence while avoiding fragmentation, a key challenge in large-scale TCC systems.
