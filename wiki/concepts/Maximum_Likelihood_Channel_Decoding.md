**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The optimal decoding strategy that selects the codeword maximizing the likelihood of the received signal, often computationally intractable for large codes. Provides a benchmark for evaluating neuromorphic decoders against classical optimal performance, guiding hardware design for error correction in communication systems.
