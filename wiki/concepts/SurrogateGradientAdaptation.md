# SurrogateGradientAdaptation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A training technique that dynamically adjusts surrogate gradient shapes or scales during backpropagation to improve spike-based learning in transformer architectures. Addresses the non-differentiability of spike activations, making it feasible to train deep spiking networks with high temporal precision for real-time neuromorphic inference.
