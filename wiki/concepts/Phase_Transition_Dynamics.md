# Phase_Transition_Dynamics

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The study of how collective oscillator behavior changes abruptly as a control parameter (e.g., coupling strength or nonreciprocity) is varied. Understanding these transitions allows engineers to tune network parameters to achieve desired computational regimes, from ordered to chaotic, in TCC/iNEST devices.
