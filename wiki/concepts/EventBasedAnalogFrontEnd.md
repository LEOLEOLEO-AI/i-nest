# EventBasedAnalogFrontEnd

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An analog front-end circuit that directly outputs event-based spikes from bio-signals without conventional Nyquist-rate sampling, using threshold-triggered encoding. Critical for building distributed neuromorphic sensing nodes that can interface with spiking neural networks in real-time, minimizing power and bandwidth in TCC architectures.
