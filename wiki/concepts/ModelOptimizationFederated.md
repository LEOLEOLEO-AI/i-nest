## Definition
A technique for updating shared machine learning models across distributed nodes without centralizing raw data, often using gradient aggregation or knowledge distillation. Enables privacy-preserving, bandwidth-efficient learning in TCC/iNEST systems where data is generated at the edge and communication is constrained.
