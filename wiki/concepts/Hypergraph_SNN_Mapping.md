# Hypergraph_SNN_Mapping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A mapping methodology that models spiking neural networks as hypergraphs, where each hyperedge captures the many-to-many fan-out communication pattern of a spiking neuron, rather than as a conventional pairwise graph. Hypergraph modeling better reflects the intrinsic multicast nature of spike propagation, enabling more accurate neuron-to-core placement for TCC/iNEST mesh NoC platforms.
