## Definition
The ratio of current at the selected cell to the current at unselected cells in a crossbar array, quantifying the effectiveness of self-rectification in suppressing sneak paths. A key performance metric for evaluating memristor devices in crossbar arrays, directly influencing the achievable array size and computational accuracy in TCC systems.
