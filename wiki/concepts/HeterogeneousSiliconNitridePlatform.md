# HeterogeneousSiliconNitridePlatform

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A silicon nitride (SiN) waveguide platform co-integrated with other material systems (e.g., III-V or silicon) to combine low-loss passive routing with active photonic functions. Provides a low-loss, CMOS-compatible backbone for TCC networks, enabling efficient optical interconnects with reduced power dissipation compared to silicon-only platforms.
