## Definition
A Hebbian learning variant that incorporates attention-like weighting of inputs, where synaptic updates are modulated by the relevance or salience of the pre-synaptic signal. Enhances the selectivity and efficiency of learning in distributed or in-network systems, allowing resources to focus on critical information streams.
