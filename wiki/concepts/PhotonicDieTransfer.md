# PhotonicDieTransfer

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A process step where individual photonic micro-dies (e.g., modulators, lasers, detectors) are picked and placed onto a host photonic wafer with sub-micron alignment. Provides modular, pre-tested component placement, reducing fabrication complexity and enabling rapid prototyping of heterogeneous TCC optical switches and routers.
