# L2_L3_Reconfigurability

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability to modify the mapping between different graph abstraction layers (L2 and L3) in neuromorphic chips, but at different levels for Loihi and SpiNNaker. Understanding which layer is reconfigurable in each chip is essential for correctly mapping TCC graph structures onto hardware without mismatches.
