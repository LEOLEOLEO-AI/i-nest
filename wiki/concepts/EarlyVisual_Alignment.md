# EarlyVisual_Alignment

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The observation that early visual cortical areas (e.g., V1, V2) exhibit highly similar representational geometry across species, despite later higher-order areas diverging in functional ranking. Provides a stable, cross-species anchor for designing low-level sensory processing layers in neuromorphic systems, ensuring robust input encoding before higher-order cognitive modules.
