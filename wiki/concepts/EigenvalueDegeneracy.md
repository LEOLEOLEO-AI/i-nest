## Definition
The coalescence of two or more eigenvalues in a non-Hermitian matrix, often associated with exceptional points where eigenvectors also merge. Degeneracies signal critical transitions in network behavior, relevant for designing robust or reconfigurable topological and neuromorphic architectures.
