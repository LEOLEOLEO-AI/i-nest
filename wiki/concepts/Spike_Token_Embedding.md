**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of encoding input tokens as temporal spike patterns, replacing continuous vector embeddings in the GPT architecture. This enables direct interfacing of language models with spiking neural networks, preserving temporal information and supporting spike-based processing in iNEST.
