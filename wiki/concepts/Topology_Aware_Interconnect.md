# Topology_Aware_Interconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An on-chip or inter-chiplet interconnect architecture whose physical layout and routing logic are directly derived from the logical topology of the target neural or graph structure. Reduces communication overhead and latency in TCC systems by aligning physical wiring with the dataflow requirements of the topology, avoiding generic mesh or bus bottlenecks.
