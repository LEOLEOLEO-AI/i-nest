# ProbabilityDensityNeuralField

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A continuous representation of neuron states (e.g., membrane potential) as a probability density function evolving under a transport equation, capturing the statistical distribution of neural activity. Enables analytical prediction of network-level dynamics without simulating every spike, crucial for understanding emergent computation in large-scale iNEST architectures.
