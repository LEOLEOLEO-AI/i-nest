# SimplicialComplexReduction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that iteratively removes faces (nodes, edges, triangles, etc.) from a simplicial complex based on their dynamical redundancy, rather than purely structural criteria. Provides a principled way to prune higher-order connectivity in TCC/iNEST architectures, reducing hardware complexity and energy consumption without sacrificing dynamic performance.
