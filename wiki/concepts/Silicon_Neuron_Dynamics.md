## Definition
Mathematical and circuit-level models of biological neurons (e.g., integrate-and-fire or Hodgkin-Huxley) implemented in silicon to replicate spiking behavior. Provides the computational substrate for mimicking pathological and healthy neural patterns, enabling biologically plausible control in adaptive stimulation systems.
