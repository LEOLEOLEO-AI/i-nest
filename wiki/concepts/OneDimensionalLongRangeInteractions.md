# OneDimensionalLongRangeInteractions

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A model where spins interact over arbitrary distances with a power-law decay, rather than only nearest neighbors, in a one-dimensional chain. Enables the study of non-local coupling effects relevant to long-range connectivity in network topologies and in-memory computing substrates.
