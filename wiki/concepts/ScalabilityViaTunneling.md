# ScalabilityViaTunneling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The principle that using quantum tunneling-based annealing (instead of thermal or simulated annealing) allows Ising machines to scale to larger problem sizes without exponential slowdown. Addresses the dark silicon and energy bottleneck by enabling more powerful optimization per unit area and power in manycore neuromorphic architectures.
