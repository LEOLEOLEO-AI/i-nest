# advanced packaging

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
Silicon interposer, bridge, and 2.5D/3D packaging technologies (e.g., CoWoS-class approaches) that physically realize dense chiplet-to-chiplet interconnects and thereby fix the achievable topology. Packaging technology bounds the feasible topology space that TCC architectures can instantiate and determines their manufacturability and cost.
