## Definition
The collective, intermittent plastic deformation behavior of disordered solids, characterized by localized shear transformations that coalesce into avalanches. This provides a physical model for non-linear, history-dependent responses that can be exploited for memory or computation in novel materials, bridging mechanics with neuromorphic and topological computing concepts.
