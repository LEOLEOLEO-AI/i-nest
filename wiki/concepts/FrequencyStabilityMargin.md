**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The allowable deviation in grid frequency before triggering under-frequency load shedding or other protective actions, used as a constraint in inertia estimation. Defines the operational envelope that TCC/iNEST controllers must enforce, guiding the design of low-latency, in-network decision-making for emergency response.
