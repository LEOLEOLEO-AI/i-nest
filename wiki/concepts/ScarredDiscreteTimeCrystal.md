## Definition
A discrete time crystal phase that emerges from quantum many-body scars in a periodically driven dimerized spin chain, exhibiting subharmonic response without full thermalization. Provides a robust, non-thermalizing temporal order that could be used for stable periodic signal generation or synchronization in topological network architectures.
