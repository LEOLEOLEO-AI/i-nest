## Definition
A phase where disorder-induced topological Anderson localization coexists with trivial insulating states in a Chern insulator, leading to a mixed topological-trivial insulating regime. This concept is critical for TCC because it demonstrates how disorder can engineer or destroy topological protection, directly impacting the reliability of topological states used in topology-centric computing architectures.
