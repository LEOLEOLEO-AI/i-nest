# PinAssignmentOptimization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of jointly optimizing the assignment of I/O pins to package pads or interposer bumps alongside floorplanning, to minimize routing congestion and signal delay. Pin assignment critically affects routability and signal integrity in TCC interconnects, and joint optimization avoids suboptimal sequential design.
