# Discrete_Event_Spike_Simulation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-25

## Definition
A simulation paradigm where neural activity is modeled as a sequence of discrete spike events rather than continuous time dynamics, aligned with Petri net semantics. Reduces computational overhead and improves simulation speed, making it feasible to prototype large-scale biological circuits in real-time or near-real-time hardware.
