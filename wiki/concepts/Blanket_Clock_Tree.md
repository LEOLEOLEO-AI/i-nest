# Blanket_Clock_Tree

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A clock distribution network that is active across the entire FPGA or chip regardless of logic utilization, causing continuous dynamic power dissipation. This constant clock power is a fundamental barrier for fine-grained topological reconfiguration, pushing TCC designs toward locally-gated or asynchronous interconnect.
