# AllOpticalHebbianLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning mechanism where synaptic weight updates are performed entirely through optical signals, without electronic conversion, based on the correlation of input and output light intensities. Enables ultra-fast, energy-efficient unsupervised learning in photonic neuromorphic networks, a key step toward practical optical neural accelerators.
