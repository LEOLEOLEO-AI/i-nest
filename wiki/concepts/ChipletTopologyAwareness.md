# ChipletTopologyAwareness

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The design principle where scheduling and placement decisions incorporate the physical and logical interconnection structure of chiplets, including link latencies and bandwidth asymmetries. Prevents naive load balancing that ignores the cost of data movement, which is a primary energy and latency overhead in TCC systems.
