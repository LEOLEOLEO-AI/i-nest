# DirectTrainingFromScratch

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training methodology for spiking neural networks that optimizes network parameters directly via surrogate gradients without pre-training or conversion from analog neural networks. Overcomes the inefficiency and accuracy loss of ANN-to-SNN conversion, making it feasible to deploy high-performance SNNs on neuromorphic chips for practical tasks.
