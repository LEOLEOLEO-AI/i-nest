# DisorderDrivenQuantumPhaseTransition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A phase transition in amorphous carbon where increasing structural disorder drives the system from a metallic to an insulating quantum critical state without thermal fluctuations. Offers a mechanism for tunable, noise-resilient switching in neuromorphic or topological devices, where disorder can be engineered to control criticality.
