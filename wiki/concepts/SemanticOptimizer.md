**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A large language model (LLM) that interprets high-level routing objectives expressed in natural language and translates them into multi-objective optimization parameters for network path selection. Enables flexible, context-aware routing decisions in topology-centric computing, where diverse workloads require dynamic adaptation beyond fixed heuristics.
