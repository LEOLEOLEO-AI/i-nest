# AnalogSwitchingDynamics

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The continuous, incremental change of device conductance across many intermediate levels rather than discrete binary switching, driven by gradual filament modulation. Analog switching is the physical basis for graded synaptic weights and homeostatic plasticity in neuromorphic in-network computing substrates.
