# Inter_Wafer_Bandwidth_Bottleneck

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-11

## Definition
The structural asymmetry in which bandwidth available between separate wafers is far lower than bandwidth available within a wafer's on-die mesh, creating a dominant constraint on scaling wafer systems. It is the key limiting factor for rack-scale TCC designs, driving research into partitioning, locality-aware placement, and data movement reduction across wafer boundaries.
