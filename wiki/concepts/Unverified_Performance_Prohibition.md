# Unverified_Performance_Prohibition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A rule that forbids citing unlabeled performance, energy, area, cost, latency, or throughput numbers in TCC research. Ensures that TCC claims are grounded in measurable, reproducible hardware results rather than speculative estimates.
