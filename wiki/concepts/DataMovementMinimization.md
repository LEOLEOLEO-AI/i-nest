# DataMovementMinimization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design principle that reduces the physical transfer of data between memory, sensors, and processors to lower energy consumption and latency. This principle directly impacts TCC interconnect topology and routing, as minimizing data movement is key to scaling chiplet-based systems with high bandwidth and low power.
