# DirectSNNTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Training spiking neural networks end-to-end with spike-based forward passes and surrogate gradients, avoiding conversion from analog neural networks. Critical for deploying neuromorphic processors in-network where conversion overhead and latency are prohibitive, enabling real-time adaptive processing.
