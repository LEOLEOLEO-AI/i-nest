# SurrogateGradientTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training method for spiking neural networks that uses a differentiable surrogate function to approximate the non-differentiable spike operation during backpropagation. It enables training TTFS SNNs from scratch, overcoming the challenge of non-differentiability and making complex temporal learning feasible for iNEST applications.
