# WaferLevelMolding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A process where epoxy molding compound is applied to the entire wafer before dicing, encapsulating all dies simultaneously. Enables uniform protection and planarization of 3D-IC structures, critical for maintaining signal integrity in dense TCC interconnects.
