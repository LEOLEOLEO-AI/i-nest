# AdaptiveCombinerWeighting

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism where the contribution of each input pathway to a neuron's output is dynamically adjusted based on local error signals, rather than fixed or globally optimized. This enables flexible reconfiguration of information flow in both TCC topologies and iNEST networks, supporting dynamic task adaptation.
