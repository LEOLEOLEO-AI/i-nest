**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A training paradigm that finds the exact optimal parameter set for an SNN given a task, rather than converging to local minima via gradient descent. Overcomes the non-differentiability and local minima issues in SNNs, making them more reliable for real-time neuromorphic inference.
