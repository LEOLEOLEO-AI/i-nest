**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuron model that divides the soma and dendrites into functional compartments, each with distinct membrane dynamics and synaptic integration properties. Provides biologically plausible and computationally powerful building blocks for in-network neuromorphic processors, enabling richer temporal computations than point neurons.
