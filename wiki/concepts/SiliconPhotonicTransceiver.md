# SiliconPhotonicTransceiver

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A compact optical transceiver fabricated on a silicon substrate that converts electrical signals to optical signals and vice versa for chiplet-to-chiplet communication. This is essential for TCC because it enables dense, high-speed optical I/O integration directly on the chiplet, improving interconnect density and energy efficiency.
