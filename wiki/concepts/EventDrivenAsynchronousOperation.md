# EventDriven_Asynchronous_Operation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A no-global-clock design in which computation is triggered only by incoming spike events, avoiding continuous clock-driven switching. It is the key mechanism behind μBrain's ultra-low power consumption, enabling always-on but energy-efficient processing for IoT edge devices.
