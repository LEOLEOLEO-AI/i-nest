# LLMDrivenPathSelection

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The use of a pre-trained LLM to generate or refine routing paths by reasoning over network topology and traffic semantics, rather than relying solely on algorithmic search. Bridges natural language task descriptions to low-level network control, enabling adaptive and explainable routing in iNEST and TCC environments.
