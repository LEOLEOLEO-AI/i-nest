## Definition
Graph neural networks that incorporate symplectic structure from Hamiltonian mechanics to preserve energy and phase-space properties during temporal evolution of node states. Provides stable, long-horizon dynamics for neuromorphic computing, where energy conservation and reversible computation are key for efficient in-network processing.
