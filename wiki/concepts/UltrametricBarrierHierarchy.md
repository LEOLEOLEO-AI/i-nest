# UltrametricBarrierHierarchy

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A hierarchical organization of energy barriers on random Erdős–Rényi graphs where barrier heights form an ultrametric structure, meaning the distances between local minima satisfy the strong triangle inequality. This provides a topological origin for barrier hierarchy, enabling prediction of metastability and transition pathways in complex energy landscapes relevant to neuromorphic computing and network dynamics.
