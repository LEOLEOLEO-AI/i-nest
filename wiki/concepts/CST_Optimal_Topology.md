## Definition
The optimal network topology for a given communication primitive as derived from the Communication Structure Topology (CST) framework, minimizing steps and resource overhead. It provides a systematic mapping from communication primitives to the most efficient hardware topology, which is central to TCC's goal of co-designing computation and communication.
