# Dynamic_Structural_Hole_Reconfiguration

**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A graph-theoretic and reinforcement-learning algorithm that monitors node connection strength and traffic flows in software-defined interconnects to identify and fill structural holes by dynamically adjusting link weights and paths. It establishes the 'perception-decision-reconfiguration' closed loop needed for topology-driven superlinear computing gains in mesoscale wafer-scale systems.
