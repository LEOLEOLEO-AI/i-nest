# FullyAnalogReadoutPath

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
The signal chain from memristive crossbar currents through analog readout circuitry to spiking neuron output that avoids intermediate digital conversion. Maintaining a fully analog path reduces interface energy and latency, which is essential for scalable spiking neuromorphic accelerators in iNEST.
