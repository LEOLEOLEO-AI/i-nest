## Definition
A decoding method that uses prior probability distributions and likelihood models to compute the most probable error pattern given syndrome measurements. Offers near-optimal error correction for topological codes, applicable to both TCC's fault-tolerant interconnects and iNEST's probabilistic neural inference.
