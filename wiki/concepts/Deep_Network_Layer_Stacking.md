## Definition
The architectural practice of stacking many processing layers (e.g., 80 layers) where each layer processes and forwards both raw and processed data upward. Illustrates the extreme depth and data redundancy that TCC/iNEST designs must handle, motivating topology-aware optimizations for in-network computation.
