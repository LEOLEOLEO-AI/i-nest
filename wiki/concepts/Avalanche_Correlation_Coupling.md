## Definition
The relationship by which neuronal avalanche size, duration, and branching statistics shape and are shaped by the flow of correlations across the network. It links measurable in-network spiking statistics to learning efficacy, enabling iNEST designs to use avalanche observables as feedback for on-chip adaptation.
