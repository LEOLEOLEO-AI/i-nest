## Definition
Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity, a biologically inspired learning rule where synaptic weights are updated based on the relative timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes. Enables on-chip, event-driven learning in neuromorphic hardware, aligning with iNEST's goal of low-power, adaptive in-network intelligence.
