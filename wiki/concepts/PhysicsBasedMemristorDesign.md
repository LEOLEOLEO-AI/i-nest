# PhysicsBasedMemristorDesign

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A methodology for engineering resistive switching devices using physical models of ion migration and filament formation to predict and control multi-level conductance behavior. This provides a deterministic path to reliable, scalable synaptic devices, overcoming variability issues that plague empirical memristor fabrication.
