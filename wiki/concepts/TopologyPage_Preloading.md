# TopologyPage_Preloading

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

## Definition
A mechanism that preloads candidate topology pages (precomputed network configurations) into switch nodes before task execution, allowing rapid phase transitions and dynamic reconfiguration. Preloading reduces reconfiguration latency during runtime, which is critical for time-sensitive TCC workloads and seamless stage switching.
