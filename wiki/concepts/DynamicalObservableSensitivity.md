# DynamicalObservableSensitivity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A measure of how much a given higher-order interaction influences a chosen dynamical observable (e.g., synchronization error or activity variance), used to rank interactions for reduction. Guides selective removal of interactions in in-network neuromorphic systems, ensuring that only non-essential couplings are eliminated, preserving critical information processing pathways.
