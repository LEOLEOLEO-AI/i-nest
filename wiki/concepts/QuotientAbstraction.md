## Definition
A formal method that reduces the state space of a probabilistic spiking neural network by grouping equivalent neuron states into abstract representatives, enabling tractable verification. This enables scalable verification of large-scale neuromorphic systems, which is critical for ensuring correctness in iNEST deployments where exhaustive state exploration is infeasible.
