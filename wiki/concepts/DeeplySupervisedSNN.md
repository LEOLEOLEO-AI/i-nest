## Definition
An SNN architecture where auxiliary supervision signals are injected at multiple hidden layers, mitigating vanishing gradients and improving convergence in deep spiking networks. Enables training of deeper SNNs for complex pattern recognition, which is essential for robust traffic sign detection in resource-constrained network edge devices.
