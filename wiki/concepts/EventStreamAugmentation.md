# EventStreamAugmentation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A data augmentation strategy that generates diverse event streams by simulating temporal jitter, dropout, and noise to improve spiking tracker robustness. Addresses the scarcity of event-based tracking datasets, enhancing generalization of SNN models in real-world TCC/iNEST deployments.
