# Audio_to_Spikes_Encoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A front-end transduction scheme that converts continuous acoustic waveforms directly into sparse asynchronous spike trains suitable for spiking neural network consumption. It defines the sensory interface layer of iNEST systems, determining the temporal precision and sparsity that downstream in-network neuromorphic processors must handle.
