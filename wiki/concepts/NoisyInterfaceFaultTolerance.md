# NoisyInterfaceFaultTolerance

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A set of techniques for performing logical quantum operations and state preparation that remain correct despite errors introduced at the interfaces between modules. Enables reliable computation in modular quantum systems where interconnects are a primary error source, a core challenge for TCC-based quantum networks.
