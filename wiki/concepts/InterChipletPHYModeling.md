# InterChipletPHYModeling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A detailed physical-layer (PHY) modeling approach for inter-chiplet links that captures end-to-end signal integrity, timing, and power characteristics across the entire chiplet-to-chiplet interface. Enables accurate simulation of chiplet-based systems, which is critical for validating TCC architectures that rely on high-bandwidth, low-latency inter-chiplet communication.
