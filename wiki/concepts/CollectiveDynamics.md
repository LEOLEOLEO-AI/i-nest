**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The emergent, coordinated spatiotemporal activity patterns in spiking neural networks arising from the interplay of individual neuron dynamics and network topology. Understanding collective dynamics is essential for designing neuromorphic systems that can robustly perform computations through population-level coding rather than single-neuron precision.
