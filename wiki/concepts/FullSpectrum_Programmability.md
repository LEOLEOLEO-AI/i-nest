# FullSpectrum_Programmability

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The capability of a photonic memristor to respond and be programmed across an ultrawide bandwidth from ultraviolet to near-infrared (375–1064 nm). This allows wavelength-multiplexed optical computation and flexible integration with diverse photonic signal sources in iNEST architectures.
