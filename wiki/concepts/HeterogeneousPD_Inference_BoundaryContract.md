## Definition
A formalized boundary contract governing the interaction between heterogeneous prefill and decode phases in inference, transitioning from KV cache management to a boundary-object abstraction. Enables systematic decoupling and optimization of P/D phases in distributed neuromorphic inference, critical for scaling iNEST systems.
