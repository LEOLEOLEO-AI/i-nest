**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The Drosophila FC2 (fan-shaped body) neural circuit encodes a single behavioral goal through normalization of competing inputs rather than winner-take-all selection. This provides a neuromorphic principle for goal-directed decision-making in compact neural substrates, relevant to designing energy-efficient in-network controllers.
