# MemristiveSignedCouplings

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A coupling mechanism in oscillatory neural networks where memristive devices implement both positive and negative synaptic weights, enabling programmable sign-dependent interactions between oscillators. Enables hardware-realizable, adaptive synaptic plasticity in neuromorphic oscillatory systems, critical for scalable in-network learning.
