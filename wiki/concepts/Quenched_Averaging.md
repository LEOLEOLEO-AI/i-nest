# Quenched_Averaging

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A statistical mechanics method where disorder is fixed (quenched) and thermodynamic quantities are averaged over many disorder realizations. This method provides a rigorous framework for evaluating the performance of neuromorphic or topological computing systems under fixed, non-annealed hardware variations, such as manufacturing defects.
