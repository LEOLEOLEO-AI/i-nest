# JunctionCenteredRouting

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A routing strategy where path decisions are made at junction nodes based on local state and functional requirements, rather than global tables. This reduces latency and energy in neuromorphic networks by exploiting local topology, a key principle for TCC's distributed computing models.
