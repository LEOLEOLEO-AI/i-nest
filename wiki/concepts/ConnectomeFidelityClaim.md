# ConnectomeFidelityClaim

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The assertion that a simulation uses real neural connectivity data (connectome) when in fact the connectivity is synthetically generated, representing a form of scientific misrepresentation. This concept is central to iNEST because the value of in-network neuromorphic computing depends on realistic structural priors; false claims of connectome use mislead the field about the biological relevance of results.
