**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The fusion-tractability tradeoff is the balance between the fidelity of a fused Bayesian network and its computational cost after edge pruning. It guides TCC and iNEST designers in choosing fusion pipelines that fit strict memory, bandwidth, and latency budgets.
