## Definition
A mechanism where individual nodes or subnetworks persist only if they actively serve a system-level function, with non-serving elements being pruned or repurposed. Enables energy-efficient, self-sustaining neuromorphic networks that dynamically allocate resources to functionally relevant pathways, reducing waste in in-network processing.
