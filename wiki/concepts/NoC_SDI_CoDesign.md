# NoC_SDI_CoDesign

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The joint design of Network-on-Chip (NoC) routing and Serial Die-to-Die (SDI) physical links to optimize data movement across chiplet boundaries. Enables TCC to treat the entire package as a single logical network, reducing protocol overhead and improving end-to-end performance for topology-centric workloads.
