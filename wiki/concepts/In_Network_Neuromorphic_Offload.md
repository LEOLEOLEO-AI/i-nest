# In_Network_Neuromorphic_Offload

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
The offloading of neuromorphic spiking neural network workloads directly onto network-attached NPU chiplets, bypassing traditional host processing for real-time sensory data. This reduces latency and energy overhead, aligning with iNEST's goal of embedding neuromorphic computation directly into network paths.
