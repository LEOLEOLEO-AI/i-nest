# HeterogeneousSiNIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The co-integration of SiN waveguides with other material systems (e.g., active gain or detector layers) on a single substrate via transfer printing, rather than monolithic growth. Overcomes material lattice mismatch and thermal budget constraints, enabling high-performance active/passive photonic circuits for TCC network-on-chip architectures.
