# Indirect_Reciprocity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A cooperation mechanism where agents decide to help others based on their reputation rather than on direct past interactions with those specific others. Enables scalable cooperation in large-scale TCC/iNEST networks where direct reciprocity among all node pairs is impractical.
