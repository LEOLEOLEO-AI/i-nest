# PottsModelDisorder

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A generalization of the Ising model with q-state spins, here studied with quenched random couplings to model the effects of disorder on topological phase transitions. Captures the interplay between disorder and topological order, essential for predicting the stability of quantum states in noisy TCC hardware.
