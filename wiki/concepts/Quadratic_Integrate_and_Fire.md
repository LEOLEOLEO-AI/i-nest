## Definition
A spiking neuron model where the membrane potential dynamics follow a quadratic differential equation, leading to a bifurcation that generates action potentials. This model captures more realistic neuronal dynamics with fewer parameters, making it suitable for efficient hardware implementations in neuromorphic computing.
