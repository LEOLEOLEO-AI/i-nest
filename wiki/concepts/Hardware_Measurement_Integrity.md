# Hardware_Measurement_Integrity

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The principle that proposals, simulation results, or model-derived estimates must not be presented as actual hardware measurements. Prevents misleading conclusions in TCC architectures where physical implementation constraints are critical.
