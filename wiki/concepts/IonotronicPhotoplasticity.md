# IonotronicPhotoplasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where light stimuli cause persistent changes in ionic conductivity or charge distribution, leading to short-term or long-term plasticity in iontronic devices. Provides a mechanism for implementing spike-timing-dependent plasticity and learning rules in neuromorphic vision systems without external electronic programming.
