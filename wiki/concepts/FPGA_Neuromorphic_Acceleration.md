## Definition
The use of Field-Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs) as reconfigurable hardware platforms to implement spiking neural network (SNN) architectures, offering flexibility and parallelism for neuromorphic computing. Enables rapid prototyping and customization of neuromorphic designs, bridging the gap between software simulation and ASIC-based neuromorphic chips for iNEST research.
