# Time_Based_Readout

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A readout scheme that encodes the result of analog computation as the timing of an output spike or pulse rather than as a voltage or current amplitude. Enables low-overhead, energy-efficient interfacing between analog memristive SNN cores and downstream spiking logic without power-hungry ADCs.
