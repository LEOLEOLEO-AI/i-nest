# PseudoSpikeBridge

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A distillation mechanism that converts ANN activations into pseudo-spike trains to bridge the heterogeneous representation gap between ANN and SNN clients in federated learning. Enables heterogeneous ANN-SNN federated learning without requiring direct spike transmission, preserving temporal coding advantages of SNNs.
