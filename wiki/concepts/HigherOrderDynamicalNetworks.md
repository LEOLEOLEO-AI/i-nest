# HigherOrderDynamicalNetworks

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A dynamical network model where interactions occur among groups of nodes (simplices) rather than only pairwise edges, capturing multi-body coupling in complex systems. Enables representation of multi-agent or multi-synaptic interactions in TCC/iNEST architectures, which are essential for realistic neuromorphic and topological computing beyond pairwise models.
