# YieldOptimizedPhysicalDesign

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A physical design flow that incorporates manufacturing yield constraints (e.g., lithography, etch variations) into the placement and routing of photonic and electronic components. Ensures manufacturability of complex photonic interconnects at scale, reducing cost and improving reliability for TCC systems with dense optical networks.
