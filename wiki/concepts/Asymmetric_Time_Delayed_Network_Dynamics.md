## Definition
The combined study of network behavior where both asymmetric interactions and time delays coexist, leading to non-equilibrium dynamics and potentially oscillatory or chaotic regimes. Captures realistic conditions in in-network neuromorphic systems where synaptic asymmetry and propagation delays are unavoidable, enabling design of more faithful and capable neural computing substrates.
