## Definition
The process of designing network-on-chip interconnects that exhibit small-world properties (short average path length and high clustering) to minimize communication latency and energy. Directly aligns with TCC's goal of optimizing topology-centric computing architectures, and this work provides a quantum-assisted method to explore such topologies more efficiently than classical heuristics.
