# Computation_Access_Gating

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism that selectively enables or disables access to specific computational modules or memory regions within a topology based on learned relevance signals. Enables fine-grained control over which parts of a distributed topology are active, reducing energy consumption and latency in iNEST-style neuromorphic systems.
