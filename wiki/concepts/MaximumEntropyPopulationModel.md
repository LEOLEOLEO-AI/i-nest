## Definition
A statistical model that fits neuronal population spike patterns by maximizing entropy subject to constraints on measured pairwise correlations, producing the least-biased distribution consistent with data. Provides a principled baseline for characterizing collective dynamics in neuromorphic circuits, enabling comparison of engineered networks to biological criticality.
