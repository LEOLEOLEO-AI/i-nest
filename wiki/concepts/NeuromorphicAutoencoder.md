# NeuromorphicAutoencoder

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An autoencoder architecture implemented in neuromorphic hardware that learns compressed representations of high-order Ising problem instances, facilitating efficient mapping onto physical spin networks. Bridges the gap between problem formulation and hardware constraints, enabling scalable deployment of Ising machines in network-edge and in-network computing environments.
