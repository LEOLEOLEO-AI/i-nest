## Definition
A recurrent neural network framework where a fixed, randomly connected reservoir projects inputs into a high-dimensional state space, and only a readout layer is trained. Its training simplicity and temporal processing capability make it ideal for lightweight, in-network prediction tasks without complex backpropagation in distributed TCC environments.
