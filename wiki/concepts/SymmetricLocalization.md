# SymmetricLocalization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method for confining fractional topological insulator edge states to symmetric spatial regions, preserving the topological order and fractional statistics under confinement. Enables controlled manipulation of fractional edge modes, which is critical for fault-tolerant topological quantum computation and routing in TCC architectures.
