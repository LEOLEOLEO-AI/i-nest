# Event_Driven_Computation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A computation paradigm where operations are triggered only by the occurrence of spikes or events, rather than on a fixed clock cycle, leading to sparse and asynchronous processing. Reduces power consumption and latency in in-network neuromorphic accelerators by avoiding unnecessary computations during idle periods.
