# Heterogeneous_Magnetic_Metamaterials

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Magnetic metamaterials with spatially varying properties (e.g., different magnetic anisotropies or damping) used as physical substrates for reservoir computing. Provides a reconfigurable, high-dimensional nonlinear mapping medium for TCC/iNEST, enabling energy-efficient in-network computation without traditional digital logic.
