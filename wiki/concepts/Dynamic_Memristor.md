# Dynamic_Memristor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A memristor device whose resistance state evolves through internal ionic dynamics and history-dependent conductance modulation, enabling faithful emulation of synaptic and neural temporal behaviors. Dynamic memristors provide the physical substrate for in-network neuromorphic computation by allowing stateful, time-varying synaptic responses to coexist with on-wafer signal propagation.
