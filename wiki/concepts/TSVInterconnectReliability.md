# TSVInterconnectReliability

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The mechanical and electrical reliability of through-silicon vias (TSVs) under encapsulation-induced stress and thermal expansion mismatches. TSVs are critical vertical links in 3D-IC topologies; encapsulation-induced stress can cause delamination or cracking, directly impacting TCC's signal integrity and fault tolerance.
