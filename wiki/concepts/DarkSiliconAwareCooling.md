# DarkSiliconAwareCooling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A design methodology that integrates thermal management and power gating at the hardware level to mitigate the dark silicon effect, where portions of a manycore chip must remain idle to stay within thermal limits. Directly addresses the core TCC challenge of optimizing energy and thermal budgets in densely packed manycore systems, enabling more active cores per watt.
