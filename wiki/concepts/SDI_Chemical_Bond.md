**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A software-defined interconnect design principle where inter-node links are formed as 'chemical bonds' through SDI, enabling composable and reversible connections between topology elements. It provides the operational mechanism for realizing meta-topologies, making liquid-like reconfigurable computing topologies physically and logically feasible.
