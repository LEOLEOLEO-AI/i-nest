# Normalization_Over_Selection

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A computational strategy where multiple goal-related signals are combined via divisive normalization to produce a unified output, instead of selecting a single dominant signal. This offers a robust, biologically-inspired alternative to hard selection mechanisms, potentially improving stability and adaptability in neuromorphic hardware.
