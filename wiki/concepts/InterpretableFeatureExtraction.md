## Definition
The process of deriving physiologically meaningful EEG features (e.g., band-limited power, phase-amplitude coupling) that map directly to known neural circuit mechanisms. Ensures that neuromorphic implementations are grounded in biologically validated parameters, improving the fidelity and clinical relevance of in-network simulations.
