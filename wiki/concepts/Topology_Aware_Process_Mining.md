# Topology_Aware_Process_Mining

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A process-mining variant that treats the physical and logical network topology of IoT deployments as a first-class explanatory variable when discovering, conforming, and predicting process models. Lets TCC researchers attribute emergent or anomalous process behavior to structural network properties rather than only to task-level control flow.
