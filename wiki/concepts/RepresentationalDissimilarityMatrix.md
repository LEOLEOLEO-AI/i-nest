**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A matrix capturing the pairwise dissimilarity of neural responses to stimuli, used as a common metric to compare representational geometry across modalities and species. Provides a modality-agnostic and species-agnostic framework for validating neuromorphic models against biological data.
