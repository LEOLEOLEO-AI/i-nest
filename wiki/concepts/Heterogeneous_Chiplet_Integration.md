## Definition
A system-level integration method that combines separately manufactured chiplets of different process nodes (logic, memory, FPGA, memristive) into a single package/wafer.</definition> Allows domain-specific modular scaling and heterogeneous resource composition, which is critical for flexible topology-centric microsystems.
