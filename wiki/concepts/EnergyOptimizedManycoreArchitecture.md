# EnergyOptimizedManycoreArchitecture

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design approach for manycore chips that integrates low-power modes, dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS), and power gating at the core or cluster level to minimize energy consumption under varying workload demands. This is fundamental to TCC because it provides the physical substrate on which topology-centric algorithms run, and energy optimization at this level directly impacts the efficiency of data-centric computations and the feasibility of large-scale in-network processing.
