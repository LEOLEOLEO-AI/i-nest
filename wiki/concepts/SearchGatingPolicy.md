# SearchGatingPolicy

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A policy that dynamically selects and applies quantization schemes to spiking vision transformers based on layer-specific sensitivity and input-dependent search, rather than using a fixed quantization configuration. This enables adaptive compression of spiking neural networks, which is critical for deploying energy-efficient neuromorphic vision systems on resource-constrained hardware.
