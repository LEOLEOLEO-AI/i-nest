# DualMemoryPathwaySNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture with two distinct memory pathways that preserve both short-term and long-term temporal context, overcoming the rapid forgetting of feedforward membrane potentials. It enables neuromorphic systems to retain hundreds of time steps of context for sparse asynchronous edge perception, a key requirement for iNEST vehicle applications.
