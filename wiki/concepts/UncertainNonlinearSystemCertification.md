## Definition
The process of formally proving whether a nonlinear system with uncertain parameters can undergo a bifurcation, using interval arithmetic to enclose all possible trajectories. Supports the design of neuromorphic hardware that must guarantee stable operation across manufacturing variations and environmental perturbations.
