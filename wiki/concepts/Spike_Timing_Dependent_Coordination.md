# Spike_Timing_Dependent_Coordination

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning rule that adjusts synaptic delays and weights based on the relative timing of spikes between CPG neurons and motor neurons to refine coordination patterns. This enables online adaptation of gait patterns in neuromorphic hardware, supporting robust locomotion in unstructured environments.
