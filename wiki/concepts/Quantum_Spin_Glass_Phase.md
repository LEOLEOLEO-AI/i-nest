## Definition
A low-temperature phase characterized by frozen, disordered spin configurations with no long-range order, arising from competing interactions and quenched disorder. Its emergence in 2D systems challenges conventional topological order, informing the design of memory and logic elements in TCC architectures that rely on stable magnetic states.
