# Through_Silicon_Via

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A vertical electrical connection passing through a silicon die or wafer, used to route signals and power directly between stacked layers in a 3D integrated system. TSVs provide low-latency, high-bandwidth vertical links that are essential for wafer-scale TCC architectures to overcome planar interconnect bottlenecks.
