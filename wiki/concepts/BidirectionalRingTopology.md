# BidirectionalRingTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A multi-FPGA interconnection scheme where each node connects to two neighbors in a closed loop, allowing data to flow in both clockwise and counterclockwise directions simultaneously. Enables scalable SNN deployment across multiple FPGAs with reduced communication latency and improved fault tolerance compared to unidirectional rings or mesh topologies.
