## Definition
A hardware design paradigm where data flows continuously through a pipeline of processing elements without centralized control, matching the event-driven nature of spiking neural networks. Aligns computation and communication in SNNs, minimizing memory access overhead and enabling high-throughput, low-latency inference on FPGA clusters.
