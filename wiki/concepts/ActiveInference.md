## Definition
A corollary of the free energy principle where agents select actions that are expected to minimize future free energy, thereby actively sampling the environment to confirm their predictions. Enables TCC/iNEST nodes to autonomously decide which data to forward or process, reducing uncertainty and improving network efficiency in real-time.
