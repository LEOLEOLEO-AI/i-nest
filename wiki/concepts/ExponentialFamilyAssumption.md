# ExponentialFamilyAssumption

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The assumption that probability distributions in the predictive coding model belong to the exponential family, simplifying variational free-energy calculations and enabling tractable updates. This assumption allows for closed-form update rules in neuromorphic hardware, reducing computational overhead and facilitating real-time, low-power inference in distributed topologies.
