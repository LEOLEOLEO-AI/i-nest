# ChipletPHYAccuracy

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The degree to which PHY-level parameters (e.g., crosstalk, jitter, voltage droop) are represented in chiplet simulation models to match real silicon behavior. High accuracy reduces design margin overestimation, enabling denser and faster inter-chiplet networks in TCC systems.
