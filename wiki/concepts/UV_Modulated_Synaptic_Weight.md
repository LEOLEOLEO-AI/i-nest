# UV_Modulated_Synaptic_Weight

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The use of 254 nm ultraviolet light intensity to tune the high-resistance states of the Ga2O3 memristor, thereby modulating synaptic weights optically. This optical tuning mechanism demonstrates non-volatile photonic control of synaptic plasticity, advancing optoelectronic neuromorphic systems.
