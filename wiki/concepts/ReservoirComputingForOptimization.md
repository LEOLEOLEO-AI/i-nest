# ReservoirComputingForOptimization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The application of reservoir computing, a recurrent neural network with fixed random weights and only output-layer training, to approximate or solve combinatorial optimization problems by mapping problem states to reservoir dynamics. It provides a neuromorphic-friendly, low-training-cost approach to optimization that can be implemented in hardware, aligning with in-network neuromorphic computing goals.
