# HeterogeneousIntegration

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The co-fabrication or monolithic stacking of diverse material systems (e.g., ferroelectric, memristive, and CMOS) into a single neuromorphic chip to exploit complementary device functionalities. This is a key enabler for TCC/iNEST because it allows the construction of multi-functional crossbar arrays and spiking circuits that cannot be achieved with homogeneous silicon technology.
