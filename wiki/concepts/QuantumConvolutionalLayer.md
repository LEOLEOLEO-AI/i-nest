# QuantumConvolutionalLayer

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A layer in a QCNN that applies a translationally invariant sequence of two-qubit gates across a qubit lattice, mimicking classical convolutional filters but in the quantum state space. Understanding the limits of such layers informs TCC/iNEST research on whether hierarchical, locality-preserving topologies in network fabrics can yield emergent intelligence beyond classical feature extraction.
