# Fidelity_Readout

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A measurement technique that extracts the output of a quantum reservoir by estimating the overlap (fidelity) between the evolved quantum state and a set of reference states. It provides a robust and efficient way to map quantum dynamics to classical outputs, essential for integrating quantum reservoirs into TCC/iNEST pipelines.
