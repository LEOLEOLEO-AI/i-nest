# CommunicationAsComputation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design principle where data movement operations (e.g., forwarding, merging, splitting) are intentionally overloaded to perform arithmetic or logical functions during transit, eliminating separate compute steps. This principle is central to TCC because it reduces latency and energy by fusing communication and computation, a key advantage for wafer-scale and in-network neuromorphic systems.
