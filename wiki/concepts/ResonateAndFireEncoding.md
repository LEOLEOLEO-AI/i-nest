# ResonateAndFireEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An encoding scheme where input signals are converted into spike trains via resonating neurons that fire when their intrinsic frequency matches the input's local spectral content. This provides a sparse, time-local representation of spectral features, reducing data bandwidth and enabling efficient downstream neural processing in iNEST pipelines.
