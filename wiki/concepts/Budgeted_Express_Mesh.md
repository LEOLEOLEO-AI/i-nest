# Budgeted_Express_Mesh

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A mesh network topology augmented with a limited number of long-range 'express' links whose count or cost is constrained by a fixed placement budget. It provides a cost-aware topology design primitive for TCC, letting architects trade link budget against diameter, latency, and throughput in on-chip and chiplet networks.
