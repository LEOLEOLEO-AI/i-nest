# On_Chip_Local_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The property that all weight-update computations of a learning rule are performed by circuits co-located with the synapses, avoiding weight movement between memory and compute units. It eliminates the memory-bandwidth bottleneck of conventional accelerators and is essential for scalable, energy-efficient in-network neuromorphic learning.
