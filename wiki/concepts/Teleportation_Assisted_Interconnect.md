# Teleportation_Assisted_Interconnect

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An interconnect architecture that uses entanglement teleportation as a primitive for transferring quantum states or classical information between neuromorphic cores, rather than relying solely on classical packet switching. This could enable ultra-low-power, high-throughput communication in iNEST systems, where traditional electrical interconnects become a bottleneck for large-scale spiking neural networks.
