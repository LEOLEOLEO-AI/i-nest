# PhysicalWaferTriplet

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A formal representation of the physical wafer substrate as a triplet Gphys=(V,E,Φ), where V is the node set, E the edge set with per-edge bandwidth W, and Φ the total bandwidth budget, with |E|=Θ(N^(3/2)) for a 3D torus. Provides a quantitative foundation for reasoning about physical topology constraints and bandwidth limits in TCC wafer-scale designs.
