# PVA_Passivation_Mechanism

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
The use of polyvinyl alcohol (PVA) to form hydrogen bonds with perovskite surfaces, suppressing random ionic migration and enhancing thermal stability in synaptic devices. This passivation strategy provides a chemical route to overcome the nonlinearity and instability of perovskite synapses, making them more suitable for embedded neuromorphic computing.
