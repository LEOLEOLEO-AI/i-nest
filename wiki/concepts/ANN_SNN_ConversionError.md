# ANN_SNN_ConversionError

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The discrepancy between the outputs of an original analog neural network and its converted spiking neural network due to discrete spike timing and membrane potential dynamics. Minimizing this error is critical for practical neuromorphic systems, as it determines whether pre-trained ANN models can be efficiently ported to iNEST spiking accelerators.
