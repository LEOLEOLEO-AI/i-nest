## Definition
The use of non-Hermitian lattice symmetries and gain/loss profiles to actively control wavefunction amplitude and phase distributions beyond conventional Hermitian constraints. Enables robust, topology-inspired wavefunction engineering that could underpin fault-tolerant information transport in topological photonic circuits.
