## Definition
A transistor that uses an electrolyte as the gate dielectric, enabling ionic modulation of the semiconductor channel for synaptic-like analog switching. Provides a low-voltage, flexible neuromorphic device platform that can be integrated into in-network sensing nodes for energy-efficient local processing.
