**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuromorphic network architecture that integrates two distinct memory mechanisms, such as synaptic and intrinsic plasticity, to support complementary temporal processing functions. This provides a structured way to separate short-term and long-term information processing in spiking neural networks, guiding hardware-software co-design for iNEST systems.
