# Temporal_Dynamics_Integration

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The inclusion of time-dependent neural processes such as Hodgkin-Huxley spiking, STDP plasticity, and time-series outputs in network simulations. Without temporal dynamics, iNEST models cannot capture learning, memory, or real-time neural behavior, making them biologically irrelevant.
