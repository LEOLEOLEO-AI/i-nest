# NoCPlacementAndRoutingCoOptimization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A joint optimization framework that simultaneously determines the physical placement of NoC routers and the routing paths for packets, rather than treating them as separate steps. Enables holistic design space exploration in TCC, leading to better resource utilization and performance than sequential approaches, especially in heterogeneous FPGA fabrics.
