# WholeOrganismToxicityScreening

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method using entire C. elegans organisms to assess toxicological effects, capturing systemic biological responses rather than isolated cellular assays. Provides a biologically relevant readout that can be mapped to neural and physiological network states, enabling in-vivo validation of neuromorphic models.
