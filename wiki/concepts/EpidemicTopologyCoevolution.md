# EpidemicTopologyCoevolution

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A dynamic process where epidemic parameters (fatality and waning immunity) simultaneously reshape the network topology, and the altered topology in turn feeds back to modify epidemic spread. Provides a framework for designing adaptive interconnect topologies that respond to load or failure conditions, analogous to biological immune-feedback mechanisms.
