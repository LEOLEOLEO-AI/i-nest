# CrossLinkInterferenceMitigation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A technique to suppress interference from non-target links (e.g., other users or RIS reflections) during localization, often via beam-domain filtering or ML-based interference rejection. Addresses a key bottleneck in dense mmWave networks where cross-link interference degrades positioning accuracy, directly impacting reliable topology mapping in TCC systems.
