# GradientAsymmetry

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The deliberate use of different gradient scaling for positive versus negative errors in surrogate gradient training, rather than a symmetric approximation. Improves convergence and reduces training instability in deep SNNs, which is essential for reliable learning in resource-constrained iNEST environments.
