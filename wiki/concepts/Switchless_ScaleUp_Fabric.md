# Switchless_ScaleUp_Fabric

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-10

## Definition
A network design where compute nodes are linked directly via high-density optical or electrical ports without top-of-rack or core switches, forming a mesh or torus-like structure. It shifts performance burden from switch hardware to topology planning and routing, making it a core subject for TCC research on scalable interconnects.
