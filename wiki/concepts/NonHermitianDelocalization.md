## Definition
A phenomenon in non-Hermitian systems where eigenstates become delocalized due to non-reciprocal hopping or gain/loss, contrasting with Anderson localization in Hermitian systems. Provides a mechanism for robust signal propagation and state delocalization in neuromorphic or topological networks, potentially enabling fault-tolerant information flow in TCC/iNEST architectures.
