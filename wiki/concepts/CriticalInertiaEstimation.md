# CriticalInertiaEstimation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A method to determine the minimum system inertia required to maintain frequency stability in an interconnected power grid under specified disturbance conditions. Enables TCC architectures to provision dynamic resource allocation for grid stability, ensuring that in-network computation can adapt to varying inertia levels across interconnections.
