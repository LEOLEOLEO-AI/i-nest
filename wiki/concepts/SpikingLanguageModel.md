## Definition
A language model implemented using spiking neural networks, where information is encoded in binary spikes over time rather than continuous activations. This is foundational for iNEST as it bridges natural language processing with event-driven neuromorphic computing, allowing for energy-efficient and temporally aware language understanding in network edge devices.
