**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
Aerospace vehicles capable of repeated high-speed atmospheric flight, requiring rapid reconfiguration of onboard computing and communication systems between missions. Demands adaptive, low-latency in-network computation (iNEST) and topology reconfiguration (TCC) to handle extreme thermal and dynamic operational envelopes.
