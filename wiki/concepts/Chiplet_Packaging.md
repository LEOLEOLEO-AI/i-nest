## Definition
Heterogeneous integration of multiple dies, such as compute, memory, and I/O chiplets, within a single advanced package using die-to-die interconnects. It enables TCC to explore package-level topologies and partition near-memory or in-memory compute resources across heterogeneous chiplets.
