# Chiplet_Packaging

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of assembling multiple smaller silicon dies into a single package to create a larger, more complex chip, improving manufacturing yield and modularity. Enables advanced switch chips with higher port counts and bandwidth, directly impacting the feasibility of TCC's dense topologies and iNEST's embedded processing.
