# EquilibriumManifoldProjection

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The mathematical projection of observed node states onto the manifold of possible relative equilibria, used to separate intrinsic dynamics from coupling-induced effects. Allows neuromorphic circuits to self-calibrate coupling strengths without external supervision, supporting in-network learning and adaptation.
