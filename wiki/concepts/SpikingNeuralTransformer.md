## Definition
A transformer architecture that replaces traditional artificial neural network activations with spiking neurons, enabling event-driven, energy-efficient sequence processing for handwritten text recognition. Bridges the gap between transformer-based sequence modeling and neuromorphic hardware, enabling low-power, real-time text recognition on edge devices.
