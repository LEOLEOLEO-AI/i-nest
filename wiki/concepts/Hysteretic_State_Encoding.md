## Definition
A method of encoding binary or multi-valued information in the persistent on/off states of hysterons distributed across a network, rather than in discrete memory cells. Bridges TCC and iNEST by showing how physical state variables (pressure, flow) can represent computational states, relevant for neuromorphic reservoir computing in flow networks.
