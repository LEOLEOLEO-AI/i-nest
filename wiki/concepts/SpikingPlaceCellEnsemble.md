# SpikingPlaceCellEnsemble

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A population of spiking neurons that collectively encode a specific place or location through their spatiotemporal firing patterns, analogous to hippocampal place cells. Offers a biologically inspired mechanism for spatial memory in neuromorphic systems, which can be mapped to network topologies for distributed place recognition in TCC architectures.
