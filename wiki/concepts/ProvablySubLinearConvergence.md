# ProvablySubLinearConvergence

**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A theoretical guarantee that the optimization error decreases at a rate faster than linear in the number of iterations, even with two interacting timescales. Provides formal performance bounds for TCC/iNEST systems, ensuring that online plasticity does not lead to instability or unbounded regret.
