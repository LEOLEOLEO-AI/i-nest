**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A scalar value assigned to each agent, updated based on its action history, influencing future reward shaping and partner selection in multi-agent systems. Provides a lightweight, localizable trust metric that can be implemented in network routers or neuromorphic cores to guide resource allocation and collaboration.
