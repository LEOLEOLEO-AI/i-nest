# PostTrainingQuantizationSNN

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A quantization pipeline for spiking neural networks that does not require retraining, instead calibrating quantized parameters directly from a pre-trained model. Reduces the computational cost of adapting large SNNs to resource-constrained in-network neuromorphic accelerators.
