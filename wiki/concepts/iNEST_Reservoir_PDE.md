## Definition
A reservoir computing paradigm in iNEST that projects PDE states into high-dimensional spiking reservoir dynamics to approximate solution operators without explicit discretization. It provides a model-free, topology-aware approach to PDE solving, leveraging the recurrent connectivity of neuromorphic networks for complex spatiotemporal tasks.
