# ConnectomeWiringSeparability

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A methodological framework for decomposing the influence of specific synaptic connections from the influence of global network statistics on observed dynamics. Enables principled abstraction in neuromorphic systems, allowing engineers to discard wiring details that do not affect function while retaining those that do.
