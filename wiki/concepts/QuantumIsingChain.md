## Definition
A one-dimensional lattice of spin-1/2 particles with nearest-neighbor interactions and a transverse magnetic field, serving as a canonical model for quantum criticality. Acts as a testbed for understanding complex quantum dynamics that can be mapped to network topologies, informing the design of TCC and iNEST hardware.
