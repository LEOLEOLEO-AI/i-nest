**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A design principle where collective communication operations are mapped and optimized based on the physical network topology instead of logical rank order or flat heuristics. This is the core TCC concept, reducing congestion and latency by aligning communication flows with the actual data-center or HPC interconnect structure.
