# EventDrivenPlasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning rule where the arrival of sensor events modifies the interconnection strength of polymer dendrites, mimicking synaptic plasticity. This provides a neuromorphic mechanism for in-network adaptation without centralized processing, key for iNEST architectures.
