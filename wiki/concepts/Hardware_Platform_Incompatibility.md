# Hardware_Platform_Incompatibility

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
The condition where ANN-optimized and SNN-optimized hardware architectures cannot be directly reused or interoperated, due to contradictory data representation and compute style requirements. Overcoming this incompatibility motivates iNEST-oriented in-network and topology-level solutions that transparently bridge multiple neural computation paradigms.
