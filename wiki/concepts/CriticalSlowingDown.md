# CriticalSlowingDown

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where relaxation times diverge as a system approaches a critical point, making traditional Monte Carlo sampling inefficient. Motivates the need for accelerated sampling methods, which are crucial for simulating large-scale spin-glass networks that mimic TCC/iNEST architectures.
