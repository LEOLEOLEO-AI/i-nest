# QuantumChaos

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The manifestation of classical chaotic dynamics in a quantum system, characterized by sensitivity to initial conditions and spectral statistics, here emerging in a dissipative driven system. Enables complex, unpredictable dynamics that can be harnessed for random number generation or as a rich nonlinearity source in neuromorphic computing.
