# StateSpaceConnectivityShift

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-26

## Definition
A quantitative measure of how the adjacency or proximity relationships between points in the reconstructed state space change during a dynamical transition. Enables the use of graph-theoretic invariants to track regime changes, offering a new tool for reservoir computing and in-network processing where state-space geometry matters.
