## Definition
The staged replacement of classical public-key primitives in RAN control, management, and fronthaul interfaces with lattice- and code-based algorithms resistant to quantum adversaries. Migration changes packet sizes, handshake latency, and per-node compute cost, directly affecting topology-centric scheduling and neuromorphic in-network offload feasibility.
