## Definition
The physical and logical interconnection architecture on a wafer-scale integrated circuit, where the topology itself becomes the substrate for both routing and transformation operations. This concept is central to TCC because it leverages the massive parallelism and low-latency of wafer-scale integration to realize Route≡Transform at scale, making communication-as-computation practical.
