# NoisyInterface

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The physical or logical communication channel between quantum modules that introduces errors during state transfer or entanglement distribution, distinct from intra-module noise. Noisy interfaces are the primary bottleneck in modular quantum systems, driving the need for topology-specific error mitigation and logical operation design.
