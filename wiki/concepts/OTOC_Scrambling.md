# OTOC_Scrambling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The out-of-time-order correlator, a four-point correlator whose decay measures how rapidly initially local quantum information is scrambled into highly non-local, inaccessible degrees of freedom. OTOC decay quantifies the loss of locally addressable information, guiding TCC resilience metrics against information degradation and iNEST assessments of how faithfully local signals survive distributed processing.
