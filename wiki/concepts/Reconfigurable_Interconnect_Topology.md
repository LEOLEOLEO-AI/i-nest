# Reconfigurable_Interconnect_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A runtime-switchable routing fabric whose physical adjacency and switch configuration determine PE-to-PE communication cost and mapping legality. Interconnect topology is the central resource that TCC optimizes, since CGRA-style mapping quality is bounded by the achievable communication graph rather than by compute units.
