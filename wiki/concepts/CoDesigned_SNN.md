# CoDesigned_SNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture co-designed with the specific hardware constraints and capabilities of Intel Loihi 2, optimizing both network topology and learning rules for on-chip continual learning. Enables efficient online learning on neuromorphic hardware by aligning algorithmic requirements with physical substrate limitations, a core principle for iNEST systems.
