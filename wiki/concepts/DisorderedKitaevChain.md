## Definition
A finite Kitaev chain model with random onsite potentials or hopping amplitudes, studied via large deviation theory to characterize the probability of topological phase transitions. Provides a rigorous statistical framework for understanding how disorder impacts topological order, which is essential for designing robust neuromorphic or topological computing substrates.
