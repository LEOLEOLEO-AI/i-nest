# Space_Time_Multiplexing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
An architectural technique that reuses a single physical optical circuit (e.g. a delay loop or interferometer mesh) across many temporal modes so that an N-component device emulates a far larger effective interferometer. It is precisely the topology-to-time folding that TCC needs, letting a fixed chip-level network topology realize arbitrarily large logical graphs without adding physical links.
