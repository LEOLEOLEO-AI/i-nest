**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A design methodology that tunes TGV dimensions (e.g., conductor radius, dielectric thickness, ground ring spacing) to minimize insertion loss and return loss at multi-GHz frequencies. Directly impacts the achievable clock/data rates in TCC interconnects, determining the bandwidth ceiling for distributed in-network computing.
