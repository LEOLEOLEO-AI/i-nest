## Definition
A spin model with random exchange couplings on a two-dimensional lattice, used to study quantum spin glass phases and localization phenomena. Serves as a benchmark for understanding disorder-driven phase transitions, relevant to designing robust interconnect topologies that mimic spin-glass energy landscapes.
