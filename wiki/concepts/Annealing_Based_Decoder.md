# Annealing_Based_Decoder

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A decoder that solves the Ising formulation by annealing (quantum or classical) to find the ground state corresponding to the most probable transmitted codeword. Exploits the natural dynamics of neuromorphic or quantum hardware, offering a scalable alternative to Viterbi or belief propagation decoders.
