# GradientFreeLowRankTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training method for spiking neural networks that avoids gradient computation by using low-rank approximations or perturbations to update synaptic weights. This reduces computational and memory overhead in neuromorphic hardware, making it feasible to train large-scale spiking networks in resource-constrained TCC/iNEST environments.
