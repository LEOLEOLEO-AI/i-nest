# SynapticConnectivityAwarePlacement

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A placement strategy that assigns neurons and synapses to compute nodes based on the network's synaptic connectivity graph, co-locating densely connected groups. This improves both TCC and iNEST performance by minimizing long-distance spike transmission and leveraging locality in neuromorphic hardware.
