**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A Hebbian learning rule modified to respect structural constraints on synaptic connectivity, such as fixed topology or limited fan-in/fan-out, during weight updates. Enables biologically plausible learning in neuromorphic systems where physical interconnect topology limits plasticity, bridging TCC's structural focus with iNEST's neural computation.
