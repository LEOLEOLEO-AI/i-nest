# DopamineArousalSwitch

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A dopaminergic signaling pathway in Drosophila that bidirectionally regulates arousal and sleep-wake transitions, acting as a neuromodulatory switch. Offers a compact neuromodulatory control mechanism that can be emulated in in-network neuromorphic hardware for dynamic task prioritization and wakefulness-like states.
