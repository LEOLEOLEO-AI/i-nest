## Definition
The principle that deep thermalisation dynamics can be understood through the locality of entanglement teleportation across a quantum network, where information transfer is bounded by the entanglement structure of the topology. This provides a framework for designing TCC interconnects that exploit entanglement-based communication to achieve low-latency, topology-aware information transfer.
