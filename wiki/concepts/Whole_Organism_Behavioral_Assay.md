# Whole_Organism_Behavioral_Assay

**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A high-throughput screening method that measures the behavioral response of entire C. elegans populations to chemical stimuli, capturing integrated neural output. Provides a scalable biological benchmark for evaluating neuromorphic systems that must process complex, multimodal sensory inputs and produce coordinated motor outputs.
