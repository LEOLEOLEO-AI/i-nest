# PhaseLockedTopologyMapping

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A technique that exploits phase-locked states in coupled oscillatory networks to infer the underlying graph structure from phase differences alone, without amplitude information. Relevant for neuromorphic systems where spike-timing or phase encodes information, allowing topology extraction from spike-phase data in event-driven iNEST architectures.
