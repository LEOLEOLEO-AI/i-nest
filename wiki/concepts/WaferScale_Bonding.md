**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A packaging technique that bonds multiple bare dies onto a single wafer using solder bumps and re-routes them into one integrated logic architecture. It enables wafer-scale integration with high bandwidth and fault tolerance, directly addressing AI/HPC communication bottlenecks.
