# NoiseModulatedNeuralNetwork

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A neural network architecture where controlled noise is injected into forward propagation to encode gradient signals implicitly in the output statistics. Provides a biologically plausible and hardware-friendly training mechanism for in-network neuromorphic systems that cannot support exact backpropagation.
