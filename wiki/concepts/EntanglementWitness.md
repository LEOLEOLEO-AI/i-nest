# EntanglementWitness

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
An observable (often a local operator or correlation function) whose expectation value can certify the presence of entanglement in a quantum state without full state tomography. Enables efficient detection of quantum correlations in impurity-critical systems, which is essential for validating quantum resources in TCC/iNEST hardware.
