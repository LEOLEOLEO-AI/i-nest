# DisorderInducedLocalization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where quantum interference in a disordered material leads to electron localization, suppressing conductivity and altering critical behavior. Critical for understanding how structural disorder in amorphous carbon affects charge transport, which is key for designing reliable in-network or neuromorphic devices.
