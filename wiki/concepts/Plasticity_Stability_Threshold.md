# Plasticity_Stability_Threshold

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A spectral bound on Hebbian weight updates that, if exceeded, leads to unstable learning or overfitting, while below it ensures stable generalization. Guides the design of adaptive learning rates and synaptic scaling in neuromorphic chips to maintain long-term stability and performance.
