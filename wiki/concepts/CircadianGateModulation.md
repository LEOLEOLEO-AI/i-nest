## Definition
The mechanism by which circadian clock neurons modulate the excitability of sleep-promoting neurons, creating time-of-day-dependent sleep propensity. Demonstrates temporal gating of neural activity, relevant for designing neuromorphic systems that incorporate time-based scheduling and state-dependent processing.
