## Definition
The disruption of hormone-mediated signaling between the gut and peripheral tissues, influenced by microbial products, that contributes to colorectal cancer pathogenesis. Offers a concrete example of long-range, slow-timescale signaling that contrasts with fast neural signals, useful for designing hierarchical temporal processing in iNEST and multi-timescale network topologies in TCC.
