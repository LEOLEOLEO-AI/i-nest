**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A packaging technology that integrates multiple chiplets directly on a full silicon wafer, eliminating traditional substrate layers. Enables ultra-high bandwidth and low-latency interconnects, essential for TCC's global topology optimization and in-network compute.
