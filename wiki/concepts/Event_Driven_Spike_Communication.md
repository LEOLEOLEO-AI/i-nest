# Event_Driven_Spike_Communication

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A communication paradigm in SNNs where neurons emit binary spikes only upon threshold crossing, creating sparse and asynchronous event-driven data flow. Underpins the energy efficiency and temporal dynamics of neuromorphic computing by minimizing unnecessary data movement.
