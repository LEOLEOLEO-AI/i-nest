# Packaging_Aware_NoC_Design

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The practice of co-designing the NoC topology and routing with the physical packaging constraints (e.g., bump pitch, interposer routing) of the chiplet assembly. It ensures that the logical network topology is physically realizable and optimized for the specific packaging technology, a key enabler for scalable TCC systems.
