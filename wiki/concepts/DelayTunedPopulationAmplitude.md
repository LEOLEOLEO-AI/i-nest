# DelayTunedPopulationAmplitude

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where varying synaptic delay values systematically alters the amplitude of population oscillations, independent of firing rate changes. Offers a novel control knob for gain modulation in in-network neuromorphic systems without altering synaptic weights.
