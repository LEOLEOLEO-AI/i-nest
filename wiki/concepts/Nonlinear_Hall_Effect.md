## Definition
A second-order transverse charge or spin response to a longitudinal current that arises in inversion-asymmetric or magnetically ordered materials and can be governed by Berry curvature dipole or quantum-metric contributions. It provides a nonreciprocal, nonlinear transport primitive for in-network neuromorphic signal mixing, rectification, and topological routing in TCC/iNEST architectures.
