# DualTopologyProductSplit

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A design strategy where a hardware vendor ships two separate chip variants—one optimized for training (3D Torus) and one for inference (non-torus)—to serve different network diameter requirements. This validates TCC's argument that a single static topology is insufficient, but TCC proposes a more flexible single-base multi-page solution instead of two separate chips.
