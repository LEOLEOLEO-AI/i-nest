# AsymmetricCoupling

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A network connectivity pattern where the influence of neuron i on neuron j is not equal to the influence of j on i, breaking detailed balance and enabling directed information flow. Asymmetry is crucial for implementing non-reciprocal signal propagation and recurrent loops in neuromorphic circuits, expanding the functional repertoire beyond symmetric associative memories.
