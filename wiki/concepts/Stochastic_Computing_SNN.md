# Stochastic_Computing_SNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of representing and processing spike signals as stochastic bit streams, where the probability of a 1-bit corresponds to the signal value, enabling simple logic gates to perform complex arithmetic. Reduces the hardware footprint and power consumption of SNN accelerators, making them viable for integration into network switches and routers for in-network intelligence.
