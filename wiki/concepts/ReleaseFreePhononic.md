# ReleaseFreePhononic

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A phononic crystal design that does not require a release step (suspension) in fabrication, maintaining structural integrity and simplifying integration. Reduces manufacturing complexity and improves mechanical robustness, making it practical for on-chip TCC interconnect layers.
