# DualSided_Routing_Resource

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The additional routing resources (vias, metal layers, and bonding pads) available on the backside of a wafer, used to offload interconnect traffic from the front-side logic. This provides TCC with extra physical channels for topology optimization, reducing contention and enabling more complex non-planar network graphs.
