## Definition
A robust algorithmic method for identifying chimera states, which are spatiotemporal patterns where synchronized and desynchronized domains coexist in a network of coupled oscillators. Enables reliable detection of partial synchronization in neuromorphic and topological networks, which is critical for understanding and controlling emergent dynamics in iNEST systems.
