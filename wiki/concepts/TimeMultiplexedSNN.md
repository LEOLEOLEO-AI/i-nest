# TimeMultiplexedSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A hardware scheduling technique where multiple spiking neural network layers or neuron groups share the same physical compute resources across different time slots, rather than being instantiated in parallel. Enables large SNN inference on resource-limited FPGAs, directly addressing scalability constraints in in-network neuromorphic deployment.
