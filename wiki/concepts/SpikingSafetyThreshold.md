**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A concept that reinterprets traditional safety thresholds in control systems as the spiking threshold of neurons in a spiking neural network, where crossing the threshold triggers a spike representing a safety violation. This bridges safety-critical control theory with neuromorphic computing, enabling direct implementation of safety monitoring on low-power spiking hardware.
