**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A technique that computes gradients of a system's output with respect to internal control parameters by back-propagating adjoint fields through the same physical medium, enabling gradient-based learning in situ. Facilitates on-chip training of neuromorphic hardware, eliminating the need for external digital models and enabling real-time adaptation in iNEST systems.
