# Membrane_Space_Watermarking

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-29

## Definition
A watermarking technique that embeds ownership information directly into the membrane potential dynamics of spiking neurons rather than into weights or spike trains. It enables non-intrusive IP protection for spiking neural networks in neuromorphic hardware, where traditional weight-based watermarks may be unstable or inaccessible.
