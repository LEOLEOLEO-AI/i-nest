## Definition
A transport process where the mean squared displacement grows sub-linearly or super-linearly with time, governed by fractional-order derivatives in the diffusion equation. Provides a theoretical framework for understanding anomalous signal propagation and energy transport in complex network topologies, which is critical for designing efficient interconnect architectures.
