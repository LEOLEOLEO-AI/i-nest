**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuron model that dynamically adjusts its resonant frequency based on input signal statistics, enabling spectral estimation of streaming radar signals. This enables neuromorphic hardware to perform real-time spectral analysis of radar data without external clocking or fixed filter banks, crucial for low-power edge sensing.
