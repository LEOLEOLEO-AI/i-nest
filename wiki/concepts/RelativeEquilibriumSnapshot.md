# RelativeEquilibriumSnapshot

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A single time-point observation of a network's dynamical state taken when the system is at a relative equilibrium, where all nodes evolve at a common rate but with distinct phases. Enables inference of coupling topology from one measurement, drastically reducing data requirements for interconnect characterization in large-scale TCC fabrics.
