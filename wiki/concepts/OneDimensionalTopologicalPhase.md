## Definition
A topological phase of matter in one dimension, where edge states or zero modes are protected by symmetries, even in the presence of disorder or non-Hermiticity. Enables the realization of topologically protected qubits or logic elements in 1D chains, which are building blocks for scalable TCC architectures.
