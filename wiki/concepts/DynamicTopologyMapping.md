# DynamicTopologyMapping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of mapping a spiking neural network's logical graph onto the physical reconfigurable interconnect at runtime, adapting to workload changes. This enables efficient utilization of hardware resources for varying network structures, a key enabler for flexible in-network neuromorphic systems.
