# Spike_Timing_Marginalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
An encoding in which posterior marginals are represented by relative spike latencies and marginalized through coincidence detection at synaptic integration sites. It replaces explicit arithmetic summation with temporal coincidence, exploiting the native timing primitives of neuromorphic hardware to reduce inference energy and latency.
