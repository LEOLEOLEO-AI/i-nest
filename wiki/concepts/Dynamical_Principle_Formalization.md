# Dynamical_Principle_Formalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mathematical approach to formalize the underlying dynamical equations that govern habituation, allowing for quantitative prediction and control of adaptive behavior in neural systems. Offers a rigorous basis for implementing habituation in neuromorphic circuits, ensuring predictable and tunable adaptation in in-network computing tasks.
