## Definition
The nonlinear response of individual photonic nodes (e.g., semiconductor optical amplifiers, microring resonators) that transforms input signals into rich, high-dimensional feature spaces within the reservoir. These nonlinearities are essential for separating complex input patterns, enabling the reservoir to perform tasks like classification and prediction that linear systems cannot.
