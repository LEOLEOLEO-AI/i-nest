# Spike_Communication_Fabric

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The dedicated packet- or event-based interconnect layer responsible for routing sparse spike events asynchronously between neuromorphic cores. Its topology and routing policy govern the latency, throughput, and energy per spike that constrain real-time in-network neural computation.
