# SpikingLocalMinimaTraps

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A phenomenon in spiking neural networks where gradient-based learning converges to suboptimal solutions due to the non-differentiable spike generation process, trapping the network in poor local minima. Understanding and breaking these traps is critical for improving the reliability and convergence of neuromorphic learning algorithms in iNEST systems.
