## Definition
The mechanism by which the emergent replica clock couples to the underlying topological structure of the network, influencing how synchronization propagates through interconnects. Guides the placement of clock distribution in TCC interconnects to optimize emergent synchronization for energy-efficient, fault-tolerant computation.
