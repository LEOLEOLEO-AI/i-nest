## Definition
The mathematical framework describing how physical observables (e.g., conductance, spin polarization) scale near a topological phase transition, governed by critical exponents and universality classes. Understanding this scaling enables prediction of abrupt changes in topological properties, which can be leveraged for adaptive reconfiguration of network topologies in TCC systems.
