# ContinuousTimeSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spiking neural network model where neuron dynamics and spike generation are governed by continuous-time differential equations rather than discrete simulation steps. This more accurately mimics biological neurons and enables event-driven computation, which is foundational for energy-efficient neuromorphic computing.
