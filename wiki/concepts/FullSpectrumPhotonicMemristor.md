# FullSpectrumPhotonicMemristor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A memristor device that can be programmed by light across an ultrawide bandwidth from ultraviolet to near-infrared (375-1064 nm) using a boron nitride-silicon heterostructure. Enables optical-control-based synaptic emulation with broad spectral coverage, advancing in-memory and neuromorphic computing paradigms for iNEST.
