**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The property that the strength and detectability of early warning signals depend on the underlying graph structure, including degree distribution, clustering, and modularity, rather than on node dynamics alone. Establishes that warning-signal design in TCC and iNEST cannot be topology-agnostic, directly linking graph architecture to the predictability of emergent network behavior.
