## Definition
A phase transition in a quantum system that is induced by increasing the strength of random disorder, rather than by temperature or external fields, often leading to exotic critical behavior. Understanding these transitions is vital for developing in-network neuromorphic circuits that can harness or mitigate disorder-induced effects for adaptive computation.
