# Spike_Energy_Floor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The minimum achievable energy per inference in a spiking network, set by the sum of unavoidable fixed costs and the minimum spike count required for functional correctness, regardless of further activity pruning. Provides a realistic target for neuromorphic chip design in iNEST, preventing over-optimization on sparsity metrics that do not translate to system-level energy gains.
