## Definition
A reservoir computing framework where the recurrent neural network's weights and states are represented and evolved as tensor network decompositions (e.g., matrix product states) for chaotic time series prediction. Enables scalable and structured high-dimensional state representations on neuromorphic hardware, bridging tensor network topology with in-network computing for efficient chaotic dynamics modeling.
