# Ellipsoid_Fitting_Threshold

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The critical ratio of sample count to ambient dimension at which fitting an ellipsoid to data abruptly switches from feasible to infeasible. Provides a quantitative capacity bound for representing and learning convex topological manifolds in high-dimensional TCC and iNEST data structures.
