# Network_Centric_Computing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A computing paradigm that shifts primacy from the von Neumann CPU-memory model to the network topology and data movement patterns as the primary drivers of computation. It is the foundational premise of TCC, redefining computation as an emergent property of interconnect structure rather than sequential instruction execution.
