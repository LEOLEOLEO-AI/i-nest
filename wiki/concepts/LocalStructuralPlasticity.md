# LocalStructuralPlasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism where neural network topology adapts through local, activity-dependent growth and pruning of connections without global error signals. Enables self-organizing, energy-efficient architectural adaptation critical for in-network neuromorphic hardware where global backpropagation is impractical.
