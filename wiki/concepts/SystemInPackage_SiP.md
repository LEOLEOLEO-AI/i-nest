## Definition
A packaging approach that integrates multiple chiplets or dies into a single package to form a complete high-performance system, overcoming monolithic chip physical limits. Provides the physical substrate for TCC's distributed, non-von-Neumann topologies, allowing flexible mapping of computational graphs onto heterogeneous silicon.
