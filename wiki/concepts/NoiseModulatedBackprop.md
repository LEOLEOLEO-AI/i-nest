# NoiseModulatedBackprop

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that reconstructs gradient information for backpropagation by analyzing forward-pass fluctuations induced by injected noise in neural network activations. Enables training of neuromorphic hardware where explicit backward passes are costly or physically unsupported, using only forward dynamics.
