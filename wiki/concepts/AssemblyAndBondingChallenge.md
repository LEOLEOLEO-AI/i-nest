# AssemblyAndBondingChallenge

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The set of technical difficulties in alignment, bonding, thermal dissipation, and warpage control during the assembly of heterogeneous 2.5D/3D chiplets. These challenges constrain the achievable size and density of topology-centric architectures, making them a core design consideration for TCC hardware.
