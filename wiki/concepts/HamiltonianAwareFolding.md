## Definition
A VQE-based protein-folding approach where the molecular Hamiltonian is directly encoded into the quantum circuit structure, making the simulation topology reflect the protein's physical interaction graph. This bridges quantum chemistry and topology-centric computing by showing how molecular graph structure can dictate quantum circuit layout, relevant for TCC's graph-based execution models.
