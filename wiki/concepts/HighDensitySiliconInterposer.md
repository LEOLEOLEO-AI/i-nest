# HighDensitySiliconInterposer

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A silicon substrate with fine-pitch wiring used to integrate multiple dies or components into a wafer-scale or large-scale packaged system. It serves as an alternative to true wafer-scale fabrication, enabling high-bandwidth, topology-rich interconnects for heterogeneous TCC assemblies.
