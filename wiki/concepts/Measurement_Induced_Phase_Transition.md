# Measurement_Induced_Phase_Transition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A dynamical phase transition occurring in monitored quantum systems where increasing the measurement rate drives the steady-state entanglement from volume-law to area-law scaling, without any change in the Hamiltonian parameters. Establishes measurement rate as a tunable control knob analogous to a coupling constant, giving TCC/iNEST a paradigm in which read-out density, not only connectivity, selects the computational or memory phase of a network.
