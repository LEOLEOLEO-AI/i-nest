**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A spike-driven interconnect architecture that routes event-based signals between spiking neurons using topology-aware pathways rather than fixed wiring. Reduces communication latency and energy in neuromorphic systems by exploiting topological locality, a core requirement for scalable iNEST fabrics.
