# CorticalEfficiencyReliabilityExchange

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A principle observed in human cerebral cortex networks where communication efficiency is deliberately sacrificed to increase the reliability of distributed neural computation. Provides a bio-inspired justification for iNEST designs that allow redundant or non-optimal communication in order to protect the correctness of in-network neuromorphic computation.
