# Input_Proportional_Energy

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A design property of neuromorphic systems where energy consumption is near zero without input and grows proportionally with incoming neural activity. It defines the key energy-efficiency target for iNEST edge neuromorphic hardware and distinguishes it from always-on conventional AI accelerators.
