# SpikeCodedSafetyMonitoring

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of encoding safety state variables as spike trains, where the rate or timing of spikes reflects the proximity to a safety boundary, allowing continuous monitoring without explicit threshold comparisons. This leverages the temporal dynamics of SNNs to provide analog-like safety information with digital event-based outputs, reducing computational overhead and enabling distributed, in-network safety processing in iNEST architectures.
