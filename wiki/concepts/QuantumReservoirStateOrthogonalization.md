# QuantumReservoirStateOrthogonalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method of transforming quantum reservoir states to be mutually orthogonal in the Fisher information metric, thereby decorrelating the readout features for improved linear separability. This enhances the computational power of quantum reservoirs without increasing physical qubits, critical for scaling neuromorphic processing in resource-constrained in-network environments.
