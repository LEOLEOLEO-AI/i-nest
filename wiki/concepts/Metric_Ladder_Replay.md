# Metric_Ladder_Replay

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A replay mechanism that uses the Urysohn ladder's contracted metric spaces to selectively reactivate past neural patterns during new learning, mimicking hippocampal replay. Provides a neuromorphic-friendly implementation of replay that reduces computational overhead while preserving topological relationships, enhancing continual learning in spiking networks.
