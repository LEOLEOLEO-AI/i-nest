# Global_Self_Attention_Bottleneck

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-29

## Definition
The scalability limitation caused by computing attention scores across all token pairs, which grows quadratically with sequence length and is especially costly in spike-driven neural architectures. Identifying and mitigating this bottleneck is critical for realizing practical, scalable Transformer-based spiking networks in resource-constrained neuromorphic and in-network computing environments.
