# DynamicalMeanFieldAnalysis

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A statistical physics method that approximates the collective behavior of a large network by replacing individual interactions with an average field, enabling analysis of dense catalytic reaction networks. Provides a tractable framework for predicting emergent dynamics in dense TCC/iNEST topologies where pairwise interactions are computationally prohibitive.
