# TopologyCentricInterconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An interconnect design principle where the network topology itself is the primary computational substrate, with routing and communication patterns directly encoding neural connectivity. Shifts the design focus from computation-centric to topology-centric, enabling efficient mapping of neural graphs onto physical hardware and reducing data movement overhead.
