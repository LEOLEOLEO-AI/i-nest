# SelfInhibitionFeedbackLoop

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A local negative feedback loop where a node's activity suppresses its own future excitation, acting as a homeostatic control mechanism in recurrent networks. Critical for preventing runaway excitation in neuromorphic hardware, enabling stable spike-timing-dependent plasticity and long-term memory retention.
