# DirectTrainingSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Training spiking neural networks end-to-end using backpropagation through time with surrogate gradients, without converting from pre-trained artificial neural networks. Enables task-specific optimization of spike-based models, which is fundamental for adapting neuromorphic cores to dynamic network traffic patterns in iNEST.
