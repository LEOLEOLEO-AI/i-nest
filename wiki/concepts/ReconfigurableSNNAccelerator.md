# ReconfigurableSNNAccelerator

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A hardware accelerator for spiking neural networks that can alter its computational and communication architecture post-fabrication to match varying network structures and spike dynamics. It enables flexible, energy-efficient execution of diverse SNN topologies, which is essential for both TCC's topology-aware computing and iNEST's neuromorphic processing goals.
