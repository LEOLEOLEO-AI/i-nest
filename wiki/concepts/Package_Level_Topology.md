# Package_Level_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The overall geometric and logical topology of chiplets, NoC, and SDI interconnects within a single package, as opposed to board-level or system-level topology. Defines the physical substrate on which TCC algorithms execute, influencing routing paths, fault tolerance, and energy efficiency at the package scale.
