# Criticality_Gated_Learning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A learning regime in which synaptic plasticity is gated by proximity to the critical point separating ordered from chaotic network dynamics, so that correlation flow is maximally informative. It ties the operating point of a neuromorphic substrate directly to its trainability, implying iNEST hardware must actively hold criticality rather than merely tolerate it.
