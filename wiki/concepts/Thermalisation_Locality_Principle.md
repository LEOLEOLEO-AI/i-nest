**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The principle that thermalisation dynamics in a quantum network are governed by local interactions and the spatial arrangement of nodes, rather than global properties alone. This principle informs the placement of computational and memory resources in TCC to minimise thermal noise and maximise computational coherence.
