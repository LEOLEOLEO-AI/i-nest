**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A technique that approximates the non-differentiable spike function with a smooth surrogate during backpropagation, enabling gradient-based training of SNNs. Bridges the gap between local learning rules and global optimization, offering a practical training pathway for iNEST hardware that supports both modes.
