# InNetworkFieldQuery

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mechanism where queries to a neural field world model are executed directly within network switches or routers, using the network topology as a spatial index for field evaluation. Reduces latency and bandwidth by avoiding round-trips to a central server, critical for real-time neuromorphic control in distributed environments.
