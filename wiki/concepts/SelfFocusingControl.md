# SelfFocusingControl

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A technique that dynamically adjusts laser focal depth during processing to compensate for nonlinear refractive index changes, ensuring consistent cutting depth in transparent materials. Critical for maintaining layer uniformity and minimizing defects in SiC substrates used in high-performance TCC interconnects.
