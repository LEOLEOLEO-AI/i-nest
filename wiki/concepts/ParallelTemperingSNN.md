## Definition
A Markov Chain Monte Carlo method adapted for spiking neural networks that runs multiple replicas of the network at different temperatures and exchanges states to escape local minima in constraint satisfaction problems. Enables SNN-based solvers to overcome local-minimum traps, a critical limitation for neuromorphic computing applied to combinatorial optimization.
