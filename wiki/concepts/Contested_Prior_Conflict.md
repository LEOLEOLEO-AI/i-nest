**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A state where multiple internal models or priors compete for influence over inference, causing oscillatory or relapse-like behavior in neuromorphic circuits. Understanding this helps design iNEST architectures with explicit arbitration mechanisms to avoid unstable recurrent loops in spiking neural networks.
