## Definition
A network of binary neurons whose state updates are governed by probabilistic rules, allowing for noise-driven exploration of state space rather than deterministic convergence. Stochasticity is key for energy-efficient probabilistic computing and sampling-based inference in neuromorphic systems, aligning with TCC's focus on unconventional computing paradigms.
