# ReplicaSynchronization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process by which multiple copies (replicas) of a quantum state or computational unit converge to a shared temporal phase, enabling coherent operation across distributed nodes. Enables fault-tolerant and coherent computation in TCC/iNEST architectures by providing a mechanism for distributed state alignment without centralized control.
