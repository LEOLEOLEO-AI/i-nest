## Definition
A Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev model variant with power-law decaying all-to-all interactions, tunable by a range parameter that interpolates between sparse and dense connectivity. Provides a tractable many-body framework for studying spectral transitions in neuromorphic and topological systems with non-local coupling.
