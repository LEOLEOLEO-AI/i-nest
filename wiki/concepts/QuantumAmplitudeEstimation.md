# QuantumAmplitudeEstimation

**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A quantum algorithm that estimates the amplitude of a target state in a superposition, providing quadratic speedup over classical Monte Carlo sampling for expectation value estimation. Enables efficient estimation of stochastic travel times in routing problems, a key computational bottleneck that TCC/iNEST architectures could accelerate through quantum-inspired or hybrid processing.
