# NeuralPartitioning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The algorithmic division of an SNN graph into subgraphs that are mapped onto separate FPGAs, minimizing inter-FPGA spike traffic while balancing compute load. Optimal partitioning reduces communication bottlenecks and maximizes utilization of distributed FPGA resources, essential for scaling.
