# WaveBasedComputation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A computational paradigm where information is encoded and processed via the phase, amplitude, or direction of traveling waves rather than discrete spikes. Offers an alternative to spike-based computing, enabling low-latency, continuous-time processing suitable for in-network neuromorphic hardware.
