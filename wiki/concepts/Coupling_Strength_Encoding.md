# Coupling_Strength_Encoding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The technique of translating channel reliability information (e.g., log-likelihood ratios) into the coupling coefficients of an Ising formulation for decoding. Allows the network to incorporate real-time channel state information into the decoding process, making TCC adaptive to dynamic link conditions.
