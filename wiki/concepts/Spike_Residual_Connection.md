# Spike_Residual_Connection

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A skip connection that transmits spike-based signals directly across layers, bypassing non-linear transformations, to preserve temporal information and improve trainability in SNNs. This enables the construction of deeper spiking networks within iNEST, overcoming the inherent difficulty of training deep SNNs due to discrete spike events.
