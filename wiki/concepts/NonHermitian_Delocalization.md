# NonHermitian_Delocalization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A phenomenon in non-Hermitian systems where eigenstates become spatially extended (delocalized) due to non-reciprocal hopping or gain/loss, contrasting with Hermitian localization. Enables robust signal propagation and reconfigurable routing in topological photonic or phononic networks without requiring fine-tuned Hermitian symmetry.
