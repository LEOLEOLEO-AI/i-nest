**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A method for embedding continuous metric spaces into discrete topological graphs while preserving neighborhood and connectivity relationships, enabling topology-centric computation on continuous data. Enables TCC systems to process real-world analog signals directly within graph-based computational frameworks, avoiding costly analog-to-digital conversion and preserving spatial-temporal correlations.
