## Definition
An architecture where the network topology itself is designed to mirror or support spiking neural connectivity, allowing communication and computation to be co-optimized. Reduces latency and energy overhead by aligning data flow with neural signal propagation, a core principle for scalable neuromorphic computing in network environments.
