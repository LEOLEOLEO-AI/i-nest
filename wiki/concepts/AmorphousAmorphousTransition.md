# AmorphousAmorphousTransition

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A first-order-like phase transition between two distinct amorphous solid states (low-density and high-density) in compressed glasses, driven by pressure without crystallization. Provides a physical mechanism for abrupt, reversible state switching in disordered materials, which could be exploited for binary or multi-state neuromorphic memory elements.
