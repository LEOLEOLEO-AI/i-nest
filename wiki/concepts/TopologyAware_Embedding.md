## Definition
The process of mapping virtual network functions or SFC segments onto physical nodes while considering the underlying topology's connectivity, bandwidth, and node capabilities. Critical for optimizing communication overhead and ensuring that topology-centric computing systems achieve performance guarantees under varying network conditions.
