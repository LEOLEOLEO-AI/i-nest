**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The deliberate injection of stochastic noise into spiking neuron dynamics to improve generalization and prevent overfitting in SNN training. Noise injection is a key technique for enhancing the robustness and adaptability of neuromorphic processors, particularly in noisy physical environments.
