## Definition
Topological phases that have an inverse under stacking, meaning they can be trivialized by combining with another phase, and are classified by cobordism groups or generalized cohomology theories. Understanding invertible phases is crucial for identifying which topological orders are stable against perturbations, informing the design of fault-tolerant neuromorphic or computational substrates.
