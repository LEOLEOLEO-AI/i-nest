## Definition
A method to estimate the reliability of each hyperedge by analyzing the agreement among its constituent nodes' features or predictions, often using attention or uncertainty quantification. Enables selective information propagation in TCC architectures, allowing the network to prioritize high-confidence hyperedges and reduce error propagation in multi-hop communication.
