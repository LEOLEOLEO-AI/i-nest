## Definition
A hardware architecture that uses coupled spiking neurons or oscillators to simulate the energy landscape of an Ising model, converging to low-energy states that represent solutions. Offers a massively parallel, low-power alternative to digital decoders, enabling real-time error correction within network switches or routers.
