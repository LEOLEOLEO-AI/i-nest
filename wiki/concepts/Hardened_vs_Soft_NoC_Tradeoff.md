# Hardened_vs_Soft_NoC_Tradeoff

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
The architectural trade-off between dedicated silicon NoC fabrics, which offer high bandwidth and low latency at fixed topology, and soft-logic NoCs, which offer topological flexibility at higher area and delay cost. Quantifying this trade-off determines when a topology-centric design should harden its interconnect versus reconfiguring it, a core design decision in TCC systems.
