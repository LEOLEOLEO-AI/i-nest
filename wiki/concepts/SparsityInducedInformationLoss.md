# SparsityInducedInformationLoss

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The degradation of representational fidelity in spiking networks caused by excessive spike suppression, leading to missed temporal patterns or insufficient signal-to-noise ratio. Sets a lower bound on useful sparsity, informing iNEST architecture choices for spike encoding and recurrent connectivity to preserve information.
