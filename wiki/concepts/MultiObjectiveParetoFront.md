## Definition
A set of non-dominated solutions in the objective space where no single solution improves one objective without degrading another, representing trade-offs in topology planning. Provides a principled way to evaluate and select among competing topology configurations, applicable to both TCC (network efficiency) and iNEST (neuromorphic resource allocation).
