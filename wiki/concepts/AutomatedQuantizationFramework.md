## Definition
An end-to-end pipeline that automatically searches and applies optimal quantization policies for spiking transformers without manual tuning, often using reinforcement learning or evolutionary search. This reduces the expertise barrier for deploying spiking models, accelerating the adoption of neuromorphic computing in TCC/iNEST systems.
