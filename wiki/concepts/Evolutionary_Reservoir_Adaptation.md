# Evolutionary_Reservoir_Adaptation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of applying evolutionary or swarm-based search to adapt reservoir parameters (e.g., synaptic weights, node thresholds, or topology) in response to task demands, rather than using fixed random initialization. Offers a scalable alternative to gradient-based training for recurrent networks, particularly relevant for hardware-constrained TCC/iNEST implementations where backpropagation is impractical.
