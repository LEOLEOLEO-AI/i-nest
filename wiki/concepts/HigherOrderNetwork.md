**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A network model where interactions are represented not only as pairwise edges but as multi-body relations among groups of nodes. Shifts TCC from node-centric computation to relation-centric structure, enabling representation of collective dynamics that pairwise graphs miss.
