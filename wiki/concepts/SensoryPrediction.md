**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A computation where motor-related corollary signals generate expected sensory consequences, allowing the brain to subtract self-generated input from the sensory stream. Enables neuromorphic systems to distinguish external stimuli from self-generated or predictable network traffic without explicit global coordination.
