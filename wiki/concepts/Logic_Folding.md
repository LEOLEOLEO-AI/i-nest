# Logic_Folding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-07

## Definition
A method of reconfiguring data center chip logic into folded topological structures, where multiple logical operations are mapped onto shared physical resources via spatial-temporal reuse, reducing hardware redundancy. Enables significant reductions in chip area and power consumption while maintaining throughput, making it a core technique for scaling TCC-based data center accelerators beyond conventional transistor limits.
