# OpportunisticRouting

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A routing paradigm where data forwarding decisions are made on-the-fly based on current network conditions and contact opportunities rather than predetermined paths. Supports resilient communication in intermittently connected networks, a key challenge for topology-centric and in-network neuromorphic deployments.
