# Course_Based_FPGA_Prototyping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A pedagogical framework where university courses (e.g., Cornell ECE 5760) guide students to build complete FPGA projects, including SNN implementations, with open-source code. Fosters a reproducible research culture and provides a rich source of validated hardware designs that can be reused or adapted for TCC/iNEST experiments.
