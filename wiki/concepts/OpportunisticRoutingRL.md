# OpportunisticRoutingRL

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A reinforcement learning-based approach for selecting next-hop relays in delay-tolerant networks by exploiting transient contact opportunities rather than relying on pre-established paths. It demonstrates how in-network neuromorphic principles can be applied to make real-time routing decisions in sparse, intermittently connected networks, a key challenge for distributed TCC systems.
