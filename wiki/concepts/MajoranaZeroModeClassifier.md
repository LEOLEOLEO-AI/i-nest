## Definition
A machine-learning or threshold-based classifier that processes conductance or tunneling spectra to label a signal as a genuine Majorana zero mode versus a disorder-induced trivial state. Bridges TCC device-level detection with iNEST-style in-network processing, enabling real-time classification at the edge of a topological quantum network.
