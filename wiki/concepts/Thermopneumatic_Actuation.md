**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A mechanism that generates mechanical displacement or force through the thermal expansion of a sealed gas or liquid, enabling localized and rapid actuation. Provides a low-voltage, robust actuation method suitable for integrating tactile feedback into neuromorphic or topology-aware haptic interfaces.
