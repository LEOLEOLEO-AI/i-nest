# SimplicialComplexTransition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
A state-space representation where random walk states are simplices (points, edges, triangles, etc.) and transitions are defined by boundary or coboundary maps. Provides a natural framework for modeling multi-scale dynamics in complex networks, supporting TCC's goal of exploiting topological features for computation.
