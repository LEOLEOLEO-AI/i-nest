# ForwardFluctuationGradient

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The gradient estimate derived from the correlation between noise perturbations and output errors during a single forward propagation. Provides a biologically plausible and hardware-friendly alternative to error backpropagation for on-chip learning in neuromorphic systems.
