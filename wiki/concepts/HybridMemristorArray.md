# HybridMemristorArray

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An architecture combining different types of memristor devices (e.g., analog and digital) in a single array to balance precision, speed, and endurance for synaptic weight storage. Leverages complementary device characteristics to overcome individual limitations, critical for scalable and reliable in-network neuromorphic computing.
