# StorageCompute_Separation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The architectural bottleneck in von Neumann systems where memory and processing units are physically separated, causing data movement overhead and energy inefficiency. Overcoming this separation is a foundational motivation for both TCC and iNEST, driving novel compute-in-memory and neuromorphic designs.
