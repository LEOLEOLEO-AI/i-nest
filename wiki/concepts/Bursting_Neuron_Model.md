**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A neuron model that generates periodic bursts of action potentials (spikes) followed by quiescent periods, characterized by distinct fast and slow dynamics. Enables temporal coding and richer information processing in neuromorphic systems beyond simple spiking, critical for mimicking biological neural behaviors in iNEST.
