**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method for discretizing PDE variables and derivatives into spike events and spike-timing relationships, rather than continuous numerical values. It provides a natural interface between physical PDE models and neuromorphic hardware, allowing iNEST devices to process continuous phenomena with discrete spikes.
