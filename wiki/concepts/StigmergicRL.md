**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A reinforcement learning paradigm where agents coordinate indirectly through modifications to a shared environment, using stigmergic traces as both communication and state signals. Enables decentralized, scalable swarm coordination without direct inter-agent communication, relevant for distributed TCC/iNEST topologies.
