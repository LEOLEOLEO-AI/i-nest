## Definition
A joint design approach that simultaneously optimizes the mapping of SNN graph structures onto hardware resources and the temporal scheduling of spike events to maximize resource utilization and minimize conflicts. This co-optimization is essential for achieving high efficiency in TCC/iNEST systems where communication and computation are tightly interlinked, and naive separate optimizations lead to bottlenecks.
