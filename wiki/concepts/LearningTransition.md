# LearningTransition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A sharp phase transition in the generalization error as a function of model capacity or data size, separating under- and over-parameterized regimes in extensive-width networks. Identifies the precise capacity thresholds that must be matched in network-embedded neuromorphic processors to avoid catastrophic overfitting or underfitting in dynamic topologies.
