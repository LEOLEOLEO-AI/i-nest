# PhysicalDesign_ReliabilityTradeoff

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-26

## Definition
The tradeoff between adding redundancy (e.g., TMR) and the physical design costs such as area, power, routing congestion, and timing closure in NoC routers. This tradeoff determines the feasibility of fault-tolerant TCC/iNEST hardware, as excessive redundancy can negate the low-latency and energy benefits.
