**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A recursive algorithm that simulates the branching of cracks by iteratively subdividing existing fracture segments based on local stress and material properties. Provides a scalable method to generate complex, biologically-inspired branching networks that can serve as neuromorphic connectivity patterns for in-network processing.
