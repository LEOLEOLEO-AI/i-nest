# SpikeTemporalCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of encoding fMRI-derived features into precise spike timings, where the relative order or latency of spikes represents semantic information. Enables efficient temporal processing of neural signals in neuromorphic hardware, reducing latency and power consumption compared to rate-based encoding.
