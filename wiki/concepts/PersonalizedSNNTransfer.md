# PersonalizedSNNTransfer

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training paradigm where a base spiking neural network is first trained on a large population dataset, then fine-tuned on a small per-subject EEG sample to adapt to individual neural signatures. Enables practical deployment of neuromorphic EEG processors by reducing per-user calibration data and computational cost, critical for wearable health monitoring.
