**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A photonic sampling-based computational model in which squeezed Gaussian light states are injected into a linear interferometer and the output photon-number distribution is sampled by photon-number-resolving detectors to solve problems conjectured to be classically hard. It supplies a non-von-Neumann compute primitive whose interferometer connectivity is a literal physical topology, giving TCC a concrete substrate for topology-encoded computation.
