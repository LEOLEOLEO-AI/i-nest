**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The ability of an alternative metal to form reliable, low-resistance joints in hybrid bonding processes used for 3D stacking and TSV integration. Determines whether new metals can replace copper in fine-pitch interconnects without compromising thermomechanical reliability in multi-layer TCC systems.
