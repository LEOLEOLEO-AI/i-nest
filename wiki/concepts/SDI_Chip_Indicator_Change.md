**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A formal process for revising the technical specification indicators of a Software-Defined Infrastructure (SDI) chip, typically involving expert review and arbitration by a program office. This concept is critical for TCC because SDI chips are foundational to programmable network infrastructure, and indicator changes directly affect performance and compatibility in topology-centric computing environments.
