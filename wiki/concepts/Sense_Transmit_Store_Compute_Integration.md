## Definition
The integration of sensing, communication, memory, and computing into one unified substrate so that data is processed where it is sensed instead of being shuttled across bus, memory, and processor boundaries. This is the core cure for the von Neumann bottleneck and motivates both TCC and iNEST to co-design topologies and neuromorphic learners.
