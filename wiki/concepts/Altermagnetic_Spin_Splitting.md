# Altermagnetic_Spin_Splitting

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The momentum-dependent spin splitting of electronic bands in altermagnets, caused by rotational magnetic sublattice symmetry rather than relativistic spin-orbit coupling alone. It enables spin-polarized carrier manipulation relevant to low-power TCC logic and iNEST spintronic/neuromorphic device operations.
