# DelayCodedWorkingMemory

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A working memory paradigm where information is retained in the network through the precise timing of spikes and the distribution of synaptic delays, rather than through persistent activity or attractor states. This concept is essential for iNEST because it enables energy-efficient, spike-based memory retention that aligns with the event-driven nature of neuromorphic hardware.
