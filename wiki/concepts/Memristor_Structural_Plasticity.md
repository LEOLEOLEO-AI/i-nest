# Memristor_Structural_Plasticity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The ability of memristor arrays, using NMOS pruning switches, to physically cut or form connections and thereby reconfigure network topology. It provides the hardware foundation for iNEST's dynamic topology self-growth and controllable rewiring.
