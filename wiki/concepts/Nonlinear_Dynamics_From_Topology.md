# Nonlinear_Dynamics_From_Topology

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The principle that specific topological arrangements of synaptic connections directly generate nonlinear dynamical regimes (e.g., chaos, oscillations, multistability) in neuromorphic systems. Guides the design of in-network neuromorphic substrates that exploit topology for computation without explicit external control, advancing autonomous neural processing.
