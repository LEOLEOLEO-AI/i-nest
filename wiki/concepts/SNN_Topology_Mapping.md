## Definition
The process of mapping spiking neural network topologies onto physical or virtual computing substrates, considering constraints like neuron placement, synaptic routing, and communication delays. This mapping is fundamental to TCC/iNEST because it determines how well the network's computational structure aligns with the underlying hardware, directly impacting performance, energy efficiency, and scalability.
