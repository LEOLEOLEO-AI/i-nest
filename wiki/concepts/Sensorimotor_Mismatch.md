## Definition
The condition where the sensory input space and motor output space of a neural controller are not matched to the original organism's body plan, requiring the network to adapt to new physical constraints. This concept is critical for iNEST because it reveals how neuromorphic systems must handle domain shifts when deployed in different robotic or embedded platforms.
