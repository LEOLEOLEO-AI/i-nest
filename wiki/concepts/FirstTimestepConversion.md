# FirstTimestepConversion

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A conversion paradigm where ANN weights and activations are mapped to SNN spikes such that the network produces correct outputs immediately at the first timestep, without requiring multiple timesteps for spike accumulation. This eliminates the temporal latency bottleneck of traditional SNN conversion, making neuromorphic systems viable for real-time edge applications.
