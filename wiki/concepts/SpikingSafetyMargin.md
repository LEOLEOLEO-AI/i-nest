**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The margin between the neuron's resting potential and its spiking threshold, mapped to the allowable deviation from a safety boundary in the physical system. This provides a continuous, biologically-inspired measure of safety that can be dynamically adjusted via neuromorphic plasticity mechanisms.
