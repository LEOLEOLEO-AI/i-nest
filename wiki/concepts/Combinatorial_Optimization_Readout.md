# Combinatorial_Optimization_Readout

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A lightweight readout layer that maps reservoir states (derived from DP processes) to solutions of combinatorial optimization problems, such as shortest path or knapsack variants. This reduces the need for complex, problem-specific solvers, making neuromorphic hardware more versatile for optimization tasks in network-centric computing.
