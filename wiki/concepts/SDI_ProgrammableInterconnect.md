## Definition
Software-Defined Interconnect (SDI) that exposes global topology programmability, allowing runtime reconfiguration of network paths and connections across chips and wafers. SDI is the enabling mechanism for TCC, turning static physical topologies into dynamic, computation-aware communication fabrics.
