# FrequencyBasedReservoir

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A reservoir computing paradigm where the reservoir's internal state is encoded and processed in the frequency domain rather than the time domain, using oscillatory or spectral dynamics. This enables efficient hardware implementations in neuromorphic systems by leveraging frequency multiplexing and reducing temporal synchronization constraints.
