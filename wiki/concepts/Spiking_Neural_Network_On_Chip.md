# Spiking_Neural_Network_On_Chip

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A complete SNN implementation—including neurons, synapses, and learning rules—mapped onto a single FPGA device, often with custom routing and event-driven processing. Enables standalone, real-time neuromorphic inference and learning in edge devices, critical for in-network intelligence where low power and low latency are paramount.
