# ResistivityScalingChallenge

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where metal resistivity increases sharply as wire dimensions shrink below the electron mean free path, degrading interconnect performance. This is a fundamental bottleneck for TCC's dense, fine-grained interconnect fabrics, driving the need for alternative metals and novel integration schemes.
