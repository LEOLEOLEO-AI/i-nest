## Definition
A wafer-level silicon substrate that serves as a large-scale integration platform for heterogeneous chiplets, replacing traditional multi-chip modules on smaller dies. Enables massive chiplet density and global interconnectivity, which is foundational for TCC's topology-centric computing at scale.
