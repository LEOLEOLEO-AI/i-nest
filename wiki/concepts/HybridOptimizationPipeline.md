# HybridOptimizationPipeline

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A two-stage pipeline where a convex solver provides a globally smooth initial solution, then a non-convex solver refines it to escape local minima. Provides a scalable and robust method for complex floorplanning problems, applicable to both TCC and iNEST where multi-objective constraints are common.
