# Train_Infer_Integrated_AI

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An architecture that unifies learning and inference on the same edge processor, avoiding the separation of training and inference across different hardware. Overcomes the limitation of edge AI chips that can only infer, enabling online adaptation for autonomous driving and drone corner cases.
