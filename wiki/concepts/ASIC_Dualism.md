## Definition
A design philosophy where specialized ASICs are built around two opposing architectural principles (e.g., dataflow vs. systolic) to cover diverse AI workloads. Challenges the single-architecture GPU paradigm, enabling topology-aware specialization that can be mapped onto reconfigurable network fabrics.
