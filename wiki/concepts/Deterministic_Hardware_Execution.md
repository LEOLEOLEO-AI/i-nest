**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A microarchitectural principle where instruction execution and data movement have fixed, repeatable latency independent of data values, contention, or runtime scheduling. Determinism is critical for reproducible neuromorphic and topology-centric computing where timing and ordering affect correctness.
