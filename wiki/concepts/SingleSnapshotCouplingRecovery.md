## Definition
The algorithmic process of extracting the full adjacency matrix of a network from a single temporal frame of node state measurements, bypassing traditional multi-time-series approaches. Enables real-time or low-latency topology mapping in reconfigurable TCC fabrics where rapid rewiring occurs and continuous monitoring is infeasible.
