# PathIntegration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The neural computation that maintains a continuous estimate of an agent's position and heading by integrating self-motion cues over time, without external landmarks. Critical for autonomous navigation in edge devices and robotics, and demonstrates how local network dynamics can yield global spatial representations in neuromorphic substrates.
