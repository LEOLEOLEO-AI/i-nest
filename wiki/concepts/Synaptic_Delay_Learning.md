# Synaptic_Delay_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The process of adapting axonal or synaptic transmission delays in spiking neural networks to modulate spike arrival times and network dynamics. It is critical for in-network neuromorphic computing because delay parameters directly shape temporal computation and spike-based communication.
