# EventDrivenInference

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An inference paradigm where SNN computations are triggered only by incoming spike events rather than continuous frame processing. This event-driven approach reduces energy consumption and computational overhead, aligning with iNEST's goal of efficient in-network neuromorphic processing.
