# DigitalSpikingNeuralNetwork

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A spiking neural network implemented using digital circuits and discrete spike events rather than analog continuous-value neuron models. Digital spike-based computation offers robustness, scalability, and straightforward integration with standard chip fabrication flows for embedded neuromorphic systems.
