## Definition
The process of mapping the physical spin-lattice topology of disordered magnets onto interconnect architectures to emulate quantum critical dynamics in network hardware. Bridges quantum many-body physics with TCC, enabling the design of interconnect topologies that mimic spin-glass criticality for advanced in-network processing and memory.
