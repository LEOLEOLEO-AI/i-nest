**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A non-volatile memory element used as a synaptic weight in neuromorphic circuits, whose conductance can be precisely modulated by applied voltage or current pulses. Serves as the physical substrate for implementing STDP and dense synaptic connectivity in iNEST, enabling energy-efficient, in-memory computing within the network.
