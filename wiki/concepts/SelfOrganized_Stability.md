**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The property of a neuromorphic network to autonomously adjust its internal coupling strengths or activity patterns to preserve global stability in response to local disturbances. Enables in-network adaptation that mimics biological resilience, critical for energy-efficient and fault-tolerant neuromorphic hardware.
