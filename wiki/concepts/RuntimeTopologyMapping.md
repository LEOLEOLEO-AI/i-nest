# RuntimeTopologyMapping

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of dynamically assigning logical neural network connections to physical interconnect links at runtime, based on current workload requirements and resource availability. This enables adaptive reconfiguration of the hardware to match evolving SNN structures, a key enabler for flexible, topology-centric computing in heterogeneous environments.
