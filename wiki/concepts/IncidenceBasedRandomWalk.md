# IncidenceBasedRandomWalk

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A random walk process defined on the incidence structure of a simplicial complex, where transitions occur between simplices of adjacent dimensions via their incidence relations. Provides a principled way to explore higher-order topological structures, enabling TCC/iNEST systems to process data beyond pairwise interactions.
