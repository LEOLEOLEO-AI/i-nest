## Definition
A principle stating that the benefit of modular architectures in continual learning depends on the intrinsic dimensionality of the task data, with modularity helping only when dimensionality is sufficiently high. Guides the design of adaptive topology in neuromorphic systems, where the network structure can be reconfigured based on input dimensionality to optimize continual learning performance.
