# SpikingNeuralNetwork

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-22

## Definition
A spiking neural network (SNN) is a neuromorphic computing model that processes information through discrete, event-driven spikes rather than continuous activations. SNNs provide the temporal, event-driven computation substrate that makes in-network neuromorphic processing and TCC-compatible sparse communication feasible.
