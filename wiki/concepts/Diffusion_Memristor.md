# Diffusion_Memristor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A memristor device that emulates biological ion diffusion dynamics instead of relying on electrothermal phase transitions. Enables low-power implementation of edge-of-chaos neural dynamics, closely mimicking biological ion kinetics in neuromorphic circuits.
