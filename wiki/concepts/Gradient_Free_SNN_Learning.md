# Gradient_Free_SNN_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning approach for spiking networks that does not rely on surrogate gradients or backpropagation through time, using instead direct parameter reconstruction. Reduces computational overhead and hardware complexity, enabling low-power, real-time learning in edge neuromorphic devices.
