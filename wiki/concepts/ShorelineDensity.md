# ShorelineDensity

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-24

## Definition
The achievable optical I/O bandwidth per millimeter of chip edge, cited as 2 Tb/s/mm in imec's OIO architectures. It is a key constraint and optimization metric for topology-centric computing because edge bandwidth density determines feasible inter-node connectivity and network topologies.
