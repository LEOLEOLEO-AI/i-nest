## Definition
The property of a spiking neural network that allows it to be effectively trained to converge when scaled to many layers, often enabled by architectural innovations like residual connections. This is a fundamental challenge in iNEST, as deep SNNs are needed for high-level cognitive tasks but suffer from training instability and gradient degradation.
