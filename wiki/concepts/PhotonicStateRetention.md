# PhotonicStateRetention

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The ability to store optical information in a vapor medium without converting to electronic signals, preserving phase and amplitude for subsequent processing. Supports topology-centric computing by allowing optical data to be buffered and rerouted within network nodes, reducing optoelectronic conversion bottlenecks.
