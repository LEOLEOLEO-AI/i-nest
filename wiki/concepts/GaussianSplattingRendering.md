# GaussianSplattingRendering

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method for real-time radiance field rendering that represents scenes as collections of 3D Gaussian primitives with learnable parameters, projected and rasterized directly to pixels. Provides a highly parallelizable, topology-friendly rendering primitive that could be mapped onto in-network or neuromorphic hardware for low-latency visual processing.
