# Bipartite_Interconnect_Fabric

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A physical or logical network layout where compute nodes and memory/control nodes form two separate partitions, with all communication crossing between partitions. Relevant to both TCC and iNEST because it offers a clean separation of data-plane and control-plane traffic, improving isolation and fault containment.
