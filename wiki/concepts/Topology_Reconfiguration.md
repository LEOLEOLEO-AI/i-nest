# Topology_Reconfiguration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The ability to dynamically alter the physical interconnect topology of a chip (e.g., wafer-scale links) in microseconds, as opposed to fixed wiring determined at fabrication time. This shifts the core optimization problem from 'given a fixed network, optimize traffic flow' to 'given traffic patterns, optimize the network itself', enabling unprecedented adaptability in TCC architectures.
