## Definition
A memristor design framework that uses molecular-level control of metal-organic complexes to achieve predictable electrical behavior and reconfigurable neuromorphic functions. It overcomes the precision, consistency, and functional limitations of traditional inorganic memristors for large-scale neural network applications.
