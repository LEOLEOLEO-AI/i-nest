# MembranePotentialBeliefState

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A framework where the membrane potential of spiking neurons is interpreted as a probabilistic belief state over the input point cloud, enabling anytime recognition. This bridges neuromorphic computation with Bayesian inference, allowing spiking networks to provide progressively refined 3D recognition without fixed inference deadlines.
