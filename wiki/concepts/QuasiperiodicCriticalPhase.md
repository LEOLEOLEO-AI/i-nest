## Definition
A phase of matter in quasiperiodic systems where the system exhibits critical behavior (e.g., multifractal wavefunctions) without translational symmetry, often between localized and delocalized regimes. Understanding these phases can guide the engineering of in-network computing substrates that leverage criticality for enhanced information processing and memory.
