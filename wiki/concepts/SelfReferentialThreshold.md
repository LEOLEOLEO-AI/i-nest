## Definition
The critical threshold in a large language model's self-referential processing where it can reliably evaluate and modify its own reasoning without external supervision. This threshold determines whether recursive self-improvement is feasible in iNEST architectures, as crossing it enables autonomous learning loops that could be embedded in network topology.
