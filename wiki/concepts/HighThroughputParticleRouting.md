## Definition
A method for directing large numbers of bioparticles along predefined photonic pathways using integrated optical tweezers, analogous to packet routing in networks. Demonstrates a routing principle that can be abstracted for TCC, where data or physical entities are steered through a network using optical forces, inspiring new forms of in-network control.
