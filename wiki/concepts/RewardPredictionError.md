# RewardPredictionError

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A neural signal, often encoded by dopamine, that represents the difference between expected and actual reward and drives learning only when outcomes deviate from predictions. It offers a concrete, spike-based learning signal that can be mapped directly onto asynchronous neuromorphic circuits for in-network adaptation.
