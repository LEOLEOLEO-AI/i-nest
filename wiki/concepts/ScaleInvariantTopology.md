# ScaleInvariantTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A network topology that exhibits self-similarity across different scales, where sub-structures mirror the global structure, often associated with discrete scale invariance. Enables the design of modular, hierarchical computing architectures that can efficiently process information at multiple resolutions, a key requirement for scalable TCC systems.
