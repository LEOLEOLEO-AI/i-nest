**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neural network architecture where connections are restricted to a band of neighboring nodes, mimicking local synaptic connectivity in biological brains. Reduces hardware implementation complexity in neuromorphic chips while preserving essential dynamics, making it a practical design choice for scalable iNEST systems.
