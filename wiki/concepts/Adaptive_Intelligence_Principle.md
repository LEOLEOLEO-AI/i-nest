# Adaptive_Intelligence_Principle

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A theoretical framework positing that intelligence emerges from the ability to flexibly access a repertoire of computations, not from the computations themselves. Provides a unifying goal for TCC and iNEST: optimize access paths rather than compute kernels, guiding architecture design toward dynamic connectivity.
