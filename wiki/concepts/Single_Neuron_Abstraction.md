# Single_Neuron_Abstraction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The computational principle that a single neuron, through its intrinsic dynamics, can execute abstract reasoning operations such as ranking or sorting inputs without requiring multi-neuron circuits. This motivates iNEST designs where individual neuro-synaptic elements encapsulate high-level cognitive primitives for efficient in-network decision-making.
