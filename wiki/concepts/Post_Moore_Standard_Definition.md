## Definition
The process of establishing new interface, packaging, and interconnect standards for heterogeneous 3D systems that replace traditional 2D transistor scaling as the primary performance driver. Critical for TCC because it codifies the physical and logical rules that enable interoperable chiplets and topology-aware architectures to achieve system-level performance gains beyond Moore's Law.
