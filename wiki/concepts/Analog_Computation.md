# Analog_Computation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-07-30

## Definition
Computing using continuous physical quantities (e.g., conductance, voltage) rather than discrete digital logic, often realized in memristor crossbars for vector-matrix multiplication. It provides natural support for neural network operations in the analog domain, offering high throughput and low energy per operation essential for scalable TCC/iNEST hardware.
