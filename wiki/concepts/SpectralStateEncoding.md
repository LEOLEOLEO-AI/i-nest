# SpectralStateEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of representing reservoir states as spectral coefficients or frequency components, allowing linear readout in the frequency domain. It simplifies the readout layer and can improve computational efficiency in in-network neuromorphic processors.
