**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The concept that habituation mechanisms can be generalized and implemented across different physical substrates (e.g., biological, silicon, photonic) and at various scales (from single neurons to networks). Enables the transfer of habituation-based algorithms from biological inspiration to engineered TCC/iNEST hardware, facilitating scalable and robust neuromorphic architectures.
