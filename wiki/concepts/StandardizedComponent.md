## Definition
Each of the four factors (λ_eff, Φ, Ψ, Θ) in the Tc formula is normalized to a [0,1] range to ensure uniform contribution to the criticality metric. Standardization allows fair comparison and combination of diverse network properties, crucial for robust TCC system design across different topologies.
