# SymmetricPlasticityFree

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A learning regime that abandons the assumption of matched potentiation and depression (symmetric STDP windows or balanced LTP/LTD) as a prerequisite for producing ordered forward and reverse replay. Relaxing the symmetry constraint lowers the precision and device-matching burden on TCC topologies and iNEST synaptic hardware, since asymmetric or unidirectional weight updates suffice for replay-based learning.
