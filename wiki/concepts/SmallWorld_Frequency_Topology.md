## Definition
A brain-inspired memristor network topology with local dense connectivity for high-frequency signals and long-range sparse connectivity for low-frequency signals. Enables automatic learning of input frequency characteristics without external control, mimicking cortical oscillatory processing in neuromorphic interconnects.
