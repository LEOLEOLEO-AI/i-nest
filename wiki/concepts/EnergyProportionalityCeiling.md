# EnergyProportionalityCeiling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The maximum extent to which a neuromorphic system's energy consumption scales proportionally with actual neural activity, limited by non-activity-dependent components. Guides TCC and iNEST hardware design toward more activity-proportional energy profiles, essential for realizing the promise of event-driven computing.
