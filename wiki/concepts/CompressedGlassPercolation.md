**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The specific percolation process of high-density clusters within a low-density glass matrix under increasing pressure, leading to a system-spanning rigid network. Enables tuning of macroscopic transport and mechanical properties via pressure, which can be mapped to synaptic weight plasticity in neuromorphic hardware.
