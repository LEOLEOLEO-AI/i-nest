# Wafer_Scale_CGRA_Fabric

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A monolithic or die-bonded wafer-level integration in which many CGRA tiles are tiled across a full wafer and linked by a hierarchical on-wafer interconnect network. Wafer-scale tiling removes chip-boundary bandwidth limits, making it the physical platform where TCC topology design and iNEST in-network processing converge.
