**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A scaling relationship between communication delay magnitude and the effective time constant of the system's dynamical evolution, preserving qualitative behavior while stretching the temporal axis. This concept provides a predictive framework for TCC/iNEST engineers to estimate how network-induced delays will impact system performance without requiring full re-simulation.
