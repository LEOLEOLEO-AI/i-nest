## Definition
The translation of EEG-derived connectivity or dynamics into parameters for spiking neural networks or neuromorphic hardware, such as synaptic delays or firing thresholds. Creates a direct pipeline from clinical electrophysiology to TCC/iNEST implementations, enabling brain-inspired computing that reflects real pathological states.
