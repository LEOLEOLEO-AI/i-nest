# RareEventCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A coding scheme where information is conveyed by the precise timing and co-activation of sparse neuronal ensembles, rather than by firing rates or dense population codes. Reduces synaptic and metabolic overhead, making it suitable for in-network neuromorphic systems with limited bandwidth and power budgets.
