## Definition
A network model where edges or nodes dynamically switch between competing interaction types (e.g., cooperative vs. inhibitory) based on local state or global conditions. Enables topology to self-organize under load, critical for reconfigurable neuromorphic substrates that must balance excitation and inhibition.
