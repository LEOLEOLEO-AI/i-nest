# Brain_Inspired_Switchless_Architecture

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The principle that biological neural networks contain no centralized switches, and thus TCC/iNEST architectures should mimic this by distributing routing and switching within each processing element. Provides a theoretical justification for switchless design, aligning TCC with neuromorphic principles and guiding future iNEST hardware that emulates cortical connectivity.
