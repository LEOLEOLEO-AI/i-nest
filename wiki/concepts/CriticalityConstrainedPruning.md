## Definition
An iterative pruning method that explicitly preserves connections deemed 'critical' based on a criticality threshold derived from the combined importance score, while removing less critical ones. Provides a principled way to maintain network functionality during aggressive compression, ensuring that pruning does not disrupt the spiking dynamics critical for neuromorphic computing.
