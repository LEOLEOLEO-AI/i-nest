# Spike_Rate_Encoding_Fidelity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The degree to which the spike rate output of an SNN accurately represents the continuous-valued activations of the original ANN, especially under dynamic input conditions. This is critical for iNEST because low fidelity in rate encoding directly causes the error amplification described, affecting the reliability of neuromorphic control in real-world applications.
