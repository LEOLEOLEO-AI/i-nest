# AnalogMemristiveSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
A spiking neural network implementation in which memristive devices store synaptic weights and both vector-matrix multiplication and readout remain in the analog domain. This architecture enables dense, nonvolatile in-memory synaptic computation with low latency and power, aligning with iNEST goals for in-network neuromorphic processing.
