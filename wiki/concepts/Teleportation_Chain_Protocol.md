# Teleportation_Chain_Protocol

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of transferring quantum states across multiple nodes via sequential entanglement teleportation, where each hop preserves the locality of the original state. This protocol informs neuromorphic computing architectures by providing a blueprint for low-latency, locality-preserving information transfer in neural-inspired networks.
