# SelfOrganization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process by which a system's global order or structure emerges from local interactions without external control, often driven by energy minimization or entropy production. Underpins the development of adaptive, fault-tolerant neuromorphic architectures that can reconfigure themselves in response to stimuli or damage.
