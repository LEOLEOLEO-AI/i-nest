**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The arrangement and interconnection of chiplets or dies directly on a wafer, leveraging topological patterns to optimize signal paths and thermal distribution. Enables scalable, high-density integration for TCC architectures by reducing inter-chip latency and enabling novel 3D topologies.
