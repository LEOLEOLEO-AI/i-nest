# Time_Delayed_Coupling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A coupling mechanism where the state of one node at time t influences another node at a later time t+τ, introducing explicit temporal delays into the network dynamics. Critical for modeling realistic physical interconnects and neuromorphic hardware where signal propagation latency is non-negligible, enabling temporal computation and memory effects in TCC architectures.
