# HypergraphRumorPropagation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A model of rumor spreading where interactions occur among groups of nodes (hyperedges) rather than pairwise links, capturing higher-order social dynamics. Provides a framework for designing distributed consensus or information-spreading algorithms in network-on-chip and neuromorphic systems where multi-node interactions are common.
