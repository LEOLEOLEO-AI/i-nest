**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The gradient approximation method used to backpropagate through the non-differentiable spike generation process in SNNs, often via surrogate gradients or spike-timing-dependent plasticity. This is fundamental for training and attacking SNNs, and its fidelity directly impacts the transferability of adversarial perturbations in iNEST architectures.
