# PlanarNetworkSimulation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A computational method for producing planar graphs that mimic the geometric and topological constraints of crack patterns, ensuring no edge crossings. Enables the creation of physically plausible, non-crossing network topologies that are essential for efficient routing and layout in topology-centric computing systems.
