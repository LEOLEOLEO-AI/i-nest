# Synaptic_Polarity_Annotation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
The classification of each connectome edge as excitatory or inhibitory (e.g., cholinergic, GABAergic, glutamatergic) inferred from transmitter prediction, converting an undirected wiring graph into a signed, directionally meaningful network. Signed edges are essential for iNEST in-network computation, since excitatory/inhibitory balance determines stability, oscillation and routing behavior in any neuromorphic mapping of the connectome.
