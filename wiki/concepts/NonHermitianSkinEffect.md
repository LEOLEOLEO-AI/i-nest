## Definition
The accumulation of eigenstates at boundaries in non-Hermitian systems due to non-reciprocal hopping, leading to a directional localization of modes. Can be exploited for directional signal routing and edge computing in neuromorphic networks, enhancing the efficiency of in-network processing.
