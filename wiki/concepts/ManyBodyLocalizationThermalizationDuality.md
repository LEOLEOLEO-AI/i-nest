# ManyBodyLocalizationThermalizationDuality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The principle that localized and thermalized phases in disordered quantum systems are two manifestations of the same emergent replica-clock dynamics, rather than distinct fundamental phenomena. Enables design of adaptive TCC/iNEST architectures that can switch between stable (localized) and highly interactive (thermalized) computational states via clock manipulation.
