# DeviceConductanceTuning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of precisely programming the conductance states of analog memory devices (e.g., memristors or ferroelectric cells) to represent synaptic weights with minimal error. High-precision tuning is essential for reducing inference error in TCC-based neuromorphic accelerators, enabling practical deployment in edge and data-center applications.
