# 2_5D_PIM_Chiplet_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A packaging approach where processing-in-memory (PIM) chiplets are integrated on an interposer with shared power delivery and communication infrastructure. Provides a scalable and modular way to combine compute and memory chiplets, but introduces unique PDN and thermal challenges that must be addressed for TCC systems.
