# Switchless_Dragonfly_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A network topology inspired by Dragonfly but implemented without external switches, where inter-node links are directly managed by embedded micro-routers on the wafer. Demonstrates a practical switchless design on wafers, showing how to achieve high bisection bandwidth while eliminating switch ASIC bottlenecks.
