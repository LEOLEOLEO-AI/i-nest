# StructuralConstraintAwarePlasticity

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A plasticity mechanism that adjusts synaptic weights only within the allowable connectivity patterns defined by the underlying network topology, rather than assuming full connectivity. Aligns learning dynamics with real hardware interconnect limitations, ensuring that trained models are directly implementable on TCC architectures without post-hoc rewiring.
