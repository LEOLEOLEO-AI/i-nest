# Dielet_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The assembly of multiple small chiplets (dielets) on a wafer or interposer, where the topology of their connections is explicitly designed to match the computational graph. Allows flexible, modular construction of large-scale topological networks while maintaining high yield and customizing connectivity patterns for specific TCC workloads.
