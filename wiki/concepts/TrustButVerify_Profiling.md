# TrustButVerify_Profiling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A rigorous measurement methodology that accepts a system's advertised capabilities only after independently and reproducibly profiling its actual behavior under load. Provides the validation discipline needed before topology claims about wafer-scale or in-network neuromorphic hardware are used to justify architectural decisions.
