# IrreversibilityFlow

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The directional propagation of entropy production in a Markov chain, quantified by the divergence between forward and backward transition probabilities along a path. Enables the design of directed interconnects that enforce non-reciprocal signal flow, improving fault isolation and energy efficiency in topology-centric computing.
