# EventDrivenTrajectoryEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A scheme that represents continuous motion paths as discrete spike events, triggered by changes in polar coordinates rather than fixed time steps. Reduces energy and bandwidth usage in neuromorphic systems, aligning with iNEST's goal of efficient in-network processing.
