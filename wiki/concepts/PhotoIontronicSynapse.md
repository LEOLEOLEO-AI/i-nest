## Definition
A synaptic device that uses light-induced ion migration or photochemical reactions to modulate synaptic weight, enabling optical control of neuromorphic functions. Enables energy-efficient, high-bandwidth optical inputs for neuromorphic vision systems, directly supporting iNEST's goal of in-network sensory processing.
