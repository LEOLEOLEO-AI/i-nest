## Definition
Chip-on-Package-on-Substrate (CoPoS) is a TSMC advanced chiplet integration method that stacks chiplets on a package which is then mounted on a substrate, enabling high-density 3D interconnectivity. Enables scalable, high-bandwidth chiplet interconnects critical for TCC's topology-aware data movement and in-network processing.
