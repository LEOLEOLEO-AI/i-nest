**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A dataflow paradigm where computation is organized around the network's physical or logical topology, rather than fixed processing units. This reduces data movement bottlenecks and leverages network structure for parallel and distributed computation, critical for scalable TCC systems.
