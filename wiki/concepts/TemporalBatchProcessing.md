# TemporalBatchProcessing

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A simulation strategy that groups multiple timesteps into a single matrix operation, amortizing overhead and increasing data-level parallelism across time. This is important for iNEST as it maximizes utilization of SIMD and tensor cores, which is essential for achieving high simulation speed in large-scale SNNs.
