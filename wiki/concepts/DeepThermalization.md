# DeepThermalization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A process where a subsystem of a many-body quantum system relaxes to a state that is indistinguishable from a random state drawn from a suitable ensemble, beyond the standard thermal equilibrium. Provides a framework for understanding how quantum resources (e.g., entanglement) are distributed and measured in large-scale interconnected systems, relevant for designing TCC/iNEST architectures that rely on quantum coherence.
