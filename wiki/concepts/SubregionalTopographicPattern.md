# SubregionalTopographicPattern

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
DMN subregions exhibit distinct topographic patterns, with parahippocampal areas showing smooth gradient changes and prefrontal areas showing pronounced interdigitated or high-fluctuation arrangements. These local topographic differences imply that topology-centric computing architectures must encode region-specific spatial variability rather than assuming uniform cortical wiring.
