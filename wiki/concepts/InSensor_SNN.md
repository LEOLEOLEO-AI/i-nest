# InSensor_SNN

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture where optical sensing, spike encoding, and synaptic weight storage are co-located within the sensor array, performing in-sensor computing. This avoids sensor-to-memory-to-processor data movement, greatly reducing latency and energy consumption in edge neuromorphic vision systems.
