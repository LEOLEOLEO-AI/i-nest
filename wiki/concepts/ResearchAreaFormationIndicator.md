# ResearchAreaFormationIndicator

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A measurable signal derived from citation network topology that indicates the maturation of a nascent research field from a set of disjoint intellectual bases into a unified domain. For TCC/iNEST, this provides a quantitative way to track the emergence of new subfields (e.g., in-network neuromorphic computing) and to anticipate when foundational frameworks become stable enough for hardware/software co-design.
