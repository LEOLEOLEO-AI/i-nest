## Definition
The mathematical representation of the Core Spacetime Tensor (CST) as a product of spatial and temporal coupling factors multiplied by an exponential of the spacetime coupling coefficient, rather than a linear additive term. This exponential form is foundational to TCC theory, as it enables nonlinear amplification of spacetime interactions, which is critical for proving the '1+1>2' emergent property in topological computing.
