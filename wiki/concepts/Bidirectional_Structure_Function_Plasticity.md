**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The principle that anatomical structure constrains functional connectivity (SC→FC) while functional activity rewrites effective connectivity through plasticity (FC→SC). It motivates self-organizing, recurrently plastic neuromorphic substrates that can sustain near-critical dynamics and adaptive intelligence.
