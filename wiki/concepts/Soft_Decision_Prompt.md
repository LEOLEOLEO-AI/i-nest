# Soft_Decision_Prompt

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A trainable, continuous-valued prompt vector that modulates expert selection in spiking networks, unlike hard binary routing, allowing gradient-friendly dynamic fusion. Facilitates end-to-end training of dynamic routing in spiking networks, overcoming non-differentiability challenges inherent to spike-based communication.
