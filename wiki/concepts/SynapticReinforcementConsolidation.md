**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A mechanism that stabilizes learned synaptic weights during reinforcement learning to prevent catastrophic forgetting when adapting to new terrain conditions. Ensures long-term robustness of neuromorphic locomotion policies across varying operational contexts, a core challenge for deployable iNEST systems.
