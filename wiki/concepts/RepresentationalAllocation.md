# RepresentationalAllocation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process by which neural resources (neurons, synapses) are assigned to represent different inputs or features under constraints imposed by the network's physical topology. Critical for optimizing information capacity in TCC/iNEST systems where interconnect constraints dictate how efficiently representations can be stored and processed.
