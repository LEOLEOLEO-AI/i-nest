## Definition
The performance limitation in a compute-in-memory LLM accelerator caused by the chosen network-on-chip topology's bandwidth, latency, and connectivity constraints. It directly motivates TCC's focus on optimizing interconnect topology to relieve compute-in-memory inference bottlenecks.
