# SpatialPhotonicWeightUpdate

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The process of updating synaptic weights in a neuromorphic array by applying a spatially distributed optical pattern, where each pixel corresponds to a specific device's conductance change. This allows for simultaneous, localized tuning of many synaptic connections, accelerating learning and inference in photonic neural networks.
