# NeuralQuantumStateOptimization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of iteratively updating the parameters of a neural network-based wavefunction ansatz to minimize the variational energy of a quantum Hamiltonian. This optimization dynamics is central to making neural quantum states practical for simulating strongly correlated systems, which is a key target for TCC's in-network computational paradigms.
