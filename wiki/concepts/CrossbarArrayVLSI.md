# CrossbarArrayVLSI

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A VLSI layout of memristive or ferroelectric devices in a crossbar configuration, enabling analog vector-matrix multiplication and in-situ weight storage for neural networks. This is the core hardware fabric for TCC/iNEST, providing the dense, reconfigurable synaptic connectivity needed for spiking neural networks in wafer-scale or network-integrated systems.
