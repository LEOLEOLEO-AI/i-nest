**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The phenomenon where an inference system's performance abruptly changes at critical connectivity or information thresholds, mirroring phase transitions in evolutionary dynamics. Informs the design of neuromorphic hardware that can operate near critical points for maximal computational efficiency and adaptability.
