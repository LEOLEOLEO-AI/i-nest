# ComplexValuedPatternLearning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A learning paradigm where networks of oscillators are trained to store and recall patterns represented as complex-valued vectors, encoding both amplitude and phase information. Enables high-capacity, phase-sensitive memory and computation in neuromorphic and topology-centric computing substrates.
