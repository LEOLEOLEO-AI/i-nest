# SynapticFatigueModel

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A computational model that mimics the temporary reduction in synaptic transmission efficacy after high-frequency activity, implemented via memristor conductance decay. Adds temporal dynamics to neuromorphic systems, enabling adaptive behavior and preventing runaway excitation in recurrent network topologies.
