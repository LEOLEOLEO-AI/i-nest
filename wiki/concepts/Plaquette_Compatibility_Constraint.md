# Plaquette_Compatibility_Constraint

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A consistency condition requiring the ordered product of gauge links around each elementary network plaquette to match the identity or a specified flux. It enables elementary-loop checks for in-network fault detection and gauge-invariant routing in TCC and iNEST architectures.
