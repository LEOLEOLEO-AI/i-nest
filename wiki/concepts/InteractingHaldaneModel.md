## Definition
A tight-binding model on a honeycomb lattice with complex next-nearest-neighbor hopping (Haldane term) plus on-site or short-range electron-electron interactions, used to study correlated topological phases. It serves as a minimal theoretical framework for exploring how interactions modify topological invariants, relevant for designing quantum materials or simulators in TCC/iNEST architectures.
