# ConstructedRealityState

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A neuromorphic operational mode where the system's internal generative model overrides external sensory input, creating a self-sustaining belief state that can persist despite contradictory evidence. Critical for understanding and preventing pathological persistence in in-network neuromorphic processors, especially in closed-loop control applications.
