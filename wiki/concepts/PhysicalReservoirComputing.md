## Definition
A computational framework where a physical substrate (e.g., optogenetic synaptic array) acts as a fixed nonlinear reservoir, and only readout weights are trained. Bridges TCC's topology-driven design with iNEST's neuromorphic hardware, offering low-energy, high-speed temporal signal processing without full network training.
