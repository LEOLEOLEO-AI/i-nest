**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method for dynamically adjusting the bit-width of synaptic weights and activations during inference to match the precision requirements of individual neural network layers. Enables energy-efficient neuromorphic inference on edge devices by reducing memory bandwidth and compute overhead without sacrificing accuracy.
