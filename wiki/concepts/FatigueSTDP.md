# FatigueSTDP

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spike-timing-dependent plasticity learning rule that incorporates synaptic fatigue dynamics, where repeated stimulation reduces the efficacy of weight updates over time. Enables more biologically realistic learning in memristor-based SNNs, addressing device degradation and improving long-term stability in neuromorphic hardware.
