## Definition
Coordinate-Based Neuroevolution is a neuroevolution approach where genotype encodes network properties as functions of spatial coordinates, enabling indirect and scalable generation of topology. It aligns with TCC/iNEST goals of generating and optimizing network topologies from spatial or coordinate representations.
