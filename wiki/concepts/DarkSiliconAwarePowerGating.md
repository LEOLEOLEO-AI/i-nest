# DarkSiliconAwarePowerGating

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A hardware-level technique that selectively powers off idle cores or functional units to mitigate the dark silicon problem in manycore processors, where a significant fraction of the chip cannot be simultaneously active due to thermal and power constraints. This is central to TCC because it enables energy-proportional computing in manycore architectures, allowing topology-aware scheduling to exploit only the active regions while keeping the rest dark, thus optimizing both performance and thermal budget.
