## Definition
The concept that different microscopic spin Hall insulator systems share identical critical behavior at their phase transitions, belonging to the same universality class. This universality allows transfer of critical exponent results across material systems, potentially guiding design of neuromorphic devices in iNEST that exploit topological robustness for memory or computation.
