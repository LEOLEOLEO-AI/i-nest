# Halide_Perovskite_Synapse

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-05

## Definition
An artificial synaptic component based on halide perovskite materials like CsPbI₃, which emulate biological synaptic plasticity through ion-migration-mediated conductance changes. Halide perovskites offer promising analog switching properties for scalable neuromorphic systems, but their practical use depends on mitigating stochastic ion migration and thermal instability.
