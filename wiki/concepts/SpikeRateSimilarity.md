# SpikeRateSimilarity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A similarity metric computed from spike rates of spiking neurons in the tracker's output layer, used to localize the target within the search region. Provides a biologically plausible and hardware-friendly scoring method, replacing traditional correlation filters in neuromorphic tracking pipelines.
