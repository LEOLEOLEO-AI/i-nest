# LatencyCoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A neural coding scheme where information is encoded in the timing of the first spike, with earlier spikes representing higher values, enabling sparse and rapid computation. Reduces spike count and inference latency in neuromorphic systems, critical for real-time edge processing and energy-efficient in-network computation.
