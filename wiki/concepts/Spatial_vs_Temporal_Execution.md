# Spatial_vs_Temporal_Execution

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
An architectural distinction where spatial execution (CGRA) dedicates distinct hardware resources to concurrent operations across a fabric, while temporal execution (GPU) time-multiplexes a fixed datapath via SIMT instruction streams. Frames why topology-centric computing favors spatial mapping over instruction-driven temporal pipelines, motivating TCC's graph-to-fabric placement strategies.
