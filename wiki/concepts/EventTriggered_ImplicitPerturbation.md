**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A fine-tuning mechanism that applies implicit perturbations to spiking transformer parameters only when event-triggered conditions are met, avoiding explicit gradient computation. Enables low-overhead adaptation of neuromorphic spiking models in event-driven TCC and iNEST systems.
