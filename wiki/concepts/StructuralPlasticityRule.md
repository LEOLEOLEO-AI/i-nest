## Definition
A local update rule that governs the creation, elimination, or rewiring of synaptic connections based on recent activity statistics (e.g., firing rates, correlations). This provides a biologically inspired, distributed algorithm for topology evolution, directly applicable to in-network neuromorphic circuits that lack central control.
