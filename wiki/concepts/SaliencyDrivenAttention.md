## Definition
An attention mechanism that weights tokens according to a foveation map, concentrating compute on high-saliency regions while suppressing background tokens. This provides a biologically inspired prior that can guide topology design in TCC, reducing redundant connections and enabling scalable vision processing in resource-constrained networks.
