## Definition
Spike-timing-dependent plasticity implemented on memristor crossbar arrays, where synaptic weights are updated based on the relative timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes. Bridges neuromorphic learning rules with physical memristive devices, enabling on-chip, energy-efficient synaptic plasticity.
