# MultiSynapticSpikingNeuron

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A spiking neuron model with multiple synapses carrying distinct temporal dynamics to jointly encode spatial and temporal information. It overcomes the rate-coding versus temporal-precision tradeoff, enabling more efficient and biologically plausible neuromorphic processing.
