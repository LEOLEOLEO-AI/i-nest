**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A reliability mechanism that dynamically adjusts redundancy, retransmission, or error-correction strength based on LLM traffic semantics and network conditions. It balances fault tolerance and latency overhead, which is critical for real-time TCC-based LLM systems where over-reliable communication can harm performance.
