# Edge_Cut_Limitation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The known deficiency of classical edge-cut graph partitioning objectives, which underestimate the communication cost of one-to-many spike broadcasts and thus misguide SNN placement on NoCs. Identifying this limitation motivates topology-centric cost models that count multicast volume rather than only severed pairwise links.
