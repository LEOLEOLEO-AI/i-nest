# SuperdiffusiveAxonalGrowth

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A model of axonal growth where the displacement of the growth cone follows superdiffusive dynamics, characterized by a nonlinear scaling of mean squared displacement with time, often associated with Lévy flights or fractional Brownian motion. This concept provides a more realistic biophysical basis for neural network formation, enabling neuromorphic architectures that mimic the stochastic and long-range connectivity patterns of biological axons.
