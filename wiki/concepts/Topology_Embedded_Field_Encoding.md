# Topology_Embedded_Field_Encoding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of encoding neural field parameters or activations directly into network packet headers or switch metadata to propagate world-state information across the topology. Allows distributed world models to be updated and queried in-network, reducing latency and bandwidth for real-time adaptive control.
