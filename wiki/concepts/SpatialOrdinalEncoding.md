# SpatialOrdinalEncoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A technique that converts multivariate time-series from spatially distributed sensors or nodes into ordinal sequences based on the rank order of instantaneous phase values across space. Provides a robust, noise-resistant feature extraction method for analyzing spatiotemporal patterns in both TCC interconnect fabrics and iNEST neural architectures.
