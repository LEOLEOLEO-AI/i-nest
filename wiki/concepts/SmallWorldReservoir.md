**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A reservoir computing architecture where the recurrent neural network is constructed with ultra-small-world topology to enhance memory capacity and nonlinear dynamics. Offers a practical blueprint for in-network neuromorphic hardware that achieves high computational performance with minimal wiring and energy cost.
