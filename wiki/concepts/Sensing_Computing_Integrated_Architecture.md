# Sensing_Computing_Integrated_Architecture

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A hardware architecture that co-locates sensing elements and neural computation on a single chip, allowing direct transduction of physical signals into spiking neural processing. By eliminating separate sensor and processor stages, this architecture reduces latency and energy overhead, supporting tightly coupled perception–computation loops in iNEST systems.
