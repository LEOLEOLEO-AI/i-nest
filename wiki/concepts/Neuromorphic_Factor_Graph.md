# Neuromorphic_Factor_Graph

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A factor graph whose variable nodes and factor nodes are physically instantiated as spiking neurons and synaptic weight structures, so that edges correspond to in-network spike routes. It gives iNEST a concrete topology-centric mapping from graphical-model structure to physical neuron/synapse placement, making the computation layout itself the inference architecture.
