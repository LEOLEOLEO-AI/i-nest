# ChargeDomainAttention

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An attention mechanism that performs computations directly in the charge domain using nonvolatile ferroelectric capacitors, rather than in the voltage or current domain. Enables energy-efficient, in-memory attention computation for neuromorphic systems, reducing data movement and power consumption.
