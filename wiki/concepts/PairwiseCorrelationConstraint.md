# PairwiseCorrelationConstraint

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A constraint in maximum entropy models that matches the model's pairwise spike correlations to those empirically measured from neuronal populations, often sufficient to capture much of the collective behavior. Enables compact yet accurate modeling of neural population dynamics, which is essential for mapping biological circuits onto resource-limited neuromorphic hardware.
