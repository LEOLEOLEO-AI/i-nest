**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Encoding of associative memories in spatially distributed weight patterns across convolutional kernels, rather than dense global weights. Enables topology-centric data placement and parallel processing in network fabrics, facilitating distributed memory across compute nodes.
