# TopologicalDeepLearning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A framework that extends neural networks to operate on higher-order topological structures like simplicial complexes and cell complexes, not just graphs. Captures multi-scale connectivity and holes in network topologies, essential for TCC's fault-tolerant routing and iNEST's spike propagation patterns.
