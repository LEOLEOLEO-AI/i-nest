# DiscreteSTDP

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A simplified, event-driven form of Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity where synaptic weights are updated in discrete steps based on the relative timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes. Provides a computationally efficient learning rule suitable for hardware implementation in neuromorphic chips, enabling on-chip adaptation for place recognition without continuous weight updates.
