# PerovskiteSynapticWeightUpdate

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The mechanism by which the conductance state of a perovskite memristor is modulated by applied voltage pulses to represent synaptic weight changes. Demonstrates a practical, non-volatile, and analog weight update method that can be integrated into in-network neuromorphic circuits for continuous learning.
