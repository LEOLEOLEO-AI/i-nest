**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The process of mapping raw intracortical spike signals to intended motor or cognitive outputs using spiking neural networks, often in a closed-loop brain-machine interface. Core application domain for iNEST, demonstrating how neuromorphic computing can handle high-dimensional, time-varying neural data with low latency.
