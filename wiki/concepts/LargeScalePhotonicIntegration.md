**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The design and fabrication of photonic circuits with hundreds or thousands of optical components, requiring advanced layout and routing algorithms to manage complexity. Scales optical interconnect capacity to support wafer-scale chiplet topologies, a key enabler for TCC's vision of massive parallel compute and memory integration.
