# Glass_Crossover

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The dynamical transition regime separating fast ergodic relaxation from slow, cooperative, non-ergodic relaxation in disordered systems as temperature or density is varied. It provides a physical analogue for congestion-onset and synchronization-transition behavior in TCC and iNEST interconnect fabrics, where throughput regimes shift abruptly with load.
