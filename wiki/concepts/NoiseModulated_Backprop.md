**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method that reconstructs backpropagation gradients from forward-only fluctuations induced by noise modulation in neural networks, eliminating the need for explicit backward passes. Enables learning in neuromorphic hardware where bidirectional signal propagation is costly or infeasible, aligning with in-network processing constraints.
