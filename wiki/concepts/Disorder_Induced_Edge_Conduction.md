# Disorder_Induced_Edge_Conduction

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The emergence of conducting boundary channels on an otherwise trivial lattice when random potential disorder couples bulk and edge states to create metallic edge transport. This mechanism shows that controlled disorder can be exploited as a design knob to activate or reconfigure edge-conducting interconnects in TCC fabrics without changing lattice geometry.
