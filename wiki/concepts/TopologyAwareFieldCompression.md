# TopologyAwareFieldCompression

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method for compressing neural field parameters based on the connectivity structure of the underlying network, pruning or quantizing field coefficients along low-importance links. Optimizes storage and communication overhead in topology-centric computing, allowing large world models to be distributed across resource-constrained nodes.
