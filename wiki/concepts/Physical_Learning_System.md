# Physical_Learning_System

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
A physical learning system is a material or device assembly that performs training and inference through its own intrinsic dynamical and memory properties, without a digital simulation of the learning rule. This framing lets TCC treat the physical topology and the learning rule as a single coupled object, which is the prerequisite for topology-centric design of neuromorphic hardware.
