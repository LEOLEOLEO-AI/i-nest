# HeavyTailedWaitingTimes

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Waiting time distributions with power-law tails that lack a characteristic timescale, leading to memory effects and non-Markovian dynamics. Enables modeling of trapping and intermittency in spin transport, which is essential for designing robust memory or delay elements in TCC/iNEST architectures.
