# SynapticConsolidation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A mechanism inspired by biological memory consolidation that selectively strengthens or freezes synaptic weights to preserve previously learned action patterns during continual learning. Critical for balancing plasticity and stability in neuromorphic hardware, preventing overwriting of prior knowledge while allowing adaptation to new event-driven actions.
