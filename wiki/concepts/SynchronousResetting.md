# SynchronousResetting

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A mechanism where all neurons in a group reset their membrane potentials simultaneously at a defined event, aligning their firing dynamics and improving temporal precision. Provides a stable temporal reference for group activity, reducing spike timing jitter and enabling more reliable information encoding in neuromorphic circuits.
