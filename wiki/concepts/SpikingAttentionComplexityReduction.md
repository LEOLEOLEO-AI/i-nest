## Definition
A method that replaces dense global attention with sparse, locality-constrained attention patterns in SNNs, leveraging event-driven sparsity to cut computational and memory overhead. Directly addresses the O(N²) scaling issue in spiking transformers, making them feasible for real-time, low-power iNEST hardware implementations.
