# Lifelong_Learning_Capacity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The ability of a neural system to continually acquire new knowledge without overwriting previously learned representations. This capacity is a core requirement for in-network neuromorphic processors that must adapt to streaming data in real time, making plasticity management a key design constraint.
