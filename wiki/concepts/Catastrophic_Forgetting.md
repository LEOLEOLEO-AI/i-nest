# Catastrophic_Forgetting

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where a neural network trained sequentially on new tasks rapidly loses performance on previously learned tasks due to overwriting of shared weights. Understanding and mitigating catastrophic forgetting is essential for in-network neuromorphic processors that need to continuously learn from streaming data without retraining.
