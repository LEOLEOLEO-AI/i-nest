## Definition
A pruning technique specifically designed for low-bit spiking neural networks that removes redundant synapses while preserving the temporal dynamics of spike-based computation. Reduces memory footprint and computational load in neuromorphic chips, facilitating scalable in-network neuromorphic systems.
