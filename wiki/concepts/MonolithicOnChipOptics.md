# MonolithicOnChipOptics

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The integration of optical components (e.g., isolators, waveguides) directly onto the same silicon substrate as electronic or photonic logic, rather than using discrete or hybrid components. This reduces packaging overhead and latency, enabling tighter coupling between optical and electronic layers in TCC and iNEST architectures.
