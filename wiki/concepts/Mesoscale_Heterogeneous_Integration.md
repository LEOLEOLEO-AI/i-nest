## Definition
A system integration paradigm that combines diverse functional components (e.g., logic, memory, sensors) at an intermediate scale between chip and wafer, enabling heterogeneous material and process co-integration. This is the foundational architectural principle of iNEST, enabling TCC to leverage diverse computing elements (e.g., neuromorphic cores, chiplets) on a single wafer-scale platform for topology-aware dataflow.
