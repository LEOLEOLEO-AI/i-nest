## Definition
Approximately 90% of DMN cortex is eulaminate, with a significant overrepresentation of eulaminate-I (heteromodal cortex, ~18% increase) relative to other cortical types. This skewed composition suggests that TCC architectures should prioritize high-bandwidth, multi-modal integration hubs to mirror DMN's heteromodal dominance.
