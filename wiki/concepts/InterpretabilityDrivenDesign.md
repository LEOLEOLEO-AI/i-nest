# InterpretabilityDrivenDesign

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A design methodology where model architecture is chosen or constrained specifically to ensure that its internal representations and outputs are naturally human-understandable. For neuromorphic in-network computing, this approach can guide the design of spiking neural networks or reservoir computers to produce inherently interpretable state representations, enabling trust in edge devices.
