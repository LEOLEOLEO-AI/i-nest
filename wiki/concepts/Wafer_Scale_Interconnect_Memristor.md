## Definition
The integration of memristive devices within wafer-scale interconnect fabrics, combining TCC's topological routing with iNEST's neuromorphic memory elements. Enables dense, non-volatile, and adaptive synaptic-like connections across an entire wafer, critical for realizing large-scale brain-inspired computing.
