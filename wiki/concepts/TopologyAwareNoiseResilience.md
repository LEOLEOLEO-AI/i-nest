**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The design principle that network topology (connectivity structure) can be optimized to enhance resilience against random input noise without retraining. Enables TCC/iNEST co-design where topology itself becomes a noise-filtering mechanism, reducing need for error correction in neuromorphic circuits.
