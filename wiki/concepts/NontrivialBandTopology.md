# NontrivialBandTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A band structure with nonzero topological invariants (e.g., Chern numbers) that cannot be continuously deformed to a trivial atomic limit without closing the gap. Underpins the realization of protected edge or surface states in photonic or electronic lattices, which can be mapped to stable signal pathways in TCC interconnects.
