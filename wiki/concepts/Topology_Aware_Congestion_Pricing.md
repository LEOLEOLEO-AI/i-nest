## Definition
A routing mechanism that sets path or edge costs using topological features such as Forman-Ricci curvature to discourage traffic from congesting structurally critical network regions. It bridges TCC topology analysis with iNEST-style adaptive in-network routing by turning curvature signals into distributed congestion-control decisions.
