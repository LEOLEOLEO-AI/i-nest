## Definition
A recurrent neural network cell implemented with spiking neurons, where internal state is maintained via spike-based dynamics rather than continuous activations. Enables energy-efficient temporal processing in neuromorphic hardware, critical for iNEST's goal of in-network intelligence with low power budgets.
