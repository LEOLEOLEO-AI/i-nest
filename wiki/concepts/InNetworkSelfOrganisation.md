# InNetworkSelfOrganisation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The ability of network-embedded neuromorphic elements to collectively reorganize their synaptic and routing patterns in response to data flows, without external supervision. This is crucial for iNEST, enabling in-situ learning and adaptation within the network fabric, reducing latency and energy costs associated with off-chip training or control.
