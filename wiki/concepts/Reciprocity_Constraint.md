# Reciprocity_Constraint

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A symmetric coupling condition in mechanical networks where the interaction between any two nodes is equal in both directions, limiting the space of learnable input-output mappings. It identifies a fundamental physical symmetry that can halve learning capacity, guiding TCC and iNEST designs toward nonreciprocal interconnect strategies.
