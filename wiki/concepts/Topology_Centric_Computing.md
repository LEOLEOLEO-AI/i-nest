## Definition
A computing paradigm where the physical or logical topology of interconnects (e.g., mesh, torus, custom graphs) is the primary driver for data routing, processing, and memory access patterns. This is the core theoretical framework of TCC, optimizing for data locality and communication efficiency in distributed neuromorphic systems like iNEST.
