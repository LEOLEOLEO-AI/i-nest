# Connectome_Inspired_Neuromorphic

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A design methodology that derives neuromorphic computing architectures from empirically mapped connectomes, such as the fruit fly brain-ventral nerve cord, to emulate biologically validated computational motifs. This grounds iNEST hardware in real biological network topologies, offering proven solutions for distributed control and efficient sensory-motor integration in-network.
