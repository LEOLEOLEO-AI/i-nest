## Definition
A dynamical regime in recurrent neural networks where the network operates near the edge of stability, balancing between subcritical (decaying) and supercritical (explosive) activity propagation. Enables optimal information processing, memory retention, and computational capacity in neuromorphic systems, making it a target for in-network neural implementations.
