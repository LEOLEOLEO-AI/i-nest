# MeasurementEfficientProtocol

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A protocol that minimizes the number of measurements required to distinguish Majorana zero modes from trivial Andreev bound states under disorder, using adaptive or sparse sampling strategies. Reduces readout latency and energy overhead in topological quantum circuits, directly impacting the throughput of TCC-based interconnects and memory operations.
