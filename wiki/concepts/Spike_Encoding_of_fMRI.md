# Spike_Encoding_of_fMRI

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A technique that transforms fMRI BOLD signals into temporal spike trains, preserving both spatial and temporal dynamics for SNN processing. This enables efficient, event-driven processing of brain imaging data on neuromorphic hardware, reducing energy consumption and latency in semantic decoding tasks.
