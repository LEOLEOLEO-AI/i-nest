# ContrastiveFeatureAlignment

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A technique that pulls representations of similar samples from different domains closer together while pushing dissimilar ones apart, aligning feature spaces across domains. Facilitates robust feature extraction in TCC/iNEST, enabling topology-agnostic learning and reducing the need for domain-specific tuning.
