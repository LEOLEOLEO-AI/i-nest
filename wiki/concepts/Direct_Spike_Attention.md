# Direct_Spike_Attention

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A self-attention mechanism that operates directly on spike sequences without converting to dense floating-point representations, preserving the event-driven nature of SNNs. Allows transformers to be implemented in neuromorphic hardware with high efficiency, as it avoids expensive dense matrix multiplications and maintains sparsity.
