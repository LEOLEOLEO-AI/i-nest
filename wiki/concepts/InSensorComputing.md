# InSensorComputing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A hardware-software co-design approach where the sensor itself performs computational tasks (e.g., filtering, classification) directly on the sensed signal, often using memristive or ferroelectric crossbar arrays. Enables ultra-low-power, high-throughput AI inference at the point of sensing, reducing reliance on external processors and enabling distributed intelligence in flexible systems.
