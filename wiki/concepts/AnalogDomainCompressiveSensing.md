## Definition
A technique that performs compressive sensing directly in the analog domain using reservoir dynamics, bypassing the need for high-rate analog-to-digital converters. Enables energy-efficient, high-throughput sensing and reconstruction within network nodes, critical for scalable TCC data acquisition and processing.
