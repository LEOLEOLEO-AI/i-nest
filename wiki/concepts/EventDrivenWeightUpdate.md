# EventDrivenWeightUpdate

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning mechanism where synaptic weights are updated only upon the occurrence of spike events, rather than on a fixed clock cycle, aligning computation with neural activity. Minimizes unnecessary computation and power draw in iNEST systems, where sparse spike activity is the norm and energy efficiency is paramount.
