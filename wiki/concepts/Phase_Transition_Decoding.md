# Phase_Transition_Decoding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where decoder performance sharply changes at a critical error rate, separating a region of reliable decoding from one of failure, analogous to thermal phase transitions. Defines operational limits for TCC hardware, informing the design of error-resilient topologies and adaptive routing protocols.
