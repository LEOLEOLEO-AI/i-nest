# Physical_Reservoir_Computing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

## Definition
A computing paradigm in which the high-dimensional, nonlinear transient dynamics of a physical substrate—rather than a simulated recurrent neural network—serve as the untrained reservoir whose state is linearly read out. It grounds TCC/iNEST research by showing how the intrinsic topology and physics of a material or network substrate can be exploited as the computational core, decoupling reservoir richness from training cost.
