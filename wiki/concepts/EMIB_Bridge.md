## Definition
Embedded Multi-die Interconnect Bridge (EMIB) is Intel's approach using a small silicon bridge embedded in the package substrate to provide local high-density interconnects between adjacent chiplets. Offers a cost-effective, localized interconnect alternative for TCC architectures requiring selective high-bandwidth links without full 3D stacking.
