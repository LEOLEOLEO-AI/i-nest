**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Techniques that identify and eliminate redundant computations in deep models, such as channel pruning or weight sharing, to achieve speedup without significant performance loss. Reduces the computational footprint of models, making them feasible for deployment in resource-constrained in-network or neuromorphic environments where energy and latency are critical.
