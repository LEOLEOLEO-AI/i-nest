# NeuronModelAbstractionLayer

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An abstraction layer that defines core spiking neural network components, supporting classical neuron dynamics models such as LIF and Izhikevich, and spike-based synaptic rules like STDP. This layer acts as a 'translator' between neuron models and hardware architectures, enabling flexible mapping of diverse SNN dynamics onto neuromorphic substrates.
