# Biological_Neural_Circuit_Formalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of converting empirical biological neural circuit diagrams into formal mathematical structures (Petri nets) that preserve functional connectivity. Enables verification and simulation of neural circuits before hardware implementation, reducing errors in neuromorphic design.
