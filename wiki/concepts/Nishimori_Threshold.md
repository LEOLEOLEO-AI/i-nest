## Definition
A critical error rate in Bayesian inference and statistical physics below which optimal decoding is possible, derived from the Nishimori line condition. Provides a theoretical performance limit for topological error-correcting codes, guiding the design of reliable interconnect and memory architectures in TCC systems.
