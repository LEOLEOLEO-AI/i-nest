# WaferScaleMesoscopicNetwork

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A TCC framework treating a wafer-scale system of chiplets and interconnects as a complex network at the mesoscopic scale of semiconductor integration, not the microscopic scale of neurons or macroscopic scale of brain regions. This anchors TCC's scale reference and prevents the concept of mesoscale from being conflated with neuroscience brain-atlas mesoscale in policy documents and papers.
