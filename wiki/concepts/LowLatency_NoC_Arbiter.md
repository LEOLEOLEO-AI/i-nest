## Definition
A specialized arbitration mechanism in NoC routers that minimizes per-hop latency, often using speculative or lookahead techniques to reduce pipeline stages. Low-latency arbitration is a key enabler for TCC's tight coupling of compute and communication, where each hop's delay directly impacts overall system performance.
