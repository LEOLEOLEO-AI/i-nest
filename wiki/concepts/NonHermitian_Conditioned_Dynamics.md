## Definition
The effective evolution of a quantum trajectory between measurement outcomes, described by a non-Hermitian Hamiltonian that captures measurement backaction through gain and loss terms and renormalizes the state after each detection. Supplies iNEST with a physical mechanism for non-reciprocal, dissipative, and history-dependent signal propagation in neuromorphic hardware, where gain/loss asymmetry plays the role of synaptic weighting and leakage.
