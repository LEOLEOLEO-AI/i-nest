**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
Dynamical processes defined on simplicial complexes or hypergraphs, where state transitions depend on collective states of node subsets, not just pairwise edges. Enables modeling of complex collective behaviors in TCC architectures and neuromorphic circuits, where multiple processing elements or neurons interact simultaneously.
