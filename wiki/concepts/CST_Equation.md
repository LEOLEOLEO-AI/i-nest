## Definition
The Core Scaling Theory formula CST = (Sc · Tc) · exp(α · Γst) that relates computational scaling (Sc), topological complexity (Tc), and a coupling parameter α to the network's topological structure Γst. This equation formalizes how TCC systems scale with topology, but the discovery that α is device-physics-dependent rather than topology-dependent reveals a critical consistency issue in TCC design.
