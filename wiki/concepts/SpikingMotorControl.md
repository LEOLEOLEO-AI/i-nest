# SpikingMotorControl

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The use of spiking neural networks to map neural spike trains directly to motor commands for prosthetic limbs, bypassing traditional rate-based encoding. This enables energy-efficient, low-latency motor control that is essential for practical neuroprosthetic devices in iNEST architectures.
