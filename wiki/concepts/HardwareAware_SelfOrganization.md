**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A design principle where the network topology and unit parameters self-organize based on physical hardware constraints (e.g., memory, energy, bandwidth) rather than purely algorithmic objectives. Enables deployment of neuromorphic and topology-centric systems on resource-limited edge devices, bridging theoretical models with physical implementation.
