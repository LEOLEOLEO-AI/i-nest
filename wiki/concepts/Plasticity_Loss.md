# Plasticity_Loss

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The progressive decline in a neural network's ability to adapt to new information or tasks during training, often due to saturation of weights or loss of gradient signal. Understanding plasticity loss is critical for designing self-modifying or lifelong-learning systems in TCC/iNEST, where networks must continuously adapt to changing data distributions without catastrophic forgetting.
