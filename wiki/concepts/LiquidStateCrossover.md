## Definition
A sharp transition between two liquid phases in helium-4, triggered by quantum fluctuations, that changes the system's transport characteristics without a thermodynamic phase transition. Enables a novel computing primitive where information can be encoded in the state of a quantum fluid, potentially used for analog memory or thresholding in in-network processing.
