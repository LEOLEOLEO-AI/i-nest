**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Networks where nodes have different internal dynamics and are connected via communication links with non-uniform, potentially distinct time delays. Captures realistic conditions in distributed TCC/iNEST systems where compute nodes and links have varying latencies and dynamics, moving beyond idealized homogeneous models.
