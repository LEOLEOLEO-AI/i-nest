## Definition
The property that fairness metrics of a spiking neural network change when deployed on different neuromorphic hardware due to device-level noise, quantization, or energy constraints. Bridges iNEST hardware implementation with TCC's topology-aware optimization, showing that fairness must be evaluated at the physical layer, not just algorithmically.
