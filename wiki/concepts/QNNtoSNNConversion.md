# QNNtoSNNConversion

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of transforming a trained quantized neural network (QNN) into a spiking neural network (SNN) with equivalent functionality, often used to leverage mature ANN training while deploying on neuromorphic hardware. This bridges the gap between conventional deep learning and neuromorphic computing, enabling the reuse of existing models and training pipelines for TCC/iNEST systems.
