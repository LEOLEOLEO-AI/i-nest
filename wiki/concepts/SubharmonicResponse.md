## Definition
A system's output oscillating at a fraction of the driving frequency (e.g., half), indicating broken time-translation symmetry and stable period-doubling. Can be exploited for frequency division or clocking in neuromorphic hardware, providing a robust temporal reference for spike-timing-dependent plasticity.
