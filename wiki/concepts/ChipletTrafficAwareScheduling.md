# ChipletTrafficAwareScheduling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A scheduling policy that prioritizes compute relocation decisions based on the current inter-chiplet communication graph, including link utilization and hop counts. Shifts scheduling from purely compute-centric to topology-centric, a key principle of TCC for reducing latency and energy in data-intensive workloads.
