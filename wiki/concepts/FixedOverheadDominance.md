# FixedOverheadDominance

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The regime in spiking network energy models where static costs (e.g., memory access, leakage, communication setup) dominate over dynamic spike-driven costs, making further spike reduction ineffective. Highlights the need for TCC-aware co-design of interconnect and memory hierarchies to lower fixed overheads, enabling deeper sparsity benefits in iNEST.
