# Dynamic_Expert_Routing

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of routing input spikes to different expert subnetworks in real-time based on learned or prompt-driven policies, rather than fixed pathways. Critical for enabling task-adaptive and input-dependent processing in neuromorphic systems, improving generalization and reducing redundant computation.
