# EventDrivenWirelessTransmission

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A transmission scheme where spikes are sent only when neuron events occur, rather than on a fixed clock, reducing redundant wireless traffic. Minimizes wireless bandwidth and energy consumption, a core requirement for scalable in-network neuromorphic processing.
