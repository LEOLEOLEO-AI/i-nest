## Definition
A transformer model adapted to use spiking neural network (SNN) components, where neurons communicate via discrete spikes instead of continuous activations. Bridges the gap between transformer-based sequence learning and neuromorphic hardware efficiency, enabling energy-efficient temporal reasoning.
