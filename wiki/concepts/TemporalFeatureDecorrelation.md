**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The process of removing statistical correlations between temporal features extracted from a reservoir, ensuring each feature carries unique information about past inputs. This reduces redundant computations in neuromorphic cores, improving energy efficiency and throughput for real-time in-network processing tasks.
