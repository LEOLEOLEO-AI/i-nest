# BayesianNetworkFusion

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Bayesian network fusion is the process of merging multiple probabilistic graphical models into a single unified Bayesian network while preserving their conditional dependencies. It enables TCC and iNEST systems to combine distributed causal or topological models across nodes without centralizing raw data.
