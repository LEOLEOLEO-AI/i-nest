**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A specialized interconnect framework that integrates SDI (Software-Defined Interconnect) with iNEST (In-Network Neuromorphic) principles, allowing dynamic reconfiguration of network paths to support both conventional HPC traffic and neuromorphic spike-based communication. This hybrid interconnect is critical for TCC because it enables seamless co-existence of data-centric and event-driven computing, a key requirement for next-generation heterogeneous supercomputing systems.
