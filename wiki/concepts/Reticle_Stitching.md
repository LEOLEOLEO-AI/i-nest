# Reticle_Stitching

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A lithographic technique that creates wafer-scale systems larger than a single reticle field by seamlessly stitching together adjacent patterned regions. This enables wafer-scale integration in TCC and iNEST platforms such as Cerebras and BrainScaleS, allowing large-scale topologies and neural fabrics without chip boundaries.
