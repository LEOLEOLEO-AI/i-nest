# ImpurityQuantumCriticality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A quantum critical point induced by a local impurity (a single site or bond defect) in a many-body system, where the impurity drives the surrounding bulk into a critical state. Provides a minimal, controllable probe for studying quantum phase transitions and entanglement structure, relevant for designing topological or neuromorphic circuits with engineered defects.
