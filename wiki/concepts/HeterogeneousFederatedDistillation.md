# HeterogeneousFederatedDistillation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A federated learning framework where clients with different neural network types (ANN vs SNN) share distilled knowledge rather than raw model weights or gradients. Overcomes the fundamental incompatibility of ANN and SNN parameter spaces, allowing collaborative training across diverse neuromorphic and conventional hardware.
