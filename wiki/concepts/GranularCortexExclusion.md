## Definition
The DMN excludes the granular cortex type, meaning it lacks the densely granular layer IV typical of primary sensory areas. This exclusion informs TCC design by indicating that DMN-like networks should avoid fine-grained feedforward sensory processing and instead favor integrative, recurrent pathways.
