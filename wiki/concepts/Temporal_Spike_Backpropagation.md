**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A training technique that backpropagates errors through time in spiking transformer layers, accounting for spike timing and membrane potential dynamics. This allows effective training of deep spiking architectures like SpikeDecoder, overcoming the non-differentiability of spike functions for iNEST applications.
