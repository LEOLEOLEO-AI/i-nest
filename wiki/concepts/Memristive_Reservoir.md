# Memristive_Reservoir

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-20

## Definition
A physical reservoir built from memristive or spintronic device arrays whose intrinsic nonlinear switching dynamics and short-term plasticity supply the reservoir's state evolution. It links reservoir computing directly to in-network neuromorphic hardware, enabling co-location of memory and computation in the same device topology that TCC seeks to optimize.
