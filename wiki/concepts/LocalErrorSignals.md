# LocalErrorSignals

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Error signals computed at each neuron or layer using only presynaptic and postsynaptic activity, without requiring global loss gradients or cross-layer communication. Enables fully distributed and asynchronous training in in-network neuromorphic processors, avoiding the communication bottlenecks that plague centralized training in TCC architectures.
