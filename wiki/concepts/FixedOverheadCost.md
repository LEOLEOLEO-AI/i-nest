# FixedOverheadCost

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The constant energy expenditure per inference or per timestep that does not scale with spike count, including memory access, control logic, and communication setup. Sets the floor for energy consumption in TCC/iNEST architectures, meaning that ultra-sparse networks may not achieve expected energy savings unless overhead is also minimized.
