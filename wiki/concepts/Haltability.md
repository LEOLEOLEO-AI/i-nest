# Haltability

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A property of a network topology that allows computation to be paused or halted at specific nodes without disrupting the overall flow of information. Enables modular and reconfigurable computation in TCC, allowing for dynamic control of data flow in complex topologies.
