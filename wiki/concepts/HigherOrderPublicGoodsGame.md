## Definition
A game-theoretic model where payoffs depend on group interactions encoded as simplices (e.g., triangles) rather than pairwise edges, capturing multi-agent synergies. This framework provides a mathematical basis for understanding cooperation in multi-agent networks, which is critical for distributed decision-making in TCC and neuromorphic computing.
