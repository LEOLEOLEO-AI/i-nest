# LearningRule_Confound

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A systematic bias in model-brain comparisons caused by differences in evaluation resolution (e.g., layer granularity or ROI size) that can falsely attribute performance differences to learning rules rather than architectural or topological factors. Highlights the need for controlled evaluation protocols in TCC/iNEST, ensuring that observed advantages of specific learning rules (e.g., STDP) are not artifacts of measurement resolution.
