## Definition
Representational similarity analysis applied to compare the internal representations of an artificial neural network model against neural recordings from a biological brain region, such as early visual cortex. This provides a quantitative bridge between neuromorphic computing models and biological neural data, enabling validation of learning rules in TCC/iNEST architectures against cortical benchmarks.
