# Self_Organized_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
Self-organized topology describes a network whose connectivity graph is not fixed at fabrication but is continuously reshaped by the collective activity of its own nodes and edges. For TCC research it reframes topology as a dynamic, learnable variable rather than a static design constraint, enabling analysis of emergent connectivity as the carrier of computation.
