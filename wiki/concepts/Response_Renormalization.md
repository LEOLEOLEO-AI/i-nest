# Response_Renormalization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A renormalization-group procedure that rescales the linear response (susceptibility) of a deep equilibrium model's fixed point so that the effective Jacobian becomes scale-invariant with respect to network depth. Provides a principled mechanism for stabilizing implicit-depth computation, which maps directly onto how TCC topologies and iNEST in-network layers must self-normalize their signal gain at scale.
