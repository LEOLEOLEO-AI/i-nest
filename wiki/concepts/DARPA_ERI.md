## Definition
DARPA's Electronics Resurgence Initiative (ERI) is a research program funding novel materials, devices, and integration methods to overcome conventional CMOS scaling limits. Drives the emergence of new interconnect and packaging technologies that TCC/iNEST can exploit for low-latency, high-bandwidth in-network computation.
