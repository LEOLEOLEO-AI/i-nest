## Definition
A method for partitioning Service Function Chains (SFCs) into segments that are mapped onto network nodes, explicitly considering the sequential dependencies and ordering of virtual network functions. Enables efficient placement of chained functions across distributed topology nodes, directly impacting latency and resource utilization in TCC architectures.
