## Definition
A quantitative measure of the average number of downstream neurons activated by a single neuron's firing, with values above 1 indicating supercritical (chaotic) and below 1 indicating subcritical (stable) dynamics. This metric allows iNEST systems to tune and monitor network dynamics in real-time, enabling adaptive neuromorphic architectures that can switch between computational modes.
