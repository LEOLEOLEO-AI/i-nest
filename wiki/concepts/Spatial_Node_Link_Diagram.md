## Definition
A 3D node-link representation in which nodes and edges are assigned spatial positions and depth cues to support visual comparison of network structure. It offers a concrete rendering model for TCC to inspect interconnect topology and for iNEST to visualize spike-flow graphs and synaptic pathways in immersive environments.
