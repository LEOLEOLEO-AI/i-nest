# Cryogenic_Syndrome_Compression

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method for compressing quantum error correction (QEC) syndrome data at cryogenic temperatures before transmission to room-temperature decoders, reducing I/O bandwidth and heat load. Enables scalable QEC by minimizing the thermal and bandwidth bottleneck between cryogenic qubits and classical decoding hardware, a critical constraint for integrating neuromorphic or topological control systems.
