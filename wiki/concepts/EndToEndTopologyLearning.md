# EndToEndTopologyLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A training paradigm where the choice of topological representation (e.g., which simplices to include) is learned jointly with the network weights via backpropagation. Allows neuromorphic systems to adapt their internal topological structure to task demands, potentially improving efficiency and plasticity in in-network computing.
