## Definition
The measure of how resilient a spiking neural network is to adversarial examples, often compared against traditional artificial neural networks (ANNs) under similar attack budgets. Understanding this robustness is key for iNEST because neuromorphic systems are proposed for real-time, low-power edge applications where security against malicious inputs is a prerequisite for deployment.
