# EntropyMinimization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The principle that living systems actively resist the second law of thermodynamics by reducing their internal entropy, thereby maintaining ordered, non-equilibrium states. Guides the design of energy-efficient in-network computing architectures that maintain functional order despite noisy or fluctuating network conditions.
