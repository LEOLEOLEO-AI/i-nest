## Definition
A topological Anderson insulator phase that emerges in a non-Hermitian quasicrystal due to disorder-induced localization and non-Hermitian effects, coexisting with a multifractal critical phase. This concept bridges disorder, topology, and non-Hermiticity, offering new mechanisms for robust edge states in topological photonic or phononic networks relevant to TCC routing and iNEST signal processing.
