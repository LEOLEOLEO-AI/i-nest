# QuadraticSpeedup

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The theoretical improvement in sampling complexity achieved by quantum amplitude estimation, reducing the number of required samples from O(1/ε²) to O(1/ε). This speedup is a core motivation for integrating quantum or quantum-inspired estimators into TCC/iNEST pipelines for latency-sensitive routing decisions.
