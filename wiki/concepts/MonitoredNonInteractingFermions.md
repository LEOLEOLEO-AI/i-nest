# MonitoredNonInteractingFermions

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A theoretical model of non-interacting fermions in one dimension subjected to continuous projective measurements, used to study entanglement dynamics without particle-particle interactions. Provides a tractable platform to isolate measurement-induced effects from interaction-driven physics, crucial for understanding quantum state dynamics in topological or disordered systems.
