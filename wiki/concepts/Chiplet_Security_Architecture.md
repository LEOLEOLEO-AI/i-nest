## Definition
A hardware design approach that integrates security mechanisms at the chiplet level, treating each die as a trust boundary within a heterogeneous package. Enables scalable, modular security enforcement in TCC systems where chiplets are the fundamental building blocks, addressing trust in distributed on-package communication.
