# TopologyAwareTimescaleSeparation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design principle where the timescale of topology updates (e.g., rewiring or pruning) is explicitly separated from the timescale of weight updates, based on network structural properties. Prevents destructive interference between structural and parametric learning, enabling stable and efficient topology-centric computing.
