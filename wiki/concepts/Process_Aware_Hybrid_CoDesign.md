# Process_Aware_Hybrid_CoDesign

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A design methodology in which bitcell sizing, device selection, and tier partitioning are jointly optimised against the specific thermal-budget, alignment, and variability constraints of the target integration flow. Because fabrication limits bound which topologies are physically realisable, TCC and iNEST architecture search must couple graph-level topology choices to process-feasibility models rather than assuming an ideal interconnect fabric.
