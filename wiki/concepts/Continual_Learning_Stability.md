# Continual_Learning_Stability

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The property of a learning system to maintain performance on old tasks while integrating new information, often measured by the balance between plasticity and stability. Stability-plasticity balance determines whether a neuromorphic topology can serve as a lifelong learning substrate in dynamic network environments.
