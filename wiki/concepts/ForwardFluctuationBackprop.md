# ForwardFluctuationBackprop

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method to reconstruct backpropagation gradients from noise-induced forward fluctuations in neural networks, eliminating the need for explicit backward pass. Enables training in neuromorphic hardware where backward passes are costly or physically implausible, aligning with in-network computing constraints.
