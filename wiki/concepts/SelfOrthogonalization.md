**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The process by which attractor networks automatically adjust their synaptic weights to make stored patterns more orthogonal, minimizing crosstalk during recall. Enhances memory capacity and recall accuracy in neuromorphic systems without explicit supervision, critical for scalable in-network cognitive functions.
