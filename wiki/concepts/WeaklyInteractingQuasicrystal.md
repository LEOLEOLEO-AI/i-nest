## Definition
A two-dimensional quasicrystal system where electron-electron interactions are weak but still produce non-Fermi-liquid behavior due to the aperiodic lattice structure. Challenges conventional assumptions about Fermi-liquid theory in topologically nontrivial or aperiodic substrates, relevant for designing novel computing substrates with unconventional transport.
