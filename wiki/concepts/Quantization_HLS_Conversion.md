**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A deployment methodology that converts trained SNN parameters into fixed-point representations and uses high-level synthesis to generate FPGA hardware descriptions. Bridges the gap between software-trained neural networks and hardware acceleration, allowing efficient SNN inference on reconfigurable in-network platforms.
