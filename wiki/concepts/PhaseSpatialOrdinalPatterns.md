# PhaseSpatialOrdinalPatterns

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method that encodes the relative phase ordering of spatially distributed oscillatory signals into ordinal patterns to characterize spatiotemporal dynamics. Enables TCC and iNEST systems to extract topology-aware, low-complexity features from phase-coupled neuromorphic or in-network oscillatory data.
