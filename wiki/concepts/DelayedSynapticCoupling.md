# DelayedSynapticCoupling

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The explicit incorporation of transmission delays in synaptic connections, where the effect of one neuron on another occurs after a finite time lag. Essential for modeling realistic neural propagation and for designing neuromorphic hardware that leverages temporal coding and recurrent delay loops.
