# RepresentationCapacityMatchedTwinConstruction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method for constructing a spiking neural network (SNN) twin of a quantized neural network (QNN) by matching the representation capacity of the QNN's activation levels to the SNN's spike rate encoding resolution. This ensures that the SNN preserves the information fidelity of the original QNN, which is critical for deploying accurate neuromorphic models on resource-constrained hardware.
