# GradientReconstructionError

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The discrepancy between the true backpropagation gradient and the gradient estimated from forward fluctuations, often dependent on noise statistics. Quantifies the trade-off between learning accuracy and hardware simplicity, guiding noise design in TCC/iNEST implementations.
