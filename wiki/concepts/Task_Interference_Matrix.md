# Task_Interference_Matrix

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A representation of how learning on one task affects performance on other tasks, capturing both positive transfer and negative interference patterns. This matrix can inform topology-aware scheduling in TCC and synaptic gating in iNEST to minimize harmful interference during continual learning.
