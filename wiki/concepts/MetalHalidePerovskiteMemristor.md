# MetalHalidePerovskiteMemristor

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A memristive device using metal halide perovskites as the active switching material to emulate biological synaptic weight changes. Provides a low-cost, solution-processable hardware substrate for implementing synaptic plasticity in neuromorphic systems, relevant to iNEST's in-network synaptic devices.
