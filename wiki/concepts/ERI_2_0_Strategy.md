# ERI_2_0_Strategy

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
ERI 2.0 is DARPA's national strategic program (2023 onward) that funds disruptive microelectronics technologies, including advanced packaging, chiplets, and domain-specific computing, to sustain U.S. leadership beyond Moore's law. This strategy drives the development of packaging and interconnect standards that TCC and iNEST rely on for next-generation hardware, shaping research priorities and funding opportunities.
