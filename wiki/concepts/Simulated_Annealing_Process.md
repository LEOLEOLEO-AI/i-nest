**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A probabilistic global search method integrated into the platform that gradually lowers a temperature parameter to converge on optimal spin configurations. Mimics physical annealing processes and allows the Ising machine to solve graph/optimization problems relevant to TCC and iNEST.
