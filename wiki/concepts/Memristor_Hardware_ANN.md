## Definition
A hardware implementation of artificial neural networks using memristor devices as synaptic elements to perform analog in-memory computing. This enables energy-efficient, massively parallel neural network inference and training directly in hardware, bypassing the von Neumann bottleneck critical for TCC and iNEST systems.
