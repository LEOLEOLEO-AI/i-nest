# MeanFieldNetworkAnalysis

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A theoretical framework that approximates the collective behavior of a large spiking neural network by averaging over individual neuron dynamics, reducing high-dimensional interactions to a low-dimensional system of equations. Enables scalable prediction and control of emergent network states in neuromorphic hardware, crucial for designing robust and efficient in-network computing architectures.
