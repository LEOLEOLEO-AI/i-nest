# Bayesian_Inference_Decoding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A decoding method that uses posterior probability distributions over error configurations, often optimized via belief propagation or Markov chain Monte Carlo, to infer the most likely logical correction. Offers a principled framework for real-time error correction in TCC, balancing accuracy and latency for in-network processing.
