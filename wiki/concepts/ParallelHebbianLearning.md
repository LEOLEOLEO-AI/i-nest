# ParallelHebbianLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning rule where synaptic weights are updated simultaneously across many devices based on local activity correlations, without external supervision. Enables on-chip, real-time learning in neuromorphic hardware, reducing the need for off-chip training and improving energy efficiency.
