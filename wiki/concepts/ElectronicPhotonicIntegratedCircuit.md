# ElectronicPhotonicIntegratedCircuit

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A chip that monolithically or heterogeneously integrates electronic transistors and photonic components (e.g., modulators, detectors, waveguides) on a single substrate. Provides the foundational hardware for TCC and iNEST, combining electronic processing with optical communication for energy-efficient, high-throughput data exchange.
