## Definition
A hardened two-dimensional mesh network-on-chip fabric integrated directly into the Achronix Speedster7t FPGA die that connects compute, memory, and I/O tiles via dedicated non-programmable routing resources. It provides a fixed-topology benchmark substrate for studying how physical interconnect geometry governs bandwidth, latency, and congestion behavior in topology-centric computing architectures.
