## Definition
A technique to store and process synaptic weights in compressed matrix formats, exploiting sparsity and low-precision representations to reduce memory footprint and bandwidth. This matters for iNEST because it allows larger neural networks to fit into on-chip memory, improving energy efficiency and throughput in neuromorphic accelerators.
