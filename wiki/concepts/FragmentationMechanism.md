**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The mechanism by which simplicial closure breaks a single explosive transition into multiple smaller transitions, creating a staircase-like cooperation response. This mechanism offers a way to engineer gradual, controllable changes in collective behavior, which is valuable for neuromorphic systems requiring smooth adaptation and learning.
