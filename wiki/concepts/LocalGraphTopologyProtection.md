**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The property where the existence and flatness of bands are guaranteed solely by the local connectivity structure of the underlying graph, independent of hopping amplitudes. This guarantees robustness against parameter variations, making it ideal for hardware implementations where precise control is difficult, such as in photonic or electronic network substrates.
