# Readout_Weight_Training

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process of training only the output layer weights that map reservoir states to desired outputs, leaving the reservoir dynamics untrained. Minimizes learning overhead and hardware complexity, critical for scalable neuromorphic interconnects.
