# ClassicalSimulability

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The property of a quantum convolutional neural network (QCNN) that allows its output to be efficiently replicated by a classical algorithm, despite its quantum structure. This challenges the assumption that quantum-inspired network topologies inherently provide computational advantage, informing when TCC/iNEST architectures should rely on quantum vs. classical resources.
