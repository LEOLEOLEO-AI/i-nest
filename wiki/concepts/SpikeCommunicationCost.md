# SpikeCommunicationCost

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The energy and latency overhead incurred when transmitting spikes between neurons or across network nodes, which scales with connectivity and topology rather than just spike count. Critical for TCC because interconnect topology directly impacts this cost, making topology-aware spike routing essential for energy-efficient distributed spiking systems.
