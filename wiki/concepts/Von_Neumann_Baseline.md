# Von_Neumann_Baseline

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The classical 1945 stored-program computing paradigm characterized by serial instruction streams and separated memory, used as the first reference paradigm in the iNEST index. It establishes the baseline against which topology-centric and in-network neuromorphic approaches define their structural and operational divergence.
