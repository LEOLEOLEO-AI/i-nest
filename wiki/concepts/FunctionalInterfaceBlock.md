# FunctionalInterfaceBlock

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A modular hardware unit that encapsulates signal transformation, impedance matching, and spike-timing coordination at the interface between neuromorphic cores. These blocks enable standardized, reconfigurable interconnects, critical for scaling heterogeneous neuromorphic systems in iNEST and for topology-aware placement in TCC.
