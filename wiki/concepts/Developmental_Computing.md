**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A paradigm where computational capability emerges from the interaction of growth processes and activity-dependent refinement, rather than from a fixed pre-designed circuit. Offers a path to self-assembling neuromorphic hardware that can reconfigure itself for new tasks, reducing manual design overhead.
