# Wavefunction_Snapshot_Tomography

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A measurement protocol that reconstructs many-body quantum states or their correlation structure from repeated projective snapshots of the wavefunction rather than full state tomography. Provides a sampling-efficient readout primitive for extracting topological invariants and entanglement structure from quantum substrates used in TCC/iNEST physical-layer implementations.
