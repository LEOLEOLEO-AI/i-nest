# EventDrivenNeuromorphicLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning mechanism where synaptic updates and neural computations are triggered only by asynchronous spike events, rather than continuous or clocked processing. Enables energy-efficient, real-time processing in neuromorphic hardware, critical for scalable iNEST deployments in edge and in-network scenarios.
