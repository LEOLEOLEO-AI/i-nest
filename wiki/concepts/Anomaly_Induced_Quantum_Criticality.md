# Anomaly_Induced_Quantum_Criticality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A quantum critical point that emerges or is modified by the presence of a quantum anomaly, even under strong random disorder, where the anomaly constrains the renormalization group flow and protects universal scaling. Provides a new theoretical handle for designing robust topological quantum phase transitions in disordered, non-ideal hardware, relevant for TCC's fault-tolerant topology switching.
