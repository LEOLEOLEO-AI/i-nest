**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The process of altering a neuron's membrane potential through external current injection to control spike generation thresholds and timing for feature extraction. Allows fine-grained control over information encoding in SNNs, enabling adaptive fusion strategies that are robust to varying input conditions.
