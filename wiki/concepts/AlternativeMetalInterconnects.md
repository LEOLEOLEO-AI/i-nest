# AlternativeMetalInterconnects

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Use of metals other than copper (e.g., ruthenium, cobalt, molybdenum) for on-chip and advanced packaging interconnects to address resistivity scaling and electromigration issues. Critical for TCC as it enables higher-density, lower-latency interconnects in 3D-IC and TSV-based topologies, directly impacting signal integrity and power efficiency.
