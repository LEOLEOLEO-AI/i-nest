# Sparsity_Ceiling

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The theoretical limit beyond which further reducing spike activity in a spiking neural network yields no additional energy savings, because fixed costs (e.g., leakage, memory access) dominate dynamic spike-driven costs. Defines the fundamental energy-efficiency boundary for in-network neuromorphic hardware, guiding where activity-sparsity optimizations are worthwhile versus where they are futile.
