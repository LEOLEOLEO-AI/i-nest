# Electrolyte_Bonding_Engineering

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-20

## Definition
A materials engineering technique that optimizes ionic bonding at electrolyte-electrode interfaces to improve uniformity and switching reliability in phase-change memory cells. Enhances the endurance and consistency of synaptic devices, a prerequisite for reliable analog in-memory computing in neuromorphic systems.
