# TemporalWinnerTakeAll

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A competitive mechanism in SNNs where the neuron that spikes earliest (or with shortest latency) suppresses others, enforcing sparse and selective responses. Enhances coding efficiency and reduces redundant spikes, which is essential for low-latency decision-making in network-embedded neuromorphic nodes.
