# AsymmetricTimeDelayedInteractions

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A stochastic binary network model where pairwise interactions are asymmetric and include explicit time delays, breaking the symmetry and simultaneity assumptions of standard Hopfield-type networks. This captures realistic synaptic dynamics in neuromorphic hardware, enabling temporal coding and richer computational dynamics beyond symmetric attractor networks.
