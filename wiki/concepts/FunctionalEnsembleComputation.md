# FunctionalEnsembleComputation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A computational paradigm where groups of spiking neurons, rather than individual neurons, form stable functional ensembles that collectively encode and process information, with rare spike events carrying the primary signal. This reframes spiking network design away from dense, rate-based coding toward sparse, event-driven ensemble dynamics, which is critical for energy-efficient in-network neuromorphic processing.
