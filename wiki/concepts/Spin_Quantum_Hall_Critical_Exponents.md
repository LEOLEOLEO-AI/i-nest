**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The set of universal scaling exponents that characterize the quantum phase transition between distinct spin Hall insulating states, derived from the spin quantum Hall effect framework. These exponents provide a topological invariant-based classification of phase transitions, which can inform robust topological routing and fault-tolerant interconnect design in TCC architectures.
