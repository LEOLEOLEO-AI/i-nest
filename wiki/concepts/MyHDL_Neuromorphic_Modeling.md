## Definition
The use of MyHDL, a Python-based hardware description language, to design and synthesize neuromorphic components such as D flip-flops and asynchronous handshake blocks. Bridges high-level algorithmic design with hardware implementation, enabling rapid prototyping and verification of iNEST neural network modules.
