**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method that converts continuous gesture motion into asynchronous spike events, preserving temporal dynamics and reducing redundant data compared to frame-based encoding. Leverages the temporal sparsity of gestures to improve latency and energy efficiency in neuromorphic gesture understanding pipelines.
