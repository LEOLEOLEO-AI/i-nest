**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A design principle where the reservoir's state evolution is decomposed into distinct timescales for rotation and dissipation, allowing controlled memory and nonlinearity. Enables precise tuning of temporal dynamics in network architectures, critical for processing spatiotemporal signals in TCC systems.
