**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A quantum computing system composed of multiple smaller quantum modules interconnected via noisy interfaces, where operations must be designed to tolerate inter-module communication errors. Defines the physical and logical topology of distributed quantum systems, directly impacting how TCC interconnects and fault-tolerance strategies are designed.
