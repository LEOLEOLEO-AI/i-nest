# WaferLevelPackaging

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Encapsulation and packaging processes performed at the wafer scale before dicing, enabling batch processing of multiple dies simultaneously. Enables cost-effective and scalable fabrication of dense 3D-IC topologies, which are foundational for TCC architectures that rely on high-density vertical interconnects.
