# SpeciatedEvolutionaryCircuitSynthesis

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that applies speciation (niching) in evolutionary algorithms to synthesize circuit topologies, preserving diverse subpopulations of candidate circuits during optimization. Enables exploration of multiple distinct topology families in parallel, avoiding premature convergence to local optima in complex interconnect design spaces.
