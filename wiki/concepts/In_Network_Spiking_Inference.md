# In_Network_Spiking_Inference

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Computation performed inside the network fabric itself using spiking neural network primitives, so that synaptic accumulation and thresholding occur at intermediate nodes rather than at a central processor. It is the central iNEST mechanism that removes data movement bottlenecks for neuromorphic sensing and motivates TCC research into spike-aware network topologies.
