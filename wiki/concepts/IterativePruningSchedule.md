# IterativePruningSchedule

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A multi-step pruning process where connections are gradually removed over several training epochs, allowing the network to adapt and recover between pruning rounds. Improves the stability of SNN pruning compared to one-shot methods, leading to better energy-efficiency and robustness in neuromorphic implementations.
