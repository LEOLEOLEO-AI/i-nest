# Memristive_Device_Threats

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The inherent non-idealities and failure modes of nanoscale memristive devices, such as stochastic switching, endurance limits, and variability, that compromise their reliability in neuromorphic circuits. These threats directly impact the fidelity of synaptic weights and neuronal dynamics in iNEST systems, necessitating robust design strategies for in-network neuromorphic computing.
