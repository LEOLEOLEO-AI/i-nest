# WaferScaleHeterogeneousIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A three-layer architecture using a wafer-scale substrate, heterogeneous chiplets, and software-defined interconnect, rather than fabricating multiple functional chips directly on an 8-inch wafer. This enables scalable, high-density integration of diverse compute elements for TCC, decoupling chip design from wafer fabrication constraints.
