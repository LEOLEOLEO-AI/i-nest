# NonReciprocalCouplingSpectrum

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The spectral distribution arising from asymmetric coupling matrices (A_ij ≠ A_ji) in neuromorphic hardware, leading to complex eigenvalues and non-conservative dynamics. Directly informs the design of in-network learning rules and stability criteria for photonic or electronic neuromorphic substrates with directional signal flow.
