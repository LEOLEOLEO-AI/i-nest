## Definition
A computing system where the physical dynamics of hardware substrates, such as electrical or optical networks, directly implement neural network computations rather than simulating them in software. This is the core paradigm behind TCC, enabling computation to emerge from physical topology and interconnect behavior rather than von Neumann architecture.
