# MemoryStateEquilibria

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The set of stable intermediate conductance states that an analog filamentary ReRAM cell settles into under given bias conditions, defined by balance of filament drift and diffusion forces. Quantifying equilibrium states enables deterministic multi-bit weight storage, which is critical for accurate in-memory computing and neuromorphic state retention.
