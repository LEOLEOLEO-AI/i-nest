## Definition
An operator that modifies the boundary conditions of a partition function by introducing a twist along a defect line, capturing anyonic statistics. Serves as a key computational primitive for TCC to simulate and analyze non-Abelian statistics, which could inform fault-tolerant topological quantum computing.
