**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A dual-mode inference framework where the system performs both forward Bayesian inference (from observations to hidden states) and inverse Bayesian inference (from hidden states to observations) within the same computational substrate. This bidirectional inference capability is essential for TCC/iNEST systems to support both perception and generation tasks, enabling more versatile and autonomous neuromorphic architectures.
