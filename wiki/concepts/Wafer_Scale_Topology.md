**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The physical arrangement of neuromorphic cores and interconnects at the wafer level, forming the underlying communication topology. The wafer-scale topology determines the physical constraints for embedding TCC graphs, affecting latency, bandwidth, and scalability of computations.
