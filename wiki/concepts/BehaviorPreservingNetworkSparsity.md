## Definition
A sparsity-inducing strategy that maintains the neuron's firing behavior (e.g., firing rate and timing) as a constraint during pruning, ensuring functional equivalence post-compression. Guarantees that pruned SNNs retain their neuromorphic functionality, which is essential for reliable in-network inference tasks.
