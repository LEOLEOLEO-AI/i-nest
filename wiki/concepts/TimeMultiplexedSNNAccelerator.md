**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
An FPGA-based spiking neural network accelerator that time-multiplexes neuron computations across shared hardware resources to process multiple neurons sequentially. Enables efficient resource utilization on FPGAs for large SNN inference, critical for scalable neuromorphic deployment in network-edge devices.
