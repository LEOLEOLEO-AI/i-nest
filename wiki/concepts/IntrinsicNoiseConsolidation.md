# IntrinsicNoiseConsolidation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method that uses the inherent stochastic fluctuations of analog neuromorphic hardware as a consolidation mechanism, enabling the network to retain previously learned tasks without explicit replay or regularization. Turns a hardware limitation into a computational advantage, enabling energy-efficient and memory-efficient continual learning in physical neuromorphic systems.
