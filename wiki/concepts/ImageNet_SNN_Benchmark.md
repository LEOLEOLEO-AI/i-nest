## Definition
The use of the ImageNet dataset as a standard benchmark to evaluate the classification accuracy of spiking neural networks, often comparing against traditional ANN baselines. Provides a rigorous testbed for TCC/iNEST systems to prove that neuromorphic approaches can handle large-scale, real-world visual recognition tasks, not just toy datasets.
