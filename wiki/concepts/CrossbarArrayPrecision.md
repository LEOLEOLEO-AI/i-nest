# CrossbarArrayPrecision

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The achievable numerical accuracy of analog computations in crossbar arrays, limited by device conductance variability, programming errors, and read/write noise. Precision directly impacts the reliability and usability of in-memory accelerators in TCC systems, determining whether they can replace digital cores for real-world workloads.
