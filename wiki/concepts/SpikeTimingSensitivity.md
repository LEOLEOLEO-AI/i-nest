# SpikeTimingSensitivity

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The degree to which network output depends on precise spike arrival times, making TTFS networks highly sensitive to jitter and propagation delays. Informs topology design and interconnect scheduling in TCC, as deterministic low-jitter communication is essential for reliable TTFS-based computation across distributed neuromorphic nodes.
