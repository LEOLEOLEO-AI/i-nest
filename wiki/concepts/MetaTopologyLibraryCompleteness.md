# MetaTopologyLibraryCompleteness

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A meta-topology library is complete if it covers all six R-primitives with at least one skeleton each, and critical primitives (R.FUSE, R.SWAP) have at least two skeleton variants for optimization selection. This gives a formal, measurable definition of library completeness, replacing vague 'number of skeletons' claims with a primitive-coverage guarantee essential for reproducible TCC design.
