**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method of converting raw acoustic waveforms into sparse, asynchronous spike events that represent salient temporal features, reducing data volume and power consumption. Minimizes data transmission and processing overhead in network-centric architectures, making it feasible to run neuromorphic anomaly detection on resource-constrained edge devices.
