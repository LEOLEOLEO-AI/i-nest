# HeterogeneousInference

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A paradigm where large model inference is distributed across diverse hardware (e.g., CPU, NPU, FPGA) rather than relying solely on GPU, based on task-specific computational requirements. Challenges GPU-centric assumptions in TCC, motivating topology-aware scheduling and in-network compute for non-GPU accelerators.
