# Microsecond_Topology_Switch

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The ability of a chip to reconfigure its internal interconnect topology in microseconds, enabling dynamic 'shape-shifting' between different computational modes. This is central to TCC because it allows a single hardware substrate to rapidly adapt to training, inference, and signal processing workloads without the latency of traditional reprogramming.
