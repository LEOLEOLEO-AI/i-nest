# EventDriven_AsyncCircuit

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A hardware design paradigm where computations are triggered by asynchronous spike events rather than a global clock signal. This is essential for achieving the low-power, sparse, and real-time processing characteristics required for in-network neuromorphic computing.
