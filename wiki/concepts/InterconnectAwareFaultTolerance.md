## Definition
A design principle where fault-tolerance protocols are tailored to the specific interconnect topology and noise characteristics of the modular system, rather than assuming uniform connectivity. Bridges TCC and iNEST by emphasizing that communication infrastructure (whether quantum links or neuromorphic synapses) must be co-designed with error-correction and computation strategies.
