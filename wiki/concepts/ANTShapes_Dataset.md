## Definition
ANTShapes is a benchmarking dataset of event-based (spiking) visual stimuli designed for neuromorphic object classification tasks. It provides a standardized event-stream corpus for training and comparing spiking neural network classifiers, enabling reproducible evaluation in iNEST research.
