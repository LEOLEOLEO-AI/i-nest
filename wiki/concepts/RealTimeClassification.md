# RealTimeClassification

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability to produce classification outputs within strict time bounds (e.g., <100ms) to match the operational speed of agricultural machinery. Imposes strict latency constraints on TCC architectures, driving the need for optimized data paths and compute placement at the edge.
