# TTFS_Neural_Networks

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Spiking neural networks in which each neuron's output is encoded solely by the time it emits its first spike after stimulus onset. Provides an event-driven, latency-based computing substrate for in-network neuromorphic inference with minimal spike counts.
