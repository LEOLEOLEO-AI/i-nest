## Definition
The process of embedding inkjet-printed sensors directly onto network-relevant substrates (e.g., flexible PCBs, photonic packaging) to create co-located sensing and communication layers. Enables in-network sensing without separate bulky modules, aligning with TCC's goal of merging computation and physical data acquisition within the network fabric.
