# EventDrivenAttentionMasking

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism that dynamically masks attention based on spike timing and local event activity, allowing the network to focus only on recently active or spatially proximate neurons. Exploits the inherent sparsity of spike events to reduce computation, aligning with iNEST's goal of minimizing energy consumption in in-network processing.
