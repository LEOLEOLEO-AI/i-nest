# TimeDelayed_Synaptic_Coupling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A coupling mechanism where the effect of one binary neuron on another is delayed by a finite time interval, introducing temporal memory into the stochastic dynamics. Adds temporal depth to TCC/iNEST architectures, allowing the network to process sequential data and exhibit oscillatory or chaotic behaviors relevant for temporal pattern recognition.
