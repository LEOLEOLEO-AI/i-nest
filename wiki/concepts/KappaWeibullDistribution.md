## Definition
A statistical distribution that generalizes the Weibull distribution with an additional kappa parameter, capturing the transition from exponential to Gaussian tails in extreme event statistics. This distribution offers a precise mathematical tool for modeling latency and failure distributions in network-on-chip and neuromorphic systems, enabling better prediction of tail risks and performance guarantees.
