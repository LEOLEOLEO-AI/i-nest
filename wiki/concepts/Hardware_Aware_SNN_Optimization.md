# Hardware_Aware_SNN_Optimization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of adjusting SNN parameters (e.g., neuron thresholds, synaptic weights, and spike timings) to match FPGA resource constraints and timing requirements during hls4ml synthesis. Ensures that SNN models maintain accuracy while being deployable on resource-limited FPGAs, a key challenge for TCC and iNEST co-design.
