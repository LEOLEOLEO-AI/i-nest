# OnChipPlasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The ability of a neuromorphic chip to modify synaptic weights locally during inference or training without external computation, often using local learning rules like STDP. Enables real-time personalization and adaptation in edge devices, a core requirement for in-network neuromorphic systems.
