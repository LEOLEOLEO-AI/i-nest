# Event_Driven_Computation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A processing model where activity occurs only upon salient events or spikes, rather than on a fixed periodic clock. It is fundamental to achieving low-frequency, low-power operation in large-scale spiking neural networks.
