# Droop_Triggered_Workload_Throttling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A runtime technique that reduces or reorders computational tasks when voltage droop is detected, to prevent timing violations and data corruption. Bridges power integrity management with workload scheduling, essential for maintaining correctness in both TCC and iNEST architectures under dynamic load.
