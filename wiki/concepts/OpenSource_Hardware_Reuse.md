# OpenSource_Hardware_Reuse

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The practice of sharing and reusing FPGA project source code (Verilog) from academic courses and communities to accelerate custom hardware development. Reduces development time and lowers entry barriers for TCC/iNEST researchers, enabling rapid exploration of novel in-network or neuromorphic topologies.
