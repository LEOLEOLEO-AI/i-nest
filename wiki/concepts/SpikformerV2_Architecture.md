**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
An enhanced spiking transformer architecture that integrates a dual-branch design with spike-form self-attention and a convolutional branch to achieve high accuracy on ImageNet while maintaining low energy consumption. Demonstrates that spiking neural networks can reach competitive accuracy with non-spiking models, a critical milestone for practical neuromorphic computing in vision tasks.
