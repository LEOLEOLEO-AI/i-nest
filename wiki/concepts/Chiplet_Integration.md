## Definition
A modular processor design strategy where a large system is decomposed into small functional dies, or chiplets, joined through advanced packaging and high-bandwidth interconnects. It enables open and scalable heterogeneous AI hardware ecosystems, providing flexible physical building blocks for topology-specific and neuromorphic accelerators.
