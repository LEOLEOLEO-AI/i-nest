**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuromorphic circuit design in which computation is triggered by sparse spike events instead of a global clock, enabling event-driven processing. Essential for achieving the low latency and energy efficiency targeted by iNEST in-network neuromorphic accelerators.
