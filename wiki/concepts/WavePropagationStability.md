# WavePropagationStability

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The property of a spiking network where traveling wave patterns maintain their shape and velocity over long distances and time, despite synaptic noise and plasticity. Stable wave propagation is essential for reliable temporal coding and communication in large-scale neuromorphic systems, reducing error accumulation.
