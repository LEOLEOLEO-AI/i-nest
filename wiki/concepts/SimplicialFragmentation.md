# SimplicialFragmentation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The phenomenon where the presence of simplicial closure breaks a single global explosive transition into multiple smaller, sequential transitions or fragments, altering the system's resilience and hysteresis. This fragmentation effect provides a mechanism to soften or delay critical transitions in higher-order networks, which can be exploited for graceful degradation in TCC and for tuning sensitivity in iNEST systems.
