# ConnectionCentricParadigm

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
A modeling viewpoint that prioritizes edges and higher-order relational structures over nodes as the primary carriers of dynamical state and function. Enables iNEST and TCC to build neuromorphic systems where synaptic/connection topology, rather than neuron state, is the main computational substrate.
