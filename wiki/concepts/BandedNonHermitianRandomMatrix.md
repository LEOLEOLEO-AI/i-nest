## Definition
A random matrix with non-Hermitian entries confined to a band around the diagonal, used to model sparse connectivity in neural networks. Captures realistic sparse synaptic coupling in neuromorphic systems, enabling study of eigenvalue degeneracies that affect network stability and dynamics.
