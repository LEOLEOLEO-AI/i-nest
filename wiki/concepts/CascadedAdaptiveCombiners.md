# CascadedAdaptiveCombiners

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A multilayer neuron network architecture where each layer's output is adaptively combined with inputs from previous layers through learnable weighting mechanisms, enabling online learning in biologically accurate models. This provides a scalable method for implementing biologically plausible online learning in neuromorphic systems, addressing the challenge of training deep networks without backpropagation.
