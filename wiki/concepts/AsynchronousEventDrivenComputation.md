# AsynchronousEventDrivenComputation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A computation model where operations are triggered by incoming spikes rather than clock cycles, allowing data-dependent processing and idle-state energy savings. Reduces dynamic power and latency in neuromorphic hardware, making it viable for edge and in-network AI applications.
