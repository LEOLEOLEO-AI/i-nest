**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Factor potentials derived from the state evolution of nonlinear dynamical systems, so that belief propagation operates over continuous state trajectories governed by nonlinear ODEs rather than static discrete tables. It extends spiking inference beyond discrete graphical models into the nonlinear, continuous-time regimes that dominate TCC dynamical-system and control-oriented workloads.
