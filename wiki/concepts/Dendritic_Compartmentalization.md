## Definition
The functional segregation of a neuron's dendritic tree into distinct compartments, each capable of processing different input streams and contributing to the neuron's output in a nonlinear manner. This concept bridges TCC and iNEST by providing a biological-inspired mechanism for local computation and information routing, enabling more expressive and efficient network topologies.
