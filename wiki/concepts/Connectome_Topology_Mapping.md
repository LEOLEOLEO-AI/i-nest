# Connectome_Topology_Mapping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The process of translating the synaptic wiring diagram of the Drosophila early olfactory system into a graph-theoretic topology that can be embedded onto a topology-centric computing substrate. It provides the biological ground truth for TCC node-and-link abstractions and validates whether topological compute graphs preserve functional circuit motifs.
