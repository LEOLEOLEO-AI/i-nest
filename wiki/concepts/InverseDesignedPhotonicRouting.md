# InverseDesignedPhotonicRouting

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design automation method that uses inverse design algorithms to optimize photonic waveguide routing and component layouts for large-scale electronic-photonic integrated circuits (EPICs). Enables scalable, yield-optimized on-chip optical interconnects for chiplet and wafer-scale topologies, critical for TCC's high-bandwidth, low-latency data movement.
