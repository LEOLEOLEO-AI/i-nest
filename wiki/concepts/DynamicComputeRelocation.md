**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The runtime mechanism of moving active computation (including state and execution context) between chiplets without halting the overall system, guided by communication-aware heuristics. Provides the foundational capability for TCC to exploit temporal variations in traffic patterns, improving overall system efficiency beyond static mapping.
