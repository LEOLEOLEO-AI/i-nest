## Definition
A quantitative metric measuring the degree of alignment between representation spaces of different networks, based on pairwise distances or similarity matrices of latent vectors. Provides a practical tool for assessing when in-network neuromorphic processors can safely share or merge learned features, improving robustness and reducing redundancy.
