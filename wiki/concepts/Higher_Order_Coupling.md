# Higher_Order_Coupling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Interaction terms in which three or more nodes jointly influence a node's phase, typically encoded by the incidence structure of simplices rather than by pairwise adjacency. Higher-order coupling is the mechanism by which topology enters dynamics, making it central to modeling multi-node interactions in TCC topological interconnects and dense iNEST neuromorphic assemblies.
