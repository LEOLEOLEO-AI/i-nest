# SpikeRateResolution

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The granularity or number of distinct spike rates that an SNN can represent within a given time window, determined by the window length and neuron dynamics. This directly limits the information capacity of rate-encoded SNNs, and matching it to the QNN's quantization levels is essential for lossless conversion and efficient neuromorphic implementation.
