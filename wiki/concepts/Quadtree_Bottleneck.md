## Definition
The Quadtree Bottleneck is the scalability limitation in ES-HyperNEAT caused by its quadtree-based adaptive subdivision, which becomes computationally expensive as resolution or dimensionality increases. Identifying this bottleneck is critical for scaling coordinate-based neuroevolution in topology-centric computing and in-network neuromorphic systems.
