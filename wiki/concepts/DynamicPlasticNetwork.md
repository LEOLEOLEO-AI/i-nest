# DynamicPlasticNetwork

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A physical network that can continuously reconfigure its topology and synaptic weights in response to activity, mimicking biological neural plasticity. Captures the adaptive learning capability of the brain, which is crucial for moving beyond static simulated neural networks to truly neuromorphic systems.
