**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The phenomenon where the eigenvalues of a graph's adjacency or Laplacian matrix exhibit oscillations as a function of eigenvalue magnitude, following a log-periodic pattern due to discrete scale invariance. Offers a novel spectral signature for detecting and characterizing hierarchical structures in networks, which can be used to optimize information flow and memory capacity in iNEST reservoir computing systems.
