## Definition
Non-Hermitian degeneracies where both eigenvalues and their corresponding eigenvectors coalesce, producing extreme sensitivity and topological defects. Exceptional points offer a mechanism for tunable amplification, sensing, and mode switching in non-Hermitian neuromorphic and topology-centric hardware.
