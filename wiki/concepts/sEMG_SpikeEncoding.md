# sEMG_SpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A method for converting surface electromyography (sEMG) signals into spike trains for input to spiking neural networks, preserving temporal muscle activation patterns. Enables neuromorphic hardware to directly process biological muscle signals, critical for real-time fatigue monitoring in edge devices.
