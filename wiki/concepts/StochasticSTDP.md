# StochasticSTDP

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A probabilistic form of spike-timing-dependent plasticity where synaptic weight updates occur with a probability dependent on the timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes, rather than deterministically. Provides a biologically plausible and analytically tractable model for learning dynamics in neuromorphic systems, enabling mean-field analysis of network-level behavior.
