# StateAugmentedRepair

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A repair mechanism that augments the state of a supervised memristive AI hardware system to correct faults or drift, inspired by astrocytic modulation in biological neural networks. This enables resilient operation of memristive AI hardware under degradation, which is critical for long-term reliability in TCC/iNEST systems where interconnect and device states are tightly coupled.
