A surrogate gradient method that dynamically adjusts the shape and asymmetry of the gradient approximation for spike-based backpropagation, tailored to neuron firing dynamics. Enables stable and efficient training of deep SNNs, which is critical for deploying neuromorphic inference in iNEST in-network processing nodes.
