## Definition
The analysis and modeling of signal degradation (e.g., crosstalk, reflection, attenuation) in chiplet-to-chiplet interconnects, considering package and interposer parasitics. Essential for ensuring reliable data transfer in TCC systems, where multiple chiplets are tightly coupled and signal integrity directly impacts compute correctness and throughput.
