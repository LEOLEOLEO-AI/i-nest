## Definition
A hierarchical integration architecture where network-on-chip (NoC) connects chiplets, which are then integrated at wafer scale for massive parallelism. Bridges TCC's communication-centric topology with iNEST's physical integration, enabling high-bandwidth, low-latency inter-chiplet dataflow.
