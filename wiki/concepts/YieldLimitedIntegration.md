# YieldLimitedIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
The fundamental bottleneck where manufacturing defects across a whole wafer make the probability of producing a fully functional wafer-scale system extremely low. TCC architectures must incorporate defect tolerance and reconfiguration strategies because yield, not just logic design, determines wafer-scale feasibility.
