**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A diffusion process whose trajectories are conditioned to terminate at a specified barrier (via Doob's h-transform), converting stochastic noise into a controlled, goal-directed signal. Provides a mathematical framework to harness intrinsic analog device noise as a deterministic resource for continual learning, rather than treating it as a nuisance.
