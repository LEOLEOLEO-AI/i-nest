## Definition
Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity adapted for in-network neuromorphic computing, enabling local synaptic weight updates based on precise spike timing within network routers or switches. This enables self-adaptive learning directly inside the network fabric, reducing latency and energy overhead compared to off-chip training in traditional neuromorphic systems.
