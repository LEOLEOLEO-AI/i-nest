## Definition
A neural network model based on Ising spin dynamics with recurrent feedback connections, where binary neuron states evolve under stochastic or deterministic update rules on a graph. This model bridges statistical physics and neuromorphic computing, offering a theoretical framework for analyzing collective dynamics in recurrent spiking networks.
