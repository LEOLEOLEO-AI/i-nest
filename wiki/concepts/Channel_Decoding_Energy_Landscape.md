## Definition
The structure of the energy landscape (local minima, barriers, and ground state) induced by the Ising formulation of a channel decoding problem, which determines the difficulty and success of neuromorphic optimization. Understanding this landscape is critical for designing formulations that avoid trapping in suboptimal states, directly impacting decoding accuracy and reliability.
