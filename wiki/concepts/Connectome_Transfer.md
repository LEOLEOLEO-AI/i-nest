# Connectome_Transfer

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The concept of transplanting the neural connectivity map (connectome) of one species, such as C. elegans, into the body or motor controller of another species, like a fly, to test the universality of neural computation. This tests whether neural computation principles are hardware-independent, which is central to iNEST's goal of implementing biological algorithms on neuromorphic substrates.
