## Definition
The concept that the nervous system maintains an internal predictive model of the plant (e.g., limb or robot) to anticipate disturbances, as formalized in emulation theory. Provides a theoretical basis for designing predictive controllers in TCC and iNEST, where topology and in-network processing can leverage such models for real-time adaptation.
