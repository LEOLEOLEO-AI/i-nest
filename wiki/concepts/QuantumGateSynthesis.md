## Definition
The process of decomposing a desired quantum operation into a sequence of elementary quantum gates, optimized for a specific hardware platform. This is a core challenge for realizing practical quantum computing, and its automation via reservoir computing could enable scalable, adaptive gate compilation in hybrid quantum-classical networks.
