# WaferScaleComputing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
An architecture that uses an entire silicon wafer as a single integrated computing platform to overcome the area limits of individual chips. It enables topology-centric systems to exploit wafer-scale connectivity and massive on-wafer parallelism beyond die-level constraints.
