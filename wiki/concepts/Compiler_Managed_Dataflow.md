**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A scheduling paradigm in which the compiler fully orchestrates operand movement and instruction issue across the network of slices, replacing dynamic hardware control with static dataflow plans. It shifts topology-aware decision making into software, giving TCC researchers a tractable abstraction for optimizing mapping and communication on fixed interconnect fabrics.
