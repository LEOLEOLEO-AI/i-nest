## Definition
The design principle that unlearning algorithms must account for the physical write characteristics (e.g., asymmetric SET/RESET, write noise) of resistive memory cells when computing parameter updates. Prevents accuracy degradation and premature cell wear during unlearning operations, ensuring reliable long-term operation of neuromorphic and graph-processing accelerators.
