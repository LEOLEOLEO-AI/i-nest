## Definition
The Leaky Integrate-and-Fire model, a simplified neuron dynamics model where membrane potential integrates inputs and leaks over time, firing a spike when a threshold is reached. LIF is a computationally efficient and widely used neuron model, making it a practical choice for hardware implementation in neuromorphic systems.
