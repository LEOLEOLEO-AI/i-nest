# LocalStructureAwareSelfAttention

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A self-attention mechanism for spiking neural networks that restricts attention computation to local spatial neighborhoods while incorporating structural priors (e.g., graph or grid topology) to reduce quadratic complexity. Enables transformer-based SNNs to scale beyond global attention bottlenecks, critical for energy-efficient neuromorphic processing of large-scale spatial-temporal data.
