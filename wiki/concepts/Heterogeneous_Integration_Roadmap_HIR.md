## Definition
An IEEE-led framework that standardizes design rules, test methods, and integration processes for combining disparate chiplets (logic, memory, analog) into a single package. Provides a formal methodology for TCC/iNEST to integrate specialized neuromorphic or topological compute tiles with conventional silicon, reducing design risk and enabling scalable systems.
