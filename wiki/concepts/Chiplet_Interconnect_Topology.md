## Definition
The arrangement of physical links and routing paths among chiplets on a system-on-wafer, which can be reconfigured to support different communication patterns. Bridges the gap between software-defined topology and physical hardware, allowing scalable, high-bandwidth reconfiguration in wafer-scale systems.
