## Definition
The butterfly dataflow graph from the Cooley–Tukey FFT that recursively combines remote data pairs, later reused as a mathematical prototype for parallel communication skeletons. Establishes the theoretical origin of butterfly-structured communication used in both signal-processing algorithms and topology-centric parallel architectures.
