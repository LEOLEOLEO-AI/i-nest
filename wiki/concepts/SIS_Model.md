## Definition
A susceptible-infected-susceptible compartmental epidemic model used to simulate the spread and recovery dynamics of cyber threats across complex network topologies. Enables quantitative analysis of malware propagation and mitigation in TCC/iNEST architectures, where node interactions and recovery rates are critical for system stability.
