## Definition
A MoS2-based phototransistor that implements a leaky-integrate-and-fire neuron by directly converting optical inputs into spiking signals. It provides a compact optoelectronic neuron compatible with 2D material integration, enabling direct optical-to-spike interfaces for in-network neuromorphic sensing.
