# TopologicalEdgeStateTransport

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The propagation of electrons along the protected boundaries of a topological insulator, which remains robust against backscattering and disorder in the absence of magnetic perturbations. This is the foundational transport mechanism for TCC, as it offers lossless, unidirectional data pathways that can serve as the physical layer for topology-centric computing networks.
