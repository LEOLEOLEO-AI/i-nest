# CeYIGMagnetoOpticalIsolator

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A magneto-optical isolator built from cerium-substituted yttrium iron garnet (Ce:YIG) films, providing non-reciprocal light propagation to prevent back-reflections in photonic circuits. Isolators are essential for stable signal integrity in TCC's optical networks, preventing feedback that could disrupt topology-based routing and computation.
