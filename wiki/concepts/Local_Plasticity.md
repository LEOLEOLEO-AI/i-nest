# Local_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning rule where synaptic weights are updated based solely on local neuron activity and immediate inputs, without global error propagation. Critical for online, on-chip learning in neuromorphic systems, reducing communication overhead and enabling real-time adaptation in distributed topologies.
