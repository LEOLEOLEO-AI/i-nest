## Definition
The representation of information in the precise timing or inter-spike intervals of neuron firing events, rather than in continuous activation values. Leverages the temporal dimension of spikes for efficient event-driven computation, aligning with the low-latency and energy-efficient goals of in-network neuromorphic processing.
