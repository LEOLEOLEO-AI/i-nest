# ReservoirStateProjection

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The mapping of high-dimensional transient reservoir states onto a lower-dimensional output space to recover original signal features from aliased inputs. Provides a trainable, hardware-friendly readout mechanism for neuromorphic chips, enabling real-time signal recovery without iterative algorithms.
