# Hard_Breakdown

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A catastrophic failure mode in passive crossbar memristors where uncontrolled filament formation causes permanent low-resistance shorting. Hard breakdown is a central yield-limiting defect that must be overcome to make wafer-scale iNEST crossbar manufacturing viable.
