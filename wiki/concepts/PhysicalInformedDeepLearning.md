# PhysicalInformedDeepLearning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning paradigm that embeds physical laws (e.g., oscillator dynamics) into neural network training, constraining model behavior to follow known dynamical principles. Offers a principled way to design iNEST learning systems that respect physical constraints of neuromorphic hardware, improving interpretability and generalization.
