# PersonalizedSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture that adapts its synaptic weights and topology to individual user's EEG signal patterns, typically via online or few-shot learning. Enables subject-specific brain-computer interfaces and medical monitoring, which is a key application driver for in-network neuromorphic computing.
