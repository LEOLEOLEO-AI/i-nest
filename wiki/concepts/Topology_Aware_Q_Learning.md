**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A Q-learning formulation where the agent's state and action spaces are defined by the local interconnect topology, enabling cooperative decisions without global state knowledge. It directly addresses how topology-centric computing can support decentralized learning of cooperation in large-scale interconnects.
