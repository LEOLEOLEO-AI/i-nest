## Definition
A network-layer mechanism that encapsulates individual spikes into packets and routes them across the ring topology based on neuron destination addresses, often with priority or time-stamping for temporal fidelity. Critical for preserving spike timing and event ordering in distributed SNNs, which directly impacts learning and inference accuracy in neuromorphic systems.
