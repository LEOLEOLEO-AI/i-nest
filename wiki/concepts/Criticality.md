# Criticality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The state of a dynamical system poised at a phase transition, characterized by power-law correlations, high sensitivity to perturbations, and maximal information processing capacity. Operating at criticality is hypothesized to optimize computation and memory in neuromorphic hardware, making it a key design target for iNEST and TCC architectures.
