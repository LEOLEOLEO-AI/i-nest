# ContinuousGameOfLife

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A continuous-state, continuous-time generalization of Conway's Game of Life where cells have real-valued states and evolve via differential equations, enabling emergent cell birth and death at growth boundaries. Provides a biologically-plausible substrate for studying self-organization and edge-of-growth dynamics relevant to neuromorphic and topology-centric computing models.
