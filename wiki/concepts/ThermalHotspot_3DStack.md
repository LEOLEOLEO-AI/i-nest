# ThermalHotspot_3DStack

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Localized regions of elevated temperature caused by uneven power density in vertically stacked dies, often near high-activity compute cores or memory controllers. Hotspots degrade performance and reliability, and must be addressed via thermal-aware placement and routing in TCC architectures.
