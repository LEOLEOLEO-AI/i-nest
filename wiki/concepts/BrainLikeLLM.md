## Definition
A class of large language models architecturally inspired by the brain, using spiking or neuromorphic components rather than purely attention-based layers. It challenges the dominance of Transformers and aligns with iNEST's goal of enabling biologically plausible, energy-efficient language intelligence at the network edge.
