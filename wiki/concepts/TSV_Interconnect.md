# TSV_Interconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
Through-silicon vias that provide vertical electrical connections through a wafer, enabling dense, short-distance communication between stacked or co-integrated dies. TSVs are a key physical-layer technology for building high-bandwidth, low-latency on-wafer networks that underpin topology-centric computing architectures.
