# DataCenter_Chip_Logic_Compression

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-07

## Definition
The process of applying topological transformations to compress the logical representation of data center workloads, reducing the number of active gates and interconnects required for a given computation. Directly addresses the energy and latency bottlenecks in large-scale data centers, making it a key enabler for sustainable, high-performance TCC systems.
