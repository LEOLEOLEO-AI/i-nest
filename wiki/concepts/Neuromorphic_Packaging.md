# Neuromorphic_Packaging

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Neuromorphic packaging is the application of advanced packaging techniques (like 3DHI and chiplet integration) to assemble neuromorphic computing components, such as spiking neural network cores and synaptic memory arrays. This concept bridges TCC and iNEST by enabling high-density, low-power interconnects that support spike-based communication and co-located memory-compute for neuromorphic systems.
