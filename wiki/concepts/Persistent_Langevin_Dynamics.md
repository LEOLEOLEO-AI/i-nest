## Definition
A stochastic dynamics framework where the system evolves under Langevin equations with persistent (memory-carrying) noise, enabling exploration of rare metastable states in complex energy landscapes. Provides a physically-grounded method for probing slow relaxation and hierarchical state structures in neuromorphic substrates, relevant for understanding memory and computation in noisy neural systems.
