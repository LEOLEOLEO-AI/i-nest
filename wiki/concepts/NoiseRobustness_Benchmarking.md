## Definition
A standardized evaluation protocol that measures how well a neuromorphic system maintains performance under injected noise (e.g., synaptic, spike-timing, or input corruption) across different coding and learning configurations. This enables quantitative comparison of contemporary neuromorphic architectures and informs the design of robust network topologies for SDI and iNEST deployments.
