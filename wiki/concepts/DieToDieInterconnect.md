# DieToDieInterconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
The high-density, short-reach physical and protocol layer that connects adjacent dies or chiplets within a package, enabling data movement at the boundaries of the topology graph. It sets the per-edge bandwidth and latency constraints that TCC topology optimization must respect when mapping workloads onto wafer-scale fabrics.
