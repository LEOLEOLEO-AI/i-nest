# InterpolationLearningTransition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The sharp transition in generalization performance that occurs as a network moves from under- to over-parameterized regimes near the interpolation threshold, where training error approaches zero. Understanding this transition is critical for designing TCC/iNEST systems that must balance memory and compute resources at the edge, where operating near interpolation may yield optimal generalization with minimal hardware overhead.
