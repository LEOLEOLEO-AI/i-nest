## Definition
The continuous transition between distinct quantum phases (e.g., from metallic to pseudogapped) without a thermodynamic phase transition, driven by tuning correlation strength or temperature. Enables smooth tunability of electronic properties in devices, which is essential for adaptive or reconfigurable computing architectures in TCC/iNEST.
