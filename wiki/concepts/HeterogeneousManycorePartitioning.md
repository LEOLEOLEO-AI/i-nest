# HeterogeneousManycorePartitioning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The architectural strategy of dividing a manycore processor into specialized regions (e.g., high-performance vs. energy-efficient cores) to match workload characteristics and reduce overall power consumption. Enables TCC systems to dynamically allocate computation to the most energy-appropriate cores, improving efficiency and thermal headroom for topology-centric workloads.
