## Definition
A replica symmetry breaking (RSB) phase that emerges in the non-equilibrium dynamics of a mean-field glass model, characterized by a hierarchical organization of metastable states. Provides a theoretical framework for understanding memory and computation in disordered systems, relevant to neuromorphic hardware that exploits glassy dynamics.
