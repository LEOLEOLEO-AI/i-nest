# InNetwork_Neuromorphic_Fabric

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
A neuromorphic computing fabric in which synaptic weight storage, spike routing, and neuron updates are performed within the interconnect fabric itself rather than at dedicated processing endpoints. It motivates mapping spiking neural network traffic onto FPGA NoC topologies, making NoC bandwidth and multicast capability the primary determinant of iNEST scalability.
