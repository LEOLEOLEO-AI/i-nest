# ReconfigurableSpikingRouting

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ability to dynamically alter spike pathways and synaptic connections on-chip via programmable switches or crossbars, without halting computation. Enables adaptive network topologies and learning-driven rewiring, essential for both TCC's flexible interconnect and iNEST's plasticity requirements.
