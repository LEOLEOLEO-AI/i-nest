## Definition
A neural network-based dimensionality reduction technique that learns a low-dimensional embedding of brain connectomes while preserving sex-specific and network-specific topological features. Enables scalable analysis of high-dimensional connectome data, which is critical for mapping complex network topologies in TCC/iNEST systems.
