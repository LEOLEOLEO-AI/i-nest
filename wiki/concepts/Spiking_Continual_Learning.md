# Spiking_Continual_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning paradigm for spiking neural networks that enables sequential acquisition of new tasks while preserving previously learned knowledge, using spike-timing-dependent plasticity and synaptic consolidation mechanisms. Critical for in-network neuromorphic processors to adapt to changing data streams without retraining, enabling lifelong learning in distributed TCC/iNEST systems.
