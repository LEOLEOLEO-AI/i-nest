# LowVoltage_Thermal_Actuation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method of actuation that relies on low-voltage heating to induce phase change or gas expansion, producing mechanical displacement. Reduces power and driver complexity, making it feasible for large-scale, embedded tactile interfaces in TCC architectures.
