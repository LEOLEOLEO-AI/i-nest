**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A method to derive approximate macroscopic population-level dynamics of spiking neural networks by solving the transport equation for probability density evolution. Enables scalable analysis and prediction of network behavior without simulating every spike, crucial for designing efficient neuromorphic systems.
