# NestedLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning paradigm where the apparent depth of a deep architecture is an illusion, as the effective computation can be collapsed into a shallower nested structure without loss of representational power. Challenges the necessity of deep hierarchies in neuromorphic systems, suggesting that in-network processing can achieve equivalent function with simpler, more energy-efficient nested topologies.
