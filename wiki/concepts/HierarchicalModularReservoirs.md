# HierarchicalModularReservoirs

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A reservoir computing architecture where the recurrent network is organized into hierarchical modules with distinct timescales, enabling multi-scale temporal processing. This structure mimics cortical hierarchy and could enable neuromorphic hardware to process complex spatiotemporal signals more efficiently than flat reservoirs.
