## Definition
The integration of self-monitoring and self-evaluation mechanisms directly into the network topology, allowing each node to assess its own state and contribution to global tasks. This concept shifts intelligence from centralized controllers to the network fabric itself, enabling fault-tolerant and adaptive behavior in large-scale TCC deployments.
