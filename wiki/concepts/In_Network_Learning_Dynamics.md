**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process by which learning and representation formation occur within the network infrastructure itself, rather than in centralized compute nodes. This is foundational for iNEST as it enables distributed, low-latency adaptation directly in the data path, reducing reliance on external training systems.
