# MagnetoOpticalFilmDeposition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A thin-film deposition technique for growing Ce:YIG layers on silicon with controlled crystallinity and magnetic properties, often using sputtering or pulsed laser deposition. High-quality film deposition is a prerequisite for achieving low-loss, high-isolation performance in TCC's photonic interconnects, directly impacting network efficiency.
