## Definition
An objective function that extends traditional edge-cut minimization by weighting hypergraph cuts according to spiking activity or communication volume. It guides NoC partitioning toward low-traffic cuts that matter most for SNN inference, improving TCC mapping quality and iNEST efficiency.
