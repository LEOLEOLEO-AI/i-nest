## Definition
A representation scheme where 3D point cloud features are encoded as spike timings or rates across temporal dimensions, leveraging the temporal dynamics of spiking neurons for spatial data. Bridges the gap between neuromorphic computing and 3D geometric processing, allowing TCC architectures to handle spatial tasks using event-driven, low-power computation.
