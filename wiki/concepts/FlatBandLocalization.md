# FlatBandLocalization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where a flat dispersion band leads to complete spatial localization of vibrational or wave energy, often due to destructive interference or resonant trapping. Provides a mechanism for stable, isolated memory states or synaptic-like weights in neuromorphic hardware, enabling energy-efficient storage and processing in disordered neural substrates.
