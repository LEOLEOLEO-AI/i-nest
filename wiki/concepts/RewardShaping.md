# RewardShaping

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-24

## Definition
A technique that modifies the intrinsic reward signal to include reputation-based bonuses or penalties, steering learning toward socially beneficial actions. Enables neuromorphic learning systems to incorporate social norms directly into synaptic weight updates, improving convergence to cooperative equilibria.
