## Definition
OCA (Open Chiplet Architecture) is Tenstorrent's fully open-source chiplet-based hardware architecture that enables modular composition of RISC-V CPU and AI accelerator dies. It provides a topology-flexible substrate for TCC research by allowing customizable die-to-die interconnects and heterogeneous compute elements.
