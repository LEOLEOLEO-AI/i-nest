**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The axiom that independent subsystem gains multiply while their spatiotemporal coordinates add, enabling a separable yet synergistic combination of spatial and temporal processing. This axiom underpins the derivation of exponential gain forms and guides the decomposition of complex neural computations into modular, composable units in TCC/iNEST architectures.
