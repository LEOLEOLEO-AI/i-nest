## Definition
The co-packaging of chips made from different material systems (e.g., silicon CMOS, III-V compounds, photonic devices) within a single 3D stack. Critical for iNEST to integrate photonic synaptic devices with electronic neurons, and for TCC to enable optical interconnects between topological compute clusters.
