# QuadraticIntegrateAndFire

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A neuron model where the membrane potential dynamics follow a quadratic function, enabling faster and more precise spike generation compared to the linear leaky integrate-and-fire model. Demonstrates that neuron model choice significantly impacts gradient-based learning in spiking networks, offering a path to more efficient neuromorphic training.
