# FPGA_Implementation_Verification

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The hardware-level stage where synthesized asynchronous SNN circuits are mapped to an FPGA and verified against the software model's expected behavior. FPGA verification provides a reconfigurable and rapid prototyping platform for iNEST, bridging software-defined SNN designs and physical deployment.
