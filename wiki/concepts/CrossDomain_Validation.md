## Definition
A process of testing hypotheses or models developed in one domain (TCC or iNEST) against data or scenarios from the other domain to ensure robustness and generalizability. Strengthens the credibility of cross-domain findings and ensures that proposed concepts are not domain-specific artifacts but have broader applicability.
