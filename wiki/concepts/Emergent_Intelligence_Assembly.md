## Definition
The process by which higher-order cognitive capabilities arise from the coordinated interaction of lower-level neural modules, rather than from a monolithic, centralized controller. Guides the development of in-network learning systems where intelligence is distributed across the network, improving robustness and adaptability in dynamic environments.
