# IrregularNeuralReduction

**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A neural network computation pattern where reduction operations involve non-uniform, data-dependent or sparse connectivity across processing elements. Irregular reductions are notoriously inefficient on regular mesh NoCs, making their optimization a key target for topology-centric computing.
