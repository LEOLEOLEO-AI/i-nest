## Definition
A bias in spiking neural networks where models rely on non-causal, dataset-specific features (e.g., background textures) rather than the intended semantic attributes, leading to unfair predictions. Identifies a failure mode that undermines the reliability of neuromorphic systems in real-world TCC deployments where input distributions vary.
