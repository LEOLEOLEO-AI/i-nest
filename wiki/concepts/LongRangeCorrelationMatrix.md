# LongRangeCorrelationMatrix

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A random matrix model where the variance or coupling strength between elements decays as a power law with distance, rather than being independent or short-range correlated. Enables modeling of physical network topologies (e.g., optical or electrical interconnects) where distant nodes still interact significantly, affecting eigenmode localization and information flow.
