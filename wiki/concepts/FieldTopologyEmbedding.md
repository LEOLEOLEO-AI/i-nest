# FieldTopologyEmbedding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The process of embedding a neural field's coordinate system into the physical or logical topology of a computing network, allowing field queries to be routed along network paths. Bridges continuous neural representations with discrete network structures, enabling in-network computation of world model queries without centralized processing.
