## Definition
A dynamical phase transition in randomly coupled field systems where the network freezes into a non-ergodic glassy state with aging and history-dependent memory. Explains metastable memory and slow relaxation in neuromorphic and topology-centric networks, relevant for reservoir computing and in-network learning.
