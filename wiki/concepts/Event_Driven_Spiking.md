# Event_Driven_Spiking

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A computation paradigm in which neurons emit spikes only when their membrane potential reaches a threshold, producing sparse, event-driven activity. This sparsity is what gives SNNs their energy-efficiency advantage over dense ANN matrix multiplications in neuromorphic hardware.
