# Persistent_Machine_Monitoring

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Continuous, long-duration surveillance of industrial machinery in which the sensing node must remain operational for extended periods without maintenance or battery replacement. Sets the severe energy and lifetime constraints that motivate iNEST's event-driven, in-network computation instead of continuous data streaming.
