## Definition
A phase where wavefunctions exhibit multifractal scaling at the critical point, neither fully localized nor extended, often associated with mobility edges in quasicrystals. Understanding multifractal criticality can inform the design of reconfigurable network topologies in TCC and neuromorphic reservoirs where intermediate localization enhances computational capacity.
