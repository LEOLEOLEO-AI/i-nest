# Simplicial_Kuramoto_Model

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A generalization of the classical Kuramoto oscillator model in which phase dynamics are coupled through the higher-order simplices (edges, triangles, tetrahedra) of a simplicial complex rather than pairwise links. It provides a native dynamical substrate for TCC-style topological structures and iNEST-style collective computation, enabling synchronization to be studied on the same simplicial topologies used for network-centric computing.
