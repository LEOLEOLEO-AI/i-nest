# SimulabilityThreshold

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A critical parameter regime (e.g., shallow depth, limited entanglement) beyond which a quantum network loses its quantum advantage and becomes classically simulable. This threshold informs the design of iNEST architectures that must maintain quantum advantage for emergent intelligence, avoiding over-engineering that yields no benefit.
