**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A phenomenon where supervised training of spiking neural networks rapidly degrades the functionality of layers mimicking early visual cortex (V1) processing. This highlights a critical failure mode in neuromorphic vision systems, informing design of training regimes that preserve biologically plausible feature extraction in iNEST.
