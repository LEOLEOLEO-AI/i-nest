# ScaleFreeAvalancheStatistics

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A statistical distribution of avalanche sizes that follows a power law, indicating self-organized criticality and the absence of a characteristic length scale in the system. Scale-free statistics are a hallmark of efficient information transfer in complex networks, and understanding their breakdown at finite sizes informs the design of scalable topological computing architectures.
