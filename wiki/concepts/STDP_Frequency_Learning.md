# STDP_Frequency_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-07

## Definition
A spike-timing-dependent plasticity mechanism that dynamically adjusts memristor connection weights to encode frequency-specific activation patterns, such as gamma versus theta bands. Provides self-organizing frequency feature extraction within memristive networks, critical for scaling in-network neuromorphic systems.
