# Spatiotemporal_Complexity_Transfer

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A framework that compensates for reduced temporal complexity (clock frequency) by expanding spatial complexity (e.g., wafer-scale integration), guided by the minimum energy consumption law. This enables low-frequency, high-efficiency AI computing on non-advanced process nodes, relaxing dependence on cutting-edge lithography.
