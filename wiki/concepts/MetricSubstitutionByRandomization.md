# MetricSubstitutionByRandomization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A methodological flaw where metrics that should be computed from the network model are instead replaced by random number generation, producing meaningless or misleading quantitative results. This undermines the validity of any performance or structural analysis in TCC/iNEST, as reported metrics do not reflect the actual topology or dynamics of the system under study.
