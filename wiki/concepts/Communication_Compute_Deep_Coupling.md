## Definition
A design principle requiring interconnect technologies to be tightly integrated with computation architecture, so network behavior matches the communication patterns generated by workloads. It enables new AI chip architectures to avoid interconnect-bound stalls and sustains scaling beyond what isolated compute and network stacks can achieve.
