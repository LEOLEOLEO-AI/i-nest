**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A method of physically and logically enclosing compute nodes and their interconnects within a defined topological boundary, often using advanced packaging to preserve the intended network geometry. Preserves the mathematical topology of the compute graph in physical hardware, reducing routing overhead and enabling predictable performance in TCC architectures.
