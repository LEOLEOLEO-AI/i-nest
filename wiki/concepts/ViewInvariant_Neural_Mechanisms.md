# ViewInvariant_Neural_Mechanisms

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Neural network mechanisms that enable recognition of objects in novel orientations by learning invariant representations across different viewpoints. Critical for neuromorphic systems to achieve robust object recognition in dynamic, real-world environments without exhaustive training on all possible orientations.
