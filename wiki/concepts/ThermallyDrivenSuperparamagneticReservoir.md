# ThermallyDrivenSuperparamagneticReservoir

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A reservoir computing substrate using superparamagnetic nanoparticles whose stochastic thermal fluctuations serve as the nonlinear dynamical reservoir for computation. Enables energy-efficient, physically embodied reservoir computing that leverages intrinsic thermal noise rather than suppressing it, relevant for TCC's in-network processing nodes.
