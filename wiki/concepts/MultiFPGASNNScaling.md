# MultiFPGASNNScaling

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method for scaling spiking neural networks across multiple FPGAs by partitioning the network and using high-speed interconnects to maintain spike communication. This enables the deployment of large-scale SNNs that exceed single-chip capacity, a critical step for practical iNEST applications.
