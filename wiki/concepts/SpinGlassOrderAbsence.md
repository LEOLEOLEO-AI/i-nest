# SpinGlassOrderAbsence

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where no long-range spin-glass order emerges on certain hierarchical lattices near three dimensions, despite the presence of frustration. Challenges assumptions about disorder-driven computational states, informing whether such lattices can support stable memory or learning dynamics in neuromorphic hardware.
