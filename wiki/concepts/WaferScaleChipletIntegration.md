# WaferScaleChipletIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-10

## Definition
A manufacturing and packaging approach that integrates multiple chiplets directly on a single wafer-scale substrate, using the wafer itself as the interconnect medium. Provides ultra-high bandwidth and low latency between chiplets, forming the physical foundation for large-scale TCC architectures.
