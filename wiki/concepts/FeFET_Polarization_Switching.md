## Definition
The mechanism of controlling ferroelectric polarization states in FeFETs to represent binary or multi-level data, enabling both analog and digital memory behavior. Underpins the differential bit-cell's ability to mimic synaptic plasticity and support in-memory computing for neuromorphic topologies.
