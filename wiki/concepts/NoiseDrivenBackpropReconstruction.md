# NoiseDrivenBackpropReconstruction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training method that leverages intrinsic noise in neuromorphic hardware to reconstruct gradient signals for backpropagation, bypassing the need for exact weight transport. Enables on-chip learning in iNEST systems where conventional backprop is infeasible due to physical device variability and non-ideal connectivity.
