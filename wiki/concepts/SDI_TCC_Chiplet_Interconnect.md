## Definition
A chiplet interconnect topology based on SDI (Serial Data Interface) for TCC (Topology-Centric Computing) that enables low-latency Allreduce operations. Achieving sub-2μs Allreduce latency on FPGA prototypes validates the feasibility of TCC for high-performance distributed AI training.
