# Minimal_Computational_Unit

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
The concept that the smallest meaningful computation in biological neural systems may reside inside a single neuron rather than emerging only from network-level ensemble activity. This reframes neuromorphic architecture design to emphasize intracellular and synaptic dynamics as programmable computational elements for ultra-compact TCC/iNEST devices.
