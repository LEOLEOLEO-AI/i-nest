## Definition
The process of dispersing nanomaterials (e.g., carbon nanotubes, metal nanoparticles) into stable, printable inks with controlled rheological properties for high-resolution deposition. Critical for achieving reproducible, high-performance sensing layers in printed electronics, which can be integrated into neuromorphic or topological computing substrates.
