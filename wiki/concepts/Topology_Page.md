## Definition
A hardware abstraction layer that exposes the current interconnect topology as a pageable resource, similar to virtual memory, enabling runtime topology switching. Enables efficient and low-overhead reconfiguration of the interconnect, making topology adaptation practical for real-time intelligent workloads.
