## Definition
A crossbar array of ferroelectric field-effect transistors (FeFETs) used as analog synaptic weights in a spiking neural network, enabling non-volatile and energy-efficient weight storage. Provides a hardware substrate for in-memory computing in neuromorphic systems, directly addressing the memory-wall bottleneck in edge EEG processing.
