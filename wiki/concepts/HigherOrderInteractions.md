# HigherOrderInteractions

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Collective dynamics that arise from interactions among groups of more than two nodes, modeled via hypergraphs or simplicial complexes rather than pairwise edges. Captures multi-neuron or multi-node synchronization and avalanche dynamics that pairwise network models miss, essential for realistic neuromorphic and topological computing substrates.
