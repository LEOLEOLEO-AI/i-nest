# TopologicalStateSpace

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A state space for random walks or computations that is organized according to the faces of a simplicial complex, rather than a flat set of nodes. Offers a principled way to embed hierarchical and relational information into the state space of TCC/iNEST systems, improving representational efficiency.
