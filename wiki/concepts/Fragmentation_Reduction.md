# Fragmentation_Reduction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The property of a spiking neuron model to produce less fragmented (more coherent) spike trains or activity patterns under certain conditions. Reducing fragmentation in spike patterns can improve the reliability and interpretability of neural codes, which is critical for robust in-network computation.
