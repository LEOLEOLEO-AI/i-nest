## Definition
The problem of determining the minimum-volume (or otherwise optimal) ellipsoid that contains or approximates a given set of sample points in high-dimensional space. Provides a geometric primitive for bounding and characterizing the reachable state manifolds of topological and neuromorphic network embeddings.
