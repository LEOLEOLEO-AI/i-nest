**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The process of constructing a simplicial complex from a graph by adding higher-dimensional simplices (e.g., triangles, tetrahedra) based on local or global criteria. Provides a principled way to enrich graph data with topological features, enabling TCC models to reason about holes, cycles, and higher-order connectivity.
