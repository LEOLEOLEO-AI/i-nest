# DisorderedQuasiperiodicPotential

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A spatially varying on-site energy landscape that is either random (disorder) or deterministic but non-repeating (quasiperiodic), used to model localization effects in one-dimensional fermionic chains. Determines how localization (e.g., Anderson or Aubry-André) competes with measurement-induced entanglement, informing robustness of topological edge states in disordered TCC hardware.
