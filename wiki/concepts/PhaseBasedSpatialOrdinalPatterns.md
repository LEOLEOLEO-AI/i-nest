# PhaseBasedSpatialOrdinalPatterns

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that encodes the relative phase ordering of oscillatory signals across spatial locations into ordinal patterns, capturing phase relationships without amplitude information. Enables compact, topology-aware representation of phase dynamics in networked oscillators, useful for detecting synchronization patterns in TCC interconnects and iNEST neuromorphic circuits.
