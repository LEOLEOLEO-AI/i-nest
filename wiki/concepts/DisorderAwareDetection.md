# DisorderAwareDetection

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A detection framework for Majorana zero modes that explicitly models and compensates for spatial disorder in the device's topological nanowire, rather than assuming ideal clean conditions. Enables reliable topological qubit readout in realistic, imperfect devices, which is critical for scalable TCC architectures that depend on fault-tolerant Majorana-based qubits.
