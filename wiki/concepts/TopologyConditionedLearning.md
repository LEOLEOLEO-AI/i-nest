# TopologyConditionedLearning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A training paradigm where the learning process is conditioned on the underlying network topology, allowing the model to adapt its parameters based on the connectivity structure. Critical for TCC because it ensures that learned constructive models remain valid across different interconnect topologies, enabling transferable designs.
