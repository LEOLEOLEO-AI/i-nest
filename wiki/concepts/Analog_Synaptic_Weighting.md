# Analog_Synaptic_Weighting

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The use of memristor conductance states to represent continuous-valued synaptic weights in neural networks, enabling analog multiplication and accumulation. This is fundamental for TCC because it allows topology-centric mapping of neural connections onto physical crossbar arrays with high density and energy efficiency.
