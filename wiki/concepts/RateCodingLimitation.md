# RateCodingLimitation

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
The problem in SNNs where intensity is encoded by averaging firing rates over multiple time steps, sacrificing temporal precision and introducing high latency. It is the key bottleneck that motivates MSF and other iNEST innovations for efficient spatiotemporal computation.
