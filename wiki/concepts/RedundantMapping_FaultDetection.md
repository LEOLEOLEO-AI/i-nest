**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A fault detection and redundancy mapping process that identifies failed links or nodes and remaps traffic to redundant paths or resources during initialization and runtime. This is essential for maintaining high reliability and availability in wafer-scale TCC interconnects, where physical defects are inevitable.
