## Definition
The hypothesis that optimal network topologies for neuromorphic computing emerge from self-organized critical dynamics, where connectivity patterns co-evolve with activity to maintain criticality. Guides the design of topology-centric computing systems where the network structure itself is a computational resource, rather than a static substrate.
