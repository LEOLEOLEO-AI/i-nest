## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture that integrates multiple expert subnetworks with a gating mechanism to activate only relevant experts per input, leveraging sparse computation. Reduces energy consumption and latency in neuromorphic hardware by activating only necessary neural pathways, aligning with event-driven processing.
