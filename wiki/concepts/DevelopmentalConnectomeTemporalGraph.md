# DevelopmentalConnectomeTemporalGraph

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A temporal graph representation of the C. elegans nervous system that captures how synaptic connectivity changes across developmental stages, enabling the study of neural circuit formation over time. Provides a dynamic, time-resolved view of neural topology, essential for iNEST to model how neuromorphic systems can adapt and rewire during development rather than relying on static architectures.
