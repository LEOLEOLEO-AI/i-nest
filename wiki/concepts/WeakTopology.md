# WeakTopology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
Topological invariants that are defined along lower-dimensional subspaces (e.g., lines or planes) rather than the full Brillouin zone, often leading to boundary states that are robust only in certain directions. Weak topological phases can enable directional, robust signal propagation in network-on-chip and interconnect fabrics, guiding the layout of TCC communication paths.
