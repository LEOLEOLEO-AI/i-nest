# StochasticComputingSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method for implementing spiking neural network operations using stochastic bitstreams to represent spike probabilities and perform arithmetic with simple logic gates. Enables ultra-low-power and area-efficient SNN accelerators by replacing complex digital multipliers with stochastic logic, critical for in-network neuromorphic processing.
