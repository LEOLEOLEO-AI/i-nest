## Definition
A rare-region-induced smeared phase transition characterized by non-universal power-law scaling of the order parameter near criticality in disordered systems. Explains slow dynamics and enhanced sensitivity in disordered networks, critical for understanding resilience and computation in TCC routing and iNEST spiking dynamics.
