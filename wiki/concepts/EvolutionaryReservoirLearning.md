# EvolutionaryReservoirLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
An approach that uses evolutionary algorithms to optimize reservoir computing parameters (e.g., weights, topology) in spiking neural networks, replacing gradient-based training. This enables training of neuromorphic systems without backpropagation, which is crucial for hardware where gradient signals are unavailable or costly, advancing iNEST's on-chip learning capabilities.
