# Activity_Dependent_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A learning rule where synaptic strengths and network connectivity are modified based on the temporal correlation of neural activity, extending Hebbian principles to structural changes. Provides a biologically-plausible mechanism for continual learning and adaptation in neuromorphic systems without external supervision.
