**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A spiking neural network architecture where individual neurons possess active dendritic compartments that independently process and integrate multi-modal synaptic inputs before somatic spike generation. Enables simultaneous multi-task learning in a single network by allowing different dendritic branches to specialize on distinct tasks, reducing interference and improving energy efficiency compared to monolithic networks.
