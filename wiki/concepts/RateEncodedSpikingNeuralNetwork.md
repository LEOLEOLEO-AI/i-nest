# RateEncodedSpikingNeuralNetwork

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A spiking neural network where information is encoded in the firing rate of neurons over a time window, rather than precise spike timing. Rate encoding provides a robust, biologically plausible method for representing visual place information in neuromorphic systems, enabling compatibility with conventional vision pipelines.
