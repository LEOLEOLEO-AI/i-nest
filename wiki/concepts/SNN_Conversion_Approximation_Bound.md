**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The theoretical or empirical limit on the accuracy of ANN-to-SNN conversion, defined by the trade-off between spike timing precision, firing rate approximation, and the resulting error in continuous output signals. This matters for both TCC and iNEST as it provides a benchmark for evaluating conversion algorithms and motivates hybrid approaches that combine conversion with fine-tuning or spike-based learning to push beyond these bounds.
