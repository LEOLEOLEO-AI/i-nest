# ScribeLine_CrossDie_Routing

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A wafer-scale integration technique that uses high-layer metal traces over scribe lines to connect neighboring dies into a contiguous mesh without traditional packaging. It is the physical substrate enabling seamless topology mapping in TCC, avoiding inter-die latency bottlenecks.
