## Definition
A special point in the phase diagram of a disordered system where the Nishimori line (where disorder and thermal fluctuations balance) meets a multicritical point, exhibiting enhanced boundary criticality. Provides a theoretical framework for understanding how boundary conditions and disorder interplay at criticality, which can inform robust signal propagation and computation in neuromorphic hardware.
