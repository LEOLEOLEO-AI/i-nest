**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A technique that partitions LLM layers or attention heads across chiplets based on predicted workload patterns to balance compute and memory utilization. Enables efficient use of heterogeneous chiplets by matching model components to the most suitable compute/memory resources, reducing idle time and power.
