# AttentionAsConditioning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A theoretical reframing in which the transformer attention mechanism is treated as a conditional operator that modulates value retrieval based on the query-key interaction, analogous to conditioning in classical statistical learning. This framing lets TCC and iNEST researchers reason about attention-like routing in network topologies using established conditioning and estimation theory rather than ad hoc heuristics.
