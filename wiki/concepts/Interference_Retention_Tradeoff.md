# Interference_Retention_Tradeoff

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The fundamental tension in continual learning where acquiring new knowledge (interference) degrades previously learned representations, while preserving old knowledge (retention) limits the capacity for new learning. This tradeoff is critical for neuromorphic systems that must adapt in real-time without catastrophic forgetting, guiding the design of synaptic plasticity rules and network topologies.
