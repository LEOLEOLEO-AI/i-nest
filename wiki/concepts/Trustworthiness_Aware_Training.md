# Trustworthiness_Aware_Training

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-07

## Definition
A training paradigm that explicitly incorporates trustworthiness metrics (e.g., confidence, consistency) into the loss function to down-weight unreliable hyperedges or nodes during learning. Provides a principled way to handle unreliable data in in-network neuromorphic computing, where local processing units may produce inconsistent outputs due to hardware variability.
