# SDI_Switch_Embedded_Control

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-11

## Definition
Lightweight control logic embedded directly within each SDI (Switch-Data-Interconnect) switching node, enabling local decision-making and fast response without relying solely on the central controller. This distributed control approach reduces control-plane bottleneck and improves scalability and resilience in large-scale TCC networks.
