# ConvexNonconvexFloorplanning

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A chip floorplanning method that combines convex optimization for global placement with non-convex optimization for local refinement of block positions. Enables efficient exploration of interconnect-aware floorplans that balance global wirelength with local congestion, critical for TCC's topology-centric design.
