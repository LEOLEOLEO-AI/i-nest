# Anomaly_Mediated_Quantum_Criticality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A theoretical framework where quantum critical points under strong disorder are characterized and classified via topological anomalies rather than conventional order parameters. Provides a robust diagnostic for identifying and understanding quantum phase transitions in disordered systems, which is essential for designing fault-tolerant topological quantum devices.
