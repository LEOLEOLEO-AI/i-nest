**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A method for assigning compute and memory resources based on the physical or logical topology of the interconnect fabric, rather than assuming a flat uniform network. Critical for optimizing performance in TCC systems where communication costs dominate and topology-aware placement can drastically reduce congestion.
