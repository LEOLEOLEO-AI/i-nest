**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The use of spiking neural networks (SNNs) to perform inference on neural signals, where information is encoded in spike timing and processed asynchronously. Provides a biologically plausible and energy-efficient computational substrate for decoding brain signals, essential for implantable adaptive systems.
