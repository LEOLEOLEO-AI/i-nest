# WaferScaleMonolithicIntegration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A fabrication approach that integrates magneto-optical materials like Ce:YIG directly onto silicon wafers at full scale, enabling on-chip optical components without hybrid assembly. This enables scalable, low-cost production of photonic integrated circuits for TCC, where dense optical interconnects are critical for topology-aware data movement.
