# CurrentInjectionSNN

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A spiking neural network model where input signals are injected as analog current into neurons, modulating their membrane potential to encode information for fusion tasks. Enables direct integration of continuous sensor data (e.g., infrared/visible) into neuromorphic hardware without complex rate-coding conversion, improving energy efficiency.
