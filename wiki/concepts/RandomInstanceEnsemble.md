## Definition
A probability distribution over problem instances (e.g., random point sets) used to study typical-case behavior, as opposed to worst-case analysis, in computational complexity. Provides a statistical framework for evaluating average-case performance of TCC/iNEST algorithms, essential for designing robust systems under uncertain network topologies and workloads.
