# AsynchronousNeuromorphicHardware

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
An event-driven hardware architecture without a global clock, where computation and communication are triggered by spikes or changes in input signals. It aligns circuit timing with the inherently asynchronous nature of prediction-error signals, enabling energy-efficient, low-latency in-network learning for iNEST.
