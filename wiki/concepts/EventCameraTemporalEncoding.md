**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The method of converting asynchronous event streams from event cameras into spike-based temporal representations suitable for SNN processing, preserving precise timing information. This bridges the gap between event-based sensors and neuromorphic processors, a core challenge in iNEST for real-time, low-power perception systems.
