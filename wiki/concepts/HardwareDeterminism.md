# HardwareDeterminism

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-14

## Definition
A design principle where the timing and order of all operations are fixed in silicon, yielding repeatable, jitter-free execution for every tensor operation. Critical for TCC/iNEST because neuromorphic and network-centric systems require timing reproducibility and analyzable latency for real-time and safety-critical workloads.
