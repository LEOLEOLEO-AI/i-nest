# Emergent_Process_Topology

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A graph-theoretic representation in which macro-level business process behaviors are shown to arise irreducibly from local interactions among IoT nodes, making the network topology itself the unit of analysis rather than individual tasks. Gives TCC a formal substrate for detecting and predicting unpredictable IoT-enhanced process outcomes, and gives iNEST a target for embedding topology-sensitive computation directly in the sensor fabric.
