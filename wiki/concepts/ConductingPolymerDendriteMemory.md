# ConductingPolymerDendriteMemory

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A memory mechanism where sensor events are stored in the interconnection strength of conducting polymer dendrites, rather than in discrete digital states. This enables analog, physically distributed storage that could be directly integrated into network interconnects for event-driven computing.
