**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
A mathematical framework for reducing the dimensionality of dynamical systems defined on higher-order network structures (e.g., hypergraphs or simplicial complexes) while preserving key topological and dynamical properties. Enables scalable analysis and simulation of complex interconnected systems in TCC, where topology directly influences computation and communication dynamics.
