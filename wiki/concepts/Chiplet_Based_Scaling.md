**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An architectural approach that partitions a large neuromorphic system into multiple smaller chiplets connected via advanced packaging and interconnect. It makes interconnect topology a critical design factor for scaling spiking neural networks beyond single-die limits.
