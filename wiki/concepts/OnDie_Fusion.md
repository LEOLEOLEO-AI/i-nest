# OnDie_Fusion

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design methodology where previously external I/O or interconnect controllers are integrated into the same die as the compute fabric, eliminating separate packaging layers. This reduces packaging complexity and signal integrity issues, enabling higher bandwidth and lower energy per bit for NoC-centric computing.
