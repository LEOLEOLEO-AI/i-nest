# NeuromorphicSafetyBoundary

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A safety boundary defined by the spiking threshold of a neuron, where the neuron's membrane potential integrates safety-relevant signals and fires when the accumulated risk exceeds the boundary. This concept unifies safety theory with neuromorphic hardware, allowing safety boundaries to be implemented directly in neural dynamics, which is key for TCC's topology-aware computing and iNEST's in-network neuromorphic processing.
