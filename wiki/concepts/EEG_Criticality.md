## Definition
A measure of how close the brain's electrical activity (EEG) is to a critical state at the edge of chaos, characterized by power-law scaling of neuronal avalanches. Provides a passive, non-invasive biomarker for sleep depth that can be computed in real-time, enabling neurofeedback without active user engagement.
