A neuron model that balances biological plausibility and computational efficiency, capable of reproducing a rich repertoire of spiking patterns with two variables and a few parameters. Izhikevich neurons enable diverse neural dynamics in hardware, supporting richer computational capabilities than simpler models while remaining feasible for large-scale integration.
