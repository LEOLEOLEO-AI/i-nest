# Neuromorphic_Event_Process_Binding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The mechanism that binds asynchronous spike events emitted by in-network neuromorphic sensors to discrete business process activities, preserving temporal sparsity instead of resampling into synchronous task logs. Allows iNEST hardware to drive emergent-behavior analytics locally, avoiding cloud round trips that would destroy the temporal fidelity needed to observe emergence.
