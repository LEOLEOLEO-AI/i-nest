# Differential_Readout_Scheme

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A sensing method that compares the current or voltage from two FeFET branches to determine the stored state, improving noise margin and reliability. Enhances the robustness of memory read operations in neuromorphic circuits, critical for maintaining accuracy in analog-like computations.
