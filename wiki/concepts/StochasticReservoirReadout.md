# StochasticReservoirReadout

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A readout scheme that explicitly accounts for and exploits the stochastic fluctuations of reservoir states, using their distributional properties as features. Moves beyond deterministic reservoir computing, allowing iNEST systems to harness noise as a signal, which is essential for low-power, analog in-network neuromorphic hardware.
