**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
Charge or energy transport that is governed by the presence and motion of topological defects, rather than uniform lattice conduction. Relevant for both TCC (defect-based interconnects) and iNEST (defect-driven neuromorphic switching), enabling novel signal routing mechanisms.
