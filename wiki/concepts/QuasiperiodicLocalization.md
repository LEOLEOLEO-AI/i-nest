# QuasiperiodicLocalization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The phenomenon where wavefunctions in a quasicrystal become exponentially localized or extended depending on the non-Hermitian parameters, leading to metal-insulator transitions. Offers a mechanism for controlling signal propagation and confinement in network topologies, useful for memory and switching elements in neuromorphic computing.
