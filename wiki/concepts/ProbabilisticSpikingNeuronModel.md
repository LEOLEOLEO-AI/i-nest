**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
A neuron model that outputs stochastic spike trains whose statistics reflect underlying probability distributions, enabling direct implementation of probabilistic algorithms in spiking networks. Provides the computational primitive for spike-based belief propagation, making neuromorphic hardware capable of uncertainty-aware reasoning.
