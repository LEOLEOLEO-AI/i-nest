# Continuous_Control_Spiking_Stability

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The property of a spiking neural network to maintain stable and accurate output signals over time when applied to continuous control tasks, as opposed to classification tasks where transient errors are less consequential. This matters for iNEST as it highlights the need for SNN architectures and learning rules that ensure temporal stability, which is essential for deploying neuromorphic systems in real-world robotic or autonomous control loops.
