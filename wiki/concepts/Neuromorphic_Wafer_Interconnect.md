# Neuromorphic_Wafer_Interconnect

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A wafer-scale interconnection fabric that links neuromorphic cores and memristive arrays, treating the physical wiring topology as part of the computing substrate. This is central to TCC because it moves communication from a passive transport layer into an active computational element, enabling brain-scale topology to be realized directly on a wafer.
