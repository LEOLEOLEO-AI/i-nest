## Definition
A policy gradient method adapted for spiking neural networks, where action selection and reward-based updates occur via spike-timing-dependent plasticity or surrogate gradients. Enables on-chip learning for neuromorphic controllers, reducing reliance on cloud-based training and enabling autonomous terrain adaptation.
