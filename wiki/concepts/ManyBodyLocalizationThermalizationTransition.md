# ManyBodyLocalizationThermalizationTransition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The phase transition between a many-body localized (non-ergodic, memory-preserving) state and a thermalized (ergodic, memory-erasing) state, driven by interactions and disorder strength. Offers a physical model for understanding information retention vs. dissipation in network topologies, guiding the design of memory-preserving vs. reconfigurable computing substrates.
