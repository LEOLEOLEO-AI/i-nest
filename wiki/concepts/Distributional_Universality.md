# Distributional_Universality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A guarantee that a statistical or geometric threshold holds identically across broad classes of underlying data distributions rather than for one specific model. Allows TCC and iNEST designers to trust capacity and generalization bounds without assuming a particular input distribution.
