# BCU_Operation

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-02

## Definition
A fused computation and communication operation performed by a Broadcast/Compute Unit (BCU), such as per-layer addition during message traversal through the network. BCU operations eliminate separate compute steps by integrating arithmetic into data movement, a key mechanism for reducing latency in topology-centric architectures.
