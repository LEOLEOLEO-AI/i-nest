## Definition
A generalized Lotka-Volterra model extends the classical predator-prey framework to N interacting components with nonlinear self- and cross-interaction coefficients, governing the coexistence and stability of population-like dynamical states. It offers a canonical dynamical substrate for encoding stable computational states and coexistence regimes in TCC/iNEST network architectures.
