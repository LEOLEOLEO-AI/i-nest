# EarlyExitInference

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A dynamic inference strategy where a classifier is attached to intermediate layers of a deep network, allowing predictions to be made before the full forward pass is completed. Reduces average inference latency and energy consumption, which is essential for real-time, low-power neuromorphic and topology-centric computing pipelines.
