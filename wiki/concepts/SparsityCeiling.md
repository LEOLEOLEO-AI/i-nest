**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The theoretical limit beyond which further reducing spike activity in a spiking neural network yields diminishing or negative energy returns due to fixed overheads and information loss. Defines the practical boundary for energy-efficient SNN design, guiding where activity-accuracy trade-offs are viable in neuromorphic hardware.
