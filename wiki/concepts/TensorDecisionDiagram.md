# TensorDecisionDiagram

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A data structure that represents quantum circuit states and operations as a directed acyclic graph of tensor contractions, enabling compact memory representation of high-dimensional quantum states. Critical for simulating quantum circuits on classical hardware, as it reduces exponential memory overhead, which is essential for TCC's topology-aware resource allocation in distributed quantum-classical interconnects.
