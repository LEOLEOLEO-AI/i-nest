## Definition
The principle that complex intelligent behaviors arise from the systematic combination of simpler neural processing units, akin to grammatical composition in language. Enables modular design of neuromorphic hardware and algorithms, allowing iNEST systems to reuse and recombine basic computational motifs for diverse tasks.
