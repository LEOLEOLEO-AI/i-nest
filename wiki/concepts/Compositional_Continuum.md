## Definition
A theoretical framework describing how intelligence emerges from the graded, hierarchical reuse of neural modules, where modules can be flexibly combined at varying levels of abstraction. Provides a guiding principle for designing in-network neuromorphic systems that can dynamically assemble complex behaviors from simpler, reusable neural primitives.
