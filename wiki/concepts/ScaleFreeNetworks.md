## Definition
A network topology where a few nodes (hubs) have many connections while most nodes have few, following a power-law degree distribution. Understanding scale-free networks is critical for TCC/iNEST because their hub-based fragility and robustness trade-offs directly inform the design of resilient in-network computing architectures.
