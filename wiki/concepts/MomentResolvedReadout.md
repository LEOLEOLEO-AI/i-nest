# MomentResolvedReadout

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A readout technique that extracts statistical moments (e.g., mean, variance, higher-order cumulants) of a stochastic dynamical system's output, rather than just the instantaneous state or time-averaged value. Enables richer information extraction from Langevin-type reservoir dynamics, potentially increasing computational capacity without adding physical nodes.
