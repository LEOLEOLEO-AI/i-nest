## Definition
A fast, self-contained neural circuit within a body segment that directly links sensory inputs to motor outputs for rapid local adjustments. Demonstrates how in-network processing can achieve low-latency reflexive responses without relaying all data to a central processor, a key principle for iNEST edge intelligence.
