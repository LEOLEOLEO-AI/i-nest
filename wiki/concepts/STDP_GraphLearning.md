**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The application of spike-timing-dependent plasticity to learn graph topology or node representations through local spike-time correlations. Enables unsupervised, online learning of graph features directly on neuromorphic hardware, reducing reliance on external training and supporting in-network adaptation.
