# FrozenRateOperator

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A fixed, non-adaptive operator derived from the complete larval connectome that maps input activity to output responses without plasticity. Demonstrates that static connectome topology alone can produce complex, input-routing behaviors, relevant for neuromorphic hardware that lacks online learning.
