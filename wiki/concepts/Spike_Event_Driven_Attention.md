**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
An attention mechanism in spiking transformers that operates on spike trains, computing attention weights based on spike timing and rates rather than dense matrix multiplications. Reduces computational overhead and aligns with event-driven hardware, making it suitable for scalable TCC and iNEST implementations.
