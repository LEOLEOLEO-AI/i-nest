## Definition
A metal-insulator transition in a lattice driven by an incommensurate (quasiperiodic) potential, characterized by multifractal eigenstates at the critical point instead of simple exponential or power-law localization. Multifractal critical states are a promising substrate for topology-centric computing primitives that require robust, non-integer-dimensional information encoding in in-network neuromorphic fabrics.
