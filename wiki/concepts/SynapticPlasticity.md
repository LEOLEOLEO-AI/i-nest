**Domain**: iNEST

## Definition
The biological-inspired mechanism of modifying synaptic weights based on local activity patterns, often including Hebbian and STDP-like rules. Foundation for local learning in neuromorphic hardware, allowing adaptation without external supervision, key for in-network autonomy.
