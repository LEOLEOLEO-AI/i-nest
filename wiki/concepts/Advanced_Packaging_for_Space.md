**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The use of 2.5D/3D chiplet packaging techniques adapted for thermal, vacuum, and radiation environments to co-integrate diverse compute dies. Enables high-bandwidth, low-latency interconnects between compute elements, essential for TCC's topology-centric data flow and iNEST's neuromorphic cores.
