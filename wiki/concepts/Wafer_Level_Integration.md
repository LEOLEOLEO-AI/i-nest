# Wafer_Level_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-24

## Definition
A manufacturing and architectural approach that integrates an entire wafer of dies as one logical device using 5nm process and wafer-level packaging. Enables extreme transistor counts and uniform interconnect, offering a blueprint for topology-centric systems that avoid partitioning across multiple chips.
