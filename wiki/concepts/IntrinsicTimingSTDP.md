# IntrinsicTimingSTDP

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A variant of spike-timing-dependent plasticity where synaptic weight updates are computed directly from the intrinsic timing of pre- and post-synaptic spikes without explicit clock-based synchronization. Enables fully asynchronous, event-driven on-chip learning, critical for energy-efficient neuromorphic processors in iNEST.
