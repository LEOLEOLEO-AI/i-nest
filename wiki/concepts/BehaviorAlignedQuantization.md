# BehaviorAlignedQuantization

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A quantization method for spiking neural networks that aligns the quantization levels with the neuron's behavioral firing patterns to minimize information loss at low bit widths. Enables low-bit SNN deployment on neuromorphic hardware without sacrificing accuracy, critical for energy-efficient in-network processing.
