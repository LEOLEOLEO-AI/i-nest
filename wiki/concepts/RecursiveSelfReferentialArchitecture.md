## Definition
A cognitive architecture where the system's processing loops back on itself, enabling self-modeling and recursive refinement of its own representations. This is central to TCC/iNEST as it suggests how in-network or neuromorphic substrates could implement self-referential loops for adaptive, general-purpose computation.
