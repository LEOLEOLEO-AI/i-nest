## Definition
A computational process that mimics the physical annealing of spin glasses to find low-energy states, used to solve Ising-formulated optimization problems. Leverages the natural dynamics of neuromorphic systems to efficiently search for near-optimal solutions in decoding, potentially outperforming digital approaches in speed and energy.
