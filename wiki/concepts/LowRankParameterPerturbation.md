## Definition
A technique that restricts weight updates to a low-dimensional subspace by applying rank-constrained random perturbations, reducing the search space and improving sample efficiency in evolutionary training. Balances exploration and computational efficiency, making it scalable for large SNN models and suitable for resource-constrained TCC/iNEST deployments.
