# Topology_Bounded_Entanglement_Flow

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The concept that the rate and direction of entanglement teleportation in a network are constrained by the physical and logical topology of the interconnect, not just by quantum channel properties. This guides the design of TCC topologies that maximize entanglement flow efficiency, reducing communication overhead in distributed neuromorphic or quantum-classical hybrid systems.
