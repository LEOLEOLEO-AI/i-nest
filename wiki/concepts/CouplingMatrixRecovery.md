# CouplingMatrixRecovery

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process of estimating the full pairwise interaction matrix (weights and signs) of a network from observed state trajectories, here specifically from a single snapshot at relative equilibrium. Directly maps to discovering physical link strengths in TCC interconnects, enabling fault detection and adaptive routing without invasive probing.
