**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The practice of embedding reliability and adaptation logic directly within network forwarding elements rather than at endpoints. Bridges TCC and iNEST by moving computational reliability functions into the data plane, reducing latency and overhead.
