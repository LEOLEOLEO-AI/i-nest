# Ion_Modulated_Synaptic_Plasticity

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The mechanism by which ion migration in organic transistors alters channel conductance, emulating biological synaptic weight updates for learning. Provides a physical basis for implementing Hebbian learning and spike-timing-dependent plasticity in neuromorphic circuits, crucial for energy-efficient in-network learning.
