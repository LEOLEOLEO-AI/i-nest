# OnDeviceInference

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The execution of neural network inference directly on the edge device (e.g., agricultural robot) without cloud offloading, ensuring low latency and privacy. Critical for TCC systems where distributed, low-latency decision-making is required at the network edge, reducing reliance on centralized compute.
