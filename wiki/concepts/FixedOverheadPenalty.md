# FixedOverheadPenalty

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The energy cost incurred by a spiking network that does not scale with spike count, such as memory access, control logic, and interconnect maintenance. Sets a floor on energy savings in TCC/iNEST architectures, meaning that interconnect and memory design must be optimized to lower this overhead for effective sparsity exploitation.
