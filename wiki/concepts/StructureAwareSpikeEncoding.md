# StructureAwareSpikeEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of encoding spike trains that incorporates local topological or temporal structure into the attention computation, rather than treating spikes as independent events. This enhances the representational capacity of SNNs for structured data, improving accuracy while maintaining spike-based efficiency.
