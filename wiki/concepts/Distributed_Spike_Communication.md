**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
The efficient exchange of spike events between GPU nodes during SNN training, optimized to minimize latency and bandwidth usage in high-performance computing clusters. Reduces communication bottlenecks in distributed SNN training, making it feasible to scale up to thousands of GPUs without performance degradation.
