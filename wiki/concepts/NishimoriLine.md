# NishimoriLine

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A special line in the temperature-disorder phase diagram of spin glass models where the ferromagnetic and disordered phases meet, simplifying the analysis of phase transitions. Provides a tractable theoretical framework for understanding phase transitions in disordered systems, which can inform the design of robust neuromorphic computing architectures.
