## Definition
A coding scheme where each odorant is represented by a sparse, high-dimensional activity pattern across many neurons, maximizing discriminability with minimal energy. Offers a power-efficient encoding paradigm for neuromorphic sensors and network edge devices handling complex, noisy data streams.
