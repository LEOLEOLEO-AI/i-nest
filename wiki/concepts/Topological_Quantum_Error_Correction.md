# Topological_Quantum_Error_Correction

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A class of quantum error correction codes where logical information is encoded in global topological properties of a many-body system, providing inherent protection against local errors. This is the foundational framework for fault-tolerant quantum computation, and its interplay with disorder and rare events is central to both TCC and neuromorphic-inspired decoding strategies.
