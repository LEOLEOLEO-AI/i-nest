# CliquePercolation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A percolation process where clusters are formed by k-cliques that share (k-1)-cliques, defining higher-order connectivity in random geometric graphs. Provides a topological framework for analyzing higher-order connectivity in network architectures, relevant for robust communication in TCC and synaptic connectivity in iNEST.
