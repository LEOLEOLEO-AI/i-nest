## Definition
The process of reducing a symmetric base state into differentiated, ordered forms, categorized into four types of symmetry breaking: action, space, time, and relation. This formalizes how generative structure emerges from uniformity, giving TCC and iNEST a shared vocabulary for describing topological reorganization.
