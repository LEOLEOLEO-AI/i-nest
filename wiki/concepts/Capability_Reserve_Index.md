# Capability_Reserve_Index

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The ratio R_I(t) = C_ST_sys(t) / C_ST_env(t) comparing system capability to environmental demands, where R_I < 1, ≈1, and >1 indicate insufficiency, match, and surplus respectively. Offers a standardized way to assess whether a TCC system can meet task requirements, crucial for resource allocation and topology reconfiguration decisions.
