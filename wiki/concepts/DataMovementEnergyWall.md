# DataMovementEnergyWall

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The observed bottleneck in conventional architectures where roughly 90% of energy is spent on moving data between sensors, buses, memory, and processors rather than on actual computation. The need to break through this wall motivates TCC/iNEST's shift toward topology-centric, in-network, and neuromorphic computing designs.
