## Definition
A topological insulator state induced by disorder in an antiferromagnetic system, where Anderson localization effects and magnetic order combine to produce topologically protected edge states. Relevant for TCC/iNEST as it explores novel material platforms that could enable robust, low-energy neuromorphic devices with topological protection against scattering.
