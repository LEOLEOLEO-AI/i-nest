**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A method where sensor events are mapped to the magnitude of interconnection strength between dendrites, allowing temporal event patterns to be represented as spatial conductivity variations. This bridges event-driven sensing and neuromorphic storage, enabling TCC/iNEST systems to process and retain event streams without conventional memory hierarchies.
