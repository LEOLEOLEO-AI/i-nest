# SpikingTransformerBottleneck

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The computational and memory overhead incurred when applying global self-attention to spike-based representations, due to the dense pairwise interactions across all positions. Identifying and mitigating this bottleneck is critical for making transformer-style SNNs practical for real-time, energy-efficient in-network processing.
