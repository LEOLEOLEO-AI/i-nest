# DirectConnect_Topology

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A network topology where compute nodes are connected directly via high-bandwidth links without intermediate switches or routers, enabling low-latency, high-throughput communication. Fundamental to TCC because it eliminates switch bottlenecks, making routing decisions and data movement more predictable and efficient for distributed AI workloads.
