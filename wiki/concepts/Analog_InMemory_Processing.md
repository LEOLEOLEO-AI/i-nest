## Definition
Performing arithmetic operations (e.g., multiply-accumulate) directly in the analog domain within memory cells, avoiding digital conversion and data shuttling. Exploits physical properties of ferroelectric devices to achieve high energy efficiency and speed, essential for scalable TCC/iNEST systems.
