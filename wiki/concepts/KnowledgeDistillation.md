# KnowledgeDistillation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training method where a smaller 'student' model is trained to mimic the outputs or intermediate representations of a larger 'teacher' model, transferring knowledge while reducing complexity. Facilitates the creation of lightweight models suitable for in-network or neuromorphic deployment, preserving accuracy while meeting strict computational constraints.
