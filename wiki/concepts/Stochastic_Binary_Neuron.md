# Stochastic_Binary_Neuron

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A neuron model that outputs a binary state (0 or 1) with a probability determined by the weighted sum of its inputs, rather than a deterministic threshold. Provides a hardware-friendly, noise-tolerant computational primitive for in-network neuromorphic systems, enabling probabilistic inference and energy-efficient spike-based processing.
