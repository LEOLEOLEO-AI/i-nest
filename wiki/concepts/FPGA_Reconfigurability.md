**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The ability of FPGAs to dynamically reconfigure logic circuits in-flight, enabling adaptive compute topologies for changing space workloads. Allows TCC architectures to morph between different neural network topologies or signal processing pipelines without physical hardware swaps.
