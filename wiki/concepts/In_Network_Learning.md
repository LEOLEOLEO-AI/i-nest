# In_Network_Learning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-23

## Definition
In-network learning is the paradigm in which weight updates and error-driven or correlation-driven adaptation are executed at or near the synapse within the interconnect fabric, rather than in a centralized processor. It is the core operational principle distinguishing iNEST hardware from conventional accelerators, and it directly determines achievable energy, latency, and scalability of neuromorphic systems.
