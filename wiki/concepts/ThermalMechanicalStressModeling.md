# ThermalMechanicalStressModeling

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
Modeling of stress and deformation in encapsulated packages due to coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) mismatches between materials. Essential for designing TCC hardware where heterogeneous materials and stacked layers create complex stress fields that can degrade performance or cause premature failure in neuromorphic or topology-centric circuits.
