## Definition
Symmetry-protected topological phases in bosonic systems that remain distinct from trivial phases only when certain symmetries are preserved, characterized by short-range entanglement and gapless boundary modes. Provides a theoretical framework for classifying topologically ordered states that could be used to design robust, symmetry-protected information storage or processing in TCC/iNEST architectures.
