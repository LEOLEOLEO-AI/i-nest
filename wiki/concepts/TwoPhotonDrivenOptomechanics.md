**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A cavity optomechanical system where the optical drive is via two-photon (parametric) processes, leading to effective nonlinear phonon-photon interactions and squeezed dynamics. Offers a scalable platform for generating non-classical states and nonlinear responses useful for in-network signal processing and quantum reservoir computing.
