## Definition
A methodological framework that examines the structure and evolution of citation relationships among publications to infer the intellectual organization of a research field. This method is crucial for TCC/iNEST to quantitatively assess how different sub-topics (e.g., network topology design vs. neuromorphic computing) are becoming integrated or fragmented over time.
