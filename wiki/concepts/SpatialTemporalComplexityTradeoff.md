# SpatialTemporalComplexityTradeoff

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
A principle whereby increasing spatial complexity (e.g., wafer-scale integration) compensates for reduced temporal complexity (lower clock frequency) to maintain high computational efficiency. It provides a theoretical foundation for energy-efficient AI hardware that does not depend on the most advanced lithography, central to TCC architecture design.
