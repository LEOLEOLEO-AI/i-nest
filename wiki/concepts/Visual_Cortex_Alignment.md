# Visual_Cortex_Alignment

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A metric measuring the similarity between a neural network's internal feature representations and the neural activity patterns of the early visual cortex (V1). This alignment is a key benchmark for evaluating how biologically faithful a neuromorphic model is, and its degradation under training informs whether iNEST architectures can truly emulate cortical processing.
