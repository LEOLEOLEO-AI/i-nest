# TopologyAsFirstClassResource

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-05

## Definition
A paradigm where communication topology is treated as a software-definable, dynamically reconfigurable resource that is co-optimized with computation, rather than a fixed constraint. This is the core philosophical shift of TCC, enabling computation to adapt to and exploit network structure for performance gains.
