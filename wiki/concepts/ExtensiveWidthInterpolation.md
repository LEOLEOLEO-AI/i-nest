## Definition
A regime in shallow neural networks where the hidden layer width scales extensively with the dataset size, and the network operates near the interpolation threshold (zero training error). Defines a critical operating point for distributed or in-network learning where generalization behavior transitions sharply, informing topology design for optimal accuracy-efficiency trade-offs.
