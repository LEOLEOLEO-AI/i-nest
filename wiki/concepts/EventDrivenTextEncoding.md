# EventDrivenTextEncoding

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method to convert handwritten text images into spike trains or event-based representations that preserve temporal and spatial features for spiking neural network processing. Critical for translating continuous visual input into the discrete, asynchronous spike domain required by neuromorphic processors, enabling efficient inference.
