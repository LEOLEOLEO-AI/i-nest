# ContinualLearningOnNeuromorphic

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training paradigm where neuromorphic hardware incrementally learns new action classes from event camera streams without catastrophic forgetting of previously learned actions. Enables lifelong adaptation of event-driven systems in dynamic environments, a core requirement for in-network neuromorphic edge intelligence.
