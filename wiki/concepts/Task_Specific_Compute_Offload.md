# Task_Specific_Compute_Offload

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The method of assigning distinct inference sub-tasks (e.g., attention, FFN, tokenization) to specialized hardware based on their unique compute and memory profiles. Enables TCC to embed lightweight compute in network switches or edge nodes, reducing data movement and latency for specific inference stages.
