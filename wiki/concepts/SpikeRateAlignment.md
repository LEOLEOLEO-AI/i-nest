# SpikeRateAlignment

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
A technique that aligns the firing rates of pseudo-spikes with the activation distributions of ANN neurons to ensure effective knowledge transfer during distillation. Critical for maintaining information fidelity when converting continuous ANN activations to discrete spike trains in cross-architecture learning.
