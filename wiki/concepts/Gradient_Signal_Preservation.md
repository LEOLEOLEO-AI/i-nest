# Gradient_Signal_Preservation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-21

## Definition
The maintenance of informative gradient flow through a network to prevent vanishing or exploding gradients that impair learning. Preserving gradient signals is essential for reliable training of deep or recurrent structures in TCC/iNEST, where network topology can amplify or attenuate gradient propagation.
