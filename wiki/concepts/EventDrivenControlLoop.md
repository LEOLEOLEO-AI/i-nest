# EventDrivenControlLoop

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A closed-loop control architecture where actions are triggered by incoming spikes rather than fixed clock cycles, enabling asynchronous responses. Reduces latency and power consumption in navigation, a key advantage for resource-constrained neuromorphic edge devices.
