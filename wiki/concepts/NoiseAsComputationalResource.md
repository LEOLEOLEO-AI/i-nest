# NoiseAsComputationalResource

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The principle of treating intrinsic or injected noise not as a disturbance but as a carrier of gradient information for learning. Transforms a major hardware challenge (noise) into a functional advantage for neuromorphic chips, reducing the need for precise analog circuits.
