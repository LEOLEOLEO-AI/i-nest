# SNN_Backpropagation_Through_Time

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training technique that applies surrogate gradient-based backpropagation across the temporal dimension of spiking neuron dynamics. Enables end-to-end learning of complex tracking tasks in spiking networks, overcoming the non-differentiability of spike generation.
