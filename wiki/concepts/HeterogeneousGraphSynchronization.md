# HeterogeneousGraphSynchronization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The process by which neurons on graphs with non-uniform connectivity (e.g., varying degrees, weights) achieve phase-locked or frequency-locked synchronous activity. Understanding synchronization on heterogeneous graphs is essential for designing topology-aware computing architectures that maintain coherent timing across irregular network structures.
