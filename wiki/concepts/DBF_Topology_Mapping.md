# DBF_Topology_Mapping

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The mapping of digital beamforming algorithms onto reconfigurable topology-centric compute arrays instead of traditional imported FPGA implementations. It addresses the domestic DBF processor gap while reducing power consumption and improving flexibility for signal-processing workloads.
