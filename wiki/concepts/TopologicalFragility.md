## Definition
The susceptibility of a network's functionality to degradation or collapse when specific nodes or links are removed, often due to hub dependence. Understanding topological fragility guides the design of resilient TCC architectures and neuromorphic substrates that must tolerate link or node failures without losing computational integrity.
