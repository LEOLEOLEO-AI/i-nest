## Definition
A framework for non-Hermitian systems where topological properties are defined without requiring parity-time (PT) symmetry, allowing a broader class of gain/loss profiles. Expands the design space for non-Hermitian devices, enabling more flexible topological protection in TCC architectures and neuromorphic hardware.
