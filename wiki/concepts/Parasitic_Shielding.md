# Parasitic_Shielding

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The use of conductive shielding structures around signal paths to reduce parasitic capacitance and inductance in vertical interconnects. Mitigates signal degradation in high-density 3D stacks, a key requirement for scalable TCC architectures.
