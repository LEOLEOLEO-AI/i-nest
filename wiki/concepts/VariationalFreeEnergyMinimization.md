# VariationalFreeEnergyMinimization

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A principle stating that adaptive networks continuously adjust their internal states and connectivity to minimize surprise (free energy) about their environment, as formalized in the free-energy principle. Provides a universal optimization criterion for in-network learning and adaptation, enabling TCC architectures to self-tune under dynamic workloads.
