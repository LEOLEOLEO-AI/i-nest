## Definition
A stimulation paradigm that dynamically adjusts stimulation parameters based on real-time neural feedback, rather than using fixed, open-loop settings. Reduces side effects and improves therapeutic efficacy by responding to pathological neural activity, a core application for in-network neuromorphic processing.
