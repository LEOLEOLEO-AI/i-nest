# Hypergraph_Mapping

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A mapping methodology that models SNN neuron clusters and their multi-target spike communications as hyperedges before assigning them to mesh NoC tiles. It captures multi-way spike dependencies beyond pairwise graph edges, enabling more topology-aware TCC and iNEST deployment.
