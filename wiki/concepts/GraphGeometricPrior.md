# GraphGeometricPrior

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A prior distribution derived from geometric properties (e.g., curvature, distance) of known functional brain graphs, used to regularize generative models. Injects domain-specific structural knowledge into generation, enabling more realistic and efficient network designs for both TCC and iNEST.
