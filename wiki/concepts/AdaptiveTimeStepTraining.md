# AdaptiveTimeStepTraining

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A training methodology that dynamically adjusts the number of simulation time steps per sample during spike-based neural radiance field optimization, allocating more steps to complex scenes and fewer to simple ones. Enables efficient training of spiking neural networks for 3D scene representation, reducing computational cost while maintaining reconstruction quality—critical for real-time neuromorphic rendering.
