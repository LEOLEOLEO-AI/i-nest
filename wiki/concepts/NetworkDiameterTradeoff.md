# NetworkDiameterTradeoff

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-06

## Definition
The principle that network diameter—the maximum number of hops between any two nodes—is the primary driver for choosing a topology, and that no single topology can simultaneously minimize diameter for both training and inference workloads. This justifies TCC's core premise that a reconfigurable base with multiple 'pages' is needed to adapt topology per workload, rather than a fixed torus.
