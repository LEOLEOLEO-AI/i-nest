## Definition
Emerging non-volatile memory devices based on CMOS-compatible materials that exhibit resistive switching, used here as physical substrates for reservoir computing. These devices enable in-memory and neuromorphic computation, offering a path to energy-efficient hardware implementations of reservoir networks.
