## Definition
A hierarchical organization of energy minima on random graphs where barrier heights form an ultrametric structure, meaning distances between minima satisfy the strong triangle inequality. Provides a topological framework for understanding how barrier hierarchies emerge from graph structure, which can inform routing and state-space organization in TCC/iNEST systems.
