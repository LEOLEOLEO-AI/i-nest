# Analog_Synaptic_Behavior

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The property of memristors to exhibit continuous, programmable conductance states that mimic biological synaptic weights, enabling analog weight updates during learning. This enables high-density, low-power neural network implementations, which are essential for scalable neuromorphic systems in iNEST and TCC frameworks.
