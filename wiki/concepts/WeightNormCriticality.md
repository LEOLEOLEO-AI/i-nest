# WeightNormCriticality

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A mechanism where the interaction between weight normalization and weight decay creates a critical boundary in weight norm space, beyond which training becomes unstable and produces loss spikes. This is critical for TCC/iNEST systems because distributed training over interconnected topologies can amplify such instabilities, requiring topology-aware normalization strategies.
