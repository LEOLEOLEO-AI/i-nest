## Definition
The practice of computing or approximating the spatiotemporal complexity (CST) metric directly within the network or near-network infrastructure, rather than on centralized data collectors. This reduces the cost of full-data backhaul and enables scalable, real-time complexity assessment under TCC's interconnect and topology constraints.
