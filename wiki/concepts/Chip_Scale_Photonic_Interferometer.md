# Chip_Scale_Photonic_Interferometer

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-15

## Definition
A monolithically integrated waveguide mesh of beam splitters and phase shifters fabricated on a single chip that implements the linear-optical unitary transformation of a boson sampling circuit. It is the canonical example of a fabricated, dense, low-latency interconnection topology whose routing and loss properties TCC must model and optimize.
