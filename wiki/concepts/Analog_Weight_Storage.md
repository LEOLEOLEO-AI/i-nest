# Analog_Weight_Storage

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-01

## Definition
The use of memristor conductance levels to represent synaptic weights in a continuous analog manner, as opposed to discrete digital bits. This enables high-density, low-power neural network implementations, which is essential for scaling TCC/iNEST architectures to large-scale neuromorphic systems.
