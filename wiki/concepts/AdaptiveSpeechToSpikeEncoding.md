## Definition
A method for converting speech signals into spike trains where encoding parameters (e.g., threshold, time constant) are dynamically adjusted based on input characteristics to optimize spike timing fidelity. Enables efficient and accurate speech processing in spiking neural networks, critical for neuromorphic auditory systems in iNEST edge applications.
