**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A method of assigning distinct computational subtasks to separate dendritic branches within a single neuron, enabling parallel processing and task isolation at the sub-neuronal level. Provides a scalable strategy for distributing multi-task workloads across neuromorphic hardware, aligning with TCC's goal of topology-aware computation and reducing cross-task interference in interconnected spiking networks.
