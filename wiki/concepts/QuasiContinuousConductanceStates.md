# QuasiContinuousConductanceStates

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A memristive crossbar fabric design that achieves a near-continuous range of programmable conductance levels in resistive synapses, enabling analog weight storage for neural networks. This enables high-precision on-chip neural network training and inference without external memory, critical for energy-efficient neuromorphic computing.
