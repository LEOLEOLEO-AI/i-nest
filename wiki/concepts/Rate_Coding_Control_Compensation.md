## Definition
A method that attempts to compensate for ANN-to-SNN conversion errors by adjusting spike rates or thresholds specifically for control signals, but which fails in continuous tasks due to temporal dynamics. This highlights why simple rate-based compensation is insufficient for iNEST control applications, motivating more sophisticated temporal error-correction mechanisms.
