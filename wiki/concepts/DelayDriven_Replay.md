# DelayDriven_Replay

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
A replay mechanism in which the direction and ordering of reactivated sequences are generated by heterogeneous axonal/synaptic transmission delays in the recurrent network rather than by learned weight asymmetries. It shows TCC/iNEST architectures can obtain temporally structured, direction-selective sequence generation from delay topology alone, removing the need for precisely tuned bidirectional plasticity in on-chip recurrent fabrics.
