# NonGaussianPropagationKernel

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A propagation kernel in neuromorphic networks that deviates from the standard Gaussian spread, enabling long-range or sub-diffusive signal mixing in 2D. Allows iNEST reservoirs to achieve richer temporal dynamics and memory effects without adding physical disorder, improving computational capacity in spiking neural networks.
