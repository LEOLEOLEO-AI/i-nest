# PDN_Aware_Voltage_Droop_Control

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A control mechanism that uses power delivery network (PDN) impedance and transient characteristics to proactively mitigate voltage droops in 2.5D chiplet architectures. Enables reliable operation of PIM chiplets under high current transients, which is critical for maintaining performance and avoiding errors in dense compute-in-memory workloads.
