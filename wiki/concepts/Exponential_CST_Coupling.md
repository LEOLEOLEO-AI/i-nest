## Definition
The Core Spacetime (CST) coupling coefficient Γ_st enters the CST formula as an exponential term e^(α·Γ_st), not as a linear additive term α·Γ_st. This establishes the algebraic foundation for the '1+1>2' proof, showing that spacetime topology interactions amplify nonlinearly rather than linearly.
