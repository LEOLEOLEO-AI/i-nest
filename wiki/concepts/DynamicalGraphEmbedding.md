# DynamicalGraphEmbedding

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The mapping of a dynamical system's state space onto the graph structure of an ultra-small-world network, where node dynamics encode system variables and edges encode interactions. Bridges TCC and iNEST by providing a unified framework for implementing complex computations directly on network topology, enabling co-design of communication and computation.
