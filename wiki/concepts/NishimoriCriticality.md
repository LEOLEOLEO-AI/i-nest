# NishimoriCriticality

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A special multicritical point in disordered spin systems where the quenched disorder and thermal fluctuations are tuned to a self-dual symmetry condition, leading to universal scaling behavior. Provides a theoretical framework for understanding phase transitions in topologically ordered systems under disorder, relevant for robust quantum error correction in TCC architectures.
