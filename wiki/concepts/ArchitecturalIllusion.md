# ArchitecturalIllusion

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The phenomenon where a network's performance is attributed to its depth, but in reality stems from nested or recursive substructures that mimic depth without true hierarchical computation. Guides TCC/iNEST designers to focus on functional topology rather than layer count, enabling more compact and efficient in-network neuromorphic implementations.
