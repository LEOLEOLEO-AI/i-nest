# NoTransitionRegime

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The parameter regime where, despite varying measurement rates, the entanglement entropy scaling remains unchanged (e.g., always area-law), indicating absence of a measurement-induced phase transition. Identifies conditions under which measurement does not alter the topological or entanglement structure, guiding design of stable, measurement-immune interconnects in iNEST.
