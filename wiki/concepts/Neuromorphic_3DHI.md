## Definition
Neuromorphic 3DHI is the integration of spiking neural network hardware with 3D heterogeneous packaging to emulate brain-like connectivity and plasticity in a compact, energy-efficient form. This is critical for iNEST because 3D stacking provides the dense, local connectivity needed for synaptic arrays and reduces the energy cost of spike propagation.
