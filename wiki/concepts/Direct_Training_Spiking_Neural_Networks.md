## Definition
A training approach for SNNs that bypasses surrogate gradients or conversion from ANNs, using spike-based backpropagation through time to optimize network parameters directly. Enables more efficient and biologically plausible learning on neuromorphic hardware, reducing latency and energy consumption compared to indirect methods.
