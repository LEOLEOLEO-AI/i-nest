# HybridBayesianLearning

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A learning approach combining Bayesian inference with hybrid (discrete-continuous) optimization to estimate parameters of hierarchical stochastic models from observed beat patterns. Enables robust parameter estimation in TCC/iNEST systems where uncertainty and multi-scale temporal dependencies are inherent.
