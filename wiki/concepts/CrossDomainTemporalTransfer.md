## Definition
The ability of a large time series model to transfer learned temporal patterns from one domain (e.g., traffic) to another (e.g., neural signals) without retraining. Critical for iNEST to reuse pre-trained network intelligence across diverse sensor modalities, reducing on-chip learning overhead.
