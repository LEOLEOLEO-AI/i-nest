# SDI_Software_Defined_Interconnect

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A software-defined interconnect abstraction in which routing, bandwidth, and topology of the on-chip or in-network fabric are programmatically configured at runtime rather than fixed at fabrication. SDI lets iNEST fabrics retune their communication topology to match neuromorphic workload locality, which is essential for scalable in-network computing.
