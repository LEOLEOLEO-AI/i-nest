# ModularNeuromorphicReconfiguration

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design principle where the neuromorphic architecture is composed of interchangeable modules that can be enabled, disabled, or rewired at runtime to alter network behavior. Allows iNEST systems to adapt to changing inference tasks or environmental inputs without full system reset, improving energy efficiency and real-time responsiveness.
