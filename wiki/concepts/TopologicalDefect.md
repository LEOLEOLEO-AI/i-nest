# TopologicalDefect

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A structural flaw in Transformer architectures where the attention mechanism's permutation-equivariant processing fails to preserve the topological connectivity of input data, leading to information loss in high-dimensional relational structures. Identifies a fundamental limitation in current neural architectures that motivates topology-preserving designs in TCC and iNEST systems.
