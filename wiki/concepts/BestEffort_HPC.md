## Definition
A computing model in which high-performance execution is provided without hard guarantees on timing, throughput, or correctness, requiring empirical characterization rather than formal assurance. TCC and iNEST fabrics often run on best-effort substrates (wafer-scale links, NoC arbitration, neuromorphic event routing), so workloads must be profiled to know what reliability can actually be trusted.
