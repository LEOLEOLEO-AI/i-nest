# Quantum_Safe_Slicing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
Isolation and resource-partitioning of network slices in which each slice is provisioned with its own post-quantum or QKD-derived key material and trust policy rather than relying on a shared classical key hierarchy. It forces TCC and iNEST to treat cryptographic trust domains as slice-level topology constraints, coupling secure multi-tenancy with in-network processing placement.
