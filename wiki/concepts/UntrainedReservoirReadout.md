**Domain**: Cross

## Definition
A readout mechanism that uses only linear regression or simple classical post-processing on the quantum reservoir's output, avoiding backpropagation through the quantum circuit. Reduces training complexity and hardware feedback loops, making it feasible for lightweight in-network inference in TCC/iNEST architectures.
