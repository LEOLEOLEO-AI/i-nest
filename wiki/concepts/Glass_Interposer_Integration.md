**Domain**: TCC

## Definition
The use of glass as a substrate material for interposers, offering low dielectric loss and tunable coefficient of thermal expansion for high-frequency 3D_Integration. Provides a superior alternative to silicon interposers for TCC's high-speed, low-power interconnect fabrics.
