# Glass_Interposer_Integration

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The use of glass as a substrate material for interposers, offering low dielectric loss and tunable coefficient of thermal expansion for high-frequency 3D integration. Provides a superior alternative to silicon interposers for TCC's high-speed, low-power interconnect fabrics.
