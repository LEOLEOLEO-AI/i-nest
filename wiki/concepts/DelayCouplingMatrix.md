# DelayCouplingMatrix

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A matrix encoding the topology of connections along with the specific delay associated with each link, replacing the single scalar delay in standard formulations. Enables precise modeling of link-specific latencies in network-on-chip or in-network computing fabrics, critical for timing-aware design.
