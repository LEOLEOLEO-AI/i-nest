## Definition
A variant of the Axelrod cultural-dynamics model in which agent traits take continuous rather than discrete values, so interaction probability and convergence are governed by a continuous distance metric instead of exact trait matching. Provides a continuous-state consensus/fragmentation model that maps naturally onto analog neuromorphic state variables and topology-driven agreement dynamics in TCC networks.
