# Interpretable_FeatureExtraction

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
The process of deriving electrophysiological features (e.g., band power, phase-locking values) that have clear physiological meaning and are not black-box representations. Enables direct translation of clinical biomarkers into neuromorphic spike-timing rules or synaptic weight configurations, bridging neuroscience and hardware design.
