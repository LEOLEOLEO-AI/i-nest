# WaferScale_System

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-31

## Definition
A computing system built directly on a full silicon wafer, integrating processing elements, memory, and network-on-chip (NoC) into a single physical substrate. This substrate is the target platform for migrating SIGCOMM-level networking and topology innovations into TCC and iNEST architectures.
