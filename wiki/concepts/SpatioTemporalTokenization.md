## Definition
A method to convert spatio-temporal data into discrete or continuous tokens that preserve both spatial and temporal relationships for transformer-based architectures. Efficient tokenization is critical for mapping network topology and dynamic traffic patterns into a format suitable for neuromorphic processing units.
