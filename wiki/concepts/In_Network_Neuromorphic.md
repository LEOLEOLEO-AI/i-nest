**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A design approach where neuromorphic processing elements are embedded directly within the network infrastructure (e.g., NoC routers or wafer-level switches) to perform synaptic and neuronal functions in transit. This merges communication and computation, enabling low-latency, event-driven intelligent processing that is essential for real-time, large-scale cognitive systems.
