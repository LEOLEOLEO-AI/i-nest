# Surface_Phase_Transition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A critical phenomenon where the ordering behavior at the boundary of a system diverges from the bulk, often at a different critical temperature. Surface phase transitions are key to predicting how edge effects in network-on-chip or in-network computing systems can lead to localized computational instabilities or emergent behaviors.
