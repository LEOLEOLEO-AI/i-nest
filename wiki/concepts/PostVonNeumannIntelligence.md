## Definition
A class of computing architectures that abandon the strict separation of memory and processing, mimicking biological neural systems for energy-efficient, parallel, and adaptive computation. This framework guides the design of TCC chiplet interconnects and iNEST topologies toward non-von Neumann dataflow, reducing data movement overhead in large-scale neural systems.
