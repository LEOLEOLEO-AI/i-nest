# Demand_Robust_Routing

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-17

## Definition
A routing strategy designed to preserve performance under uncertain, time-varying, or adversarial traffic demand rather than optimizing only for a fixed demand matrix. It is essential for iNEST and TCC systems where event-driven neuromorphic traffic and dynamic topology conditions make static routing assumptions unreliable.
