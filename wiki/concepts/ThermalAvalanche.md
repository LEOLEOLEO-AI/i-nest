# ThermalAvalanche

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A sudden, cascading spread of thermal energy through a quasiperiodic quantum system, analogous to an avalanche in classical disordered systems. Understanding thermal avalanches is critical for predicting energy transport and stability in topological or neuromorphic architectures that rely on quasiperiodic lattices.
