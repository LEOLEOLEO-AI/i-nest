# EnergyEfficientOnlineLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The capability of a neuromorphic system to update its synaptic weights in real-time during inference, with minimal energy overhead per update, often enabled by local plasticity rules and low-precision arithmetic. A foundational requirement for adaptive in-network computing, allowing the network to continuously learn from streaming data without expensive offline retraining or external supervision.
