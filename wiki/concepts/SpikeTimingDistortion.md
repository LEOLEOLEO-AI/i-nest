# SpikeTimingDistortion

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
The deviation in spike emission times caused by quantization errors in membrane potential accumulation or threshold comparison, which can degrade temporal coding accuracy. Quantifying and minimizing this distortion is key to maintaining SNN performance in low-bitwidth neuromorphic implementations.
