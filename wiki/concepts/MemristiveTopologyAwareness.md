# MemristiveTopologyAwareness

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The design principle that the physical crossbar topology and interconnect layout of memristive arrays are explicitly considered during repair and learning algorithms. Bridges the gap between device-level physics and network-level computation, essential for scalable TCC architectures that rely on dense, irregular interconnects.
