# DynamicProgrammingRecycling

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A method that reuses the computational states or intermediate results of dynamic programming algorithms as inputs to a reservoir computer, rather than solving the combinatorial optimization problem from scratch. This enables efficient reuse of computational processes across similar optimization problems, potentially reducing energy and latency in in-network or topology-centric computing environments.
