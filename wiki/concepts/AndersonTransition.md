# AndersonTransition

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A quantum phase transition between a metallic (extended) state and an insulating (localized) state driven by disorder, characterized by critical exponents and universal scaling. Understanding Anderson transitions is crucial for assessing the resilience of topological interconnects to fabrication disorder and environmental noise in TCC systems.
