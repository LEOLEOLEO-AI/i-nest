## Definition
The six skeleton classes are redefined not as independent architectures but as implementation spaces for the six R-primitives (R.FUSE, R.SWAP, etc.), where each primitive maps to at least one skeleton variant. This unifies the skeleton taxonomy and provides a rigorous completeness criterion for the meta-topology library, enabling systematic search and optimization across primitive implementations.
