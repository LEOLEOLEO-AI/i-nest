# Feedforward_SNN_Temporal_Forgetting

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Definition
The limitation of feedforward spiking neural networks in which membrane potentials quickly decay, causing loss of historical context beyond a short window. This motivates dual-memory and recurrent architectures in iNEST for tasks that require long-range temporal reasoning.
