# SynapseLevelParallelism

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted

## Definition
A parallelism strategy in SNN accelerators that exploits the independent processing of individual synaptic connections, rather than neuron-level or layer-level parallelism, to improve throughput. This is a novel optimization axis for SNN hardware that can significantly reduce latency and energy consumption, which is critical for real-time neuromorphic computing in iNEST.
