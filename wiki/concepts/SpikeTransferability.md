# SpikeTransferability

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
The phenomenon where adversarial examples crafted for one spiking neural network (SNN) architecture or encoding scheme also fool other SNNs, often transferring across different spike-based models. This is critical for iNEST because it reveals fundamental vulnerabilities in spike-based neuromorphic systems, impacting the security of in-network inference and edge deployments.
