# AsymmetricStochasticInteractions

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
Stochastic binary network interactions where the influence of one node on another is not symmetric, meaning the probability of state transitions depends on directed, non-reciprocal coupling strengths. Enables modeling of realistic neural dynamics where synaptic connections are inherently asymmetric, critical for neuromorphic hardware that must replicate biological non-reciprocity.
