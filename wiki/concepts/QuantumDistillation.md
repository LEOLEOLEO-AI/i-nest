# QuantumDistillation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A process that takes multiple noisy magic states and produces fewer, higher-fidelity magic states through error-correcting encoding and measurement, often using protocols like Bravyi-Kitaev or surface-code-based distillation. Distillation is the primary method for achieving the high-quality magic states required for fault-tolerant quantum computation, and its efficiency directly affects the overall resource overhead in quantum systems, a key concern for iNEST's neuromorphic-inspired resource management.
