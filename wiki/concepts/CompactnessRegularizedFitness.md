# CompactnessRegularizedFitness

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A fitness function that penalizes architectural complexity (e.g., number of parameters, depth) alongside task accuracy, driving evolution toward minimal viable sequence models. Ensures discovered neuromorphic sequence circuits are energy-efficient and memory-light, critical for deployment in resource-limited in-network neuromorphic hardware.
