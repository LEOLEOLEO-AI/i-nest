# SelfOrganizedLearning

**Domain**: iNEST
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-04

## Definition
A learning paradigm in oscillatory neural networks where synaptic weights and network dynamics co-evolve through local activity-dependent plasticity, without external supervision or global error signals. It enables neuromorphic hardware to autonomously adapt to input statistics, reducing training overhead and supporting real-time in-network computation.
