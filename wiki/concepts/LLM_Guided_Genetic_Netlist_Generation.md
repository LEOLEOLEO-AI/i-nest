# LLM_Guided_Genetic_Netlist_Generation

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
An evolutionary synthesis loop in which a large language model proposes or rewrites candidate circuit netlists while a genetic algorithm selects and recombines them based on simulation fitness. Offers TCC and iNEST a way to automatically explore interconnect-centric circuit structures that are too large and irregular for manual or purely numeric optimization.
