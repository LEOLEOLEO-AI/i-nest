## Definition
The integration of spiking neural network architectures with neuroimaging data streams, converting continuous fMRI signals into spike-based representations for processing. This establishes a direct pathway from biological brain signals to neuromorphic computing systems, critical for TCC/iNEST applications in brain-computer interfaces and cognitive state monitoring.
