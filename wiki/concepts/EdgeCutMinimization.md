# EdgeCutMinimization

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-09-19

## Definition
The classical NoC mapping objective of minimizing the weight of graph edges spanning partitions, used here as the baseline that hypergraph-based approaches explicitly move beyond. It is the standard metric against which TCC mapping contributions are benchmarked, so understanding its limitations for multicast spiking traffic is essential.
