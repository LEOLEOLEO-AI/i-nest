# Griffiths_Phase

**Domain**: TCC
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A region in the phase diagram of a disordered system where the dynamical critical exponent diverges, leading to slow, non-universal relaxation dynamics and rare-event-dominated behavior. In topological quantum error correction, Griffiths phases imply that rare disorder configurations can dramatically slow down the decoding or error-correction process, challenging the assumption of uniform error thresholds.
