# EigenvalueLocalizationTransition

**Domain**: Cross
**First mentioned**: auto-extracted
**Last updated**: 2026-08-23

## Definition
A phase transition in the complex eigenvalue plane where eigenstates change from delocalized (extended) to localized as the correlation length or non-Hermiticity parameter is varied. Critical for designing iNEST reservoirs where controlled localization can separate input-driven dynamics from noise, improving computational capacity and memory.
