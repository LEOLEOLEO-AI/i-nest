> Spectral dimension is a key determinant of critical phenomena, but its role in nonreciprocal systems remains unexplored. Numerical phase diagrams in the $(d_s,\alpha)$ plane in complex networks, where $d_s$ denotes the spectral dimension, reveal a critical phase lag $\alpha_c$, below which spontaneous synchronization occurs.
