> Spiking neural networks (SNNs) are promoted as an energy-efficient substrate because sparse, event-driven activity replaces dense multiply-accumulates with cheap accumulates. We argue the energy dividend of sparsity is not a property of SNNs but of the task. Holding architecture fixed and swapping only the hidden unit (continuous vs. leaky-integrate-and-fire), plus a two-sided target-firing-rate probe, we measure how far activity can be pushed down before quality breaks.
