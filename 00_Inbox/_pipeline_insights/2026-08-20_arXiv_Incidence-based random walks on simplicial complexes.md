## Abstract

We introduce an incidence-based random walk on the edges of a random two-dimensional simplicial complex with a complete $1$-skeleton and independently retained triangular faces. The dynamics combine two transport channels, one mediated by vertices and the other by triangular faces, through an effective transition operator controlled by a mixing parameter $q$.
