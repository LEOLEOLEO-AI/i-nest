## Abstract

Time-evolving networks, or temporal networks, play a crucial role in modeling dynamic interactions across various domains, including biology, social sciences, and information technology. Unlike static networks, these systems undergo continuous changes in topology and edge weights, influencing processes such as information flow, transportation efficiency, and neural activity. Understanding and controlling these networks are essential for predicting future behavior and optimizing dynamic processes
