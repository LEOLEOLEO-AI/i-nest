## Abstract

To help scale to ever-larger and more complex designs, recent FPGA architectures now integrate network-on-chips (NoCs). NoCs help transfer high-bandwidth data over long distances within the chip without using scarce low-delay long routing wire segments. While NoC-enhanced FPGAs aid system integration and design reuse, they also complicate FPGA computer-aided design (CAD) flows by introducing new constraints and metrics.
