## Abstract

The growing demand for intelligent processing at the edge of IoT networks is constrained by the severe computational and memory limitations of microcontroller units, which render impractical conventional deep learning approaches. We propose a neuromorphicinspired classifier based on the Receptron model, a single-unit architecture capable of implementing non-linearly separable decision boundaries, without resorting to multi-layer networks.
