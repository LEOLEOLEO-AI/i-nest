## Abstract

The placement of chiplets on a silicon interposer is a pivotal step in 2.5D system integration, yet existing placement approaches typically assume a pre-defined interposer footprint. This creates a circular dependency: the optimal footprint cannot be known without first solving the placement, while the placement itself is constrained by the given dimensions.
