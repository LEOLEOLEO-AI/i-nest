## Abstract

As neuromorphic systems scale beyond a single core, inter-core event communication can become a dominant contributor to memory footprint, latency, and energy consumption. Biological neural systems address a similar scaling challenge through small-world organization, combining dense local connectivity with sparse long-range projections. In this work, we compare two recent multicore neuromorphic systems implemented in the same 22-nm FDSOI technology and explicitly optimized for such connectivity.
