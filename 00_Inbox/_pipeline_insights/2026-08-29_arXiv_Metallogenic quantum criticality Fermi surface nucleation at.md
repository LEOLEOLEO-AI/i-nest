## Abstract

Continuous quantum phase transitions in interacting many-body systems exhibit universal phenomena that are largely independent of microscopic details. An organizing principle distilled from canonical examples such as the magnetic transition in Ising models is that the low-energy physics of transitions between gapped phases is governed by scale-invariant quantum field theories with gapless degrees of freedom at isolated points in momentum space.
