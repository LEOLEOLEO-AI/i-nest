## 摘要

The large-scale distribution of galaxies in the Universe forms an intricate, interconnected network known as the cosmic web. Cosmological simulations within the standard Lambda-CDM framework successfully reproduce this filamentary structure and predict that the nodes and filaments are filled with tenuous plasma at temperatures ranging from 10^5-10^8 K. The hottest and luminous plasma in the nodes corresponds to the intra-cluster medium, while the cooler, more tenuous, gas extends along filaments
