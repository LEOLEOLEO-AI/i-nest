## Abstract

Modular quantum computing is a leading paradigm for scaling quantum computation beyond the resource limitations of monolithic devices. In this architecture, multiple quantum processing units (QPUs), employing identical or distinct qubit modalities, are interconnected via shared entanglement. Here, we investigate how errors at module interfaces and within individual QPUs affect fault-tolerant computation when qubits are encoded using the rotated surface code.
