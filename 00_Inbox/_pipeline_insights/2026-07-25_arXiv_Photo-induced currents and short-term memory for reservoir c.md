## Abstract

Physical reservoir computing represents an energy efficient approach for processing temporal signals by exploiting the intrinsic nonlinear dynamics and fading memory of a physical system. Recently, ferroelectric semiconductors moved into focus as reservoir materials motivated by their versatile electronic responses to external stimuli. Here, we explore the fundamental possibility to recognize time-varying light pulses via photo-induced currents, using the small-band-gap p-type semiconductor ErMn
