## Abstract

Reservoir Computing (RC) designs Recurrent Neural Networks around a fixed, i.e., untrained, recurrent layer, and is a natural candidate for neuromorphic hardware. Memristive-friendly reservoirs derive the neuron dynamics from memristive-device kinetics, but still rely on dense recurrent matrices, which are expensive to realize physically. In this paper, we replace the dense matrix with a structured orthogonal operator, built from sign diagonals, a permutation, and a fast Walsh-Hadamard transform
