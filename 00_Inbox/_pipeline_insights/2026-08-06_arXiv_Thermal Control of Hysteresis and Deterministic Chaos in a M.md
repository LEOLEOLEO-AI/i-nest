## Abstract

We investigate the nonlinear dynamics of a thermo-electro-mechanically coupled memristive resonator comprising a doubly clamped Euler--Bernoulli microbeam, an RLC circuit, and a TiO$_2$ memristor with temperature-dependent ionic mobility governed by Mott and Efros--Shklovskii hopping conduction.
