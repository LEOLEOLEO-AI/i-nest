## Abstract

Event-driven sensing supports energy-efficient Internet-of-Things (IoT) devices by activating communication only when relevant events occur. In such systems, transmission decisions are governed by the monitored process rather than predefined schedules. Consequently, jointly optimising information freshness and energy consumption is challenging because transmission decisions are restricted to randomly occurring events.
