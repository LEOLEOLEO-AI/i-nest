## Abstract

For an open quantum reservoir, how the system forgets is part of how it computes. Quantum reservoir computing processes input streams with fixed quantum dynamics and trains only a linear readout. Dissipation can make old inputs fade, but prior studies commonly fix the environmental process and tune only its strength. Here we show numerically that the coupling pattern, meaning whether transitions connect to separate or shared environmental channels, changes which parts of the input history remain
