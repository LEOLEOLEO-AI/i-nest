## Abstract

In this work, we propose a view on electronic Neuromorphic Design Automation (eNDA), which we see as a design automation flow that bridges computational neuroscience modeling with traditional Electronic Design Automation (EDA) flow. We introduce the term, give examples of how it can be implemented, and design a prototype implementation: Syn2Logic.
