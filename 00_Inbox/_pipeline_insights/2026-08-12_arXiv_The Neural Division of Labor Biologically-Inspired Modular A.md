## Abstract

Biological neural systems achieve high efficiency and robustness through compartmentalized architectures. In contrast, modern artificial neural networks rely on globally entangled structures, which obscure decision logic and suffer from catastrophic forgetting. Here, we report a Decomposable Spiking Neural Network (D-SNN) that eliminates global synaptic entanglement by structurally isolating classification pathways into independent experts.
