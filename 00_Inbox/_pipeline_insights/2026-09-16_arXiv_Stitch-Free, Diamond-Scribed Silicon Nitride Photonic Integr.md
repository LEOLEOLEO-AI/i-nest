## Abstract

Silicon nitride photonic integrated circuits for the visible band are conventionally built with a buried oxide overcladding and singulated with wafer-scale tooling, constraints that preclude evanescent access to the guided mode for externally integrated emitters. We report a PECVD grown $Si_3N_4$ platform designed around an air-clad waveguide whose evanescent field remains accessible along the full device length.
