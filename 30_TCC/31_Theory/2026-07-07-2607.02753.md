## 1. 核心创新

本文最重要的技术或理论突破？与现有方案核心区别？
verbatim: We present a spiking neural network (SNN) architecture for generating polar trajectories, using a winner-take-all (WTA) architecture with accessory populations that induce controlled transitions in neural activity. We demonstrate tuning rules for these population dynamics, and utilize a form of shunting inhibition to enable independent control of direction, speed, and radius of the resulting polar trajectories. We implement the network on the SpiNNaker2 neuromorphic processor, and demonstrate a two to three orders of magnitude reduction in wall-clock step time and three to four orders of magnitude reduction in energy expenditure when compared to conventional computing platforms.
answer: 本文最大的创新在于提出了一种专门用于极坐标轨迹生成的脉冲神经网络（SNN）新架构。主要技术突破包括：（1）采用winner-take-all（WTA）架构结合辅助神经群，能够实现神经活动的可控跃迁；（2）通过“分流抑制”，可独立调控轨迹方向、速度与半径，提升了系统的可解释性与调控自由度；（3）在SpiNNaker2类神经形态处理器上部署该架构，达到了相较传统计算平台2-3个数量级的运行速度提升及3-4个数量级的能耗降低。与现有方法相比，本文方案兼顾“神经形态动态的充分利用”与“控制逻辑的可解释性”，突破了以往端到端脉冲网络可解释性差及经典控制器移植后动态利用不足的局限。
