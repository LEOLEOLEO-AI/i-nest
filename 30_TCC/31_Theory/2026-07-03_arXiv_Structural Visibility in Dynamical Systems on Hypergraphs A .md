## 摘要

Hypergraphs encode rich multiway interactions, but not all structural information is equally accessible through the dynamics. By analyzing pattern-forming instabilities in reaction-diffusion systems on directed hypergraphs, this work develops a theory of structural visibility that characterizes which features of higher-order structure survive successive levels of dynamical reduction.
