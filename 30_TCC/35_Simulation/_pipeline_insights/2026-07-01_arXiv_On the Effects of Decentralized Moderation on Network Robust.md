## 摘要

Decentralized online social networks such as Mastodon distribute moderation power across thousands of independently governed servers, raising fundamental questions about how local block decisions shape global structure and information flow. In this paper, we analyze Mastodon at the instance level by constructing a signed, directed, temporal network in which positive edges aggregate inter-instance follow relationships and negative edges encode daily block actions.
