## 摘要

This article explore how different representations of higher-order networks-specifically simplicial complexes and hypergraphs-affect the dynamics of information spreading within heterogeneous community structures. We induce a higher-order Susceptible-Infected-Susceptible(SIS) model that incorporates both pairwise and triadic interactions, systematically simulating the spreading processes across networks characterized by varying degrees of heterogeneity, initial infection rates, and strengths of higher-order transmission. Our results show that higher-order interactions significantly reduce the epidemic threshold, with hypergraph-based representations generally facilitate more effective spreading than simplicial complexes. These results highlight the critical importance of selecting an appropriate higher-order modeling approach when analyzing complex contagion processes, particularly in heterogeneous systems.
