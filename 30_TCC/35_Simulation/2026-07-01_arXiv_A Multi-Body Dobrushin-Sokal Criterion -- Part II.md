## 摘要

We prove a sufficient condition for the absolute convergence of Mayer cluster expansions of log-partition and correlation functions applicable to lattice gases with possibly complex-valued multi-body interactions. Not only are several classical results subsumed but a partition scheme for spanning hypergraphs also makes our methods well-suited for treating stronger multi-body interactions, including higher-order hard-core repulsion in the context of hypergraph independence polynomials.
