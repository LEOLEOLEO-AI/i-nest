## 摘要

The paper proposes an approach for verifying integral persistent excitation, which is important in problems of parameter identification and adaptive control in nonlinear dynamical systems. The approach works for conservative polynomial ODEs a priori without knowledge of the parameters. Rigorous proofs of the corresponding theorems are provided.
