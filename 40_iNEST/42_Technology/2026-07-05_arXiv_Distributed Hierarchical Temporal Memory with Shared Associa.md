## 摘要

Anomaly detection in multivariate time series remains a critical challenge in large-scale distributed systems, where related entities may exhibit transferable precursor behavior prior to anomaly onset. Existing methods typically operate independently on each data stream and therefore remain fundamentally reactive. ## 核心创新
本论文提出了一种分布式层次时序记忆（D-HTM）框架，能够实现跨实体的预警功能，通过共享关联记忆来检测多变量时间序列中的异常。这种方法克服了传统方法的局限性，实现了主动预警。D-HTM框架的核心创新在于其能够处理多个实体之间的关联关系，实现跨实体的预警。
