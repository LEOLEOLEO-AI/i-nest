## Abstract

We study continuously monitored dynamics of a quasiperiodic free-fermion chain defined on a Fibonacci lattice. We focus on fluctuations of the local particle number, which exhibit a spatially uniform distribution in the unitary limit. Remarkably, we demonstrate that they exhibit a nonuniform spatial pattern originating from the quasiperiodic long-range order under continuous measurement.
