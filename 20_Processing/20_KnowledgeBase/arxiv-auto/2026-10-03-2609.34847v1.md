## 原始摘要

> Time series prediction is a common application of reservoir computing. When the training and testing time series data contains multiple dynamical regimes, because an underlying parameter is changing, or the data in fact consists of multiple distinct systems, simple application of the reservoir computing principle produces high prediction errors. Here, we propose a HyperReservoir as an extended model of reservoir computing especially designed for such cases. The HyperReservoir combines a main reservoir with a smaller context reservoir, where the latter modulates the output weights of the former
