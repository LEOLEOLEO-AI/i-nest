## 原始摘要
> The shortest path multiplicity, here denoted by $\mu$, is an important metric of complex networks. For real networks $\mu$ is high and it correlates with the network community structure. Since local network evolution induces network communities, it is possible that a high shortest path multiplicity is the natural expectation of local evolution rules. Here I demonstrate, by means of numerical simulations, that this is indeed the case. For random graphs with arbitrary degree distributions $p_k$, $\langle\mu\rangle\sim \langle k(k-1)\rangle / (\langle k\rangle e)$, growing with the network size w
