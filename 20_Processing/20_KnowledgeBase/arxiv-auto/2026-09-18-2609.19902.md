A cell persists only by self-replication: a living neighbor is cloned and its weights are mutated by a uniform perturbation, so that phenotype (cell state) is driven entirely by genotype (network weights). From a handful of seeded founders the system grows into a spatially organized ecosystem of coexisting, competing and dominating species. Our main contribution is a battery of coarse-grained diversity metrics that make such growth measurable at two scales... the substrate is persistent and self-maintaining in 20 of the 24 long configurations and exposes a clear phenotype-genotype diversity trade-off: raising phenotypic diversity collapses genotypic diversity and vice versa.
