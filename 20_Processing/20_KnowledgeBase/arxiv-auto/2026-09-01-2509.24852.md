verbatim: we introduce DelRec, the first method to jointly optimize recurrent delays with synaptic weights in RSNNs via surrogate gradient learning, compatible with any spiking neuron model. DelRec works in discrete time, leveraging differentiable interpolation to handle non-integer delays with well-defined gradients at training time. ... Analysis of trained networks show that recurrent delay optimization builds structured, depth-dependent spatio-temporal receptive fields that the network actively exploits, with delay-weight joint optimization reshaping temporal selectivity. Beyond accuracy, we show that recurrent delay learning can reduce the memory footprint and energy consumption of a network, and shapes its robustness to temporal perturbations of its inputs.
