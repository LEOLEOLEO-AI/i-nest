## 原始摘要

> We study a two-dimensional, off-lattice particle model introduced to describe absorbing phase transitions in driven non-Brownian suspensions. We numerically explore the $(\phi,\epsilon)$ phase diagram, where $\phi$ is the packing fraction and $\epsilon$ controls the amplitude of particle jumps. We use a binary mixture to suppress crystallization, which allows us to disentangle the model's distinct phase transitions between amorphous states. At large $\phi$, we find that the approach to the absorbing transition is preceded by a non-equilibrium glass transition to a non-diffusive amorphous state
