## 1. 核心创新

本文最重要的技术或理论突破？与现有方案核心区别？
verbatim: In this study we propose a new design of bursting electronic neuron constructed as a circuit implementation of the equations of a phase-locked loop system. To succeed, we use a novel hybrid approach: we start from the phenomenological equations providing the demanded, then we adjust and modify these equations to simplify the implementation rather than implementing the biophysical equations into thee hardware directly or writing equations for the already constructed circuit. The resulting circuit is simple in implementation and well matches the underlying equations.
answer: 本文的核心创新在于：提出了一种基于锁相环(phase-locked loop, PLL)系统方程实现爆发型电子神经元的新型电路设计方法，并采用了独特的“先从现象学方程出发、再删减优化便于电路实现”的混合策略。与现有绝大多数做法（如直接硬件化生物物理模型或直接搭建电路再事后建模）相比，该方案可做到“电路结构简单、与数学模型高度一致、便于小型化和扩展神经回路”，兼具理论通用性和硬件实现的经济性。
