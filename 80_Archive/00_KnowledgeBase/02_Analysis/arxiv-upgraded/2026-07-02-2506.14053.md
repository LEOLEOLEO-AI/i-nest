## 1. 核心创新

本文最重要的技术或理论突破？与现有方案核心区别？
verbatim: Abstract: The phenomenological renormalization group (PRG) has been applied to the study of scaleinvariant phenomena in neuronal data, providing evidence for critical phenomena in the brain. However, it remains unclear how reliably these observed signatures indicate genuine critical behavior, as it is not well established how close to criticality a system must be for them to emerge. Here, we rely on neuronal models with known critical points to investigate under which conditions the PRG procedure yields consistent results. We show that the PRG method detects scaling behavior in neuronal models only within a narrow vicinity of the critical point, reinforcing the interpretations drawn from PRG results in experimental data. We also demonstrate that time-binning choices can substantially affect the results and introduce a data-driven adaptive binning procedure to circumvent this issue.
answer: 本文的核心创新在于系统性检验了现有 PRG（现象学重整化群）方法在神经系统中的适用边界，证明该法仅在系统接近临界点的极窄区域才能准确检测出临界标度行为，大大增强了其在实验数据中解释的严格性。此外，首次明确指出时间分箱策略会极大影响结果，并提出了一种数据驱动的自适应分箱方法，有效规避了这一问题。与当前大多泛泛分析临界现象的研究相比，本工作首次基于已知临界点的神经模型，精准界定了 PRG 检测临界性的边界条件及其误差来源，技术和理论上均具有开创性。
